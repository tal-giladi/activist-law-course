# 02.1 · Finding the Current Text of a Law

By the end of this lesson you will be able to find the exact, current wording of any Israeli law or regulation without paying for a database, check that nobody has amended it since the copy you are reading was made, and write it down in a form you can cite in a letter, a complaint or a petition.

## Why it matters

Every document you will write in this course stands on a quoted section of law. If the section you quote was amended last year, the authority will answer that you relied on an old text, and you lose credibility before anyone reads your argument. Deadlines, fees and the official who must answer you all live in sections that change.

Israeli law is published officially in **Reshumot** (רשומות), the official gazette. Laws appear in *Sefer HaHukim* (ספר החוקים), regulations in *Kovetz HaTakanot* (קובץ התקנות). An amendment is published as a separate short law that says "in section 5, replace X with Y". Nobody reads the gazette page by page; you read a **consolidated** copy that someone has updated with all the amendments, and then you check that the copy is up to date. This lesson teaches that check.

## Key terms

| עברית | Transliteration | English |
|---|---|---|
| רשומות | Reshumot | The official gazette where laws and regulations are published |
| ספר החוקים | Sefer HaHukim | The gazette series for Knesset laws (cited ס״ח) |
| קובץ התקנות | Kovetz HaTakanot | The gazette series for regulations (cited ק״ת) |
| נוסח משולב | nusach meshulav | Consolidated version: a law re-enacted with all its amendments merged |
| נוסח חדש | nusach chadash | New version: a Mandate-era law re-enacted in Hebrew |
| תיקון עקיף | tikun akif | Indirect amendment: a change made by a different law (often a budget "arrangements" law) |
| הוראת שעה | hora'at sha'a | Temporary provision with an end date |
| תחילה | tchila | Commencement: the date a provision takes effect |
| מאגר החקיקה הלאומי | ma'agar hachakika haleumi | The Knesset's National Legislation Database |

## The law

Legislation takes effect when it is published in Reshumot, unless it says otherwise:

> (א) כל פקודה תקבל תוקף ביום פרסומה ברשומות, בלתי אם נקבע בה כי תקבל תוקף בתאריך מוקדם או מאוחר מיום הפרסום.

*Gist:* an enactment takes effect on the day it is published in the gazette unless it sets an earlier or later date, so always check the publication date and the commencement section. ([פקודת סדרי השלטון והמשפט, s. 10](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_סדרי_השלטון_והמשפט#סעיף_10))

> שעת תחילת תקפו של חיקוק היא בשעה 00.01 של יום תחילתו.

*Gist:* a provision is in force from one minute past midnight on its commencement day. ([חוק הפרשנות, s. 21](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפרשנות#סעיף_21))

> הנוסח המחייב של כל דין הוא הנוסח שבשפה שבה הוא ניתן; אולם בדין שניתן באנגלית לפני הקמת המדינה ונקבע לו נוסח חדש לפי סעיף 16 לפקודת סדרי השלטון והמשפט, התש״ח–1948 – הנוסח החדש מחייב.

*Gist:* the binding text is the Hebrew one (or the Hebrew "new version" of a Mandate law), so an English translation is never what you quote to an Israeli authority. ([חוק הפרשנות, s. 24](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הפרשנות#סעיף_24))

> נוסח משולב יכיל את כל השינויים שחלו באותו חוק מכוח חקיקה.

*Gist:* a consolidated version merges every amendment into one text; that is why some laws carry "[נוסח משולב]" in their title. ([פקודת סדרי השלטון והמשפט, s. 16(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_סדרי_השלטון_והמשפט#סעיף_16))

## How it works in practice

You use three free tools, in this order. None needs signup.

### 1. Hebrew Wikisource — "Open Law Book" (ספר החוקים הפתוח)

[ספר החוקים הפתוח](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/ספר_החוקים_הפתוח) is an A–Z index of Israeli laws and many regulations, each as a full, searchable, consolidated text maintained by volunteers. It is the fastest place to read a law.

- Search the law's name, or open the index and find it alphabetically. Search engines also work: type the law's Hebrew name plus "ויקיטקסט".
- At the top of each law is a box listing every publication in Reshumot: the original law and each amendment, with year and page, each linked to the scanned gazette PDF. **The last entry tells you how up to date the copy claims to be.**
- Section anchors work: add `#סעיף_15` to the address to jump to section 15.
- Look for notes in the text such as "(החל מיום …)" — a new wording that starts on a future date — and "(בוטל)" for repealed items. Courts Law s. 68, for example, shows a second wording marked to start on 29.7.2027. Until that date the first wording is the law.
- Regulations that update amounts every year (fees, ceilings) carry a note such as "כל הסכומים מעודכנים לשנת 2026". If the note shows last year, the amounts may be stale.

### 2. The Knesset National Legislation Database (מאגר החקיקה הלאומי)

The [National Legislation Database](https://main.knesset.gov.il/apps/legislation/main/laws) is the Knesset's official record. Each law has a page at `main.knesset.gov.il/apps/legislation/main/laws/<number>`; for example, the Decisions and Reasons Law is [2000832](https://main.knesset.gov.il/apps/legislation/main/laws/2000832). Many Wikisource pages show this number in their top box and link to it.

The page lists the law's original publication, **every amendment including indirect ones**, bills still in progress that would amend it, and links to the gazette PDFs. Compare its newest amendment with the newest entry in the Wikisource box. If the Knesset lists something newer, the Wikisource text is behind: open the amendment PDF and apply it by hand.

The site is built with JavaScript and loads slowly; give it a few seconds. It covers Knesset laws well; for regulations it is less complete.

### 3. The gazette itself

When it matters — a deadline, a fee, a section you will build a petition on — open the gazette PDF of the latest amendment. Recent laws are on `fs.knesset.gov.il`; older gazettes, including regulations, are scanned at [olaw.org.il](https://olaw.org.il/), which Wikisource links to. The gazette is the official text.

### A fourth, optional check

Nevo's statute pages (`nevo.co.il/law_html/…`) are free and useful as a second consolidated copy. Nevo's case-law pages are paid; you will not need them (lesson 02.2).

### How long it takes

Finding a law: 2–5 minutes. Checking it is current: 5–10 minutes. A full gazette check: 15 minutes. Fees and deadlines that change every year are collected for you on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md), with the date each was last checked.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Yael, who runs a moshav farmers' association, wrote to a ministry eight weeks ago asking it to renew a water allocation. No answer. A friend tells her "the law says they must answer within 45 days". Before she writes a follow-up letter, she wants the exact section.

1. **Find it.** She searches "חוק החלטות והנמקות ויקיטקסט" and lands on *חוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות), התשי״ט–1958*. Section 2(א) reads: "נתבקש עובד הציבור, בכתב, להשתמש בסמכות שניתנה לו על פי דין, יחליט בבקשה וישיב למבקש בכתב בהקדם, אך לא יאוחר מארבעים וחמישה ימים מיום קבלת הבקשה."
2. **Read the box.** The top box lists the original law (ס״ח תשי״ט) and amendments up to תשנ״ה (1995), and shows the Knesset database number 2000832.
3. **Cross-check.** She opens the Knesset page for 2000832. The newest amendment listed is also from 1995, and there is no pending bill. The Wikisource copy is current.
4. **Read around the section.** She reads s. 2(ב): the 45 days do not apply where another law sets a different deadline, or the matter needs investigation. She checks whether the water law sets its own deadline (it does not, for her request). She also notices s. 6(ב): no answer within three months counts as a refusal without reasons — useful later.
5. **Record it.** In her notes: *חוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות), התשי״ט–1958, s. 2(א). Checked on Wikisource and the Knesset database (2000832) on 12.10.2026; last amendment 1995.*

Her letter (lesson 02.5) now quotes the exact words, and she knows the exceptions the ministry might raise.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A cause or problem you care about (you will use the same one through the whole module and in the project).
- A computer and 45 minutes.
- A notes file or the research-memo template: [research-memo.md](../../templates/research-memo.md).

### Steps

1. Write one sentence describing your problem, for example "the council removed our protest signs" or "the clinic in our town is not wheelchair accessible".
2. Find **one law** that governs it. If you do not know which, search Wikisource and Kol Zchut with two or three Hebrew keywords; Kol Zchut pages end with a "מקורות משפטיים ורשמיים" list that names the laws (lesson 02.3 goes deeper).
3. Open the law on Wikisource. Find the one or two sections that matter most and copy them exactly.
4. Read the top box. Write down the newest amendment and its year.
5. Open the law's page in the Knesset database. Compare the newest amendment. Note any pending bill.
6. Search the law page for "החל מיום", "הוראת שעה" and "בוטל" near your sections.
7. If your problem involves a regulation (fees, forms, procedures), repeat steps 3–6 for it.
8. Write a **law card**: full Hebrew title with year, section numbers, the exact quote, a one-line English gist, Knesset database number, newest amendment, and "checked on" date.

### Template

Use section 3 ("Law") of [research-memo.md](../../templates/research-memo.md) as your law card.

### How you know it worked

- Your newest amendment matches on Wikisource and in the Knesset database, or you found and applied the newer amendment from the gazette.
- Someone else could find your exact section in under a minute from your card.

### Self-check

- [ ] I quoted the Hebrew text exactly, without translating or paraphrasing it.
- [ ] I wrote the full title, including the Hebrew year.
- [ ] I compared the amendment list with the Knesset database.
- [ ] I checked for future-dated wording, temporary provisions and repeals.
- [ ] My card has a "checked on" date.

## Learn more

None. The three tools above are the learning material; spend the time using them.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quoting an outdated copy (old PDF, blog, a law-firm site) | The authority answers that the section was amended; your letter loses weight | Send a short correction quoting the current section | Always start from Wikisource and cross-check the Knesset database |
| Quoting an English translation | The Hebrew text binds (Interpretation Law s. 24); the translation may differ | Re-quote from the Hebrew | Use translations only to understand, never to cite |
| Missing a future-dated wording | You argue from a rule that has not started yet | Check the start date and argue from the wording in force today | Search the page for "החל מיום" |
| Ignoring an indirect amendment in an "arrangements law" | Your deadline or fee is wrong | Apply the amendment from the gazette | Read the Knesset page's full amendment list, not only amendments that carry the law's own name |
| Using last year's fee or ceiling | Your filing is rejected or underpaid | Pay the difference or refile | Use [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) and the regulation's "מעודכנים לשנת" note |

## Review

- The binding text is the Hebrew text published in Reshumot; everything else is a copy.
- Read on Wikisource, verify against the Knesset National Legislation Database, and open the gazette when it matters.
- Compare the newest amendment in both places; a mismatch means the copy is behind.
- Watch for "החל מיום", "הוראת שעה" and "בוטל" around your section.
- Every law you cite gets a card with its full title, section, quote and "checked on" date.
