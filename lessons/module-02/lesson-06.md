# 02.6 · Affidavits

By the end of this lesson you will be able to write your own affidavit — the sworn written statement that proves facts in petitions, applications and many administrative procedures — choose who will certify it, know what it should cost, and avoid the errors that get affidavits rejected or turn them against you.

## Why it matters

Courts and many authorities do not take your word for facts in a letter. A petition, an urgent application, a request for a fee exemption and many government forms must be **supported by an affidavit**: a written statement you sign after being formally warned that you must tell the truth. Petitions are regularly delayed or struck out because the affidavit is missing, unsigned, uncertified or covers facts the signer did not see.

An affidavit is also serious: a false affidavit is a criminal offence, and anyone who signs one can be called to court and cross-examined on it. Write only what you will stand behind in a witness box.

## The law

> מקום שמותר לאדם על־פי דין, או שנדרש אדם, להוכיח דבר על־ידי תצהיר בכתב, בשבועה או בהן צדק, יהיה תצהירו בכתב ראיה כשרה, אם הוזהר המצהיר כי עליו להצהיר את האמת וכי יהא צפוי לענשים הקבועים בחוק אם לא יעשה כן.

*Gist:* a written affidavit is valid evidence only if the declarant was warned to tell the truth and of the penalties for not doing so. ([פקודת הראיות, s. 15(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_הראיות#סעיף_15))

> (ב) האזהרה לפי סעיף קטן (א) תינתן, ונתינתה תאושר על פני התצהיר, על ידי אחד מאלה:
> (1) שופט;
> (2) דיין בבית דין דתי;
> (3) היועץ המשפטי לממשלה, פרקליט המדינה, פרקליטי מחוז, פרקליטי נפה, וסגני פרקליט המדינה ופרקליטי המחוז ועוזריהם;
> (4) עורך־דין;
> (5) ראש רשות מקומית;
> (6) אדם אחר שהסמיכו לכך שר המשפטים.

*Gist:* the warning must be given and certified on the affidavit by a judge, a religious-court judge, certain State Attorney lawyers, any lawyer, the head of a local authority, or someone the Minister of Justice authorised. ([s. 15(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_הראיות#סעיף_15))

> מי שביודעין מוסר תצהיר שקר, בין בשבועה או בהן צדק ובין שלא בשבועה ושלא בהן צדק, לפני מי שמוסמך לקבל את התצהיר, דינו – מאסר שלוש שנים.

*Gist:* knowingly making a false affidavit carries up to three years' imprisonment. ([חוק העונשין, s. 239](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_העונשין#סעיף_239))

> (א) תצהיר יהיה ערוך בגוף ראשון, מחולק לסעיפים ומכיל רק עובדות שהמצהיר יכול להעיד מתוך ידיעתו הוא; בתצהיר המצורף לכתב טענות רשאי הוא להצהיר גם לפי מיטב ידיעתו או אמונתו ובלבד שיציין את מקור אמונתו או ידיעתו.

*Gist:* in civil courts an affidavit is in the first person, in numbered paragraphs, and contains only facts the declarant knows personally; in an affidavit supporting a pleading you may add beliefs if you state their source. ([תקנות סדר הדין האזרחי, reg. 178(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_178))

> הוראות סעיף 15 אינן גורעות מכוחו של בית המשפט לצוות כי המצהיר ייחקר בבית המשפט, ובית המשפט ייעתר לבקשתו של בעל דין לצוות על כך; לא התייצב המצהיר, רשאי בית המשפט לפסול תצהירו מלשמש ראיה.

*Gist:* the other side can ask to cross-examine you on your affidavit, and if you do not appear the court may disregard it. ([פקודת הראיות, s. 17(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_הראיות#סעיף_17))

The Ordinance's Second Schedule adds form rules: the affidavit states the declarant's **full name, occupation and address** (item 2), and any correction made **before** signing must be initialled by the certifier in the margin (item 4).

## How it works in practice

### The structure

1. Title: "תצהיר".
2. Opening: "אני הח״מ, [full name], ת״ז [number], מ[address], לאחר שהוזהרתי כי עליי לומר את האמת וכי אהיה צפוי/ה לעונשים הקבועים בחוק אם לא אעשה כן, מצהיר/ה בזה בכתב כדלקמן:"
3. Paragraph 1: who you are and why you are giving the affidavit ("אני העותר/ת בעתירה…", "אני חבר/ת הוועד…").
4. Numbered paragraphs of **facts**, in date order, each one a single fact. Say how you know each one ("ראיתי", "שמעתי מ…", "המסמך מצורף כנספח 2").
5. Last paragraph: "זהו שמי, זו חתימתי ותוכן תצהירי אמת."
6. Your signature.
7. The **certification** (אישור), completed by the certifier: date, place, your identification, the warning given, your signature in their presence, their name, signature and stamp.

### Facts, not arguments

An affidavit proves facts. Legal arguments, opinions and adjectives belong in the petition or letter. "On 3 May at 10:00 I saw two municipal inspectors remove the sign" is a fact. "The municipality acted illegally" is an argument; leave it out.

### Who certifies, and what it costs

| Certifier | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Any lawyer** (עורך דין) | No fixed price. The Bar's *recommended* minimum fee for drafting **and** certifying an affidavit is about ₪512 for 2026; certifying a document you wrote yourself usually costs less. Ask the price before you go. | The usual choice. You sign in front of the lawyer, who checks your ID and gives the warning. |
| **Lawyer by video call** | As above | Allowed under Bar ethics decisions made permanent in 2021, if conditions are met: identification through the government digital-identity system, both of you in Israel, ID shown on camera, the warning read out and the process recorded (details on [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/תצהיר)). |
| **Notary** (נוטריון) | Fixed by regulation: about ₪200 plus VAT for the first declarant in 2026 | Mostly needed for documents used abroad; more than you need for an Israeli court. |
| **Head of a local authority** (ראש רשות מקומית) | Not set by law | Allowed by s. 15(ב)(5); in practice ask the mayor's office whether they do it. |
| **Judge** | — | Usually only during a hearing. |

The amounts are on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) once merged; always check before you go. If cost is a problem, ask a university legal clinic or an NGO you work with whether a volunteer lawyer can certify it (see [Where to Get Help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).

### On the day

- Bring the **unsigned** affidavit, printed, plus your ID card and the attachments.
- Read it once more. Correct mistakes **before** signing; the certifier initials each correction.
- Sign in front of the certifier. Never sign in advance and bring it signed.
- Never change anything after it is certified. A changed affidavit is invalid; make a new one.
- Attachments are marked "נספח 1", "נספח 2", and the lawyer may stamp them too.

### Writing for yourself only

You write and sign **your own** affidavit. Drafting affidavits for other people as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20). In a group, each witness writes their own, in their own words.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Daniel heads an independent local list running in municipal elections. On a Friday morning municipal inspectors removed his list's campaign signs from a street where other lists' signs stayed up. His group plans an urgent administrative petition, and the petition must be supported by an affidavit.

1. **List the facts Daniel saw himself.** Time and place; two inspectors in municipal uniform; the van's number; which signs were removed; which other signs remained; his photos taken at 10:05.
2. **Separate what he did not see.** A neighbour told him the inspectors said "orders from above". Daniel did not hear it, so it does not go in his affidavit as fact; the neighbour can give her own affidavit.
3. **Write.** Daniel uses the template, one fact per paragraph, each photo attached as a numbered exhibit with the paragraph that refers to it. No adjectives, no argument.
4. **Check against the petition.** Every fact the petition relies on appears in an affidavit, either his or the neighbour's.
5. **Certify.** He calls three lawyers in town, asks the price for certifying an affidavit he wrote himself, and picks the cheapest. He signs in front of the lawyer, who checks his ID, gives the warning and completes the certification.
6. **Be ready.** He knows the municipality may ask to cross-examine him, so he rereads the affidavit and is ready to repeat every line.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your issue and argument from lessons 02.4–02.5.
- The template: [affidavit.md](../../templates/affidavit.md).
- 50 minutes.

### Steps

1. Choose the facts in your issue that someone could dispute. List them.
2. Mark each fact: **K** (I know it personally), **B** (I believe it, from a source), or **O** (someone else knows it).
3. Write your affidavit with the template, using only K facts, one per numbered paragraph, in date order. Add B facts only if your procedure allows it, and name the source.
4. For each O fact, note who would need to give their own affidavit.
5. Attach your evidence as numbered exhibits and refer to each one in a paragraph.
6. Remove every adjective, opinion and legal argument.
7. Find two certifiers near you (a lawyer, a clinic) and ask the price of certifying an affidavit you wrote yourself. Write down the answers and the date.
8. Do **not** sign. This is a practice draft; you sign only in front of a certifier when you need it for a real filing.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you use the affidavit in a real filing: act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. A false affidavit is a criminal offence.

### How you know it worked

- Every paragraph is a single fact you could repeat under cross-examination.
- The opening has your full name, ID number and address, and the warning text.
- You know who will certify it and what it will cost.

### Self-check

- [ ] First person, numbered paragraphs.
- [ ] Only facts I know personally (or beliefs with their source, if allowed).
- [ ] No arguments, opinions or adjectives.
- [ ] Every exhibit is numbered and referred to.
- [ ] Left unsigned until I am in front of the certifier.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petition or application filed without an affidavit | Court orders it filed, or strikes the filing | File a certified affidavit quickly | Check the procedure's requirements before filing |
| Signed at home, then "certified" | No valid warning; the affidavit is not evidence | Sign a new affidavit in front of the certifier | Sign only in the certifier's presence |
| Changing a paragraph after certification | Invalid affidavit | Make and certify a new one | Correct before signing; certifier initials corrections |
| Facts you heard from others stated as your own | Collapses under cross-examination; risk of a false affidavit | Withdraw and replace; get the witness's own affidavit | Mark every fact K / B / O before writing |
| Arguments and adjectives in the affidavit | The court disregards those parts; weakens credibility | Move arguments to the petition | One fact per paragraph |
| Not appearing for cross-examination | The court may disregard the affidavit (s. 17) | Ask to reschedule in advance | Only sign what you are ready to defend in court |

## Review

- An affidavit is evidence only after a warning given and certified by an authorised person (Evidence Ordinance s. 15).
- Any lawyer can certify it; notaries charge a regulated fee; prices for lawyers vary, so ask first.
- First person, numbered paragraphs, facts you know personally, exhibits numbered.
- A false affidavit is an offence (Penal Law s. 239), and you can be cross-examined on it.
- Write your own affidavit; never draft them for others for payment.
