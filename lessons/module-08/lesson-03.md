# 08.3 · Threat Letters and SLAPP Suits: What to Do

By the end of this lesson you will know what to do in the first hour, the first day and the first week after a lawyer's letter threatens you with a defamation claim, how to choose your answer, and how to write it in Hebrew — and you will be able to recognise a suit whose real aim is to silence you, and the tools the courts have against it.

> [!WARNING]
> A demand letter or a statement of claim is a legal threat with real money behind it. Do not ignore a statement of claim that a court served on you: missing the deadline for a defence can lead to judgment against you without a hearing. Your reply to a letter can be used as evidence later. If the sum is large or the facts are disputed, get a lawyer or a clinic to read your reply before you send it (see [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)). This lesson is not legal advice.

## Why it matters

Most defamation disputes involving activists start, and many end, with a letter. A lawyer writes on behalf of a company, an official, an organisation or a public figure: remove the post, publish an apology, pay a large sum "without proof of damage", within seven days. The letter is designed to frighten, and it often works: people delete everything, apologise for things that were true, or stop campaigning.

Some of those letters are justified — the post really went too far, and a quick correction is the cheapest and fairest outcome. Others are the first step of a SLAPP (Strategic Lawsuit Against Public Participation, תביעת השתקה): a claim brought less to win than to make criticism expensive. Activists on every side have been on both ends of such letters. Knowing how to tell the difference, and how to answer, keeps both your money and your voice.

## The law

There is no statute that tells you how to answer a demand letter, and no anti-SLAPP statute in force. The rules you work with are the defences (08.2), the mitigation rule in s. 19, and the Civil Procedure Regulations.

> (ב) כתב הגנה יוגש בתוך שישים ימים ממועד המצאת כתב התביעה לידי הנתבע ... בית המשפט רשאי להאריך את המועדים כאמור אם השתכנע כי קיימים טעמים המצדיקים זאת.

*Gist:* in the regular civil courts you have 60 days from service of a statement of claim to file a defence; the court may extend. ([Civil Procedure Regulations, reg. 9(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי)) Small claims have their own, shorter procedure — read the summons (Module 11).

> (א) בית המשפט רשאי להורות על מחיקת כתב תביעה בכל עת על יסוד אחד מנימוקים אלה:
> (1) כתב התביעה אינו מגלה עילת תביעה;
> (2) מכתב התביעה עולה כי התביעה היא טרדנית או קנטרנית;

*Gist:* a court may strike out a claim that discloses no cause of action or is vexatious on its face (reg. 41); reg. 42 adds striking out for abuse of process, and reg. 4 defines abuse as acting to disrupt, delay or harass a party, or disproportionately to the dispute.

> (ג) סבר בית המשפט שבעל דין עשה שימוש לרעה בהליכי משפט או לא מילא אחר תקנות אלה, רשאי הוא לחייבו בהוצאות לטובת הנפגע או לטובת אוצר המדינה

*Gist:* a party who abuses the process can be ordered to pay costs to the injured party or to the state. ([reg. 151(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי))

*Gist:* the proposed s. 7ב would let a court make an abusive defamation plaintiff pay the defendant up to the amount claimed. It is a bill, not law. ([P/6532/25](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lst_11195949.pdf))

## How it works in practice

### First hour: preserve

1. **Do not reply in anger and do not post the letter with comments about the sender yet.**
2. **Archive** the publication exactly as it is: screenshot with date and URL, the comments, the number of views or shares, and a PDF or web-archive copy. Do this *before* you change anything.
3. Write down the dates: when the post went up, when the letter arrived, the deadline in the letter.

### First day: analyse

4. Read the letter line by line. Which text exactly? Which words are said to be defamatory? What is demanded (removal, apology, correction, money, a promise)?
5. Run the four questions (08.1) and the defence analysis (08.2) on those words. What is your sting, and can you prove it?
6. Check the sender: is the claim plausible on the facts, or does it show SLAPP signs (below)?
7. If the sum is large, the facts are disputed or a claim is likely, contact a lawyer, a university clinic or a public-interest organisation from [where to get help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md). Organisations across the spectrum support activists threatened over speech.

### First week: decide and reply

| Option | When it fits | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Stand by it** — reply that the publication is true and in the public interest, or protected opinion, with no admission | You have the evidence and a clear defence | A claim may follow; you must be ready to prove it |
| **Correct part** — fix one sentence, keep the rest | One detail was wrong or overstated | Low; shows good faith (s. 19) |
| **Remove without admission** — take it down "to avoid a dispute", no apology | The point is made; the fight is not worth it | A claim for the past publication is still possible, but less likely |
| **Apologise** | You were wrong | Unconditional apology in the same place mitigates (s. 19); a vague one may not |
| **No reply** | The letter is plainly baseless and no claim seems likely | The court may later see silence as a lost chance to resolve; usually reply briefly |

Whatever you choose:

- Reply **in writing**, marked "בלי לפגוע בזכויות", short and polite.
- **Do not repeat** the disputed accusation in the reply in a way that spreads it further, and do not copy the reply to a large list.
- Do not promise "never to write about the company again". Agree, at most, not to repeat a specific sentence.
- Do not pay a demand sum because of a deadline in a letter: only a court can order payment.
- Keep every exchange. If it ends in court, the correspondence shows your good faith.

### If you are sued

- Note the service date; count the deadline for a defence (60 days in the Magistrates' Court, shorter in small claims); calendar it.
- Consider mediation: many defamation claims settle, often with a correction and no payment.
- If the claim shows SLAPP signs, ask the lawyer about striking out (regs. 41–42) and costs for abuse of process (reg. 151(ג)).

### SLAPP signs

Based on the IDI study and the Knesset Research Center review:

- A powerful plaintiff (company, public body's officials, wealthy individual) against individuals or a small group.
- The publication is part of public debate: a planning objection, a consumer review, a protest, a petition, a report.
- High sums, usually the s. 7א ceiling or several times it, against many defendants at once.
- Little attention to the defences, or claims over opinions and over true, documented facts.
- Timing that matches a campaign milestone (a hearing, a vote, a tender).

## Worked example

**Situation.** Residents of a moshav in the Lower Galilee oppose a planned quarry expansion and file planning objections (Module 10). In a Facebook group, twelve residents comment on the company's application. Three weeks later each receives a letter from the quarry company's lawyers: remove your comments, publish an apology and pay ₪169,000 within 7 days. One resident, Michal Hadad, wrote: "This company lies in its environmental report — the dust figures are invented."

1. **Preserve.** Michal screenshots her comment, the post it was under and the thread, with dates; she saves the letter and notes the deadline.
2. **Analyse.** "Lies" and "invented" accuse the company of deliberate falsehood (a fact). Her evidence: the company's report gives dust levels lower than the Ministry of Environmental Protection's monitoring station 2 km away. She can prove a discrepancy, not that anyone invented anything.
3. **SLAPP signs.** A company against twelve residents, the maximum sum for each, comments made during a planning objection process, a letter timed two weeks before the planning hearing. Strong signs.
4. **Decision.** Michal stands by the substance and corrects the overstatement. She edits her comment to: "The dust figures in the company's report are far lower than the Ministry's station 2 km away (link). The planning committee should demand an independent measurement."
5. **Reply.** Using the [reply template](../../templates/reply-to-threat-letter.md), option B: the publication concerns a planning matter of clear public interest; the figures are documented; the comment has been rephrased to state the documented discrepancy; there is no basis for compensation; any claim will be defended, and costs will be sought.
6. **Together.** The twelve residents coordinate, consult a clinic once for all of them, and reply separately but consistently. None pays. The planning objection continues.

**Mirror test.** The same steps apply to a right-wing blogger who receives a letter from a human-rights NGO over a post saying the NGO "fabricates testimonies": preserve, test the sting, correct what cannot be proven, stand by what can.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A demand letter: a real one you received, or the practice letter below.
- The publication it concerns, and your 08.1 scan and 08.2 evidence file for it.
- [reply-to-threat-letter.md](../../templates/reply-to-threat-letter.md).
- 60 minutes.

Practice letter (fictional): *"Our client, Green Valley Developers Ltd., demands that within 7 days you remove your post of 3 March stating that 'Green Valley built on the protected wadi without a permit and bribed someone to get away with it', publish an apology, and pay ₪84,000, failing which we will sue without further notice."* Assume you hold an inspection report showing construction in the wadi without a permit, and no evidence of a bribe.

### Steps

1. List what you would preserve and do it (for the practice letter, write the list).
2. Split the disputed text into sentences. For each: fact or opinion, evidence, defence.
3. Score the SLAPP signs (0–5).
4. Choose an option from the table, sentence by sentence (for the practice letter: stand by "built without a permit"; remove "bribed someone").
5. Draft the reply in Hebrew using the template; keep it under one page.
6. Write the corrected version of the publication, if any.
7. Calendar: the letter's deadline (not binding, but plan to answer before it) and, if sued, the defence deadline.

### Template

[reply-to-threat-letter.md](../../templates/reply-to-threat-letter.md) — a Hebrew reply with three options: standing by the publication, correcting part of it without admission, and asking for particulars.

### How you know it worked

- The reply contains no new accusation and does not repeat the disputed one more than needed to identify it.
- Each sentence of the original publication has a decision and a reason.
- Nothing in the reply admits liability or promises money.

### Self-check

- [ ] I archived the publication before changing it.
- [ ] I separated provable facts from unprovable ones and from opinion.
- [ ] My reply is marked "בלי לפגוע בזכויות", polite and short.
- [ ] I know that the letter's deadline is not a court deadline, and that a court summons is.
- [ ] I know where to get help if a claim is filed.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deleting the post before archiving it | You cannot prove what you wrote or its context | Ask friends for screenshots; check web archives | Archive first, always |
| Paying the demand sum because of the 7-day deadline | Money lost; may be read as an admission | Get advice before any payment | Only a court can order payment |
| A reply that repeats the accusation to a wide list | A new publication; a new claim | Stop circulating it | Reply only to the sender |
| An apology "if anyone was offended, but the facts are true" | No s. 19 mitigation; the dispute continues | Decide: stand by it, or apologise plainly | Choose one option per sentence |
| Ignoring a court-served statement of claim | Judgment without a hearing | Apply at once to set aside, with reasons | Calendar the defence deadline on the day of service |
| Each of twelve defendants replying differently | Inconsistent positions used against all | Coordinate future replies | Consult together and agree the facts |

## Review

- First preserve, then analyse with 08.1–08.2, then decide and reply in writing, "בלי לפגוע בזכויות".
- The letter's deadline is a pressure tactic; a court summons sets a real deadline (60 days for a defence in the regular courts).
- Choose per sentence: stand by provable facts and opinions, correct or remove what you cannot prove, apologise plainly if wrong.
- SLAPP signs: powerful plaintiff, public-participation speech, maximum sums, many defendants, timing.
- No anti-SLAPP law is in force; courts use striking out (regs. 41–42) and costs (reg. 151(ג)). The 2026 bills were only tabled, and the Knesset has dissolved.
