# 19.2 · A Real Freedom of Information Campaign

In this lesson you run a real Freedom of Information campaign in your own name: not one request, but a planned set of at least three requests to at least two public bodies, chasing one public question until you have an answer you can use. By the end you will have filed, tracked and escalated real requests, analysed what came back, published or sent something useful, and recorded it all in your portfolio.

## Why it matters

In Module 5 you learned to write one good FOI request. Real campaigns are different. The information you need is usually spread across bodies — a ministry holds the policy, each municipality holds its own data — and one body's answer tells you what to ask the next. Comparing answers from several bodies is also what turns raw documents into a story: one city spends twice as much per resident as its neighbour; one ministry answered in ten days and another needed a complaint.

A campaign also teaches persistence. Many requests are answered late, partly, or not at all on the first try. Knowing when to send a reminder, when to complain to the FOI Unit, and when a refusal is weak enough to challenge is the practical skill this lesson builds.

## The law

> לכל אזרח ישראלי או תושב הזכות לקבל מידע מרשות ציבורית בהתאם להוראות חוק זה.

*Gist:* every Israeli citizen or resident has the right to receive information from a public authority under this law. ([חוק חופש המידע, s. 1](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע))

> (א) בקשה לקבלת מידע תוגש בכתב לממונה או למי שהוסמך לכך על ידו; אין המבקש חייב לציין את הטעם לבקשתו.

*Gist:* the request is made in writing to the FOI officer and needs no reason. ([s. 7(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע))

> (ב) הרשות הציבורית תודיע למבקש המידע ללא שיהוי, ולא יאוחר מ־30 ימים מקבלת הבקשה, על החלטתה בבקשתו;

*Gist:* the authority must decide without delay and within 30 days, with reasoned extensions allowed under s. 7(ב)–(ג); the exact periods are on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). ([s. 7(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע))

> רשות ציבורית שלא טיפלה בבקשה לקבלת מידע לפי מועדים הקבועים בחוק, לרבות אם לא האריכה את המועדים לפי סעיף 7 לחוק, לא תגבה אגרות טיפול והפקה.

*Gist:* a body that misses its deadline may not charge handling or production fees. ([תקנות חופש המידע (אגרות), reg. 6א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_חופש_המידע_%28אגרות%29))

Sections 8 and 9 list the grounds for refusal, s. 11 requires partial disclosure where possible, and s. 17 lets you petition the Administrative Court against a refusal (lesson 05.3).

## How it works in practice

### Eligibility

- **Who may file:** any Israeli citizen or resident (s. 1), including a registered amuta or company. A non-resident may ask only about their own rights in Israel (s. 12).
- **What you need:** National Digital Identity login for the government online form, an email address, a payment card for the request fee (or proof of an exemption), and a question that matters to the public or to you.
- **Cost:** a request fee per request, plus handling and production fees above the free hours, up to the cap you commit to. All amounts: [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Designing the campaign

1. **One public question.** Not "everything about the shelters", but "Are the public shelters in our three border towns inspected and fit for use, and how much is spent on them?"
2. **Data map.** For each piece of information, which body holds it, and in what form. Check what is already published (annual reports, s. 6 guidelines, the FOI Unit's answers database, the authority's website).
3. **Requests.** At least three, to at least two bodies. Each names record types, a date range and a format (lesson 05.2). Comparison works best when you send the same request to several similar bodies.
4. **Sequence.** Send the requests that unlock others first. A ministry's guideline may tell you which reports each municipality must keep.
5. **Output.** Decide now what you will do with the answers: a one-page summary for residents, a letter to the authority, a position paper to a council committee (lesson 12.3), a data file shared with a journalist, or a pre-petition letter if a refusal is unlawful (lesson 05.5).

### Escalation

| What happened | Ministry or government unit | Local authority, government company, other body |
|---|---|---|
| No answer by day 30, no extension notice | Reminder citing s. 7(ב) and reg. 6א; then a complaint to the FOI Unit | Reminder to the FOI officer, copy to the head of the authority; then consider a petition |
| Partial answer | Ask for the rest, citing s. 11; complaint to the FOI Unit if ignored | Same, without the FOI Unit |
| Refusal | Check section, type and reasons (lesson 05.3); appeal through a petition within 45 days if worth it | Same |
| Fee demand above the cap | Refuse until they ask your consent (reg. 4); appeal a fee decision to the Magistrates' Court | Same |

The Government FOI Unit supervises ministries and their units only; it does not handle complaints about municipalities (lesson 12.5). Filing a petition is optional in this lesson and has costs risks; a petition-ready file is the Module 5 project.

### Expected timeline

| Stage | Hands-on time | Calendar time |

| Tracking, reminders and complaints | 1.5 hours | weeks 5–10 |
| Analysis and output | 2 hours | when answers arrive |
| Portfolio entry and rubric | 30 minutes | at the end |

Expect 6–12 weeks from the first request to a usable result, and longer if you complain or a body uses its full extensions.

### What to record in the portfolio

One [portfolio entry](../../templates/portfolio-entry.md), P-02, with: the question and the data map; every request as filed (exhibits); receipts and payment proofs; the tracking table; every reminder, complaint and answer; your analysis; the output you published or sent; and the rubric score.

### Staying safe and lawful

- File in your own name, or in the name of a group or amuta that authorised you in writing. Do not file "on behalf of" a person who asked you to — they can file themselves with your blank template (lesson 18.4).
- Answers may contain personal details of officials or third parties. Before publishing, remove what is private and check the defamation and privacy rules (Module 8).
- Do not harass officials. One reminder, one complaint, one pre-petition letter: escalate in writing and politely.
- Information released under s. 9(א) exceptions by mistake (for example a security detail) should not be republished. Tell the authority.

## Worked example

**Situation.** After a year of rocket alarms, Dana, a resident of Kiryat Shmona, wants to know whether public shelters in her town and two other northern towns are inspected and maintained, and how much each municipality spends on them.

1. **Question.** "Are the public shelters in Kiryat Shmona, Shlomi and Ma'alot-Tarshiha inspected, and what did each municipality spend on them in 2024–2026?"
2. **Data map.** The municipalities hold maintenance budgets, inspection reports and lists of shelters. The Home Front Command sets standards. Dana searches the FOI Unit's answers database and finds an older answer on shelter standards. She decides to ask only the municipalities, and to treat the location of specific shelters as information that may be withheld for security (s. 9(א)(1)).
3. **Requests.** Three identical requests, one per municipality: "דוחות הבדיקה של המקלטים הציבוריים בתחום הרשות בשנים 2024–2026, ותקציב התחזוקה שאושר ושבוצע בכל אחת מהשנים, בקובץ Excel. אם חלק מהמידע חסוי, אבקש לקבל את יתרתו תוך סימון ההשמטות." She pays the request fee for each and keeps the receipts.
4. **Tracking.** Filed 2 November; day 30 is 2 December for all three.
5. **Answers.** Shlomi answers in full in three weeks. Ma'alot-Tarshiha extends by 30 days with reasons. Kiryat Shmona is silent. On 3 December Dana sends Kiryat Shmona a reminder citing s. 7(ב), copied to the mayor, and notes that it may no longer charge handling fees (reg. 6א). An answer arrives on 20 December, with the addresses of the shelters removed under s. 9(א)(1) — which Dana accepts, since she asked for partial release.
6. **Output.** A one-page comparison of spending per shelter and the share inspected in each year, sent to the three council members who chair the emergency committees and shared with residents' groups. No addresses, no names of inspectors.
7. **Portfolio.** P-02 holds the three requests, the receipts, the reminder, the answers and the one-pager. She scores 12 out of 14: she lost points on output, because she did not follow up with the council.

The same campaign shape fits any cause: kosher-certification costs in municipal kitchens, police enforcement data on illegal construction, school-transport contracts, accessibility audits, or settlement and Bedouin-village infrastructure budgets.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A public question you care about, and 8 hours spread over two or three months.
- National Digital Identity login (for ministries), an email address, a payment card.
- The templates: [foi-request.md](../../templates/foi-request.md), [foi-unit-complaint.md](../../templates/foi-unit-complaint.md), [formal-letter.md](../../templates/formal-letter.md), [research-memo.md](../../templates/research-memo.md), [portfolio-entry.md](../../templates/portfolio-entry.md).

### Steps

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Check the fees on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) and each body's filing channel before you submit.

1. Open P-02 with the [portfolio entry](../../templates/portfolio-entry.md) template. Write the question in one sentence.
2. Build the data map. Search the FOI Unit's answers database and each body's published reports.
3. Draft at least three requests with the [FOI request](../../templates/foi-request.md) template: records, date range, format, partial disclosure, reasons for refusal.
4. File them. Record the receipt and payment date of each in the tracking table and your calendar.
5. On day 30 of each, check the status. Send a reminder where needed; for ministries, file an FOI Unit complaint with the [FOI Unit complaint](../../templates/foi-unit-complaint.md) template if the reminder fails.
6. Analyse each answer or refusal with the six-question check from lesson 05.3. Record what you accept and what you challenge.
7. Write your analysis as a short memo with the [research memo](../../templates/research-memo.md) template.
8. Produce the output: a one-page summary, a letter to the authority with the [formal letter](../../templates/formal-letter.md) template, or a position paper. Send or publish it.
9. Close P-02 and score it with the rubric below.

### How you know it worked

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 11 or more out of 14 means this part of the portfolio is complete; below 8, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question and data map | Vague topic | Clear question, no map | One-sentence question, map of bodies and records, published sources checked |
| Requests | Fewer than three, or "all information" | Three, some vague or missing format | Three or more to two or more bodies; records, dates, format, partial release |
| Filing and fees | Not filed | Filed, fee or receipt missing | Filed in own name; fees paid or exemption proved; receipts kept |
| Tracking | No dates | Dates, but from the wrong start | Day 30 from receipt and payment; extensions; one table |
| Escalation | None, or to the wrong body | Reminder only | Reminder, then the right route (FOI Unit for ministries; other route for others) |
| Analysis and output | No output | Data collected, no conclusion | Short analysis and an output sent or published, private details removed |
| Portfolio entry | Missing | Partial timeline | P-02 complete with exhibits and score |

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| One huge request to one body | Refused for unreasonable resources (s. 8(1)) | Split it into precise requests | Data map first; one record type per item |
| Different wording to similar bodies | Answers you cannot compare | Send a clarified identical request | Use one master text for comparable bodies |
| Complaining to the FOI Unit about a municipality | No jurisdiction; time lost | Write to the head of the authority; consider a petition | Check the escalation table |
| Not tracking receipt and payment dates | Missed reminder dates; weak complaint | Rebuild the dates from receipts | Tracking table from day one |
| Publishing an answer with officials' phone numbers or residents' names | Privacy complaint, loss of trust | Take it down and redact | Redact before publishing (Module 8) |
| No output | Data sits in a folder | Write a one-pager now | Decide the output at the design stage |

## Review

- A campaign is one public question, a data map, and at least three precise requests to at least two bodies.
- Deadlines run from receipt and payment; a body that misses them may not charge handling fees (reg. 6א).
- Escalate in order: reminder, FOI Unit complaint for ministries, and a petition only when it is worth it.
- The value of a campaign is in comparison and use: analyse, redact, and send or publish an output.
- Record everything in P-02 as it happens.
