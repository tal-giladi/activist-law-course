# 19.3 · A Real Complaint to an Oversight Body

In this lesson you file one real complaint, in your own name, about something a public body actually did to you — or to a person who asked you to complain for them and consented in writing. You choose the right oversight body, write to the body concerned first, file a complaint an investigator can act on, follow it to a result, and record it. By the end you will have used one of the cheapest and most underrated accountability tools in Israeli law.

## Why it matters

Oversight bodies — the State Ombudsman (נציב תלונות הציבור), the local authorities' complaints commissioners, the police complaints units, the equality and privacy regulators — are free, need no lawyer, and can make a body explain itself and fix a defect. Many activists skip them because they cannot issue a binding order. That is a mistake: a justified finding is a powerful exhibit in a later petition, letter or media story, and many complaints end with the body quietly fixing the problem.

This lesson is also about honesty. Oversight bodies investigate real harm. A complaint invented to complete a course wastes public resources and your credibility. If you have no real grievance today, keep this lesson open until you do — most people meet one within a few months.

## The law

> כל אדם רשאי להגיש תלונה אל נציב תלונות הציבור.

*Gist:* anyone may complain to the State Ombudsman; it is free. ([חוק מבקר המדינה, s. 33](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה))

> (1) מעשה הפוגע במישרין במתלונן עצמו, או המונע ממנו במישרין טובת הנאה, או מעשה הפוגע במישרין באדם אחר או המונע ממנו במישרין טובת הנאה והמתלונן קיבל, להנחת דעתו של נציב תלונות הציבור, את הסכמתו של אותו אדם להגיש תלונה בענינו

*Gist:* the act must harm you directly, or harm another person who consented to your complaint; and under the rest of s. 37 it must be unlawful, without authority, contrary to proper administration, excessively rigid or flagrantly unjust — including a failure to act or a delay. ([s. 37](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה))

> (2) תלונה שהוגשה לאחר שעברה שנה מיום המעשה שעליו נסבה התלונה או מהיום שהמעשה נודע למתלונן, לפי המאוחר יותר.

*Gist:* complaints over a year old are investigated only for a special reason (s. 39(2)); matters pending in court or decided by a court are not investigated (s. 38(5)). ([ss. 38–39](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה))

> כל אדם רשאי להגיש תלונה לממונה על תלונות הציבור על הרשות המקומית ומוסדותיה, על עובד, על נושא משרה או על ממלא תפקיד ברשות המקומית, וכן על גוף עירוני מבוקר

*Gist:* every local authority has its own complaints commissioner, with the same direct-harm and defect tests (s. 5(ב)); a matter filed with the state Ombudsman is not investigated locally too (s. 7), and a complaint does not extend any court deadline (s. 14). ([חוק הרשויות המקומיות (ממונה על תלונות הציבור), s. 5(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_(ממונה_על_תלונות_הציבור)))

The Ombudsman's findings cannot be challenged in court, give you no new legal right, and do not extend any legal deadline (State Comptroller Law s. 45).

## How it works in practice

### Eligibility: do you have a real complaint?

Answer all four with "yes" before you start:

1. **Real event.** Something a public body did, failed to do or delayed, in the last year.
2. **Direct harm.** It harmed you, or a person who asks you to complain and signs a consent.
3. **A defect.** Unlawful, without authority, against proper administration, excessively rigid or unjust — not just a decision you dislike.
4. **No court case.** The matter is not pending in court or decided by one; and if a deadline to go to court is running, you have marked it separately.

If you fail test 2 because the harm is to the public at large, the complaint route is wrong: send information to the State Comptroller's audit (lesson 05.4), use FOI (lesson 19.2) or a council member's question (lesson 12.5).

### Writing to the body first

Oversight bodies expect you to have asked the body itself to fix the problem, and complaints without that step are often sent back. Send a short [formal letter](../../templates/formal-letter.md) to the department or the body's public-inquiries officer, wait a reasonable time (two to four weeks, or the 45 days of the Decisions and Reasons Law if you asked for a decision), and attach the letter and any answer to your complaint.

### A complaint an investigator can act on

- **Who you are,** your signature, address and contacts; for a consenting person, their signed consent.
- **The body and the official** (by role if you do not know the name).
- **Facts in date order,** each with an exhibit.
- **The harm** to you, concretely: money, time, a lost right, a service you did not get.
- **The defect:** which rule, procedure or principle was broken.
- **What you already did,** and the answer you got.
- **The remedy** you ask for: a decision, a refund, a correction, a changed procedure.
- **Declarations:** not pending in court; not filed with another commissioner.

Keep it to two pages plus exhibits.

### Expected timeline

| Stage | Hands-on time | Calendar time |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility check and choice of body | 30 minutes | day 1 |
| Letter to the body and waiting for its answer | 1 hour | 2–6 weeks |
| Drafting and filing the complaint with exhibits | 2 hours | 1 week |
| Answering the investigator's questions, follow-up | 1 hour | 1–6 months |
| Portfolio entry and rubric | 30 minutes | at the outcome, or at a set review date |

The State Ombudsman usually takes from a few weeks to several months; a local commissioner must finish within a year (s. 8(ז) of the local law). If your outcome has not arrived by the end of the practicum, close the portfolio entry as "pending" with the timeline to date.

### What to record in the portfolio

One [portfolio entry](../../templates/portfolio-entry.md), P-03, with: the four-test eligibility check; the letter to the body and its answer; the complaint as filed with its reference number; the investigator's questions and your answers; the finding; what the body did afterwards; and your rubric score.

### Staying safe and lawful

- **Own name, or with written consent.** You may complain for another person only with their consent (s. 37(1)). Collect it in writing, and keep their personal details out of anything you publish.
- **Truth.** A complaint is a statement to an official body. Say only what you know, and mark what you believe and why. A complaint made in good faith to a competent body is generally protected, but a knowingly false one is not (Module 8).
- **Court deadlines keep running.** A complaint does not extend the 45 days for an administrative petition, the 60 days for an appeal against closing a police file, or any other deadline. Mark them separately.
- **Police matters.** If you were hurt, get medical documentation first and see lesson [06.4](../module-06/lesson-04.md); consider the Public Defender or a lawyer if you may be a suspect yourself.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Yaakov owns a small carpentry workshop in Sderot's industrial zone. For eight months the Israel Land Authority (רשות מקרקעי ישראל) has not answered his request to approve the transfer of his lease to his son, and he cannot get a bank loan without it.

1. **Eligibility.** Real event (eight months' delay), direct harm (no loan), a defect (delay against proper administration; the 45-day duty to decide on a written request), no court case. All four tests pass.
2. **Body.** The Israel Land Authority is a state body: the State Ombudsman. Template: [ombudsman-complaint.md](../../templates/ombudsman-complaint.md).
3. **Letter first.** He sends a [formal letter](../../templates/formal-letter.md) to the ILA district's public-inquiries officer, citing his file number and the Decisions and Reasons Law (lesson 02.5). An automated reply arrives; nothing else in four weeks.
4. **Complaint.** On the online form he gives the dates of his request and three phone calls, attaches the request, the letter and the automated reply, describes the harm (the bank's letter refusing the loan), names the defect (unreasonable delay), and asks for a decision within 30 days. He declares there is no court case.
5. **Investigation.** The Ombudsman's investigator writes to the ILA and asks Yaakov for a copy of the lease. Two months later the ILA approves the transfer and explains that the file was missing a valuation. The Ombudsman ends the investigation because the matter was fixed (s. 42).
6. **Portfolio.** P-03 records the dates, the reference number and the result. Yaakov scores 13 out of 14; he lost a point because his first letter did not ask for a decision by a specific date.

The same pattern fits a rejected disability-parking permit at a municipality (local commissioner), a delayed National Insurance decision (Ombudsman), an officer who refused to identify himself at a protest (Police Public Complaints Unit), or a ministry that published your ID number (Privacy Protection Authority).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A real grievance that passes the four eligibility tests.
- Your documents: the request, letters, answers, receipts, photos.
- The templates listed in the "Which body" table, plus [formal-letter.md](../../templates/formal-letter.md) and [portfolio-entry.md](../../templates/portfolio-entry.md).
- For a complaint for someone else: their written consent.

### Steps

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Complain only about something that really happened; check the body's current filing channel and time limit first.

1. Open P-03 with the [portfolio entry](../../templates/portfolio-entry.md) template. Write the four eligibility answers.
2. Choose the body with the table above. Read the matching lesson again (05.4, 06.4, 08.4, 09.4, 10.5 or 12.5).
3. Mark any court deadline in your calendar now, separately from the complaint.
4. Write to the body concerned with the [formal letter](../../templates/formal-letter.md) template. Wait for its answer or the time you gave it.
5. Draft the complaint in the matching template. Number your exhibits.
6. File it through the official channel. Save the confirmation and reference number.
7. Answer any investigator's questions promptly and in writing.
8. When the finding arrives, record it, and check two months later whether the body actually did what it promised.
9. Score the complaint with the rubric below and close P-03 (or mark it pending).

### Template

- [formal-letter.md](../../templates/formal-letter.md) — the letter to the body first.
- One of: [ombudsman-complaint.md](../../templates/ombudsman-complaint.md), [municipal-complaint.md](../../templates/municipal-complaint.md), [police-complaint.md](../../templates/police-complaint.md), [privacy-complaint.md](../../templates/privacy-complaint.md), [accessibility-complaint.md](../../templates/accessibility-complaint.md), [environmental-complaint.md](../../templates/environmental-complaint.md).
- Optional, to analyse the decision first: [decision-audit.md](../../templates/decision-audit.md).
- [portfolio-entry.md](../../templates/portfolio-entry.md) — entry P-03.

### How you know it worked

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 11 or more out of 14 means this part of the portfolio is complete; below 8, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eligibility | Invented or ineligible grievance | Real, but one test unchecked | All four tests checked and recorded |
| Choice of body | Wrong body, or two commissioners at once | Right body, reason not stated | Right body with the reason; parallel court deadline marked |
| Letter first | None | Sent, no date for an answer | Sent with a clear request and date; answer attached |
| Facts and exhibits | Story without dates | Dates, some exhibits missing | Facts in date order, every one with a numbered exhibit |
| Defect and remedy | Complaint is only about feelings | Defect named, remedy vague | Rule or principle named; a concrete remedy |
| Follow-up | Filed and forgotten | Outcome recorded | Questions answered; outcome recorded; implementation checked |
| Portfolio entry | Missing | Partial | P-03 complete with timeline, exhibits and score |

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaining about harm to the public at large | Rejected for lack of direct harm (s. 37) | Send it as audit information to the Comptroller | Run the four tests first |
| Skipping the letter to the body | Complaint returned | Write to the body and refile with its answer | Letter first, with a date for an answer |
| Filing with both the local commissioner and the state Ombudsman | The local one will not investigate (s. 7) | Withdraw one | Choose one route |
| Waiting for the finding while the 45-day petition deadline passes | Petition late; may be dismissed | Ask the court to extend, explaining why | Mark court deadlines separately (s. 45; local s. 14) |
| Complaining for a friend without written consent | Not investigated | Get the signed consent | Consent form before drafting |
| An angry two-page story with no exhibits | Slow, weak investigation | Rewrite: facts, harm, defect, remedy | Use the template's structure |

## Review

- Complain only about real, direct harm (to you, or to a consenting person) caused by a defect in a public body's conduct.
- Choose one body: the State Ombudsman, a local commissioner, a police unit or a specialised regulator.
- Write to the body first; then file facts, harm, defect, remedy and exhibits.
- A complaint is free but does not stop any court deadline; mark those separately.
- Record everything in P-03 and check whether the body actually fixed the problem.
