# 06.4 · Complaints About Police Conduct

By the end of this lesson you will know which body handles which kind of police misconduct, how to write a complaint that investigators can act on, and how to keep your rights alive when a file is closed — by appealing within 60 days. You will draft a complaint from a real or realistic protest incident.

> [!WARNING]
> If you were hurt, get medical care first and ask for a written medical record — it is the most important piece of evidence. If you may face charges from the same event, talk to a lawyer or the Public Defender before you give any statement to investigators: what you say in a complaint can be used in your own case. Never exaggerate; a knowingly false complaint is an offence. This lesson is not legal advice.

## Why it matters

Complaints against police officers are often closed for the same few reasons: the officer could not be identified, there was no medical record, the complaint came months late, or the complainant never appealed. Each of those is avoidable. A complaint that names the time, place and unit, attaches a medical document and a video, and is followed by an appeal when needed, gets a real investigation far more often.

Complaints also matter beyond your own case. Investigation statistics, disciplinary findings and closed-file reasons are the raw material for later Freedom of Information requests, Comptroller inquiries and policy campaigns — whichever side of the political map the protesters came from.

## The law

> על אף האמור בכל דין, חקירה של עבירה כמשמעותה בתוספת הראשונה, אשר בביצועה חשוד שוטר, לא תיערך בידי משטרת ישראל, אלא בידי המחלקה לחקירת שוטרים שבמשרד המשפטים

*Gist:* offences by police officers are investigated by the Ministry of Justice department, not by the police. ([פקודת המשטרה, s. 49ט(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_המשטרה#סעיף_49ט))

> כל עבירה למעט: (1) עבירה שעונשה מאסר שאינו עולה על שנה, אלא אם כן פרקליט המדינה והמפקח הכללי קבעו שהעבירה תיחקר בידי המחלקה; (2) עבירת תעבורה

*Gist:* the department's remit is every offence punishable by more than one year, except traffic offences. ([First Schedule](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_המשטרה#תוספת_1))

> על החלטה שלא לחקור או שלא להעמיד לדין, משום שנסיבות העניין בכללותן אינן מתאימות לפתיחה בחקירה או להעמדה לדין, לפי העניין, שלא נמצאו ראיות מספיקות או שנקבע שאין אשמה, רשאי המתלונן לערור

*Gist:* a complainant may appeal a decision not to investigate or not to prosecute. ([חוק סדר הדין הפלילי, s. 64(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_64))

> הערר יוגש באמצעות המשטרה או התובע, הכל לפי הענין, בתוך שישים ימים לאחר שנמסרה למתלונן ההודעה לפי סעיף 63, אולם רשאי בעל הסמכות להחליט בערר כאמור בסעיף 64 להאריך את המועד להגשת הערר.

*Gist:* the appeal is due within 60 days of the closing notice; the deciding authority may extend. ([s. 65](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_65))

> אדם הטוען כי נפגע או ניזוק מפעולת מכת"זית בהפגנה, רשאי לפנות למשטרה לשם קבלת התיעוד החזותי של האירוע שבו נפגע או ניזוק

*Gist:* from 15.2.2027, a person hurt by a water cannon at a protest may ask the police for the video of the event. ([פקודת המשטרה, s. 89א(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_המשטרה#סעיף_89א))

## How it works in practice

### Which channel?

| What happened | Channel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Violence, threats, false arrest, theft, perjury by an officer (offences over 1 year) | **מח״ש** (Police Investigations Department) | Police Ordinance s. 49ט; First Schedule |
| Rudeness, insults, refusing to give name or number, missing name tag, sloppy handling, abuse of authority without a criminal offence | **Public Complaints Unit** of the police district or station area | Disciplinary offences under the Police Law 2006 |
| Both (a push plus insults) | Start with **מח״ש**; it passes disciplinary aspects to the police if it closes the criminal side | The gov.il pages describe this routing |
| A municipal inspector, a private security guard, a prison guard | Not מח״ש — their employer, or the police for a criminal complaint | Outside s. 49ט |
| A systemic practice (for example, routine failure to wear tags at protests) | FOI request (05.2), Comptroller inquiry (05.4), letter to the police legal adviser | Not an individual complaint |
| Money for an injury or damaged property | Civil claim (Module 11) — separate from the complaint | Different deadlines and burden |

### Filing with מח״ש

- **How:** the online form on gov.il is the only route for filing a complaint. The information line is *2906 (Sunday–Thursday, 8:00–16:00); email DIPI@justice.gov.il for inquiries.
- **When:** as close to the event as possible; investigators say speed is critical for evidence such as body-camera and security footage, which may be overwritten.
- **What to attach:** medical records, names and phone numbers of witnesses, photos and videos (originals), and any detention or arrest document.
- **What happens:** an initial review in about 3 working days; then a preliminary check or an investigation under caution, usually up to about 75 days; then a prosecution decision, usually up to about 6 months. You will be asked to give a detailed statement to investigators.
- **If closed:** you receive a notice (s. 63). You may request copies of the investigation material, then file an **appeal within 60 days** (s. 65). It goes to the department's prosecution division and then to the State Attorney's appeals unit. If copying the material takes too long, ask the appeals unit in writing for an extension before day 60.

### Filing with the Public Complaints Unit

- **How:** the online form on gov.il (police "תלונות ופניות ציבור" page), or in writing (post, fax, email) to the public-complaints officer of the relevant district or station area; contacts are on the same page.
- **Who decides:** the area or district complaints officer; complaints against officers of superintendent (סנ״צ) rank and above, or against national units, go to the national unit.
- **Appeal:** an area decision may be appealed to the district complaints officer, and a district decision to the head of the national unit.
- **Mediation:** for rudeness and similar conduct, the unit may offer voluntary mediation without lawyers; if it succeeds, the matter is closed for good.

### Writing the complaint

Use [police-complaint.md](../../templates/police-complaint.md). A strong complaint:

1. states facts in time order, in the first person, with exact times and places;
2. identifies the officer by name, number, unit, vehicle number or a physical description and photo;
3. separates what you saw from what you were told;
4. describes the injury or damage and attaches the medical record;
5. lists evidence and witnesses;
6. says what you ask: a criminal investigation, a disciplinary hearing, the preservation of body-camera footage.

It is not an affidavit, but the same discipline applies (02.6): facts only, no adjectives, no guesses presented as facts.

## Worked example

**Situation.** During the evacuation of an unauthorised outpost in the West Bank (Judea and Samaria), Yehuda Peretz, 19, sits on the ground with others and is carried out by Border Police officers. After he is handcuffed and seated by a jeep, an officer strikes him twice on the back with a baton. A friend films part of it from 15 metres. Yehuda is released three hours later without charges. That night, a clinic records bruising on his back.

1. **Channel.** A blow to a handcuffed person is suspected assault by an officer — an offence over one year. Border Police are police officers, so the matter belongs to מח״ש, whether the event was in Israel or in the West Bank.
2. **Lawyer first?** Yehuda was released without charges, but the evacuation could still produce a file for obstruction. He calls a lawyer (from a volunteer legal-aid organisation) before filing. The lawyer advises him to describe only the blow, not the earlier sit-in.
3. **Complaint.** He files online the next day. It gives the time (about 14:20), the place (by the white jeep at the outpost's entrance), a description of the officer (tall, beard, helmet number partly visible in the video) and the jeep's number. It describes the two blows, attaches the clinic record and the video, and names the friend who filmed. It asks for an investigation and for preservation of the unit's body-camera footage.
4. **After 5 months.** The file is closed for "insufficient evidence": the officer could not be identified with certainty. Yehuda receives the notice on 3 March.
5. **Appeal.** He requests copies of the investigation material on 5 March and marks the deadline: 60 days from 3 March is 2 May. When the copies arrive on 20 April, he and his lawyer show in the appeal that the jeep's duty roster identifies the crew. They file on 28 April.
6. **Mirror test.** The steps would be identical for an anti-government protester hit by an officer at a Jerusalem road block, or an Arab citizen struck during a protest in Umm al-Fahm.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- An incident: one you experienced, saw, or a realistic invented one (say so at the top).
- The [police-complaint.md](../../templates/police-complaint.md) template.
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write the incident as a timeline: time, place, who did what, in the first person.
2. Classify each act: criminal (over one year) → מח״ש; conduct only → Public Complaints Unit; both → מח״ש first.
3. Fill in the identification section with everything that can point to the officer: name tag, number, unit, vehicle, photo, description.
4. List your evidence and what is missing (a medical record, witness contacts); add how you will get it this week.
5. Write the request: what you want the body to do, including preserving body-camera footage.
6. Mark the dates: filing date, expected decision window, and — if a closing notice arrives — the 60-day appeal date and the date to request copies of the material.
7. If you might face charges from the same event, write "lawyer first" at the top and do not file until you have spoken to one.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Filing is optional; a dry run is enough. If you file for real: act in your own name only, about an incident you witnessed or experienced. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### How you know it worked

- A stranger reading your complaint could find the officer from your identification details.
- Each act is in the right channel, with a reason.
- Your calendar shows the 60-day appeal date, counted from the day you received the notice.

- [ ] Medical record obtained and attached (if hurt).
- [ ] Officer identifiers listed; originals of photos and videos kept.
- [ ] Criminal acts to מח״ש, conduct to the Public Complaints Unit.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing months later | Body-camera and security footage overwritten; witnesses forget | File anyway, explaining the delay | File within days; ask for footage preservation |
| No medical record | "Insufficient evidence" | Get a record now, even late, and explain | Go to a clinic or ER the same day |
| Filing a rudeness complaint with מח״ש | Forwarded to the police; time lost | File directly with the Public Complaints Unit | Use the channel table |
| Missing the 60-day appeal | The closure becomes final in practice | Ask the appeals unit for an extension, with reasons | Calendar the date when the notice arrives |
| Giving a statement that admits a separate offence | Your words are used in your own prosecution | Get a lawyer before any further statement | Lawyer first if you may face charges |
| Exaggerating ("they beat me for an hour") | Credibility lost; risk of a false-complaint charge | Correct the record in writing | Facts only, as precise as you can |

## Review

- Criminal acts by police officers (offences over one year) go to מח״ש in the Ministry of Justice; conduct and service complaints go to the police Public Complaints Unit.
- File fast, with a medical record, originals of video and photos, witnesses and every identifier of the officer; ask for body-camera footage to be preserved.
- מח״ש aims at about 75 days for investigation and 6 months for a decision; you may appeal a closure within 60 days of the notice (s. 65).
- Get a lawyer first if you may face charges from the same event.
- Complaints are your own record and feed systemic tools such as FOI and Comptroller inquiries.
