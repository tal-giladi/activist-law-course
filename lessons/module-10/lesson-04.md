# 10.4 · The Hearing and the Appeal

By the end of this lesson you will be able to present your objection at a public hearing, read the decision, choose the right appeal route before its short deadline, write an appeal (ערר) to the district appeals committee, and know when the next step is a court petition.

## Why it matters

Objections are won and lost at the hearing: committees decide a dozen objections in an afternoon, and the objector who arrives with a five-minute summary, a map and one clear request is the one who gets a change. After the decision, the clocks are the shortest in this course — 15 days, sometimes 7 — and the route depends on which body decided. Activists across the map have lost good cases because they appealed to the wrong body, or filed a court petition before using the planning appeal the law gave them.

## The law

> לדיון בהתנגדויות יוזמנו המתנגד ומגיש התכנית; ... והם יהיו רשאים להשמיע דבריהם לפני המוסד המכריע; שמיעת ההתנגדות תהיה בפומבי.

*Gist:* the objector and the plan's initiator are invited and may speak; the hearing is public. ([Planning and Building Law s. 107](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_107))

> המתנגד יטען תחילה, אחריו מגיש התכנית ויתר הזכאים להשמיע דבריהם לפי סעיף 107 לחוק; למתנגד תהא זכות תשובה.

*Gist:* the objector speaks first, then the initiator and the others; the objector has the right of reply. ([Objections Procedure Regulations, reg. 12](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_התכנון_והבניה_(סדרי_נוהל_בהתנגדויות_לתכנית,_סמכויות_חוקר_וסדרי_עבודתו))) Reg. 14 adds that you may argue only the reasons in your written objection, unless the body allows otherwise.

> הודעה על הכרעה בדבר התנגדויות ועל נימוקיה תינתן בכתב למתנגד ולמי שהשמיע טענות לפי סעיף 106.

*Gist:* the decision on objections and its reasons are given to the objector in writing. ([s. 108(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_108))

> (א) על החלטת הועדה המקומית בדבר אישור תכנית שבסמכותה ... או דחייתה, רשאים לערור בפני ועדת הערר: ... (6) מי שהתנגדותו לתכנית נדחתה; (7) מי שהשמיע טענות לפי סעיף 106(ב).
> ...
> (ב) הערר יוגש בתוך חמישה עשר ימים מיום שהומצאה לעורר החלטת הועדה המקומית.

*Gist:* an objector whose objection to a local-committee plan was rejected may appeal to the appeals committee within 15 days of receiving the decision. ([s. 112](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_112))

> (א) כתב ערר יכלול את כל אלה: (1) שם העורר ומענו; ... (3) פרטי התכנית או הבקשה שלגביה ניתנה ההחלטה נושא הערר; (4) פרטי ההחלטה שעליה מוגש הערר, והעתק הודעת הועדה או הרשות על ההחלטה; (5) פרטי המשיבים, שמותיהם ומענם; (6) נימוקי הערר; (7) עיקר הראיות שהעורר מבקש להביא בפני ועדת הערר.

*Gist:* the appeal must include the appellant, the plan or request, the decision and a copy of it, the respondents, the reasons and the main evidence. ([Appeals Committees Procedure Regulations 1996, reg. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_התכנון_והבניה_(סדרי_הדין_בועדות_ערר)))

## How it works in practice

### Before the hearing

- **Invitation.** You get at least 14 days' notice by registered mail or fax (now often e-mail), and the hearing is advertised (reg. 6). If you were not invited properly, say so at the first opportunity (reg. 8(ג)).
- **Read the other side's reply.** The initiator may file a written response with an affidavit at least 5 days before (reg. 13(ב)). Ask for it.
- **Investigator.** With many objections, the body may appoint an investigator (חוקר) to hear them and recommend (s. 107א). Treat the investigator's hearing like the committee's.
- **Identical objections.** The body may hear only some of a group of identical objections (s. 106(ג)); agree in advance who speaks for whom.

### At the hearing

1. Arrive early, bring copies of your objection, the map with your points marked, and photos.
2. Speak first (reg. 12). Five minutes: who you are, the harm, your **specific requested changes**, the strongest two arguments. Do not read the whole objection.
3. Stay within your written reasons (reg. 14). New evidence needs permission (reg. 15).
4. If asked, answer questions on your affidavit; refusing without a reason can lead to striking out (reg. 9).
5. Listen to the initiator; use your right of reply for errors of fact only.
6. Ask the committee for a reasoned written decision (s. 108(ב)) and, if relevant, for a site visit (reg. 15(ה)).
7. Take notes; later read the protocol and, if needed, ask to hear the recording (s. 48ד).

### After the decision: the appeal map

| Decision by | Who may appeal | Where | Deadline (see [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local committee: approves a plan in its powers / rejects your objection | You, if your objection was rejected or you were heard under s. 106(ב) | District appeals committee | 15 days from service of the decision (s. 112(ב)) |
| Same, but an **independent** local committee | As above, but only with the appeals committee chair's **leave** | Request for leave to the chair; then the appeal | Request within 15 days; appeal within 15 days of leave (s. 112(א1)) |
| Appeals committee decision on a plan | Parties, objectors | Request for leave to the appeals committee chair, then the district committee | 7 days for the request; 7 days from leave to appeal (s. 111) |
| District committee: approves or rejects a plan | Objector whose objection was rejected — with leave of the district committee chair | National Council (appeals subcommittee) | Leave request within 15 days; appeal within 30 days of service of the decision or the leave (s. 110) |
| Local committee or licensing authority: rejects your objection to a הקלה or non-conforming use, or refuses a permit | Whoever is harmed | District appeals committee | 30 days from service (s. 152(א)(1)) |
| Appeals committee decision on a permit or הקלה | — | Final within the planning system (s. 12ב(ד)(1)) | Court only |

While an appeal against a permit decision is pending, no permit is issued unless the appeals committee decides otherwise (s. 152(ב)). After an appeal against a local plan is filed, the local committee may not publish the plan's approval until the appeal is decided (reg. 19).

### Writing and filing the appeal

- **Contents.** Reg. 3: your name and address; the plan or request; the decision and a copy of it; the respondents with addresses (reg. 4 says who: the local committee, the initiator, and anyone heard on the point); your reasons; your main evidence. Use the [planning-appeal template](../../templates/planning-appeal.md).
- **Focus.** Argue why the **decision** is wrong: an objection point not addressed, a mistake of fact, a contradiction with a higher plan, missing reasons, an unlawful consideration. Repeat your objection only as background.
- **Filing.** Either six printed copies plus copies for each respondent, or two printed copies plus respondents' copies **and** the online appeal form on the Planning Administration's site, by the deadline (reg. 2). Online filings after 16:00 on Sunday–Thursday, or on Friday or a rest day, count as filed on the next working day (reg. 2(ב)). The online form needs the National Digital Identity; an e-mail confirmation follows, but sending is not acceptance — the secretariat checks and may ask for corrections.
- **Fee.** An appeal fee is payable under regulations made under s. 265(17ב); the secretariat confirms receipt after payment (reg. 2(ג)). Ask the secretariat for the current amount before the deadline; objections are free, appeals are not.
- **After filing.** The secretariat sends the appeal to the respondents within 5 days (reg. 5). They answer within 30 days for plans, 15 for permits (reg. 6). You are invited at least 7 days before the hearing (reg. 8). The appeals committee decides a plan appeal within 60 days of filing (s. 112(ג)).

### When the planning route ends: court

- **Administrative petition** to the District Court sitting as an Administrative Court, for most planning decisions (First Schedule item 10), within 45 days (lesson 05.6). Use the planning appeal first: the court expects you to exhaust it.
- **High Court**, for decisions on national and district outline plans, ministers' and Government decisions (including national infrastructure plans), and matters in the enforcement chapter, which the First Schedule leaves out.
- **Judea and Samaria.** Certain Civil Administration planning decisions go to the Administrative Court in Jerusalem (Fourth Schedule item 2); outline plans, offences and enforcement, and the commander's decisions go to the High Court.
- **Enforcement petitions.** Groups on different sides ask courts to make authorities act on illegal building: Regavim has petitioned for enforcement against unpermitted construction by Palestinians in Area C and by Bedouin in the Negev; Peace Now has petitioned for enforcement against unauthorised settlement outposts; residents and Bimkom have petitioned against demolitions and for plans that would make building lawful. Before any of these, write a demand letter to the enforcement body (lesson 05.5) and keep the reply: courts give authorities wide discretion in setting enforcement priorities.

## Worked example

**Situation.** In a Jerusalem neighbourhood with many religious families, the local committee (an independent committee) approves a plan under s. 62א(א)(4א) raising a building on the corner from 4 to 9 floors without adding total floor area. Neighbours led by Avraham Klein objected: the higher building shades the synagogue courtyard and the kindergarten playground. The committee heard them, rejected the objection in a two-line decision, and the decision reached Avraham on 1 September.

1. **Hearing review.** The protocol shows the committee did not address the shading study Avraham attached. The decision gives no reason on shading.
2. **Route.** Local plan, independent committee → request for leave from the appeals committee chair (s. 112(א1)), within 15 days of service: by 16 September. Avraham calendars 12 September.
3. **Request for leave.** Using the template's leave section, with the details reg. 20(ב) requires: the decision, the plan, a copy of the notice, the service date, and why the appeal raises a real ground (unaddressed shading of public facilities, no reasons).
4. **Appeal after leave.** Leave granted on 25 September; the appeal is filed by 10 October with reg. 3 contents, respondents under reg. 4 (the local committee and the plan's initiator), the fee paid and the online form submitted before 16:00.
5. **Hearing.** Avraham asks for a narrow remedy: reduce to 7 floors on the corner and move the extra floors to the back of the plot. The appeals committee orders an updated shading study.
6. **If he loses.** A request for leave to appeal to the district committee within 7 days (s. 111), or an administrative petition within 45 days.

**Mirror test.** A young-families' group in the same neighbourhood that **supports** the plan for more housing can be heard under s. 106(ב) and respond to the appeal as an interested party. The same calendar applies to them.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Appeal deadlines are very short and some routes need leave; if you are acting for real, confirm the route and the fee with the appeals committee secretariat on the first day.

### You need

- Your objection from lesson 10.3 (or a realistic decision rejecting it: invent a two-paragraph decision with a service date).
- [planning-appeal.md](../../templates/planning-appeal.md).
- 75 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write a five-minute hearing summary: harm, three requested changes, two arguments, one map.
2. Read (or invent) the decision. List each of your objection points and whether the decision answered it.
3. Using the appeal map, decide the route, whether leave is needed, the body and the deadline. Calendar a personal deadline three days earlier.
4. List the respondents (reg. 4) with addresses.
5. Draft the appeal (or the request for leave) with the template: decision, why it is wrong, remedies, evidence.
6. Plan the filing: copies, online form, fee, proof.
7. Write down the court route if the appeal fails, and its deadline.

### Template

[planning-appeal.md](../../templates/planning-appeal.md) — a Hebrew appeal (כתב ערר) under reg. 3, a request for leave to appeal, and a filing checklist.

### How you know it worked

- Your hearing summary fits on one page and opens with what you want.
- Your appeal attacks the decision, not only the plan, and contains every reg. 3 element.
- The route, body, deadline and next court step are written with their sections.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading the full objection aloud at the hearing | Time runs out before your request | Ask for two minutes to state the remedies | Prepare a five-minute summary |
| Raising a new argument at the hearing | Not heard without permission (reg. 14) | Ask for permission and explain why it is new | Put every argument in the written objection |
| Appealing directly to an independent committee's decision without leave | The appeal is not properly before the committee | File the leave request at once, within the period | Check s. 112(א1) |
| Petitioning the court before the planning appeal | Dismissed for not exhausting remedies | Withdraw and appeal, if still in time | Follow the appeal map first |
| Missing the 15 or 7 days | No appeal | Ask for an extension for special reasons; consider a petition | Calendar the service date the day the decision arrives |
| Wrong respondents or no copy of the decision | The secretariat demands corrections; delay | Add them quickly | Use the reg. 3–4 checklist |

## Review

- The hearing is public; the objector speaks first and has a reply; stay within your written reasons and ask for a reasoned decision.
- Local plans: appeal to the appeals committee in 15 days (with leave for independent committees); district plans: leave and then the National Council; permits and הקלות: 30 days; appeals committee decisions on permits are final.
- An appeal needs the reg. 3 contents, the right respondents, the fee and a timely filing (online or printed).
- Court comes after the planning route: Administrative Court for most decisions, High Court for outline plans, ministers, the Government and enforcement.
- Enforcement petitions are used by groups on opposite sides; start with a demand letter.
