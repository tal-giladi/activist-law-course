# 10.3 · Writing an Objection

By the end of this lesson you will be able to write and file a planning objection (התנגדות לתכנית) in your own name or for your group: with standing, on time, to the right body, with planning arguments a committee can act on, with the affidavit the law requires — and to object within days to a neighbour's request for a הקלה.

## Why it matters

The objection is the strongest tool an individual has in planning. It is free, it obliges the committee to hear you in public and to decide in writing with reasons, and it is the gateway to everything after it: only someone whose objection was rejected (or who was heard under s. 106(ב)) may appeal a local plan, and a court will ask why you did not object.

It is also unforgiving. An objection without an affidavit "shall not be accepted and shall not be heard". A day late is too late. An objection that is only "we don't want this" is easy to reject. Planners at Bimkom, at environmental NGOs, at Regavim and at residents' committees win by writing specific, evidence-based objections that offer the committee a better alternative.

## The law

> כל מעונין בקרקע, בבנין או בכל פרט תכנוני אחר הרואה את עצמו נפגע על ידי תכנית מיתאר מחוזית או מקומית או תכנית מפורטת שהופקדו, רשאי להגיש התנגדות להן

*Gist:* anyone with an interest in land, a building or any other planning detail who sees themselves harmed by a deposited plan may object; so may local authorities, ministries and public bodies approved by order (the list includes, among others, the SPNI, Adam Teva V'Din, Bimkom and the Arab Center for Alternative Planning). ([Planning and Building Law s. 100](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_100)) Kol Zchut notes that the Supreme Court (CA 2962/97) held that prima facie harm to an interest is enough.

> התנגדות לתכנית תוגש תוך חודשיים מהיום שפורסמה הודעה על הפקדתה; ואולם רשאי מוסד התכנון ... לקבוע להגשת התנגדויות תקופה ארוכה יותר, שלא תעלה על שלושה חודשים ... מועד פרסום ההודעה יהיה המועד המאוחר שבין הפרסומים בעתון.

*Gist:* the period runs from the later newspaper publication of the deposit; the planning body may lengthen it, up to three months. ([s. 102](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_102); numbers on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md))

> (א) התנגדות לתכנית לא תתקבל ולא תידון אלא אם כן הוגשה בכתב בפירוט הנמקות ובליווי תצהיר המאמת את העובדות שעליהן היא מסתמכת.
> (ב) על אף האמור בסעיף קטן (א), בנושא ציבורי שענינו שמירת אתרים, טבע, נוף, ואיכות חיים וסביבה, יכול שמתנגד המייצג קבוצת מתנגדים יגיש התנגדות מנומקת, המלווה בתצהיר שלו, ומתנגדים אחרים שהתנגדותם דומה במהותה רשאים להסתמך על התצהיר שהגיש אותו מתנגד.

*Gist:* an objection must be in writing, with detailed reasons and an affidavit verifying its facts; on public issues of heritage, nature, landscape, quality of life and environment, one representative's affidavit can serve a group with similar objections. ([s. 103א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_103א))

> כתב ההתנגדות ... ייערך בארבעה עותקים, ובנוסף לנימוקי ההתנגדות יפרט –
> (1) את ענינו של המתנגד בקרקע, בבנין או בפרט תכנוני אחר שהתכנית נוגעת בהם, והאופן בו פוגעת התכנית בענין זה; ...
> (2) את מענו של המתנגד ...

*Gist:* the objection is made in four copies and must state your interest, how the plan harms it, and your address. ([Objections Procedure Regulations 1989, reg. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_התכנון_והבניה_(סדרי_נוהל_בהתנגדויות_לתכנית,_סמכויות_חוקר_וסדרי_עבודתו)))

> (1) פורסמה, על חשבון המבקש, בעתון הודעה המפרטת את מהות הבקשה להקלה או להתרת שימוש חורג ... והקובעת כי התנגדויות לבקשה כאמור יוגשו בתוך 15 ימים ממועד הפרסום ...

*Gist:* before granting a הקלה or a non-conforming use, the local committee must publish the request, put a notice on the property and notify neighbours; objections are due within 15 days of publication or of personal notice. ([s. 149(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_149))

## How it works in practice

### Four checks before you write

1. **Standing.** What is your interest (owner, tenant, neighbour, user of the park, member of an approved body)? How does the plan harm it? Write it in two sentences.
2. **Deadline.** Count from the later newspaper date; compare with the date on the plan page; ask the secretariat if they differ. For ותמ״ל plans anyone may object, and the 60-day period runs from the latest of the sign, newspaper and website publications (2014 Law s. 18). For ות״ל plans you file a השגה (lesson 10.1).
3. **Where (s. 103 and reg. 4).** Local-committee plan: to the local committee, copy to the district committee, and keep proof of the copy. District plan: to the district committee, copy to the local committee, with proof. District outline plan: to the National Council, copy to the district committee.
4. **Cost.** Filing an objection is free. The affidavit costs whatever the certifier charges (lesson 02.6; see [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).

### What a strong objection contains

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Heading | "התנגדות לתכנית מס׳ ___", the body, the date |
| Objector | Name and address (and, on paper, contact details); group: the representative and a signatures page |
| Plan details | Number, name, gush and chelka, deposit dates (from lesson 10.2) |
| Interest and harm | Your connection to the land or planning detail, and how the plan harms it (reg. 3(1)) |
| Summary | The requested changes, in a numbered list, in the first page |
| Reasons | One heading per argument, strongest first, each pointing to the plan (chapter, table row, map) and to evidence |
| Remedies | Reject the plan, or change specific provisions, or add conditions; ask to be heard and for written reasons |
| Exhibits | Photos, measurements, expert opinion, protocols, FOI answers, numbered |
| Affidavit | Verifying every **fact** you rely on |

### Kinds of argument that work

- **Planning merits.** Density against infrastructure (schools, roads, sewage); loss or shortage of public open space and public buildings; shading, privacy, building lines; traffic and parking; heritage.
- **Legal.** The plan contradicts a higher plan (national or district) without the needed approval; the body lacks power (a local plan beyond s. 62א); defects in publication; a required environmental review is missing.
- **Fairness and equality.** The plan ignores existing homes or a community's needs; it sets different rules for similar plots.
- **Environment and nature.** Open landscape, streams, ecological corridors, air and noise (lesson 10.5).
- **Alternatives.** A different alignment, lower height with the same units, moving the public building, phasing. Committees accept changes far more often than they reject whole plans.

Avoid: personal attacks on the developer or officials (see Module 8), arguments about property values only, and facts you cannot support in the affidavit.

### The affidavit

The affidavit verifies **facts**, not arguments: who you are, where you live, what you measured, photographed or saw, the documents you attach. Use the [affidavit template](../../templates/affidavit.md) and the affidavit section of the [planning-objection template](../../templates/planning-objection.md). The online service asks for a lawyer's certification ("תצהיר עורך דין"). The committee may call you to be questioned on it and may strike out the objection if you do not come without a reasonable explanation (reg. 9).

### Filing online (Planning Administration plans)

The Planning Administration's guide describes these steps:

1. Open the plan on Mavat. Click **"הגשת התנגדות לתכנית"**; the last date for objections is shown under the button.
2. Read the instructions screen and enter the form.
3. Log in with the National Digital Identity (הזדהות לאומית).
4. Choose whether you are an **objector** (מתנגד) or a **representative** (בא כוח / מייצג) of several objectors.
5. Fill in the details: ID number, full name, mobile phone and e-mail are mandatory; for a group, each member's details (more than 20 → an Excel file).
6. Enter the gush and chelka you are objecting about.
7. Upload: the **objection-details file** (the reasons; the Planning Administration offers a sample DOCX with date, name, address and a "פירוט ההתנגדות" box), the **affidavit**, and for a group a **power of attorney** or, for public-interest group objections, a **signatures page**.
8. Send. You receive an e-mail with a reference number. Keep it.
9. After the committee secretariat checks it, the objection is published on the plan page under "התנגדויות", with your name and the file.

> [!WARNING]
> The objection file is published online. Keep your ID number, phone number and anything you do not want public **out of the reasons file**; they go only in the form fields.

### Filing on paper (local committees and others)

Many local committees accept objections by hand, by registered mail or by e-mail, as the deposit notice says. Make four copies (reg. 3), get a stamped receipt or a delivery confirmation, send the copy to the other committee, and keep proof of the copy and its date (reg. 4).

### Objecting to a הקלה or non-conforming use

A neighbour asks to build closer to your wall, add a floor, or use a flat as a clinic. The local committee must publish the request, put a notice on the building and notify neighbouring owners and occupiers (s. 149(א)). You have **15 days** from the publication, or from the notice delivered to you. Write a short objection to the local committee: your interest, the specific deviation, the harm (light, privacy, parking, noise), and what you ask. If it is rejected you may appeal within 30 days (s. 152(א)(1); lesson 10.4).

### Costs risk

A committee that finds an objection was filed in bad faith and is vexatious may order the objector to pay the initiator's costs; if the objection was justified, it may order the initiator to pay the objector's costs (s. 106(ד)). Honest, reasoned objections are not at risk.

## Worked example

**Situation.** The Southern District committee deposits a plan for an industrial zone next to an unrecognised Bedouin village in the Negev. On the map, 14 homes of the village sit inside an area marked for industry. Salma Abu Ali, who lives in one of the homes, and her neighbours want to object.

1. **Standing.** They live on the land and the plan designates their homes for industry: a direct interest and harm (s. 100). The fact that their homes have no permits does not take away their right to object to the plan.
2. **Deadline.** Later newspaper publication 4 May; the plan page shows 3 July as the last date. They calendar 26 June as their own deadline.
3. **Where.** A district plan: online on Mavat, which reaches the district committee; a copy by e-mail to the local committee with a delivery confirmation.
4. **Reading (lesson 10.2).** The rights table allows heavy industry on plots 101–104, 150 m from the homes. The environmental appendix measures distances only to "the nearest recognised locality", 4 km away.
5. **Arguments.** (a) The plan ignores an existing population; the environmental review is defective because it does not consider the nearest residents. (b) Heavy industry at 150 m conflicts with the buffer the district plan requires. (c) Alternative: move plots 101–104 east and designate the village area for a future residential plan.
6. **Group.** The arguments are about quality of life and environment, so Salma objects as representative under s. 103א(ב), with her own affidavit and a signatures page from 22 neighbours. A planning NGO (an approved body under s. 100(3)) files its own objection with an expert opinion.
7. **Affidavit.** Salma's affidavit verifies that she has lived in the home since 1998, that 14 homes and a kindergarten stand where the plan shows industry (photos and GPS points attached) and the measured distance to plots 101–104.
8. **Filing.** Online form as representative; the reasons file has no ID numbers; reference number saved.

**Mirror test.** The same steps fit residents of a Jewish moshav in the Galilee objecting to a waste-treatment site planned next to their homes, or a settlement's residents objecting to a road plan that cuts their access: standing, deadline, specific arguments, alternative, affidavit.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only (or as representative of a group that has given you its signatures). This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The default here is a dry run. File for real only if the plan really affects you and you are within the deadline.

### You need

- The plan you read in lesson 10.2, your change table and part A of the template.
- [planning-objection.md](../../templates/planning-objection.md) and [affidavit.md](../../templates/affidavit.md).
- 2 hours.

### Steps

1. Write your standing in two sentences (interest + harm).
2. Calculate the deadline from the later newspaper date and set your own deadline a week earlier.
3. Decide where to file and where the copy goes.
4. From your change table, choose three to five arguments. For each: the plan provision, the harm, the evidence, and the change you ask for.
5. Write the summary of requests (numbered) first, then the reasons.
6. Draft the affidavit with only the facts the reasons rely on, each with its exhibit.
7. Decide: individual objection, or representative objection under s. 103א(ב)? If a group, prepare the signatures page.
8. Remove personal identifiers from the reasons file.
9. Run the template's checklist.

### Template

[planning-objection.md](../../templates/planning-objection.md) — a Hebrew objection with an affidavit section, a group-signatures page, a short version for הקלות, and a filing checklist.

### How you know it worked

- A planner reading only your first page knows exactly what you want changed.
- Every factual statement in the reasons is covered by the affidavit or an exhibit.
- Filing method, copy and proof are planned, with dates.

### Self-check

- [ ] Standing written; harm specific.
- [ ] Deadline counted from the later newspaper publication; personal deadline set earlier.
- [ ] Right body; copy to the other committee with proof.
- [ ] Reasons point to specific provisions; at least one constructive alternative.
- [ ] Affidavit verifies the facts, certified; exhibits numbered.
- [ ] No ID number or phone in the published reasons file.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| No affidavit | The objection is not accepted or heard (s. 103א) | Ask at once to add it; the body may allow a correction instead of striking out (reg. 7(ב)) | Book the certifier in the first week |
| Late by a day | Not heard; no right to appeal as an objector | Ask the body to accept it for special reasons; look for other routes | Count from the later newspaper date; file a week early |
| Filed with the wrong body or no copy to the other | Delay, dispute over filing | Re-file and send the copy with proof | Check the authority level and reg. 4 |
| Only "we oppose the plan" | Rejected as unreasoned; may be skipped (s. 106(ג)) | Add specific reasons by the deadline | Point every argument to the plan and to evidence |
| ID number and phone in the reasons file | Published online | Ask the secretariat to replace the file | Keep personal details in the form fields only |
| Missing a הקלה notice on the building | 15 days pass; the permit goes ahead | Check if notice to you was given; appeal routes | Read notices on buildings near you and check your mail |

## Review

- Anyone with an interest who sees themselves harmed may object (s. 100); approved public bodies too.
- Deadline: two months from the later newspaper publication, extendable to three (s. 102); ותמ״ל and ות״ל have their own periods.
- Written, detailed reasons and an affidavit, or the objection is not heard (s. 103א); groups can rely on one representative's affidavit on environmental and quality-of-life issues.
- File online for Planning Administration plans, or on paper with four copies and a copy with proof to the other committee.
- הקלות and non-conforming uses: 15 days from publication or notice (s. 149).
