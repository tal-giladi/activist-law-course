# 10.2 · Reading a Plan on Mavat

By the end of this lesson you will be able to find a plan on the Planning Administration's site, read its instructions, map and appendices like a planner, and write down in plain words what would change on the ground and when your objection is due.

## Why it matters

An objection is only as good as your reading of the plan. Committees dismiss objections that complain about things the plan does not say, and they accept objections that point at a specific line in the rights table or a specific colour on the map. Developers and authorities have planners who read these documents every day. With an hour of method you can read them too.

The documents are public and free. The law requires every deposit to be published in the official gazette, in newspapers, on the internet, on boards in the neighbourhood and on a sign at the site. The plan documents themselves are online on Mavat, the national planning information site.

## The law

> הודעה על הפקדת כל תכנית תפורסם ברשומות ובעתון; ... כן תפורסם ההודעה על לוחות המודעות בשכונות הנוגעות בדבר.

*Gist:* every deposit is published in the official gazette (רשומות), in newspapers and on the neighbourhood notice boards. ([Planning and Building Law s. 89](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_89))

> הודעה על הפקדת תכנית מיתאר מקומית או תכנית מפורטת תפורסם על חשבון מגיש התכנית, על גבי שלט במקום בולט בתחום התכנית, למשך התקופה שנקבעה להגשת התנגדויות

*Gist:* a local outline or detailed plan must also be advertised on a sign at a prominent place in the plan area for the whole objection period; for a plan of up to 3,000 m² the neighbours must also be notified. ([s. 89א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_89א))

> (1) פרוטוקול ישיבה של מוסד תכנון שאושר יהיה פתוח לעיון הציבור והקלטת ישיבה תהיה פתוחה לציבור לצורך האזנה; עיון והאזנה כאמור יהיו בלא תשלום.
> (2) פרוטוקול ישיבה של מוסד תכנון יפורסם באתר האינטרנט של מוסד התכנון או באתר האינטרנט של רשות מקומית ...

*Gist:* approved protocols of planning bodies are open to the public free of charge, the recordings may be heard, and protocols must be posted online. ([s. 48ד(ה)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_התכנון_והבניה#סעיף_48ד))

## How it works in practice

### Finding the plan

| Where | What you find | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| [Mavat](https://mavat.iplan.gov.il/SV3) — "מידע תכנוני" | Search by plan number or name; advanced search ("חיפוש מתקדם") by gush and chelka, district, planning area, locality, street, status ("תכניות בתהליך הפקדה"), dates | Results show number, name, authority level (סמכות), location, status, and links to the instructions and map |
| The site itself | The deposit sign (s. 89א) with the plan number and deadline | Photograph it with the date: it is evidence of when publication happened |
| Newspapers | The "הודעות בענייני תכנון ובניה" section (s. 1א) | The **later** of the newspaper publications starts the clock (s. 102) |
| Local committee website | Deposit notices and protocols (ss. 1ב, 48ד) | Many committees use their own portal; look for "פרוטוקולים" and "תכניות בהפקדה" |
| [Meirim](https://meirim.org/) | Free e-mail alerts for new plans by area and topic (signup) | Set it up once for your town |

**Plan numbers.** New-format numbers look like `507-0403931`: the first three digits identify the planning area (here Tel Aviv-Jaffa), the rest is the plan's serial number. Older and special plans use other formats (ותמ״ל plans, for example, are numbered like `תמל/1061`); search by name if the number fails.

### Reading the documents in order

1. **Status and dates.** On the plan page: status (in preparation, deposited, approved, rejected), the deposit date, and under the objection button ("הגשת התנגדות לתכנית") the **last date for objections**. Write it down at once.
2. **Purpose (מטרת התכנית) and main provisions.** In most plans in the standard format (נוהל מבא״ת) this is chapter 2 of the instructions. One paragraph that says what the plan does. Read it twice.
3. **The map (תשריט).** Compare **מצב מאושר** with **מצב מוצע**. Colours are standardised; always read the legend (מקרא) instead of guessing. Find your street, your plot, the public areas, the roads.
4. **Land uses (chapter 4) and the rights table (chapter 5).** For each plot (תא שטח): use, main and service floor area, number of units, floors and height, building lines. This is where "a 28-storey tower" becomes "plot 3: 210 units, 24,000 m² main area, 28 floors, 3 m rear line".
5. **Additional provisions and implementation.** Conditions for permits, stages, what must be built before occupancy (a school, a road), demolitions, parking ratios.
6. **Appendices.** Building layout (בינוי), traffic (תנועה), landscape, drainage, environmental. Check whether each is **binding** (מחייב) or only **guiding** (מנחה): a lovely green courtyard in a guiding appendix may never be built.
7. **Environmental review.** Where the law requires it, an environmental impact statement (s. 119ג) or an environmental opinion. Look for air, noise, traffic, shading.
8. **Objections already filed.** After the secretariat checks them, online objections appear under the plan's "התנגדויות" tab with the objector's name and file. Read them: you may join, or answer them.

### Documents behind the plan

- **Protocols.** The deposit discussion explains why the committee accepted the plan and which conditions it set. They are free and must be online (s. 48ד).
- **Recordings.** You may listen to the recording of a session free of charge.
- **Everything else** (planners' opinions, correspondence with the developer, traffic surveys not attached): an FOI request to the committee (lesson 05.2). Ask early: the objection period is short.

### Turning the reading into a list of changes

Write one line per change, in plain words, with its source:

| Change | Source in the plan | Effect on you |
|---|---|---|
| Garden on plot 7 becomes a road | Map, מצב מוצע; land-use table | Loss of the only playground within 400 m |
| Height from 4 to 28 floors on plots 2–3 | Rights table, chapter 5 | Shading of my flat in winter (check the shading appendix) |

This table becomes the backbone of your objection in lesson 10.3.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Farmers of a moshav in Emek Hefer hear that a road will be widened through their orchards. Yossi Biton, who grows avocados there, wants to know what the plan says.

1. **Find.** On Mavat, advanced search by the gush and chelka of his orchard returns two plans: an old approved detailed plan and a new plan whose status is "transferred for comments by the district committees" by the National Infrastructure Committee.
2. **Read the purpose.** "Upgrading the road to a four-lane road with interchanges." The authority level is national: it is a **ות״ל** plan, so the response is a השגה under s. 76ג, not an ordinary objection (lesson 10.1).
3. **Map.** In the proposed situation, a red strip (road) 40 m wide takes the southern 18 m of his orchard; a "temporary work area" takes 10 m more. In the approved situation the strip was 25 m.
4. **Instructions.** The implementation chapter allows temporary work areas for five years and says they will be "restored". There is no drainage appendix for the orchards' irrigation lines.
5. **Environmental appendix.** A noise appendix exists; it covers houses, not the farm workers' building.
6. **Behind the plan.** The protocol of the committee's discussion shows the planners rejected a northern alignment because of "cost". Yossi sends an FOI request for the alternatives study.
7. **List of changes.** Four lines: permanent loss of 18 m, temporary loss of 10 m for five years, irrigation cut, no noise review for the workers' building. Deadline noted from the date of the transfer notice.

**Mirror test.** A neighbouring Arab town that has waited years for the same interchange to give it a second entrance reads the same plan to **support** it and to ask for the interchange to open earlier. Same documents, same method.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A plan that affects a place you know: deposited if possible, otherwise any recent plan.
- [mavat.iplan.gov.il](https://mavat.iplan.gov.il/SV3), a PDF reader, and part A ("פרטי התכנית") of the [planning-objection template](../../templates/planning-objection.md).
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. Find the plan on Mavat. Copy its number, name, authority level, status, deposit date and last date for objections.
2. Read the purpose paragraph and write it in one English sentence.
3. Open the map. List what changes between the approved and proposed situations near the place you care about.
4. Open the rights table. For the plots near you, copy: use, main area, service area, units, floors, building lines.
5. List the appendices and mark each binding or guiding.
6. Check for an environmental review and what it covers.
7. Find the protocol of the deposit discussion; note the conditions the committee set.
8. Fill in the change table above (at least three rows).
9. Write one FOI question for a document you could not find.

### Template

[planning-objection.md](../../templates/planning-objection.md), part A ("פרטי התכנית") — fill it in now; you will complete the rest in lesson 10.3.

### How you know it worked

- Every row of your change table points to a specific chapter, table row or map layer.
- You can say in one sentence what the plan does and in one sentence how it affects you.
- The objection deadline is in your calendar.

### Self-check

- [ ] I compared the approved and proposed situations, not only the proposed one.
- [ ] I read the rights table for the plots that matter to me.
- [ ] I know which appendices bind and which only guide.
- [ ] I read the deposit protocol.
- [ ] I wrote down the last date for objections from the plan page and checked it against the publication dates.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading only the proposed map | You object to things that are already approved | Check the approved situation and cut those points | Always compare both situations |
| Relying on a guiding building appendix | The promised park or setback may never be built | Ask in the objection to make the appendix binding | Mark every appendix binding or guiding |
| Counting the deadline from the sign or a friend's message | Late objection | Check the newspaper and gazette dates; ask the secretariat | Use the later newspaper date (s. 102) and the date on the plan page |
| Objecting to rumours ("they'll build 40 floors") | Objection dismissed as unfounded | Quote the rights table | Write every change with its source |
| Waiting for documents by FOI until the last week | No time to use them | File the objection on time with what you have; ask to add documents | Send FOI requests in the first days of the period |

## Review

- Deposits are published in the gazette, newspapers, online, on neighbourhood boards and on a sign at the site; the documents are on Mavat.
- Read in order: status and deadline, purpose, map (approved vs proposed), land uses and rights table, provisions, appendices, environmental review, existing objections.
- Binding appendices bind; guiding ones do not.
- Protocols and recordings are free (s. 48ד); request the rest by FOI early.
- Turn your reading into a change table with sources: it is the skeleton of your objection.
