# 18.2 · Registering an Amuta Yourself

Registering an amuta does not need a lawyer: the founders file online, in their own names, for their own association. By the end of this lesson you will be able to choose a registrable name and objectives, decide whether the model bylaws are enough, assemble the complete online application, and plan the first two months after the certificate arrives.

## Why it matters

Founders who pay a service provider to "open an amuta" often receive generic objectives that do not describe what they actually do, bylaws nobody read, and no plan for the duties that start on day one. The application itself is short. What matters is the thinking behind three of its fields — name, objectives and bylaws — because they are hard to change later: changing objectives needs a general assembly decision and, for a new unrelated objective, a court's approval (s. 11).

Founders act for themselves when they register their own amuta, so this is squarely the kind of act this course teaches you to do alone.

## The law

### The application

> בקשה לרישום עמותה תוגש בידי המייסדים לרשם העמותות (להלן – הרשם) באופן מקוון ויצויינו בה שם העמותה, מטרותיה, מענה בישראל, מענה הדיגיטלי, אחד מכל פרט המנוי בתוספת הרביעית לחוק החברות, שמות המייסדים, מעניהם ומספרי זהותם; לבקשה תצורף הצהרה חתומה בידי כל המייסדים על נכונותם לייסד עמותה, כאמור בבקשה, ולכהן בה כחברי הועד; זהות המייסדים תאומת באופן מקוון כפי שיקבע הרשם לפי סעיף 38ב(ב) או בידי עורך דין.

*Gist:* the founders apply online with the name, objectives, an Israeli address, a digital address, and each founder's name, address and ID number; every founder signs a declaration that they agree to found the amuta and serve on its committee; identities are verified online or by a lawyer. ([חוק העמותות, s. 2(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_העמותות))

### The name

> לא תירשם עמותה בשם העלול להטעות או לפגוע בתקנת הציבור או ברגשותיו.

*Gist:* no name that may mislead or offend public order or feelings. (s. 4(א)) Section 4(ב) also bars a name identical or confusingly similar to a registered body, to one struck off in the last two years, or to an amuta already being registered.

> שם העמותה יצויין בכל מסמך, שילוט או פרסום היוצא מטעמה, בצורה המלאה המופיעה בתעודת הרישום; השם יצויין באותיות אחידות וללא קיצורים והשמטות ויכלול בסופו את הציון "עמותה", "עמותה רשומה" או "(ע״ר)".

*Gist:* the full registered name, ending with "עמותה", "עמותה רשומה" or "(ע״ר)", appears on every document, sign and publication. (s. 8א)

### The bylaws

> המייסדים רשאים להגיש לרשם תקנון לעמותה; לא הגישו המייסדים תקנון לרשם כאמור, יראו כתקנונה את התקנון המצוי שבתוספת הראשונה.

*Gist:* the founders may file their own bylaws; if they do not, the model bylaws in the First Schedule apply. (s. 10) Own bylaws are registered only if they meet the provisions listed in s. 12(א); anything they leave out or get wrong is filled by the model bylaws (s. 12(ב)).

### Registration and its effect

> הוגשה בקשה בהתאם לסעיף 2, ירשום הרשם את העמותה בפנקס העמותות זולת אם ראה עצמו מנוע מלעשות כן לפי סעיפים 1, 3, 4(א) או 4(ב).

*Gist:* the Registrar must register a proper application unless the founders, objectives or name fail ss. 1, 3 or 4. (s. 5) He then publishes the registration in Reshumot and issues a certificate, which is conclusive proof that the amuta was lawfully founded (s. 7).

### Who may serve

Every person aged 17 or more, and any corporation, may be a member (s. 15). A committee or audit committee member must be a member of the amuta, must not be paid for other services to it, and must not be a minor, legally incompetent, bankrupt, or convicted of certain offences (s. 33). Nobody may sit on both the committee and the audit committee (s. 32). Until the first committee is elected, the founders are the committee (s. 26(א)).

Fees: the Amutot (Fees) Regulations set a registration fee, not including the Reshumot publication charge, and an annual fee from the year after registration. Amounts are updated every 1 January (reg. 3); see [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## How it works in practice

### Before you open the form

1. **People.** At least two adult founders (s. 1). In practice you need at least three: the model bylaws require a committee of at least two (First Schedule, item 12), and the audit committee must be someone else (s. 32) — unless the general assembly appoints an accountant or approved body instead (s. 19(ב)). Choose founders who will still be active in two years.
2. **Name.** Search [GuideStar](https://www.guidestar.org.il/) for similar names. Avoid names that suggest a government body or a profession you are not ("המרכז הלאומי ל…", "הלשכה המשפטית ל…").
3. **Objectives.** Write three to six objectives that describe what you will actually do, broad enough to grow but specific enough to be credible. Funders and the Tax Authority read them. Avoid profit-making as an aim, and avoid anything that negates the existence of Israel or its democratic character (s. 3).
4. **Bylaws.** The model bylaws are short and legally safe. Write your own only for a real need, for example: membership fees and conditions, a larger committee, decisions by e-mail or video, branches, or how members are expelled. Use the [bylaws checklist](../../templates/amuta-bylaws-checklist.md).
5. **Addresses.** An Israeli address for notices (s. 8ב) and a digital address (an e-mail the Registrar verifies, s. 8ג). Use an address the amuta will keep.
6. **Identity.** Each founder needs government online identification (הזדהות לאומית) or a lawyer's verification (s. 2(א)).

### Filing

- **Where:** the "רישום עמותה" online service of the Israeli Corporations Authority on gov.il. The founders fill in the details, upload the signed declaration and, if any, their own bylaws, and pay online.
- **Cost:** the registration fee (about ₪900 in 2026) plus the Reshumot publication charge shown at payment. No lawyer is needed. See [current numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- **Time:** the Registrar checks the name, objectives and bylaws and may ask for corrections. Plan for several weeks. Changes before registration need a majority of founders and 14 days' notice to all founders by registered mail (s. 2(ב)).
- **Help:** the Corporations Authority call centre is *5601 (from its 2026 fee table).

### After the certificate: the first 60 days

| When | What | Law |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Download the certificate; use the full name with "(ע״ר)" everywhere | ss. 7, 8א |
| Week 1 | Open a bank account in the amuta's name; decide who signs (at least two, model bylaws item 18) and notify the Registrar | s. 38(א)(2), First Schedule item 18 |
| Week 1 | Open the register of members and the register of committee members | ss. 18, 29 |
| Weeks 1–4 | Ask an accountant about Tax Authority registration (VAT status as a non-profit, employer file if you pay anyone) | — |
| Month 1–2 | Hold a first general assembly: elect the committee and the audit committee (or appoint an accountant), approve membership fees if any; keep signed minutes | ss. 20, 23, 26, 31 |
| Within 2 weeks of any change | Report new committee or audit members, address changes, signatories | s. 38(ב)(1) |
| January | Start the compliance calendar from lesson 18.3 | — |

## Worked example

**Situation.** Families of reservists from a Negev town want to run a support centre: childcare during call-ups, help with employers, and advocacy for local policies. Four parents agree to found an amuta.

1. **Name.** Their first idea, "המרכז הארצי למשפחות מילואים", suggests a national body and is close to existing names on GuideStar. They choose "משפחות המילואים [שם העיר]" — local and accurate.
2. **Objectives.** "לסייע למשפחות משרתי מילואים ב[שם העיר] ובסביבתה; להפעיל שירותי תמיכה, ייעוץ חברתי ופעילות קהילתית; לקדם מדיניות ציבורית ברשות המקומית ובמוסדות המדינה לטובת משפחות אלה." They drop "ייעוץ משפטי" from the draft: the amuta has no lawyers, and individual legal advice is reserved to them (lesson 18.4).
3. **Bylaws.** They want members to pay ₪50 a year and committee meetings by video. The model bylaws allow the committee to set membership fees with the general assembly's approval (item 2(ג)) and to regulate its own meetings (item 15), so they keep the model bylaws and save the drafting.
4. **People.** Two parents on the committee, one on the audit committee, the fourth as a member who keeps the books. Nobody sits on both bodies (s. 32).
5. **Filing.** Each founder logs in with national online identification; they upload the declaration signed by all four, give the town address of one founder and a new e-mail for the amuta as the digital address, and pay the fee.
6. **After registration.** Bank account with two signatories; registers opened in a shared spreadsheet; first general assembly six weeks later, minutes signed by the chair.

**Mirror test.** The same steps serve an amuta for Bedouin women's employment in the same region, a residents' association against a planned landfill, or a Torah-study group that wants to rent a hall.

## Do it yourself

The default level is a dry run: prepare the full file without submitting it. Submit only if your group decided in lesson 18.1 to register.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Only the founders themselves fill in and sign the application.

### You need

- Your lesson 18.1 decision and group agreement.
- At least three people who agree to serve, each with national online identification.
- [Amuta bylaws checklist](../../templates/amuta-bylaws-checklist.md) and the [Amutot Law](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_העמותות) First Schedule.
- 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. Search GuideStar for names like yours; write your chosen name and two alternatives.
2. Write three to six objectives in Hebrew. Check each against s. 3 and against lesson 18.4 (no "legal advice to the public" unless lawyers will give it).
3. Go through the bylaws checklist. Decide: model bylaws, or own bylaws. If own bylaws, list each change from the model and the reason.
4. List the founders and their roles: committee, audit committee, books. Check s. 33 and s. 32 for each.
5. Fill in a dry-run application sheet: name, objectives, address, digital address, founders' details, declaration text, fee.
6. Write your first-60-days plan from the table above, with names and dates.

### Template

[Amuta bylaws checklist (רשימת בדיקה לתקנון עמותה)](../../templates/amuta-bylaws-checklist.md) — model bylaws or own bylaws, the s. 12 provisions, and the clauses activist amutot most often add.

### How you know it worked

- No name on GuideStar is identical or confusingly similar to yours.
- Each objective says what you will do, and none promises legal advice or profit.
- Your bylaws choice is explained; if you wrote your own, every change from the model has a reason.
- Every role is filled without anyone sitting on both the committee and the audit committee.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name close to an existing body | Registrar refuses or later orders a change (s. 4) | File again with a distinct name | Search GuideStar first |
| Generic or inflated objectives ("כל מטרה חוקית") | Funders and the Tax Authority doubt the amuta; later activity outside the objectives | Amend by general assembly decision (s. 11) | Write objectives that describe real activity |
| Objective "legal advice to the public" with no lawyers | Activity that may breach Bar Association Law s. 20 | Remove it; describe "information and guidance" accurately | Check objectives against lesson 18.4 |
| Two founders and no separate audit committee | Cannot comply with ss. 19 and 32 | Recruit, or have the assembly appoint an accountant (s. 19(ב)) | Plan for three people from the start |
| Own bylaws copied from the internet | Clauses that conflict with the law; filled by the model bylaws anyway (s. 12(ב)) | Adopt the model bylaws or fix with the checklist | Change the model only for a real need |
| No minutes of the first assembly | Elections hard to prove; Registrar questions | Hold a new assembly, sign the minutes | Use a minutes template from day one (s. 23) |

## Review

- The founders apply online, in their own names: name, objectives, Israeli and digital addresses, founders' details and a signed declaration (s. 2).
- The Registrar must register unless the founders, objectives or name fail ss. 1, 3 or 4 (s. 5); the certificate is conclusive proof of founding (s. 7).
- Without own bylaws, the model bylaws apply (s. 10); own bylaws must meet the s. 12 list.
- Plan for at least three people: a committee of two and a separate audit committee (s. 32).
- The duties start on day one: full name with "(ע״ר)", registers, bank signatories, the first general assembly and reporting changes within two weeks.
