# 12.3 · Writing a Position Paper

By the end of this lesson you will be able to write a short position paper (נייר עמדה) for a Knesset committee: one page of summary and requests, a few pages of reasoned arguments, each ending in a drafted amendment, with sources a committee's legal adviser can check — and to send it to the people who will actually use it.

## Why it matters

Committee members, their advisers, the committee's legal adviser and the Knesset Research and Information Center read dozens of submissions on a busy bill. Most are long, emotional and end in "we oppose the bill". The papers that change a clause are short, factual, and hand the committee a ready-made wording. A good paper also lives on: it is placed on the committee's page, quoted in the protocol, used by an MK to draft a reservation, and cited later in a court petition or an FOI request.

| הצעת נוסח | hatza'at nusach | Proposed wording of a clause |

| הערכת השפעות רגולציה (RIA) | ha'arachat hashpa'ot regulatzya | Regulatory impact assessment the government may present |

## The law

There is no statute that regulates position papers. What matters are the rules that decide what the committee can do with your proposals.

> ועדה רשאית לתקן הצעת חוק שהיא דנה בה לפי פרק זה, והיא רשאית לתקן גם סעיפי חוק שלא נזכרו בנוסח הצעת החוק שאושר בקריאה הראשונה, ובלבד שהתיקונים לא יחרגו מגדר הנושא של הצעת החוק.

*Gist:* the committee may amend the bill, and even sections of the existing law not mentioned in it, but not beyond the bill's subject — so your proposals must stay inside it. ([תקנון הכנסת, s. 85(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_85))

> חבר הכנסת, שר, או סגן שר בענייני המשרד שבו הוא מכהן, רשאים להציע תיקונים להצעת חוק שהוועדה דנה בה, כל עוד הוועדה לא אישרה את הצעת החוק לקריאה השנייה ולקריאה השלישית

*Gist:* only MKs and ministers can formally propose amendments, until the committee approves the bill for the second and third readings — so your paper must give them a wording they can adopt. ([s. 86(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_86))

> הסתייגות תנוסח כתיקון להצעת החוק, ותירשם לסעיף מסוים ... הסתייגות לא תשלול את עצם הצעת החוק.

*Gist:* a reservation is drafted as an amendment to a specific clause and cannot simply reject the whole bill. ([s. 86(ג)–(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_86))

> יושב ראש ועדה רשאי להזמין לישיבה כל מי שיש לו ידע או עניין בנושא שהוועדה דנה בו, לשמוע את דעתו ולבקש ממנו מידע.

*Gist:* the committee wants knowledge and information from you; a paper is the way to give it. ([s. 125(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_125))

## How it works in practice

### The structure

Use the [position-paper template](../../templates/position-paper.md):

| Part | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Heading | 3 lines | To the committee; the bill's name and number; who submits; date |
| Summary (תקציר) | ≤ ½ page | Who you are, your position in one sentence, numbered requests |
| Background | ½ page | Facts the committee needs; your experience or data |
| Arguments | 1–2 pages | One heading per request: problem → evidence → proposed wording |
| Proposed wording table | ½ page | Clause, current text, proposed text, reason in one line |
| Sources | as needed | Every number and quote with a link or document |
| Contact | 2 lines | Name, role, phone, e-mail |

### Writing rules that work

1. **Requests first.** A member who reads only the first page must know what you want changed.
2. **One argument per heading**, strongest first. Three good arguments beat ten weak ones.
3. **Every argument ends in a wording.** "In clause 4(ב), replace 'עד 30 ימים' with 'עד 60 ימים'." Committees adopt wordings; they rarely draft them for you.
4. **Stay inside the bill's subject** (s. 85). A good idea on a different topic is a separate bill or a motion for the agenda.
5. **Facts with sources.** Official data (Central Bureau of Statistics, ministry reports, State Comptroller, Knesset Research and Information Center), FOI answers (lesson 05.2), court rulings. Mark your own estimates as estimates.
6. **Answer the other side.** Name the strongest argument for the clause you oppose and answer it. Members trust a paper that does this.
7. **Respectful, plain Hebrew.** No slogans, no attacks on people. Criticise the clause, not the MK.
8. **Short.** Two to four pages; attach data separately.

### Sending it

- **To:** the committee secretariat (so it is placed on the committee's page for the session), with copies to the committee's legal adviser, the chair, and members who are likely to take up your point — from the coalition and the opposition.
- **When:** as soon as the session is set, and no later than a few days before it. Before the committee approves the bill for second and third readings, MKs can still propose amendments (s. 86(א)).
- **Follow up:** check the session protocol and the next draft (נוסח לדיון). If your proposal was rejected, ask an MK to file it as a reservation (s. 86(ב)).
- **Keep records:** what you sent, to whom, when, and what happened. You will need them for the project and for any later petition.

### Election recess note

In October 2026 no new committee work is expected until the 26th Knesset forms its committees. Write the paper now; date it and update it once the bill is re-tabled or continuity is applied (lesson 12.1).

## Worked example

**Situation.** A government bill would let local authorities fine shop owners whose entrances are not accessible. Disability organisations support it but say the 90-day correction period is too long; a small-business owners' association, represented by its volunteer chair Avi Mizrahi, supports accessibility but says older buildings need more time and a grant scheme.

Avi's paper (summary page):

1. **Who.** A registered amuta of 1,200 shop owners in eight towns; Avi is its unpaid chair.
2. **Position.** "We support the bill's goal and ask for three changes that make it workable for small shops in old buildings."
3. **Requests.** (1) In clause 3(ב), add: "בבניין שנבנה לפני שנת 1970 רשאית הרשות להאריך את התקופה עד 12 חודשים, אם הוגשה בקשה להיתר." (2) In clause 5, cap the first fine for a business with fewer than five employees. (3) Require the ministry to report to the committee within a year on enforcement in each district.
4. **Evidence.** A survey of 210 member shops (method attached; marked as the association's own data), an FOI answer from two municipalities on building permits for ramps (lesson 05.2), and the time a typical permit takes according to the local committee's published report.
5. **Answer to the other side.** "Disability organisations fear endless delays; our extension applies only to buildings before 1970 and only after a permit request is filed."
6. **Sent** to the secretariat, the legal adviser, the chair, two coalition and two opposition members, a week before the session.

**Result.** The committee adopts request 3 and a shorter version of request 1 (9 months). Request 2 is rejected; an opposition MK files it as a reservation.

**Mirror test.** The disability organisation writes its own paper with the same structure, asking to cut the 90 days to 60 and to add a right for a disabled customer to complain directly to the authority. Two opposite positions, same method; the committee hears both.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only (or for a group that has authorised you). This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The default is a dry run: send the paper only if there is a real bill and a real session.

### You need

- The bill you followed in lessons 12.1–12.2 (or a current תזכיר חוק) and its explanatory notes.
- [position-paper.md](../../templates/position-paper.md).
- 2 hours.

### Steps

1. Read the bill and its explanatory notes. Mark the two or three clauses that matter most to you.
2. Write your position in one sentence.
3. For each clause: the problem, the evidence (with a source), and a proposed wording in Hebrew.
4. Check each proposal against s. 85: is it within the bill's subject?
5. Write the strongest argument for the other side and your answer.
6. Fill the template: summary page first, then background, arguments, wording table, sources.
7. Cut to four pages. Read it as an MK with five minutes.
8. Plan the sending list and the date.

### Template

[position-paper.md](../../templates/position-paper.md) — a Hebrew position paper to a Knesset committee with a summary page, argument blocks, a proposed-wording table, a sources list and a sending checklist.

### How you know it worked

- A reader of page one alone can list your requests.
- Every request has a wording a legal adviser could paste into the bill.
- Every number has a source.

### Self-check

- [ ] Summary page with numbered requests.
- [ ] Each argument: problem → evidence → wording.
- [ ] All proposals within the bill's subject (s. 85).
- [ ] The other side's best argument answered.
- [ ] Sources listed; own data marked as such.
- [ ] Two to four pages; sending list and date planned.

## Learn more

- **Open Knesset — committee documents** — FREE-FULL, Hebrew, 30 minutes: <https://oknesset.org>. Open the committee that handles your bill, find a session with submitted documents, and read two position papers from opposing sides. Note which one ends in wordings. *Fallback:* the bill page on the Knesset website, "מסמכי ועדה" (30 minutes).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ten pages, no summary | Not read | Add a one-page summary and resend | Requests on page one |
| "We oppose the bill" only | No clause changes; a reservation cannot simply negate the bill (s. 86(ד)) | Add specific wordings | End every argument in a wording |
| Proposal outside the bill's subject | Blocked as a "new topic" (s. 85) | Turn it into a motion for the agenda or a separate bill | Check the bill's title and purpose |
| Unsourced numbers | Legal adviser or ministry dismisses them | Add sources or mark estimates | Source every fact |
| Sent after the vote in committee | Too late for amendments | Ask an MK for a reservation or a speech in the second reading | Send as soon as the session is set |
| Sent only to friendly MKs | Ignored by the majority | Copy the chair, the legal adviser and both sides | Plan a balanced sending list |

## Review

- A position paper is short: summary and requests first, then reasons, wordings and sources.
- Only MKs and ministers propose amendments; your paper gives them ready wordings.
- Proposals must stay inside the bill's subject (s. 85); reservations amend clauses and cannot negate the bill.
- Send it to the secretariat, legal adviser, chair and members from both sides, before the session.
- Follow up through the protocol, the next draft and, if needed, a reservation.
