# 12.5 · Your Local Authority: Council Meetings, Municipal Complaints and Local FOI

By the end of this lesson you will be able to attend your council's meetings and get their protocols and recordings, bring an issue onto the council's agenda through a council member's question or motion, complain to your authority's own Public Complaints Commissioner, and ask the authority's FOI officer for documents — and to choose which of these fits a given local problem.

## Why it matters

Most of what touches daily life — rubbish collection, playgrounds, parking, property tax (ארנונה), building inspection, community centres, synagogues and mosques on public land, school transport, street lighting — is decided by the local authority, not the Knesset. Council meetings are public by law, their protocols and recordings must be online, every authority must have a complaints commissioner, and every authority has an FOI officer. Few residents use these tools, so those who do are noticed. They are also the cheapest: all of them are free except the FOI fee, and none needs a lawyer.

## The law

### Council meetings are public, and their records go online

> ישיבות מועצה יהיו פומביות; למעט ישיבה שראש העיריה הורה על קיומה, כולה או מקצתה, בדלתיים סגורות לאחר שראה כי טעמים שבביטחון המדינה או טעמים שבצנעת הפרט מחייבים לעשות כן, בהתאם לחוות דעת של היועץ המשפטי לעיריה, שתונח לפני חברי המועצה.

*Gist:* council meetings are public; the mayor may close a meeting or part of it only for state security or privacy, based on the legal adviser's opinion laid before the members. ([פקודת העיריות, Second Schedule, s. 18(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_העיריות#תוספת_2_פרט_18))

> עיריה תקים ותפעיל אתר אינטרנט נגיש לציבור ללא תשלום, שבו תפרסם, בין השאר – ... (2) פרוטוקולים של ישיבות המועצה בדלתיים פתוחות, לא יאוחר משני ימי עבודה מיום אישורם במועצה, והקלטות או תמלילים של ישיבות כאמור;

*Gist:* the city must post protocols of open council meetings within two working days of their approval, and recordings or transcripts of those meetings, on a free website. ([s. 248ב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_העיריות#סעיף_248ב))

> לא יאוחר משבעה ימים לפני תחילת הישיבה מן המניין הקרובה, רשאי חבר המועצה להגיש לראש העיריה שאילתה בכתב על עניין עובדתי שבתחום תפקידי העיריה, לרבות בעניין תאגידים עירוניים, והיא תנוסח בקצרה ובצורת שאלה בלבד

*Gist:* a council member may submit a written factual question to the mayor at least seven days before a regular meeting; the answer is read at the meeting (or deferred once), and a member may submit no more than two such questions per meeting. ([Second Schedule, s. 36](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/פקודת_העיריות#תוספת_2_פרט_36))

Further rules you will use: a regular meeting at least once a month (s. 5); invitations with the agenda 48 hours ahead, ten days for the budget and by-laws (s. 14); the mayor sets the agenda (s. 24); a council member may submit motions for the agenda before a regular meeting, and the council decides whether to discuss them (ss. 27–28); a direct question to the mayor is answered in writing within 21 days (s. 38); every meeting is recorded and a protocol is kept (s. 50). These rules are in the Municipalities Ordinance for cities; local and regional councils have similar rules under their own orders — check the text for your council.

### The authority's Public Complaints Commissioner

> כל אדם רשאי להגיש תלונה לממונה על תלונות הציבור על הרשות המקומית ומוסדותיה, על עובד, על נושא משרה או על ממלא תפקיד ברשות המקומית, וכן על גוף עירוני מבוקר

*Gist:* anyone may complain about the authority, its institutions, staff and office holders, and municipal corporations. ([חוק הרשויות המקומיות (ממונה על תלונות הציבור), s. 5(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_(ממונה_על_תלונות_הציבור)))

> (1) המעשה פוגע במישרין במתלונן עצמו, או מונע ממנו במישרין טובת הנאה ... (2) המעשה נעשה בניגוד לחוק או שנעשה בלא סמכות חוקית או בניגוד למינהל תקין, או שיש בו משום נוקשות יתרה או אי־צדק בולט.

*Gist:* the act (or failure) must harm you directly (or a person who consented), and be unlawful, without authority, against proper administration, overly rigid or flagrantly unjust. ([s. 5(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הרשויות_המקומיות_(ממונה_על_תלונות_הציבור)))

Also: the complaint is signed and gives your name, address and a detailed description with dates (s. 6); it is not investigated if the matter is in court, was already sent to the state Commissioner, or is a judicial act, and normally not if it is over a year old or could be appealed (s. 7); the investigation ends within a year (s. 8(ז)); a justified complaint leads to a reasoned notice and a demand to fix the defect (s. 11); and a complaint does not extend any court deadline (s. 14(א)(2)). The commissioner is usually the authority's internal auditor (s. 2), independent and answerable only to the council (s. 3), and the authority must publish how to complain, including on property-tax notices (s. 17).

### Local FOI

> רשות ציבורית ... (6) רשות מקומית;

*Gist:* local authorities are public authorities under the FOI Law, so every one must appoint an FOI officer (s. 3) and answer within the statutory deadlines (s. 7). ([חוק חופש המידע, s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע))

## How it works in practice

### Watching and reading the council

- **Dates and agenda:** on the municipality's website (ישיבות מועצה / פרוטוקולים), or ask the council meetings coordinator (מרכז ישיבות המועצה). Regular meetings start no earlier than 18:00 unless all members agree (s. 6(ב)).
- **Attending:** meetings are public; many are also streamed. The public watches; the rules give residents no right to speak. Your voice reaches the council through a member.
- **Records:** the approved protocol must be online within two working days of approval, with a recording or transcript (s. 248ב). If it is missing, write to the coordinator, and then to the FOI officer.
- **Closed sessions:** only for state security or privacy, with the legal adviser's opinion laid before the members (s. 18(א)). Ask for that opinion if a meeting on your issue is closed.

### Getting your issue onto the agenda

The council deadlines below are also listed on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

| Tool | Who files | Rule | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question (שאילתה) | Council member | Written, factual, 7 days before a regular meeting; max two per member per meeting (s. 36) | Mayor answers at the meeting; on the record |
| Direct question | Council member | Written, signed, to the mayor's office; max two per month (s. 38) | Written answer within 21 days |
| Motion for the agenda | Council member | Before a regular meeting (s. 27) | Council decides whether to discuss it, move it to a committee or reject it (s. 28) |
| Special meeting | One third of the members | Written demand with agenda and draft decision (s. 9) | Meeting within 7 days |

Write to council members from both the coalition and the opposition, with a draft question ready to file. Use Part B of the [question request template](../../templates/council-question-request.md).

### Which tool for which local problem

| Problem | First tool | Next |
|---|---|---|
| You were harmed by a service, a delay or a decision (a fine, a permit, a closed facility) | Write to the department (lesson 02.5); then the local commissioner | State Commissioner (lesson 05.4) if the local one is not suitable — not both on the same matter; petition (lesson 05.6) |
| You need documents (contracts, budgets, inspection reports) | FOI request to the authority's FOI officer (lesson 05.2) | Reminder to the mayor as head of the authority; petition. The government FOI Unit does not supervise local authorities (lesson 05.3) |
| A policy issue for the whole town | Council member's question or motion | Position paper to the council committee; a public campaign |
| A systemic failure | Local internal auditor (מבקר הרשות) | State Comptroller's local-government audit |
| A protocol or recording missing online | Write to the council coordinator, citing s. 248ב | FOI request; complaint to the local commissioner |

### Complaining to the local commissioner

Find the commissioner's details on the authority's website or on your ארנונה notice (s. 17). Use the [municipal complaint template](../../templates/municipal-complaint.md): your details and signature, the body or official, the facts with dates, how it harmed you, why it is wrong (s. 5(ב)(2) grounds), what you ask, and your declarations that the matter is not in court and not filed with the state Commissioner. Attach your earlier letter and the reply. Diarise any court deadline separately: the complaint does not extend it (s. 14).

### FOI to the authority

Find the FOI officer on the authority's website (the authority must make public how to contact its officer, FOI Law s. 4(ב)) and send the [FOI request](../../templates/foi-request.md) by e-mail or the authority's form. Fees and deadlines: see [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

## Worked example

**Situation.** In a southern town, the only playground in Tamar Levi's neighbourhood has been fenced off for eight months after a safety inspection. Her written request to the municipality in April got "the matter is being handled".

1. **Council.** Tamar finds the council's monthly meeting dates and the last six protocols. One protocol mentions "שיפוץ גני משחק" in the budget, without detail. The recording of the March meeting is missing from the site; she writes to the council coordinator citing s. 248ב(2), and it is posted a week later.
2. **Council member.** She writes to two council members, one from the mayor's coalition and one from the opposition, with a draft question: "מתי נסגר גן המשחקים ברחוב [___], מה ממצאי בדיקת הבטיחות, מה התקציב שאושר לשיקומו ומהו המועד הצפוי לפתיחתו?" The opposition member files it seven days before the next meeting (s. 36); the mayor answers at the meeting: "work will start next quarter".
3. **FOI.** In parallel she asks the FOI officer for the inspection report and the contract with the playground contractor (lesson 05.2).
4. **Complaint.** Two months later nothing has changed. She complains to the local commissioner: the closure harms her children directly; eight months without a decision or a timetable is against proper administration (s. 5(ב)). She asks for a timetable and a temporary safe play area. She declares the matter is not in court and not with the state Commissioner.
5. **Result.** The commissioner asks the department to respond within 21 days; it publishes a work plan, and the playground reopens three months later. The commissioner's annual report, discussed by the council (s. 15), mentions delays in playground repairs across the town.

**Mirror test.** The same tools serve residents of an Arab town in the Triangle whose street lighting has not been repaired, a Haredi neighbourhood in a mixed city asking why a plot for a synagogue was not allocated, or secular residents asking why a community centre's Shabbat activities were cancelled.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only (or for a person who gave written consent, s. 5(ב)(1)). This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The default is a dry run; file for real only if you were really harmed.

### You need

- Your own local authority's website, and a real local problem.
- [council-question-request.md](../../templates/council-question-request.md) (Part B), [municipal-complaint.md](../../templates/municipal-complaint.md), [foi-request.md](../../templates/foi-request.md).
- 90 minutes (plus one council meeting, 1–2 hours, if you attend).

### Steps

1. Find the council's meeting schedule, the last three protocols and one recording. Note whether each was posted within two working days of approval.
2. Watch part of one meeting (live or recorded). Note how questions and motions are handled.
3. Find the names and contacts of the FOI officer and the Public Complaints Commissioner.
4. Choose a real local problem. Decide, with the table above, which tool comes first.
5. Draft a council-member question (Part B of the template) with a covering letter.
6. Draft a complaint to the local commissioner, assuming a written request went unanswered for 45 days.
7. Draft one FOI request for a document that would prove your point.

### Template

- [council-question-request.md](../../templates/council-question-request.md) — Part B: a Hebrew request to a council member to submit a question or a motion for the agenda.
- [municipal-complaint.md](../../templates/municipal-complaint.md) — a Hebrew complaint to the authority's Public Complaints Commissioner with the s. 5–7 checks.
- [foi-request.md](../../templates/foi-request.md) — the FOI request from lesson 05.2.

### How you know it worked

- You found the protocols and could tell whether the authority meets s. 248ב.
- Your question is factual and within the authority's functions.
- Your complaint shows direct harm and one of the s. 5(ב)(2) grounds, and attaches your earlier letter.

### Self-check

- [ ] Meeting dates, protocols and a recording found.
- [ ] FOI officer and complaints commissioner identified.
- [ ] Right tool chosen for the problem.
- [ ] Question drafted for a council member from each side.
- [ ] Complaint: direct harm, ground, request, declarations, attachments.
- [ ] Court deadlines diarised separately.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standing up to speak at a council meeting | Removed; no effect | Pass your point to a council member | Use a member's question or motion |
| A question asking for the mayor's opinion | Not answered as a factual question | Rewrite: dates, numbers, decisions | Factual questions only (s. 36) |
| Complaint about a general policy that does not harm you | Not investigated (s. 5(ב)(1)) | Find a direct harm, or use the council or the auditor | Check direct harm first |
| Complaining to both the local and the state Commissioner | The local one will not investigate (s. 7(א)(5)) | Choose one; withdraw the other | Decide the forum first |
| Relying on the complaint while a petition deadline runs | Petition out of time | Ask the court to extend; file at once | The complaint does not extend deadlines (s. 14) |
| FOI complaint to the government FOI Unit about a city | No power over local authorities | Remind the mayor; local commissioner; petition | Check the type of body (lesson 05.3) |

## Review

- Council meetings are public; closing them needs security or privacy grounds and a legal opinion (Second Schedule s. 18).
- Cities must post open-meeting protocols within two working days of approval, with recordings or transcripts (s. 248ב).
- Residents reach the agenda through council members: questions seven days before a meeting, direct questions answered in 21 days, motions for the agenda.
- Every authority has a Public Complaints Commissioner for direct harm from unlawful or improper acts; one year, no court case, not both commissioners.
- Every authority has an FOI officer; the government FOI Unit does not supervise them.
