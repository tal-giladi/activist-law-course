# 12.4 · MKs, Parliamentary Questions and Lobbying Rules

By the end of this lesson you will be able to pick the right Members of Knesset for your issue, write to them in a way their staff can act on, draft a parliamentary question an MK can submit as it is, search the questions and answers already on record, and tell whether what you are doing is lawful advocacy or activity that needs a lobbyist permit.

## Why it matters

A citizen cannot ask a minister a question in the Knesset. An MK can, and the minister must answer within a fixed time or explain a refusal. A well-drafted question gives an MK a ready tool and gives you an official answer you can quote, compare and use in an FOI request or a petition. MKs also file reservations, ask for quick debates and push bills. The rules on lobbyists tell you where ordinary advocacy ends and regulated, paid lobbying begins — and most activists are on the free side of that line.

## Key terms

| עברית | Transliteration | English |
|---|---|---|
| שאילתה רגילה | sha'ilta regila | Ordinary question, answered orally in the plenum |
| שאילתה דחופה | sha'ilta dchufa | Urgent question, answered in the plenum the same week |
| שאילתה ישירה | sha'ilta yeshira | Direct question, answered in writing to the MK |
| שאלה נוספת | she'ela nosefet | Follow-up question after the answer |
| שדלן | shadlan | Lobbyist: someone who, as an occupation or for payment, persuades MKs for a client |
| היתר שדלן | heter shadlan | Lobbyist permit issued by a Knesset committee |
| שדולה פרלמנטרית | shdula parlamentarit | Knesset caucus: MKs grouped around a cause |
| הצעה לסדר היום / דיון מהיר | hatza'a leseder hayom / diyun mahir | Motion for the agenda / quick debate in a committee |

## The law

> חבר הכנסת, שאינו שר או סגן שר, רשאי להפנות לשר שאילתה מנוסחת בצורת שאלה, על עניין עובדתי שבתחום תפקידיו.

*Gist:* an MK who is not a minister may ask a minister a question, phrased as a question, about a factual matter within the minister's responsibility. ([תקנון הכנסת, s. 46(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_46))

> שאילתה לא תכיל – (1) כינוי או ביטוי פוגע או גזעני או פגיעה בכבוד הכנסת; (2) בקשה לחוות דעת כללית או לחוות דעת בשאלה משפטית מופשטת, עניין היפותטי או עניין שאינו בתחום תפקידיו של השר הנשאל; (3) פרטים אישיים של אדם שיש בהם פגיעה בפרטיות ...

*Gist:* a question is disqualified if it is offensive, asks for a general or abstract legal opinion, is hypothetical or outside the minister's area, or exposes someone's private details. ([s. 47(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_47))

> שאילתה רגילה לא תכיל יותר מ־50 מילים. ... השר הנשאל יכין את תשובתו לשאילתה רגילה או יודיע על סירובו להשיב, בתוך 21 ימים מיום קבלת השאילתה.

*Gist:* an ordinary question is at most 50 words and the minister prepares an answer or a refusal within 21 days; recess days are not counted (s. 48(ט)). ([s. 48](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_48))

> שאילתה ישירה אינה מוגבלת במילים. השר הנשאל ישיב בכתב על שאילתה ישירה או יודיע על סירובו להשיב, בתוך 21 ימים מיום קבלת השאילתה, ואם התקבלה השאילתה בתקופת הפגרה – בתוך 30 ימים

*Gist:* a direct question has no word limit and is answered in writing within 21 days, or 30 days if received in the recess; if unanswered, the MK can turn it into an ordinary question (s. 50(ג)). ([s. 50](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנון_הכנסת#סעיף_50))

> ”שדלן“ – מי שדרך עיסוק או בתמורה למען לקוח, נוקט פעולות לשכנוע חבר הכנסת בקשר להצעות חוק ולחקיקת משנה בכנסת או בוועדותיה ... ולמעט אלה: (1) מי שבמסגרת עבודתו נוקט פעולות כאמור למען מעבידו

*Gist:* a lobbyist is someone who, as an occupation or for payment, works to persuade MKs on legislation and Knesset decisions for a client — not an employee acting for their own employer (and not certain public officials). ([חוק הכנסת, s. 65](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הכנסת#סעיף_65))

> לא יפעל שדלן בכנסת אלא בהיתר מאת הוועדה

*Gist:* a lobbyist may act in the Knesset, including at committee sessions held outside it, only with a permit; must wear a badge, disclose the client and register in the protocol (s. 68); may not offer benefits, mislead or pressure MKs (s. 69); and the Knesset publishes the list of permit holders and their clients (s. 70). ([s. 66(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הכנסת#סעיף_66))

## How it works in practice

### Choosing MKs

- **Committee members** of the committee that handles your bill or topic (lesson 12.2) matter most.
- **Caucuses** (שדולות) group MKs from different parties around a cause; their chairs are natural allies.
- **Both sides.** Approach coalition and opposition MKs. A coalition MK can move a minister quietly; an opposition MK can file questions and reservations.
- **Contacts.** Each MK's page on the Knesset website lists office e-mail and phone and the parliamentary advisers. Write to the adviser who handles your subject.

### The letter to an MK

One page. Who you are; the issue in two sentences; the specific request (a question, a reservation, raising it in committee, a quick debate); the facts with sources; a draft of what you want them to file. Use Part A of the [question request template](../../templates/council-question-request.md).

### Drafting the question

Word limits and answer periods are also listed on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

| Type | Length | Answer | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary | ≤ 50 words | Orally in the plenum, prepared within 21 days | A public answer on record |
| Urgent | ≤ 40 words | Plenum, same week; the Speaker approves up to four a week | A current event |
| Direct | No limit | In writing to the MK within 21 days (30 if received in the recess) | Numbers, lists, detailed facts |

Rules of thumb:

- **Factual, within one minister's area.** "How many requests … were received in 2025, and how many were approved?" — not "Does the minister think the policy is fair?"
- **No private details** of individuals; use numbers or anonymised cases.
- **Dates and units.** Ask for figures by year, district or month, so the answer can be compared.
- **Check first** whether the question was asked before (below). Ask the follow-up, not the same question.

### Finding earlier questions and answers

- **Knesset website** → פעילות הכנסת → שאילתות: search by MK, ministry or words. (JavaScript site; use a normal browser.)
- **Knesset open data**: the `KNS_Query` table of the [ParliamentInfo service](https://knesset.gov.il/Odata/ParliamentInfo.svc/) lists every question with its type, MK, ministry, submission date and answer date; the latest records in October 2026 are from July 2026, just before the recess.
- **Open Knesset** ([oknesset.org](https://oknesset.org)) for MKs' activity and committee records.

### In the election recess

The 25th Knesset continues to serve until the 26th convenes (Basic Law: The Knesset, s. 37), so MKs can still submit direct questions, which the minister must answer within 30 days if received in the recess. Ordinary questions wait: recess days do not count (s. 48(ט)) and the plenum does not meet. After the elections, the questions of the 25th Knesset do not carry over; ask a new MK.

### Do you need a lobbyist permit?

| Situation | Permit? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You advocate for your own cause, unpaid | No | Not "as an occupation or for payment for a client" (s. 65) |
| You volunteer for an NGO or residents' group | No, as long as you are unpaid and it is not your occupation | Same definition |
| You are an NGO's or a company's employee, speaking for your employer | No | Exception (1) in s. 65 |
| A local authority official acting within the authority's powers | No | Exception (2) in s. 65 |
| A consultant or firm paid by an NGO, company or council to persuade MKs | **Yes** | Acts for payment for a client; needs a permit (s. 66), must disclose the client (s. 68) |
| You were an MK's employee in the last six months (or an MK, minister or deputy minister in the last year) | Cannot get a permit | s. 67 |

A permit holder who breaks the rules can lose the permit; someone who acts as a lobbyist without one can be refused a permit or barred from the Knesset (s. 69(ב)–(ג)). Whatever your status, never offer an MK a benefit, never mislead, and never pressure with threats or promises.

## Worked example

**Situation.** In a Druze village in the Galilee, about 40 homes built on the village's private land have waited years for electricity connections. Fadi Halabi, a teacher and volunteer in the residents' committee, wants official numbers.

1. **MKs.** He writes to two MKs on the Interior Committee, one from the coalition and one from the opposition, and to the chair of a caucus on minority communities.
2. **Check first.** In the questions database he finds a 2024 direct question on the same topic with national figures only.
3. **Draft.** He sends a direct question, ready to file: "כמה בקשות לחיבור לחשמל של מבנים בכפר [___] הוגשו בשנים 2023–2026, בכמה מהן ניתנה החלטה, ומה מספר הבקשות הממתינות להחלטה יותר משנה? מה הסיבות העיקריות לעיכוב?" No names of residents.
4. **Filed.** One MK files it in September 2026, during the recess; the answer is due within 30 days.
5. **Use.** The answer gives numbers he uses in an FOI request for the decisions themselves (lesson 05.2) and in a complaint (lesson 05.4).
6. **Permit?** Fadi is an unpaid volunteer: no lobbyist permit. When the residents' committee considers paying a consultant to lobby, he tells them the consultant would need a permit and would have to disclose the client.

**Mirror test.** The same steps serve residents of a settlement in the West Bank (Samaria) asking, through an MK, how many road-safety requests for their access road were received and approved, or a Tel Aviv tenants' group asking about rent-support decisions.

## Do it yourself

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only (or for a group that has authorised you). This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The default is a dry run: send the request to an MK only if you have a real question.

### You need

- Your issue from lessons 12.1–12.3.
- Part A of [council-question-request.md](../../templates/council-question-request.md).
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. List three MKs to approach: committee members, a caucus chair, at least one from each side. Note their advisers' e-mails.
2. Search the questions database for earlier questions on your issue. Note numbers and dates of answers.
3. Choose the type: direct (written, detailed) or ordinary (public, ≤ 50 words).
4. Draft the question in Hebrew. Count the words. Check it against s. 47.
5. Write the one-page letter around it (Part A of the template).
6. Decide whether anyone in your group would need a lobbyist permit, using the table.

### Template

[council-question-request.md](../../templates/council-question-request.md) — Part A: a Hebrew request to an MK to submit a parliamentary question, with a ready-to-file question and a s. 47 checklist. Part B is for local council members (lesson 12.5).

### How you know it worked

- The MK's adviser could file your question without editing it.
- Your question asks for facts the minister's office holds, not opinions.
- Your permit verdict cites the s. 65 definition.

### Self-check

- [ ] MKs chosen from both sides and the right committee.
- [ ] Earlier questions searched.
- [ ] Type chosen; word limit met.
- [ ] No opinion, hypothesis or private details (s. 47).
- [ ] Lobbyist question decided.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asking for the minister's opinion | Question disqualified (s. 47(א)(2)) | Rewrite as a factual question | Ask for numbers, dates, decisions |
| Naming a private person with sensitive details | Disqualified or details removed (s. 47(א)(3)) | Use anonymised facts; send details separately | Never put private details in the question |
| A 120-word ordinary question | Returned for shortening | Make it a direct question or cut it | Count words: 50 ordinary, 40 urgent |
| Writing only to MKs who already agree | No movement in the majority | Add coalition and opposition members | Map the committee before writing |
| Paying a consultant who has no permit | The consultant can be barred; your cause is embarrassed | Ask the consultant to apply; disclose the client | Check the s. 65 definition before hiring |
| Expecting an ordinary question answered during the recess | Nothing happens until the plenum meets | Ask for a direct question instead | Use direct questions in the recess |

## Review

- Only MKs can ask ministers questions; give them a ready, factual draft.
- Ordinary (≤ 50 words, plenum), urgent (≤ 40, same week) and direct (written; 21 days, or 30 in the recess).
- Questions must be factual, within the minister's area, and free of private details (s. 47).
- Search the Knesset's question records before you ask.
- A lobbyist permit is needed by people paid by a client, or acting as an occupation; unpaid volunteers and employees speaking for their employer do not need one.
