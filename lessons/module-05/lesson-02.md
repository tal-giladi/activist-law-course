# 05.2 · Freedom of Information Request

By the end of this lesson you will be able to write and file a Freedom of Information request that an official cannot easily refuse: addressed to the right FOI officer, asking for specific records in a usable format, with the fee paid or the exemption claimed, and with the answer deadline in your calendar.

## Why it matters

Information is the activist's first weapon. An FOI request shows you what the authority knew, when it knew it and why it decided — before you argue. It costs a small fee, needs no lawyer and no reason, and works against ministries, local authorities, government companies, statutory corporations, universities and many other bodies.

A vague request ("all information about the park") invites a refusal for "unreasonable resources" (s. 8(1)). A precise one ("the noise measurements taken at the park between January and June 2026, in Excel") is hard to refuse, and if it is refused, the refusal is easy to challenge in court (lesson 05.3).

## The law

> (א) בקשה לקבלת מידע תוגש בכתב לממונה או למי שהוסמך לכך על ידו; אין המבקש חייב לציין את הטעם לבקשתו.

*Gist:* the request is made in writing to the FOI officer, and you do not have to explain why you want the information. ([חוק חופש המידע, s. 7(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע#סעיף_7))

> (ב) הרשות הציבורית תודיע למבקש המידע ללא שיהוי, ולא יאוחר מ־30 ימים מקבלת הבקשה, על החלטתה בבקשתו; ראש הרשות הציבורית, או מי שהוא הסמיך לכך, רשאי להאריך את התקופה האמורה, במקרה הצורך, ב־30 ימים נוספים, ובלבד שהודיע על כך למבקש בכתב, ונימק את הצורך בהארכת התקופה.

*Gist:* the authority must decide within 30 days and may extend by 30 more only with a reasoned written notice; s. 7(ג) allows a further reasoned extension of up to 60 days for large or complex requests, and s. 7(ד) requires delivery within 15 days of a decision to release. ([s. 7(ב)–(ד)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע#סעיף_7))

> (ה) המידע יועמד לרשות המבקש כפי שהוא מצוי בידי הרשות הציבורית ואין הרשות חייבת לעבד את המידע לצרכיו של המבקש; היה המידע ממוחשב, הוא יופק עבור המבקש באמצעים המשמשים דרך קבע את הרשות.

*Gist:* you get records as they exist — the authority does not have to create analyses for you — but computer data is produced with the tools the authority normally uses, so ask for the data export, not a report. ([s. 7(ה)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע#סעיף_7))

> בבואה לשקול סירוב למסור מידע לפי חוק זה, מכוח הוראות סעיפים 8 ו־9, תיתן הרשות הציבורית דעתה, בין היתר, לענינו של המבקש במידע, אם ציין זאת בבקשתו, וכן לענין הציבורי שבגילוי המידע מטעמים של שמירה על בריאות הציבור או בטיחותו, או שמירה על איכות הסביבה.

*Gist:* when considering a refusal, the authority must weigh your interest (if you stated it) and the public interest in health, safety and the environment — a reason to state your interest even though you need not. ([s. 10](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_חופש_המידע#סעיף_10))

> (א) המגיש בקשה לקבלת מידע (להלן – המבקש), ישלם אגרת בקשה, ויתחייב לשאת באגרת טיפול ובאגרת הפקה עד לסכום שלא יעלה על 150 שקלים חדשים

*Gist:* you pay the request fee and sign an undertaking to pay handling and production fees up to a cap (the 1999 figure is indexed every October; see [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)); above the cap the officer must ask you first (reg. 4(ב)–(ג)). ([תקנות חופש המידע (אגרות), reg. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_חופש_המידע_%28אגרות%29#סעיף_4))

> (א) המועדים הקבועים בסעיף 7 לחוק יימנו מיום קבלת הבקשה על ידי הממונה או מיום תשלום אגרת הבקשה, לפי המאוחר.

*Gist:* the 30 days start only when the officer has the request *and* the fee is paid (or the exemption claimed). ([reg. 9(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_חופש_המידע_%28אגרות%29#סעיף_9))

> רשות ציבורית שלא טיפלה בבקשה לקבלת מידע לפי מועדים הקבועים בחוק, לרבות אם לא האריכה את המועדים לפי סעיף 7 לחוק, לא תגבה אגרות טיפול והפקה.

*Gist:* an authority that misses its deadline loses the right to charge handling and production fees. ([reg. 6א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_חופש_המידע_%28אגרות%29#סעיף_6א))

## How it works in practice

### Who can ask, and whom

- **Who:** any Israeli citizen or resident, including a company or amuta registered in Israel (s. 2, "תושב"). A non-resident may ask only about their own rights in Israel (s. 12).
- **Whom:** a "public authority" under s. 2 — ministries and their units, the Knesset, courts (not the content of cases), local authorities, municipal companies, statutory corporations, government companies, state-funded universities (financial matters), and bodies the Minister of Justice added (for example religious councils and health funds).
- **Not covered at all:** the bodies in s. 14 (the security services, intelligence units and others).

### Before you write

1. **Search the answers database (מאגר התשובות)** on the FOI Unit site. If someone already got the data, you may have it today.
2. **Check what must already be published:** annual reports (s. 5), administrative guidelines (s. 6) and environmental information (s. 6א). Asking for published information can be refused under s. 8(4), but you will be told where it is.
3. **Find the FOI officer:** use the officer search on the FOI Unit site for ministries and many bodies, or the authority's own website ("חופש המידע"). Every authority must have one (s. 3).

### Where and how to file

| Authority | Channel |
|---|---|
| Government ministry or auxiliary unit | The online form on the FOI Unit site (gov.il), with National Digital Identity login, online fee payment and a confirmation |
| Local authority, government company, other body | Its own online form, or email to the FOI officer, with proof of fee payment or an exemption claim and the signed undertaking |

Keep the confirmation or a sent email saved as PDF. The 30 days run from receipt **and** payment, whichever is later (reg. 9(א)).

### Fees and exemptions

The amounts change every 1 October; see [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

- **Request fee:** paid when you file.
- **Handling fee:** per hour of work, from the fourth hour (the first three hours are free).
- **Production fee:** per page or disc; not charged if it would be ₪10 or less (reg. 6(ד)).
- **Undertaking:** you commit to pay handling and production fees up to the cap. If the officer expects more, they must tell you the estimate and wait for your agreement (reg. 4(ב)–(ג)).

Exemptions (reg. 6) — each exempts you from the request fee and from four more hours of handling:

| Who | What you show |
|---|---|
| Information about yourself and your rights | Nothing more; say so in the request |
| A registered amuta or public-benefit company acting for a public purpose | A valid proper-management certificate (אישור ניהול תקין); the FOI Unit keeps a list of recognised amutot |
| Academic research | A letter from the institution or supervisor |
| A recipient of income support and certain other benefits | Proof of the benefit |

A fee decision can be appealed to the Magistrates' Court within 30 days (reg. 8; FOI Law s. 18(ה)).

### Writing the request

1. **Specific records:** name document types (minutes, contracts, reports, emails, datasets), the subject, and a date range.
2. **Format:** ask for digital copies in an open, machine-readable format (Excel or CSV for data). The authority must use the cheapest means of production (reg. 3א).
3. **Partial disclosure:** ask that if any part is exempt, the rest be released with the redactions marked (s. 11).
4. **Reasons for refusal:** ask that any refusal name the exact section and explain why it applies (s. 7(ו)).
5. **Your interest and the public interest:** one paragraph, short, linked to health, safety or the environment where true (s. 10).
6. **Your contact details** and the fee status.

### What the exemptions look like

Section 8 lets an authority refuse for practical reasons (unreasonable resources, information over seven years old and hard to find, information that does not exist or is already published, information created by another authority). Section 9(א) lists information it **must not** give (security, foreign relations, someone's safety, privacy, a statutory ban); s. 9(ב) lists information it **may** withhold (policy in formation, internal deliberations, negotiations, trade secrets, disciplinary matters and others). A narrow, well-defined request avoids most of s. 8; a request that welcomes partial release limits the damage of s. 9.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Fishermen and residents near Haifa Bay suspect that a refinery's flaring has increased. Yossi, a member of a residents' forum (not a registered amuta), asks the Ministry of Environmental Protection for data.

1. **Answers database.** He searches "לפידים" and "בז״ן" and finds a 2024 answer with older data — useful, but out of date.
2. **Published information.** Some measurements are published on the ministry's site under s. 6א, but not the flaring event reports.
3. **Request (extract).** "דיווחי אירועי הלפדה שהעבירו מפעלי מפרץ חיפה למשרד בין 1.1.2025 ל־30.9.2026, וכן תוצאות ניטור האוויר בתחנות ___ באותה תקופה, בקובץ Excel. אם חלק מהמידע חסוי, אבקש לקבל את יתרתו תוך סימון ההשמטות (סעיף 11 לחוק)."
4. **Interest.** One paragraph on public health (s. 10).
5. **Filing.** Through the online form on the FOI Unit site; he pays the request fee and signs the undertaking. Confirmation on 4 November.
6. **Deadline.** Thirty days from 4 November (the day itself not counted) is 4 December. He writes 4 December and, if an extension notice arrives, 3 January.
7. **Result.** The ministry extends by 30 days with a reason (volume), then releases the monitoring data but withholds parts of two reports as trade secrets (s. 9(ב)(6)). Yossi notes that s. 9(ב)(6) does not cover information about substances released into the environment (s. 9(ב)(6)(א)) — the ground for his next step in lesson 05.3.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- An issue where documents or data would help (your tool plan from 05.1).
- The template: [foi-request.md](../../templates/foi-request.md).
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. Search the FOI Unit's answers database and the authority's published reports.
2. Identify the authority and its FOI officer; note the officer's name, email and the filing channel.
3. List the records you need: type, subject, date range, format.
4. Fill in the template. Add the partial-disclosure and reasons-for-refusal paragraphs.
5. Decide your fee status: pay, or claim an exemption and attach proof.
6. Read it as the officer would: could any line be refused as "unreasonable resources"? Narrow it.
7. Calculate the answer date (30 days from receipt and payment) and the latest date with a full extension.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Filing is optional in this lesson; a dry run is enough. If you file for real: act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Template

[foi-request.md](../../templates/foi-request.md) — a Hebrew FOI request with the undertaking paragraph and an exemption claim.

### How you know it worked

- Each item you ask for names a record type, a subject and a date range.
- You know exactly what you will pay (or why you are exempt) and the maximum you committed to.
- Your calendar has the 30-day date and the extended date.

### Self-check

- [ ] Addressed to the named FOI officer of the right authority.
- [ ] Specific records, date range and format; no "all information about".
- [ ] Partial disclosure and reasoned refusal requested.
- [ ] Fee paid or exemption claimed with proof; undertaking signed.
- [ ] Deadlines calculated from receipt and payment.
- [ ] Filed (or ready to be filed) in my own name.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| "All information about…" | Refused as needing unreasonable resources (s. 8(1)) | Re-file narrowly | Name record types, subject and dates |
| Fee unpaid, no exemption claimed | The 30 days never start (reg. 9(א)) | Pay or claim the exemption now | Pay with the request; keep the receipt |
| Asking the authority to analyse data | "We don't have to process information" (s. 7(ה)) | Ask for the raw records or data export | Ask for what exists, in its native format |
| Sending to a general inbox | Lost; receipt unprovable | Resend to the named FOI officer | Use the officer search or the online form |
| No request for partial release | All-or-nothing refusal | Ask again citing s. 11 | Include the partial-disclosure paragraph |
| Missing that information is already published | Refused under s. 8(4) | Use the place the refusal names | Check annual reports and s. 6א pages first |

## Review

- Anyone who is a citizen or resident may ask, in writing, without giving a reason (ss. 1, 7(א)).
- The authority has 30 days, plus reasoned extensions of 30 and up to 60 more (s. 7(ב)–(ג)); the clock starts at receipt and payment (reg. 9(א)).
- Fees are small and indexed every October; exemptions cover your own information, recognised amutot, academic research and some benefit recipients (reg. 6).
- An authority that misses the deadline may not charge handling or production fees (reg. 6א).
- Precise requests, open formats and a partial-disclosure paragraph make refusals rare and weak.
