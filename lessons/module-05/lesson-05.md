# 05.5 · The Pre-Petition Letter

By the end of this lesson you will be able to write the letter that comes before every petition: a formal demand that names the decision, shows what is legally wrong with it, asks for a specific remedy by a date, and becomes the exhibit that proves to the court you exhausted your remedies.

## Why it matters

Courts will not hear a petition against an authority that was never given a chance to fix the problem. A petition filed without a prior written approach is often dismissed at the threshold for failure to exhaust remedies (מיצוי הליכים, lesson 04.5), sometimes with costs. The Administrative Courts regulations go further: the petition itself must attach the correspondence with the authority, or explain why there was none (reg. 5(ב)(6)).

The letter also works. Many authorities fix a decision once a legal adviser reads a clear letter that shows a petition is coming. And if they do not, their answer tells you their arguments before you write your petition.

## The law

> (6) נעשתה פניה מוקדמת בכתב לרשות – יצורפו העתקים של ההתכתבות לענין זה; לא נעשתה פניה מוקדמת – הנימוקים להגשת העתירה בלא פניה מוקדמת;

*Gist:* an administrative petition must attach your prior written correspondence with the authority, or explain why you went to court without it. ([תקנות בתי משפט לענינים מינהליים (סדרי דין), reg. 5(ב)(6)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_%28סדרי_דין%29#סעיף_5))

> (ב) לא נקבע מועד כאמור, תוגש העתירה בלא שיהוי, לפי נסיבות הענין, ולא יאוחר מארבעים וחמישה ימים מיום שההחלטה פורסמה כדין, או מיום שהעותר קיבל הודעה עליה או מיום שנודע לעותר עליה, לפי המוקדם.

*Gist:* the pre-petition letter and its answer must fit inside the 45 days from the decision. ([reg. 3(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_%28סדרי_דין%29#סעיף_3))

> בית המשפט רשאי לדחות עתירה אם ראה כי בנסיבות הענין היה שיהוי בהגשתה, אף אם הוגשה בתוך אחד המועדים לפי תקנה 3.

*Gist:* even inside 45 days, needless delay can sink a petition — so do not give the authority months to answer. ([reg. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_%28סדרי_דין%29#סעיף_4))

> דחה בית המשפט את העתירה ללא הזמנת המשיב, רשאי הוא להטיל על העותר הוצאות לטובת המדינה בשיעור שימצא לנכון או להורות לרשם לעשות כן.

*Gist:* an HCJ petition dismissed on its face can cost the petitioner costs to the State — a petition without a prior demand is a typical example. ([תקנות סדר הדין בבית המשפט הגבוה לצדק, reg. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_בבית_המשפט_הגבוה_לצדק#סעיף_6))

The duty to exhaust remedies before an HCJ petition comes from case law, not from a regulation; lesson 04.5 covers it.

## How it works in practice

### When to send it

- **After a decision you want changed,** or after a request was refused or ignored (silence counts as refusal after three months under the Decisions and Reasons Law s. 6(ב), but do not wait that long when a petition clock is running).
- **Before any petition,** administrative or HCJ, unless the matter is so urgent that even a short letter is impossible — and then say so in the petition and still send an email the same day.

### Timing: the arithmetic

Start from the decision date. Count 45 days (the petition deadline). Work backwards:

| Step | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Write and send the letter | 3–5 days after the decision |
| Authority's response time | 7–14 days (shorter if urgent; give reasons) |
| Read the answer, finish the petition and affidavit, get the affidavit certified | 7–10 days |
| Buffer for filing problems | 3–5 days |

A request for reconsideration does not reliably restart the 45 days. Assume the clock runs from the original decision unless the authority makes a genuinely new decision on new facts.

### Structure

1. **Header:** your details; date; addressee (the decision-maker by name and post) with a copy to the legal adviser (היועץ המשפטי / הלשכה המשפטית). For a ministry, also copy the ministry's legal department; for a local authority, the city's legal adviser.
2. **"בלי לפגוע בזכויות"** and a subject line that names the decision and says "מכתב התראה לפני הגשת עתירה".
3. **The decision:** what, by whom, when, how you learned of it (attach it).
4. **The facts:** numbered, dated, with exhibits.
5. **The defects:** each in its own paragraph, with the rule (lessons 04.1–04.4) — no authority, extraneous considerations, unreasonable or disproportionate, no hearing, no reasons, discrimination.
6. **The remedy:** exactly what you want done — cancel the decision, reconsider with a hearing, give the documents, issue the permit — mirroring the remedy you would ask from the court.
7. **The date:** a specific date for a written answer.
8. **The next step:** "If no satisfactory answer is received by this date, I will consider petitioning the competent court without further notice."
9. **Documents:** ask for the documents the decision relied on (and file an FOI request in parallel if needed).

Tone: firm, factual, short. Two to four pages. No threats beyond the legal next step; no insults — the letter will be read by the judge.

### Delivery and proof

Email the decision-maker and the legal adviser and send by registered mail, or use the authority's online form. Keep proof of the date of receipt; it is an exhibit.

### After the answer

| Answer | Next step |
|---|---|
| Remedy granted | Get it in writing; close the file |
| Partial, or a new decision with new reasons | Assess the new reasons; the petition attacks the decision as it now stands |
| Refusal | Petition (05.6 or 05.7); attach the letter and the refusal |
| Silence by your date | Petition; attach the letter and proof of receipt; say no answer was received |

## Worked example

**Situation.** A city in the central region refuses to rent its municipal community hall to a religious women's organisation for a lecture evening on family and Jewish law. The refusal email of 2 May says the event "may cause controversy". The organisation (a registered amuta) is the petitioner; Michal, its chair, signs.

1. **Clock.** Decision received 2 May; 45 days → 16 June.
2. **Defects.** The hall is rented to other groups under published criteria; refusing on the content of speech is an extraneous consideration and harms freedom of expression and religion (04.2, 03.4); no hearing was given (04.4); the reason is vague (s. 2א of the Decisions and Reasons Law requires reasons).
3. **Remedy.** Cancel the refusal and approve the rental for 18 June on the usual terms; alternatively, hold a hearing within 7 days.
4. **Date.** An answer by 15 May (10 days from sending on 5 May, because the event date is close).
5. **Copies.** The city's legal adviser and the director of community centres.
6. **Answer.** On 14 May the legal adviser replies that the refusal stands because of "security concerns", with no police opinion attached. Michal notes this new reason; the petition will address both reasons.
7. **Petition.** Rental of a municipal facility is a local-authority decision, First Schedule item 8 → District Court sitting as an Administrative Court (05.6), filed by 25 May with a request for an urgent hearing before 18 June.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A decision (real or realistic) that you want changed, with its date.
- Your analysis from project m04 or lesson 04.3, if you have it.
- The template: [pre-petition-letter.md](../../templates/pre-petition-letter.md).
- 60 minutes.

### Steps

1. Write the decision, its date and how you learned of it.
2. Calculate the 45-day date and work backwards to your response date.
3. List the defects, one rule per defect, with the source of each rule.
4. Write the remedy exactly as you would ask the court.
5. Fill in the template; attach the decision and your earlier letters.
6. Address it to the decision-maker with a copy to the legal adviser.
7. Read it as the legal adviser would: is there any reason to say yes? Is there a reason a judge would think you were unfair?

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Sending is optional in this lesson; a dry run is enough. If you send it for real: act in your own name only (or in the name of the group that is acting). This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Template

[pre-petition-letter.md](../../templates/pre-petition-letter.md) — a Hebrew pre-petition letter with the defects, remedy and response-date paragraphs.

### How you know it worked

- A legal adviser could answer your letter without asking you a single question.
- Your remedy matches the remedy you will ask the court for.
- Your response date leaves at least ten days before the 45-day deadline.

### Self-check

- [ ] Decision identified with date and attached.
- [ ] Each defect stated with its rule and source.
- [ ] One specific remedy (and an alternative, if any).
- [ ] Response date fair to the authority and inside the petition deadline.
- [ ] Copy to the legal adviser; proof of receipt planned.
- [ ] Polite, factual, signed in my own name or my group's.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| No letter before the petition | Dismissed for not exhausting remedies, possibly with costs | Send the letter, then re-file quickly | Always send one; explain urgency if you could not |
| Giving the authority 30 days on day 40 | Petition filed late | Ask the court for an extension (reg. 3(ג)) | Count backwards from the 45-day date |
| Vague remedy ("reconsider the matter") | A vague answer; a mismatch with the petition | Send a short follow-up with the exact remedy | Write the remedy as the court order you want |
| Only to the clerk, not the legal adviser | Lost; no legal review | Resend with a copy to the legal adviser | Always copy the legal department |
| Insults or threats | Undermines you before the judge | Send a calm follow-up | Assume the judge will read every line |
| Assuming the answer restarts the 45 days | Petition late | Petition now and explain the timeline | Count from the original decision |

## Review

- Courts expect a written demand before a petition; the administrative-court regulations require the correspondence to be attached (reg. 5(ב)(6)).
- Name the decision, the defects with their rules, a specific remedy and a response date.
- Count backwards from the 45-day petition deadline; delay can defeat a petition even inside it (reg. 4).
- Send to the decision-maker with a copy to the legal adviser, with proof of receipt.
- The answer (or silence) and your letter become the first exhibits of the petition.
