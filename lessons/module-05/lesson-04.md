# 05.4 · The Public Complaints Commissioner and the State Comptroller

By the end of this lesson you will be able to file a complaint with the Public Complaints Commissioner (the state ombudsman) that is accepted for investigation, and to send the State Comptroller information about a systemic failure in a form an audit team can use — and to know when neither is the right tool.

## Why it matters

The ombudsman is free, needs no lawyer, can demand documents from any audited body and often gets a decision reversed in weeks. Many activists never use it, or use it wrongly: they complain about a general policy (not investigated — that is the Comptroller's audit), complain after the matter reached a court (barred), or wait for the ombudsman while a court deadline passes.

The two roles sit in one office but work differently. The **Commissioner** investigates a complaint about an act that harmed *you* (or someone who agreed you complain for them). The **Comptroller** audits bodies and systems and decides what to audit; information you send may feed a report, but you have no right to an answer.

## The law

> כל אדם רשאי להגיש תלונה אל נציב תלונות הציבור.

*Gist:* anyone may complain — no citizenship, age or fee requirement. ([חוק מבקר המדינה, s. 33](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה#סעיף_33))

> (1) מעשה הפוגע במישרין במתלונן עצמו, או המונע ממנו במישרין טובת הנאה, או מעשה הפוגע במישרין באדם אחר או המונע ממנו במישרין טובת הנאה והמתלונן קיבל, להנחת דעתו של נציב תלונות הציבור, את הסכמתו של אותו אדם להגיש תלונה בענינו

*Gist:* the act must harm you directly, or harm another person who consented to your complaint. ([s. 37(1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה#סעיף_37))

> והמעשה הוא בניגוד לחוק או ללא סמכות חוקית או בניגוד למינהל תקין, או שיש בו משום נוקשות יתירה או אי־צדק בולט; לענין זה, ”מעשה“ – לרבות מחדל ופיגור בעשיה.

*Gist:* the act must be unlawful, without authority, against proper administration, excessively rigid or flagrantly unjust — and "act" includes a failure to act and delay. ([s. 37](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה#סעיף_37))

> (5) תלונה בענין התלוי ועומד בבית משפט או בבית דין או שבית משפט או בית דין הכריע בו לגופו;

*Gist:* no investigation of a matter pending in court or decided by a court on its merits — complain first, or choose. Section 38 also excludes the President, the Knesset, the Government as a body and judicial acts. ([s. 38(5)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה#סעיף_38))

> (2) תלונה שהוגשה לאחר שעברה שנה מיום המעשה שעליו נסבה התלונה או מהיום שהמעשה נודע למתלונן, לפי המאוחר יותר.

*Gist:* a complaint filed more than a year after the act (or after you learned of it) is investigated only for a special reason; the same applies to a decision open to an objection, appeal-board or appeal route (s. 39(1)). ([s. 39(2)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה#סעיף_39))

> (ב) שום בית משפט לא ייזקק לבקשת סעד נגד החלטותיו וממצאיו של נציב תלונות הציבור בעניין תלונה.

*Gist:* the Commissioner's findings cannot be challenged in court; they also give you no new legal right, and the complaint does not extend any legal deadline (s. 45(א)). ([s. 45(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_מבקר_המדינה#סעיף_45))

## How it works in practice

### Commissioner, Comptroller, or something else?

| Your problem | Tool |
|---|---|
| A ministry, local authority, state institution or government company harmed you (or a consenting person) | Commissioner complaint |
| A systemic failure, waste or corruption that does not harm you personally | Information to the Comptroller's audit |
| A private company (bank, phone, insurer) | Not the Commissioner; use the regulator or a court (Module 11) |
| A police officer's conduct, a judge, a state attorney | Specialised bodies (Module 6 covers police complaints) |
| A decision you want overturned with a binding order, under a deadline | Pre-petition letter and petition (05.5–05.7), possibly with a complaint in parallel |

Some bodies and many large cities also have their own ombudsman or public-inquiries officer; check the body's website. The state Commissioner can still investigate local authorities.

### Filing a complaint

- **Channels:** the online form at complaint.mevaker.gov.il (Hebrew, Arabic, English, Russian), by post to the office (address on the complaints page), or orally at a regional office where it is written down and you sign it (s. 34).
- **Required:** your name and address and your signature (s. 34). Anonymous complaints are not investigated.
- **Content:** who did what, when, how it harmed you, which rule or principle it breaks, what you already did (letters, answers), and what you want fixed.
- **Attach:** your letter to the body and its answer (or proof of silence). The Commissioner expects you to have approached the body first; it is not a legal condition in s. 37, but complaints without it are often sent back.
- **Time limit:** within a year of the act or of learning of it (s. 39(2)).
- **Cost:** free.

### What happens next

The Commissioner sends the complaint to the body and can require it to answer and produce documents (s. 41). It may stop the investigation if the matter is fixed (s. 42). If the complaint is justified, it tells you and the body, and can point to the defect and the way and time to fix it; the body must report what it did (s. 43(א)–(ב)). Many cases end in mediation or with the body correcting itself.

### Sending information to the Comptroller

The Comptroller's procedure for public inquiries distinguishes complaints (personal, direct harm) from general information about an audited body, which goes to an audit division. The procedure says:

- You receive an acknowledgement within about two weeks.
- You have no standing and no right to updates on how the information is used.
- Your identifying details are kept confidential when the body is approached.

Write it like an audit lead: the body, the failure, the period, numbers, documents (FOI answers are ideal), and where the audit team can verify it. One to three pages.

## Worked example

**Situation.** In Beit Shemesh, the municipality stops the special-education transport of 11-year-old Moshe, who uses a wheelchair, after a change of contractor. His mother, Esther, asked the municipality's education department in writing on 3 September; it answered "the matter is being examined". Three weeks later Moshe still has no transport.

1. **Tool.** A municipality is an audited body (s. 9(4)); the failure harms Moshe directly; Esther complains for her minor son. No court case is pending. → Commissioner complaint.
2. **Grounds.** Delay and failure to act are "acts" (s. 37); stopping a statutory service without a decision is against proper administration.
3. **Complaint.** Online, with her letter and the municipality's reply attached, and the request: restore transport within days and pay for the taxi she used in the meantime.
4. **Parallel step.** Because transport is urgent, she also sends a short pre-petition letter (05.5) with a 7-day date. Education decisions of a local authority are in the First Schedule, so a petition would go to the Administrative Court.
5. **Result.** The Commissioner's office contacts the municipality; transport resumes within ten days, and the complaint is closed as fixed (s. 42).
6. **Systemic information.** Esther learns from other parents that 40 children lost transport after the contract change. She files an FOI request (05.2) for the tender and the list of affected routes, and sends the result to the Comptroller's local-government audit division as a public inquiry.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A problem with a public body that harmed you (or a consenting person), or a systemic failure you know about.
- The template: [ombudsman-complaint.md](../../templates/ombudsman-complaint.md).
- 45 minutes.

### Steps

1. Check the body: is it an audited body under s. 9, and not excluded by s. 38?
2. Check the harm: direct harm to you, or written consent from the person harmed (s. 37(1)).
3. Check time: within a year (s. 39(2)); no court case pending or decided (s. 38(5)).
4. Fill in the complaint template: facts with dates, the rule broken, what you did, what you want.
5. List attachments: your letter, the answer, proof of harm.
6. If your issue is systemic, write a one-page Comptroller inquiry from the second part of the template.
7. Check your court deadlines: write them next to the complaint and act on them separately.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Filing is optional; a dry run is enough. If you file for real: act in your own name only, or with the written consent of the person harmed. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### How you know it worked

- Your complaint passes all four checks: audited body, direct harm, within a year, no court case.
- A reader can see in the first paragraph what was done, to whom, and what you want.
- Any court deadline is in your calendar independently of the complaint.

### Self-check

- [ ] Body is audited (s. 9) and not excluded (s. 38).
- [ ] Harm is direct to me, or I have written consent.
- [ ] Within one year; no pending or decided court case.
- [ ] Name, address and signature included (s. 34).
- [ ] Letter to the body and its answer attached.
- [ ] Systemic issues sent to the Comptroller as information, not as a complaint.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaining about a general policy that does not harm you | Not investigated (s. 37) | Send it as information to the Comptroller | Complain only about direct harm, or with consent |
| Complaining after filing a court case | Not investigated (s. 38(5)) | Choose one route | Decide before you file anything in court |
| Waiting for the Commissioner while a petition deadline passes | Petition late (s. 45(א)(2)) | Ask the court for an extension, explaining | Run the complaint and court deadlines in parallel |
| Complaining about a bank or private company | Outside s. 36 | Go to the regulator or court | Check the body is audited under s. 9 |
| Anonymous or unsigned complaint | Not investigated (s. 34) | Resubmit with name, address and signature | Sign it; the office handles details carefully |
| Filing after a year | Investigated only for a special reason (s. 39(2)) | Explain the special reason | File within the year |

## Review

- The Commissioner investigates acts (including failures and delays) of audited bodies that harm you directly — free, no lawyer, any person.
- Exclusions: the President, the Knesset, the Government as a body, judicial acts, and matters pending or decided in court (s. 38).
- File within a year (s. 39(2)), with your name, address and signature (s. 34), and the correspondence attached.
- The complaint does not extend court deadlines and its findings cannot be challenged in court (s. 45).
- Systemic failures go to the Comptroller's audit as information; you get an acknowledgement, not an answer.
