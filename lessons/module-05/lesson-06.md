# 05.6 · Administrative Petition, Self-Represented

By the end of this lesson you will be able to prepare and file, in your own name, an administrative petition (עתירה מינהלית) to a District Court sitting as an Administrative Court: with every element the procedure regulations require, a supporting affidavit, the right respondents, the fee paid or an exemption requested, served on the other side, and ready for what the court does next.

## Why it matters

Most activist cases against local authorities, planning bodies, education decisions, tenders and FOI refusals go to the Administrative Court, not the High Court. The petition is a structured document: the regulations list what it must contain, and the court can strike out or dismiss on its face a petition that does not show a cause for intervention (reg. 7(א)(2)). Self-represented petitioners lose most often on form — no affidavit, a missing respondent, no service, a late filing — not on the law. This lesson is the checklist that prevents that.

## The law

> (ב) בכתב העתירה ייכללו כל אלה:
> (1) שם העותר, מספר זהותו, מענו ופרטי התקשרותו, ואם הוא מיוצג – שם עורך דינו, לרבות מספר רישיונו, מענו ופרטי התקשרותו;
> (2) המשיבים, מענם ופרטי התקשרותם, וענינו של כל אחד מהמשיבים בנושא העתירה;
> (3) תיאור ההחלטה נושא העתירה והמועד שבו פורסמה כדין, או שבו קיבל העותר הודעה על ההחלטה או שבו נודע לעותר עליה, לפי המוקדם;
> (3א) פירוט מקור הסמכות העניינית והמקומית של בית משפט לעניינים מינהליים לדון בעתירה, לרבות הוראות החיקוק המסמיך שלפיו התקבלה החלטת הרשות שנגדה הוגשה העתירה, והפרט שבתוספת הראשונה או שבתוספת הרביעית לחוק שלפיו מוסמך בית המשפט לעניינים מינהליים לדון בעניין זה;
> (4) הענין של העותר בנושא העתירה;
> (5) פירוט הנימוקים שבעובדה ושבחוק שעליהם מתבססת העתירה;
> (6) נעשתה פניה מוקדמת בכתב לרשות – יצורפו העתקים של ההתכתבות לענין זה; לא נעשתה פניה מוקדמת – הנימוקים להגשת העתירה בלא פניה מוקדמת;
> (7) פירוט הסעד שמבקש העותר;
> (8) סכום אגרת בית המשפט שיש לשלם בהפניה לפרט בתוספת לתקנות האגרות שלפיו יש לגבות אגרה; אם העותר פטור מאגרה, יפנה לתקנה הפוטרת אותו מתשלומה לפי תקנות האגרות;
> (9) דבר קיומו של הליך נוסף בבית משפט או בבית דין, לרבות הליך שהסתיים, בקשר לאותה מסכת עובדתית או לאותה החלטה של רשות שנגדה מוגשת העתירה.

*Gist:* the nine-plus elements every administrative petition must contain — your details; the respondents and their interest; the decision and its date; why this court has subject-matter and local jurisdiction (the empowering law and the First Schedule item); your interest; the factual and legal grounds; the prior correspondence; the remedy; the fee item or exemption; and any other proceeding on the same facts. ([תקנות בתי משפט לענינים מינהליים (סדרי דין), reg. 5(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_%28סדרי_דין%29#סעיף_5))

> (ג) כתב עתירה יהא ערוך לפי טופס 1 שבתוספת והעובדות שבו יאומתו בתצהיר אשר יצורף לו ושיהיה ערוך לפי טופס 2 שבתוספת; בתצהיר תהיה הפרדה בין עובדות שהן בידיעתו האישית של המצהיר לבין עובדות הנכונות לפי מיטב ידיעתו ואמונתו, שלגביהן יציין את מקור ידיעתו ואמונתו.

*Gist:* the facts in the petition must be verified by an attached affidavit that separates personal knowledge from belief, and names the source of each belief. ([reg. 5(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_%28סדרי_דין%29#סעיף_5))

> (א) המשיבים בעתירה יהיו הרשות שנגד החלטתה מכוונת העתירה, כל רשות אחרת הנוגעת בדבר, וכן כל מי שעלול להיפגע מקבלת העתירה.

*Gist:* name as respondents the deciding authority, any other authority involved, and anyone who may be harmed if you win — or the court may strike the petition (reg. 6(ב)). ([reg. 6(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_%28סדרי_דין%29#סעיף_6))

> (ב) לא תובא עתירה לעיון בית המשפט כאמור בתקנת משנה (א) אלא אם כן הגיש העותר לבית המשפט אישור בדבר המצאת העתירה כדין לפי תקנה 35.

*Gist:* a judge will not even look at your petition until you file proof that you served it on the respondents. ([reg. 7(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_משפט_לענינים_מינהליים_%28סדרי_דין%29#סעיף_7))

> (א) בעל דין, הטוען שאין ביכולתו לשלם אגרה, יצרף לתובענה, עם הבאתה לראשונה לבית המשפט, בקשה לפטור מתשלום אגרה בגין אותה תובענה, בצירוף תצהיר שיפרט בו את רכושו, רכוש בן זוגו ורכוש הוריו אם הוא סמוך על שולחנם, ומקורות הכנסתו בששת החודשים שקדמו לתאריך הבקשה.

*Gist:* if you cannot pay the fee, file an exemption request with the petition, with an affidavit on your and your spouse's assets and six months of income; the court may grant it if you cannot pay and the proceeding shows a cause of action (reg. 14(ג)). ([תקנות בתי המשפט (אגרות), reg. 14(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_המשפט_%28אגרות%29#סעיף_14))

## How it works in practice

### Before you write: five checks

1. **Forum.** Your matter is in the First Schedule and not excluded (lesson 05.1). Write down the item number — reg. 5(ב)(3א) requires it.
2. **Venue.** The District Court of the district where the decision was made; for land, where the land is (reg. 2(א)). No fitting venue → Jerusalem (reg. 2(ג)).
3. **Time.** Within 45 days of the earliest of publication, notice or knowledge (reg. 3(ב)); court recess periods are not counted (reg. 44), but do not plan on them.
4. **Exhaustion.** Your pre-petition letter and the answer (lesson 05.5).
5. **Other proceedings.** Any other case on the same facts must be disclosed (reg. 5(ב)(9)).

### The document

Follow the template [administrative-petition.md](../../templates/administrative-petition.md). Its order follows Form 1 practice and reg. 5(ב):

1. Heading: the court ("בבית המשפט המחוזי ב___ בשבתו כבית משפט לעניינים מינהליים"), "עת״מ ___" (the number is given on filing), the parties.
2. "עתירה מינהלית" and the remedies (סעדים) at the top, numbered.
3. Introduction: one paragraph — what decision, why it is unlawful, what you ask.
4. Jurisdiction and venue (reg. 5(ב)(3א)): the empowering law and the First Schedule item; why this district.
5. The parties and their interest (reg. 5(ב)(1), (2), (4)).
6. The facts, numbered and dated, each pointing to an exhibit.
7. Exhaustion of remedies and timing (reg. 5(ב)(3), (6)).
8. The legal grounds, one heading per defect.
9. Other proceedings (reg. 5(ב)(9)).
10. The fee item, or the exemption request (reg. 5(ב)(8)).
11. Closing request, signature, date.
12. The affidavit (Form 2 practice — use [affidavit.md](../../templates/affidavit.md)), certified by a lawyer or another authorised person (lesson 02.6).
13. Exhibits list and the exhibits, each marked.

Keep it short: a strong self-represented petition is 8–15 pages before exhibits.

### The fee

The administrative-petition fee is item 20 of the Court Fees Regulations' schedule (amount on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); indexed every 1 January). It is paid on filing, online through Net HaMishpat or the courts' payment site. If the case ends early by withdrawal or agreement, most of the fee can be refunded minus a fixed deduction (regs 15 and 6(ב), item 33) — ask the registry. If you cannot pay:

- Prepare the **exemption request** (בקשה לפטור מאגרה) with the affidavit on assets and six months of income (reg. 14(א)). The request itself costs nothing (reg. 20(4)).
- Proof that helps: a fee exemption granted by a court in the last two years, a limited-means debtor order, bankruptcy or insolvency proceedings (reg. 14(ד)), or receiving income support (reg. 14(ה)).
- The court also looks at whether the petition shows a cause of action (reg. 14(ג)), so file it with the petition, not before.
- The judiciary's service page and forms: [gov.il — fee or deposit exemption](https://www.gov.il/he/service/exempt_from_payment_of_a_fee). Help line *3852.

State Legal Aid also covers administrative petitions for eligible people; see [Where to Get Help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md).

### Filing through Net HaMishpat

1. Log in to [Net HaMishpat](https://www.court.gov.il/NGCS.Web.Site/HomePage.aspx) with National Digital Identity, as a party without a lawyer. The judiciary's guide for unrepresented parties describes filing through the "הגשות" (filings) tab and "הגשה מהירה" (quick filing).
2. Choose the District Court, the proceeding type (עתירה מינהלית) and fill in the parties.
3. Upload the petition, the certified affidavit and the exhibits as PDFs; upload the exemption request if you have one.
4. Pay the fee online or submit the exemption request.
5. Save the filing confirmation with the case number.

If anything in the system is unclear, call the judiciary information centre (*3852) before the deadline day, not on it.

### What the registry and the system check

The registry (מזכירות) opens a file only when the basics are there. Expect it to check: the right court and proceeding type; the petitioner's identity and contact details; that the fee is paid or an exemption request is attached; that an affidavit is attached; that documents open and are legible. It does **not** check whether your arguments are good — the judge does, and can dismiss on the face of the petition (reg. 7(א)(2)) with costs to the State Treasury (reg. 41(ב)).

### Service

- Serve the petition with all exhibits **directly on every respondent** as soon as possible and no later than three days after filing (reg. 35(א)). A government ministry is normally served through the District Attorney's office (פרקליטות המחוז) of the district, which represents the State; a local authority through its legal adviser; a company at its registered office. The detailed service rules are in the Civil Procedure Regulations, chapter 19 (reg. 35(ד)) — check them, or ask *3852, before you serve.
- File **proof of service** in Net HaMishpat. Until you do, the petition does not reach a judge (reg. 7(ב)).

### After filing

| Stage | What happens | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| First look | The judge may order a preliminary response, set a preliminary hearing, dismiss on the face of it, or order you to fix the petition | reg. 7(א) |
| Interim relief | A separate request, with an affidavit, served the same day; a temporary order is possible in urgent cases | regs 9, 9א, 35(ב) |
| Answer | If ordered, the respondent answers within 60 days unless the court sets another time | reg. 10(א) |
| Hearing | Often one hearing on the papers and arguments; many petitions end with an agreed outcome | reg. 8 |
| Costs | The loser may pay the other side's costs; a baseless petition may pay costs to the Treasury | reg. 41 |
| The decision itself | Filing does not freeze the decision; ask for an interim order if you need that | reg. 42 |
| Appeal | To the Supreme Court within 60 days of the judgment | reg. 33(א) |

## Worked example

**Situation.** Continuing lesson 05.2: the Ministry of Environmental Protection released Haifa Bay air-monitoring data but withheld parts of two flaring reports as trade secrets (s. 9(ב)(6)). Yossi received the decision on 10 January. His pre-petition letter of 14 January asked for the reports within 10 days, citing the carve-out for substances released into the environment; the ministry repeated the refusal on 26 January.

1. **Forum and venue.** FOI → First Schedule item 2; the decision was made by the ministry's FOI officer in Jerusalem → Jerusalem District Court.
2. **Deadline.** 45 days from 10 January → 24 February. He plans to file on 16 February.
3. **Respondents.** (1) The Ministry of Environmental Protection; (2) the refinery company whose reports are requested — it may be harmed if he wins (reg. 6(א); FOI Law s. 17(ג)).
4. **Grounds.** The carve-out in s. 9(ב)(6)(א); partial release under s. 11; the public interest in health (ss. 10, 17(ד)).
5. **Remedies.** Order the ministry to release the reports, or alternatively release them with only specific commercial data redacted.
6. **Affidavit.** Personal knowledge: the request, payment, the correspondence. Belief with source: that flaring increased, based on the ministry's own monitoring data (exhibit 3).
7. **Fee.** Yossi pays the item 20 fee online.
8. **Service.** On 17 February he serves the Jerusalem District Attorney's office and the refinery's registered office, and uploads proof of service the same day.
9. **Outcome.** The judge orders a preliminary response within 30 days; the ministry offers to release the reports with three lines redacted, and the petition ends by agreement.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The decision, your pre-petition letter and the answer from lesson 05.5 (real or realistic).
- The templates: [administrative-petition.md](../../templates/administrative-petition.md) and [affidavit.md](../../templates/affidavit.md).
- 2 hours.

### Steps

1. Do the five checks: forum (item number), venue, deadline, exhaustion, other proceedings.
2. List the respondents: deciding authority, other authorities, anyone harmed if you win.
3. Write the remedies first, as numbered court orders.
4. Fill in the petition template section by section; every fact points to an exhibit.
5. Write the affidavit: personal knowledge first, then belief with its source.
6. Decide the fee: pay (item 20) or prepare the exemption request with its own affidavit.
7. Run the reg. 5(ב) checklist from the template, line by line.
8. Write your service plan: who, which address, how, by which date; and the date you will upload proof.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In this lesson the petition is a dry run. Filing for real is part of the module project and only if you choose: act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Template

[administrative-petition.md](../../templates/administrative-petition.md) — a Hebrew administrative-petition skeleton with the reg. 5(ב) checklist; [affidavit.md](../../templates/affidavit.md) for the supporting affidavit.

### How you know it worked

- Every item of reg. 5(ב) is in your draft, and you can point to its paragraph.
- Every respondent has an address for service and a stated interest.
- Your filing, service and proof-of-service dates are all inside the 45 days.

### Self-check

- [ ] First Schedule item and empowering law stated (reg. 5(ב)(3א)).
- [ ] Decision and its date; filed within 45 days (reg. 3).
- [ ] All respondents, including anyone who may be harmed (reg. 6).
- [ ] Prior correspondence attached (reg. 5(ב)(6)).
- [ ] Specific numbered remedies (reg. 5(ב)(7)).
- [ ] Fee item cited or exemption request attached (reg. 5(ב)(8); reg. 14).
- [ ] Affidavit attached and certified, separating knowledge from belief (reg. 5(ג)).
- [ ] Service within three days and proof filed (regs 35, 7(ב)).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| No affidavit, or an uncertified one | Petition not accepted or struck out | File a certified affidavit at once | Book the certifier before the deadline week |
| Third party not joined | Struck out or delayed (reg. 6(ב)) | Ask to join the respondent | Ask: who loses if I win? |
| No proof of service | The petition never reaches a judge (reg. 7(ב)) | Serve and upload proof now | Serve within three days and upload the same day |
| Filed on day 50 | Dismissed as late | Request an extension with reasons (reg. 3(ג)) | Plan to file by about day 35 |
| Fee not paid, no exemption request | No file opened | Pay or file the exemption request with the affidavit | Decide the fee route before filing day |
| Wrong district | Transfer or delay | Ask to transfer | Check reg. 2: where the decision was made |
| Petition reads as an opinion piece | Dismissed on its face, costs to the Treasury | Rewrite as facts, rules, remedies | Every paragraph: fact + exhibit, or rule + source |

## Review

- An administrative petition goes to the District Court of the district where the decision was made, within 45 days, in a First Schedule matter.
- Regulation 5(ב) is your checklist; reg. 5(ג) requires a supporting affidavit separating knowledge from belief.
- Join every authority involved and anyone who may be harmed if you win (reg. 6).
- Pay the item 20 fee or file an exemption request with an assets-and-income affidavit (reg. 14).
- Serve within three days and upload proof — without it no judge sees the petition (regs 35, 7(ב)).
