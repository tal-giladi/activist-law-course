# 09.3 · Employment Discrimination and the Commission

Employment discrimination has its own law, its own court and its own state commission. In this lesson you learn who the law protects, when the employer must prove it did not discriminate, what the Equal Employment Opportunities Commission can do for you, and how the limitation periods work — and you write a complaint to the Commission.

## Why it matters

Hiring decisions are made behind closed doors, and the applicant rarely knows why she was not called. The law answers this in two ways. First, it moves the burden of proof to the employer in defined situations — including when the employer asked about a prohibited ground, such as pregnancy, religion or reserve duty. Second, it created a Commission that can investigate, mediate and even sue in your name, for free. Activists meet this law constantly: dismissed after appearing at a protest (political view), not hired because of a headscarf or a kippah, sidelined after months of reserve duty.

## The law

> לא יפלה מעסיק בין עובדיו או בין דורשי עבודה מחמת מינם, נטייתם המינית, מעמדם האישי, הריון, טיפולי פוריות, טיפולי הפריה חוץ־גופית, היותם הורים, גילם, גזעם, דתם, לאומיותם, ארץ מוצאם, מקום מגוריהם, השקפתם, מפלגתם או שירות במילואים שלהם …

*Gist:* the prohibited grounds; s. 2(א)(1)–(6) lists the stages covered (hiring, conditions, promotion, training, dismissal and severance, retirement benefits) and s. 2(ב) adds irrelevant conditions. ([s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה#סעיף_2))

> מעסיק לא ידרוש מדורש עבודה או מעובד את הפרופיל הצבאי שלו, ולא יעשה שימוש בפרופיל הצבאי שלו, אם הגיע לידיו …

*Gist:* an employer may not ask for or use your IDF medical profile (except the Shin Bet and the Mossad). ([s. 2א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה#סעיף_2א))

> בלי לגרוע מהוראות סעיף קטן (א), בתובענה של עובד או של דורש עבודה בשל הפרת הוראות סעיף 2, תהא חובת ההוכחה על הנתבע כי פעל שלא בניגוד להוראות הסעיף האמור, אם הוכח כי דרש, במישרין או בעקיפין, מעובד או מדורש עבודה, מידע בנושא שהפליה בשלו אסורה …

*Gist:* if the employer asked, directly or indirectly, for information about a prohibited ground, the employer must prove it did not discriminate. Section 9(א) also moves the burden when you met the employer's stated requirements (hiring, promotion) or when your conduct gave no reason for dismissal. ([s. 9(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה#סעיף_9))

> לא יזדקק בית־הדין לעבודה לתביעה אזרחית בשל הפרת הוראות חוק זה שהוגשה לאחר שחלפו חמש שנים מיום שנוצרה העילה, ולעניין תביעה של דורש עבודה – שלוש שנים.

*Gist:* a worker has five years to sue, a job applicant three (as amended in 2024–25). ([s. 14](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה#סעיף_14))

> כל אדם רשאי להגיש תלונה בכתב לנציב האזורי על הפרת הוראה לפי חקיקת השוויון בעבודה (בחוק זה – תלונה).

*Gist:* anyone may complain in writing to the regional commissioner about a breach of the equal-employment laws. ([s. 18יב](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה#סעיף_18יב))

> לדחות את התלונה על הסף, אם סבר כי … עברה יותר משנה מאז שנוצרה העילה לתלונה;

*Gist:* the commissioner may dismiss a complaint filed more than a year after the cause arose (among other reasons). ([s. 18יג(ב)(1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה#סעיף_18יג))

## How it works in practice

### Who is covered

- **Employers of six or more**, counting manpower-contractor workers (s. 21(ג)). Smaller employers are outside the law except for retaliation over sexual harassment (s. 7), but Kol Zchut notes Labour Court rulings that apply equality to them through good faith and the Basic Law.
- **Workers and applicants**, including through manpower and service contractors (s. 2(א1)).
- **Not covered as discrimination:** preferring a family member of the employer (s. 21(א)); distinctions required by the nature of the job (s. 2(ג)); affirmative action required by law.
- **Disability** in employment is covered by the disability law ss. 8–14, also in the Labour Court, with the disability Commission (lesson 09.4).

### What the employer must prove

| Situation | Burden on the employer if you show… | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring, promotion, training, severance | You met the conditions or qualifications the employer set | 9(א)(1) |
| Dismissal | Nothing in your conduct or acts gave a reason to dismiss you | 9(א)(2) |
| Any stage | The employer asked you, directly or indirectly, about a prohibited ground | 9(ג) |

Questions such as "are you planning children?", "how many reserve days do you expect this year?", "which synagogue do you go to?" or "where do your parents come from?" are evidence. Write them down with the date and the interviewer's name.

### Retaliation

The employer may not harm you because you complained or sued under this law or the Equal Pay Law, or because you helped another worker do so (s. 6). Keep a copy of every complaint you send.

### Two routes, often both

| | Commission complaint | Labour Court claim |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Court fee; lawyer advisable |
| What happens | The regional commissioner may dismiss, refer to another body, send to mediation (with both sides' consent), investigate, sue on your behalf, or ask for a general order (s. 18יג(ב)) | The court may award compensation for non-monetary damage at its discretion, and injunctions (s. 10) |
| Time | Within a year to avoid dismissal (s. 18יג(ב)(1)) | 5 years for a worker, 3 for an applicant (s. 14) |
| Pending case | The Commission will not handle a matter already before a court (s. 18יג(א)(2)) | — |
| Answer | You are told in writing, with reasons unless the Commission sues (s. 18יד) | Judgment |

### What a good complaint contains

1. Your details and the employer's (legal name, number of employees if known).
2. The stage (hiring, conditions, promotion, dismissal) and the dates.
3. The ground and why you think it was the reason: the question asked, a remark, a comparison with others.
4. The evidence you have and the evidence the employer holds (the job ad, the scoring sheet, who was hired).
5. What you want: investigation, mediation, a claim on your behalf, a change of policy.
6. Whether any court case is pending.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Shira, 31, a religious-Zionist software tester from Efrat, has served 140 reserve days since October 2023. At a job interview with a 50-person firm in Jerusalem the manager asks, "Realistically, how many reserve days will you do next year?" She answers honestly. Two days later she is told the firm "went with a candidate with more availability". The ad listed three years' experience and a testing certificate; she has both.

1. **Coverage.** An employer of 50; Shira is an applicant; the stage is hiring.
2. **Ground.** Reserve duty, including expected service and its frequency or length (s. 2(א)).
3. **Burden.** Two routes: s. 9(ג) — the employer asked about the prohibited ground; s. 9(א)(1) — she met the stated qualifications.
4. **Evidence.** Her notes written the same evening: date, interviewer, the exact question. The ad (screenshot). The rejection email.
5. **Route.** She files a Commission complaint the same month, asking for an investigation and, if justified, a claim on her behalf. Her own deadline to sue in the Labour Court is three years from the rejection (applicant).
6. **Parallel.** If she had been dismissed rather than not hired, the Discharged Soldiers (Return to Work) Law could also apply; she would ask the Commission about it.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A real situation of your own, or the fictional one below.
- The online form link and the structure above (or the [formal-letter.md](../../templates/formal-letter.md) template, adapted).
- 50 minutes.

Fictional situation: Fatma, 26, from Umm al-Fahm, wears a hijab. She applies for a front-desk job at a private medical clinic in Hadera with 12 employees. The ad asks for Hebrew, Arabic and English and a year of reception experience; she has all three languages and two years' experience. At the interview the manager says, "You're excellent, but our patients expect a certain look at the front." She is not hired; a week later the ad is still online.

### Steps

1. Check coverage: employer size, stage, ground (religion? nationality? both?).
2. Write which part of s. 9 moves the burden, and the fact that triggers it.
3. List the evidence you have and the evidence only the employer has.
4. Draft the complaint to the regional commissioner (one page, numbered paragraphs, following "What a good complaint contains").
5. Write the two dates: the last day to complain without risking dismissal (one year), and the last day to sue as an applicant (three years).
6. Write one sentence on whether you would also go to the Labour Court now, and why.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Sending is optional; a dry run is enough. If you file for real: act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### How you know it worked

- You chose the employment law and the Labour Court, not the services law.
- Your complaint names a prohibited ground, a burden-shifting fact and a specific request.
- You wrote both deadlines and noted that a pending court case stops the Commission.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing in the Magistrates' Court | No jurisdiction | Refile in the Labour Court within s. 14 | Employment = Labour Court |
| Complaining to the Commission after more than a year | Complaint may be dismissed (s. 18יג(ב)(1)) | Sue in the Labour Court within s. 14 | Complain early |
| Suing first, then asking the Commission to help | The Commission will not handle a matter pending in court | Ask the court to let the Commission be heard (s. 13) | Decide the order of steps first |
| No notes of the interview question | You lose the s. 9(ג) burden shift | Ask witnesses; request the interview records | Write notes the same day |
| Assuming a 4-person employer is covered | Claim under s. 2 fails | Argue good faith and the Basic Law; get advice | Check the headcount first |
| Answering "how many reserve days?" and arguing about it on the spot | Escalation; no record | Answer politely, then record the question | Note it; complain later |

## Review

- The employment law protects workers and applicants of employers with six or more employees, at every stage from hiring to retirement benefits.
- The burden of proof moves to the employer when you met the stated conditions, when nothing in your conduct justified dismissal, or when the employer asked about a prohibited ground.
- Reserve duty is a ground, and employers may not ask for your IDF medical profile.
- The Commission is free, can investigate, mediate, sue for you and seek a general order; complain within a year.
- Only the Labour Court hears the claim: five years for a worker, three for an applicant.
