# 09.2 · Discrimination in Services and Entry: Suing Without Proof of Damage

The services law lets you claim compensation from a club, shop, taxi company, hotel, bank or municipality that discriminated against you, without proving that you suffered any damage, and it shifts the burden of proof once you show a simple comparison. In this lesson you learn the elements of the claim, the four presumptions, the forums and the ceiling, and you write a demand letter that a business will take seriously.

## Why it matters

Discrimination at the door rarely leaves a document saying "because you are Arab" or "because you are religious". Without the law's two tools — compensation without proof of damage and the presumptions in s. 6 — most of these cases would be impossible to win. With them, a well-prepared individual can win in the Small Claims Court without a lawyer, and a single judgment often changes a business's door policy more than a protest does.

## The law

> מעשה או מחדל בניגוד לסעיפים 3 ו־4 הם עוולה אזרחית, והוראות פקודת הנזיקין [נוסח חדש], יחולו עליהם, בכפוף להוראות חוק זה.

*Gist:* discrimination under s. 3 and a discriminatory advertisement under s. 4 are civil wrongs, governed by the Torts Ordinance. ([s. 5(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_איסור_הפליה_במוצרים,_בשירותים_ובכניסה_למקומות_בידור_ולמקומות_ציבוריים#סעיף_5))

> בית המשפט רשאי לפסוק בשל עוולה לפי חוק זה פיצוי שלא יעלה על 50,000 ש״ח, בלא הוכחת נזק; סכום זה יעודכן ב־16 בכל חודש, בהתאם לשיעור שינוי המדד החדש לעומת המדד הבסיסי …

*Gist:* the court may award up to ₪50,000 without proof of damage, linked monthly to the consumer price index from October 2000; in October 2026 that is about ₪79,700 (see [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). ([s. 5(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_איסור_הפליה_במוצרים,_בשירותים_ובכניסה_למקומות_בידור_ולמקומות_ציבוריים#סעיף_5))

> הוכיח התובע בהליך אזרחי לפי חוק זה אחד מאלה, חזקה שהנתבע פעל בניגוד להוראות סעיף 3, כל עוד לא הוכיח אחרת:

*Gist:* if the claimant proves one of four situations, the court presumes discrimination unless the defendant proves otherwise. The four are: (1) refusal after asking about details related to a listed ground; (2) refusing members of a group while not refusing others in the same circumstances; (3) setting a condition for the group not required of others; (4) delaying the group while not delaying others. ([s. 6](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_איסור_הפליה_במוצרים,_בשירותים_ובכניסה_למקומות_בידור_ולמקומות_ציבוריים#סעיף_6))

> מקום שהעוולה נעשתה על ידי תאגיד, יהיה אדם אחראי גם הוא לעוולה, אם היה אותה שעה מנהל פעיל בתאגיד, שותף, למעט שותף מוגבל, או עובד בכיר האחראי לנושא הנדון, אלא אם כן הוכיח שניים אלה:

*Gist:* when a company discriminates, its active manager, partner or senior employee in charge is also liable unless they prove they did not know and took reasonable steps to prevent it. ([s. 8](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_איסור_הפליה_במוצרים,_בשירותים_ובכניסה_למקומות_בידור_ולמקומות_ציבוריים#סעיף_8))

> תובענה בשל עוולה לפי חוק זה יכול שתוגש בידי תאגיד העוסק בהגנה על זכויותיו של מי שאסור להפלותו לפי חוק זה, ובלבד שאם עילת התביעה היא הפליה כלפי אדם מסוים, אותו אדם הסכים לכך.

*Gist:* an organisation that protects the rights of a protected group may sue, with the person's consent when the claim is about a specific person. ([s. 7(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_איסור_הפליה_במוצרים,_בשירותים_ובכניסה_למקומות_בידור_ולמקומות_ציבוריים#סעיף_7))

The text above was checked on Wikisource on 13 October 2026; the last amendment shown there is from 2017. Private bills to raise the ceiling, and others to change the law, have been tabled in the Knesset; none had passed when we checked. Check the current text before you file (lesson 02.1).

## How it works in practice

### The elements

You need to show four things:

1. **A covered defendant**: someone whose business is supplying a product or public service, or running a public place — including the bouncer, manager or owner actually in charge (s. 3(ג)), and the state (s. 11).
2. **A covered act**: refusing a product or service, refusing entry, giving worse service inside, or setting irrelevant conditions (s. 3(ג1)); or publishing a discriminatory advertisement (s. 4).
3. **A listed ground** (s. 3(א), (א1)).
4. **No exception** applies (s. 3(ד)) — the defendant must raise and prove it.

### Using the presumptions

The presumptions are your most important tool. Plan the evidence around them:

| Presumption (s. 6) | What you must show | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (1) Refusal after asking about a ground | They asked your origin, religion, address or age, then refused | Your notes, a recording of your own conversation, a text message |
| (2) Group refused, others not | You were refused; others not in the group were let in at the same time and circumstances | Friends who were let in minutes later; a tester (lesson 09.5) |
| (3) Extra condition for the group | A condition demanded only of you (a deposit, "members only", a dress code) | The others' experience; the business's own website |
| (4) Delay for the group | You were kept waiting while others went in | Time-stamped photos or messages |

Once a presumption applies, the business must prove a real, non-discriminatory reason. "The place was full" fails if others were let in a minute later.

### Choosing a route

| Route | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Demand letter | Almost always first | Gives a short deadline, asks for compensation and a policy change. Many businesses settle. Use the template below. |
| Small Claims Court | You are an individual and claim up to the ceiling | Only an individual may file (Courts Law s. 60(א)); no lawyers without permission (s. 63); simple procedure; fee about 1% of the claim. Each victim files their own claim. Module 11 teaches the procedure. |
| Magistrates' Court | You want more than the small-claims ceiling, an organisation sues (s. 7), or there is a class | Full procedure and court fees; a lawyer is advisable. |
| Class action | A policy affects many (e.g., an advertisement, a rule) | Second Schedule item 7 of the Class Actions Law; needs a lawyer. |

See [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md) for the small-claims ceiling, the small-claims fee and the s. 5(ב) ceiling.

**Deadline.** The services law has no special limitation period, so the general seven years applies (Limitation Law s. 5(1)). Do not wait: witnesses forget, and recordings and CCTV are deleted within days.

**Legal Aid.** A person bringing a civil claim under this law is exempt from the Legal Aid means test, but the Legal Aid department still checks the merits (Kol Zchut, updated 2.3.2025).

**Amounts in practice.** Courts rarely award the full ceiling. Reported judgments include ₪33,000 to each of two Arab men refused entry to a club in Nazareth (Magistrates', 2018) and ₪120,000 to a young woman a bus driver refused to take because of her shorts, a sum that also covered sexual harassment under another law (Tel Aviv Magistrates', 2020). Both are Magistrates' Court judgments, not binding precedent.

**Other channels.** A complaint to the Government Unit for Coordinating the Fight against Racism (Ministry of Justice) can run in parallel when the ground is origin, race or religion. It does not award compensation.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Friday night in Rishon LeZion. Yonatan and Avi, two Ethiopian-Israeli students, are told at the door of a club, "Tonight is members only." Their two friends, Lior and Noa, who are not of Ethiopian origin and are not members either, wait five minutes and are let in without being asked anything. Lior texts Yonatan: "We're in, nobody asked about membership."

1. **Elements.** A club is a public place (s. 2 lists "דיסקוטק"); refusing entry; ground: origin or race; the "members only" claim is a condition not required of others.
2. **Presumption.** s. 6(2): others not in the group were let in under the same circumstances. Also s. 6(3): a condition imposed only on them.
3. **Evidence that night.** Yonatan writes a note with times on his phone. Lior's text, time-stamped. Noa photographs the entrance and the time. Next morning all four write signed statements.
4. **Defendant.** The company running the club, plus the active manager (s. 8). The company name and number come from the business licence or the Companies Registrar.
5. **Demand letter.** Yonatan sends a demand letter by registered mail and email: the facts in numbered paragraphs, ss. 3, 5, 6 and 8, a demand for ₪15,000 each and a written change of door policy within 14 days.
6. **No answer.** Each of them files a small claim up to the ceiling, attaching the statements, the text and the photos. Lior and Noa come as witnesses.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A real incident that happened to you, or the fictional one below.
- The template [discrimination-demand-letter.md](../../templates/discrimination-demand-letter.md).
- 60 minutes.

Fictional incident: Samah and Rami, an Arab couple from Haifa, ask by WhatsApp to rent a holiday cabin in the Golan for a weekend. The owner answers: "Where are you from?" Samah writes "Haifa, we're Arab." The owner replies: "Sorry, fully booked." An hour later Samah's colleague Michal asks for the same dates and gets a price and a booking link.

### Steps

1. List the four elements and the evidence for each.
2. Choose the presumption(s) in s. 6. Write one sentence for each saying which fact meets it.
3. Identify the defendant: the business name, and whether s. 8 adds a person.
4. Choose the route and the amount you will demand. Explain in one sentence why the amount is reasonable (the ceiling is a maximum, not a price).
5. Fill the demand letter template: numbered facts, the law, the presumptions, the demand, a 14-day deadline, the next step.
6. Write the date you would file if there is no answer, and the court.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Sending the letter is optional; a dry run is enough. If you send it for real: act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### How you know it worked

- Your letter cites s. 3 (the prohibition), s. 5 (compensation without proof of damage) and the specific presumption in s. 6, with the fact that meets it.
- The facts are dated, neutral and numbered; the evidence is listed as attachments.
- The demand is specific (an amount, a policy change, a date), with a stated next step.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| No comparison: only "they refused me" | No presumption; you must prove intent | Find who else was served that night; ask the business for its records | Bring a comparison or a tester (09.5) |
| Suing the club's trade name instead of the company | Claim cannot be served or enforced | Amend with the company's legal name and number | Check the Companies Registrar before filing |
| Demanding the full ceiling as a "fine" | Looks unreasonable; harder to settle | Justify the amount by the humiliation, publicity and repeat conduct | Ask for a reasoned amount and a policy change |
| An organisation filing in Small Claims Court | Not allowed: only an individual may file there | Each victim files, or the organisation files in the Magistrates' Court | Choose the route by who is suing |
| Waiting months before collecting evidence | CCTV deleted, witnesses forget | Ask the business in writing to preserve footage | Write notes and statements within 24 hours |
| Publishing accusations before filing | Defamation exposure (08.1) | Stick to facts you can prove | Publish facts, not labels, and only after checking 08.2 |

## Review

- Discrimination in a product, public service or entry to a public place is a civil wrong (s. 5(א)) and an offence (s. 9).
- Compensation without proof of damage is capped by an index-linked ceiling (s. 5(ב)); courts usually award less.
- Four presumptions in s. 6 shift the burden: asked about a ground; others served; extra condition; delay.
- Managers can be personally liable (s. 8); an organisation may sue with consent (s. 7).
- Start with a demand letter; an individual may then use the Small Claims Court up to its ceiling.
