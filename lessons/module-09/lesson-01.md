# 09.1 · The Map of Anti-Discrimination Laws

Israel has no single anti-discrimination code. Five or six statutes each cover one area of life, each with its own list of grounds, its own court and its own commission or none. In this lesson you learn to place any incident on that map — which law, which section, which forum, which deadline — and you build a one-page map for an incident you choose.

## Why it matters

The most common mistake in discrimination cases is not a weak case but a wrong address: a job applicant who sues in the Magistrates' Court (only the Labour Court may hear it), a wheelchair user who sues under the services law (disability has its own law), a café owner's victim who relies on a ground the statute does not list. Each law was written separately, so the details differ in ways that decide cases.

Lesson [03.4](../module-03/lesson-04.md) taught the *constitutional* right to equality: what you argue against the state in a petition. This module teaches the *statutory* rights: what you can claim against anyone — a shop, an employer, a club, a bank, a municipality — usually with compensation and often without proving damage. The two work together: a statute against a public body can be enforced in an administrative petition (05.6), and the constitutional principle helps interpret the statute.

## The law

> מי שעיסוקו בהספקת מוצר או שירות ציבורי או בהפעלת מקום ציבורי, לא יפלה בהספקת המוצר או השירות הציבורי, במתן הכניסה למקום הציבורי או במתן שירות במקום הציבורי, מחמת גזע, דת או קבוצה דתית, לאום, ארץ מוצא, מין, נטיה מינית, השקפה, השתייכות מפלגתית, גיל, מעמד אישי, הורות או לבישת מדי כוחות הביטחון וההצלה או ענידת סמליהם.

*Gist:* a business or body that supplies a product or public service, or runs a public place, may not discriminate on any of these grounds; s. 3(א1) adds place of residence, and s. 3(ה) sends disability to the disability law. ([Products and Services Law, s. 3](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_איסור_הפליה_במוצרים,_בשירותים_ובכניסה_למקומות_בידור_ולמקומות_ציבוריים#סעיף_3))

> לא יפלה מעסיק בין עובדיו או בין דורשי עבודה מחמת מינם, נטייתם המינית, מעמדם האישי, הריון, טיפולי פוריות, טיפולי הפריה חוץ־גופית, היותם הורים, גילם, גזעם, דתם, לאומיותם, ארץ מוצאם, מקום מגוריהם, השקפתם, מפלגתם או שירות במילואים שלהם …

*Gist:* an employer may not discriminate between workers or job applicants on these grounds — including reserve duty of the worker or partner — in hiring, conditions, promotion, training, dismissal and retirement benefits. ([Equal Employment Opportunities Law, s. 2(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_ההזדמנויות_בעבודה#סעיף_2))

> אין מפלים אדם עם מוגבלות ואין פוגעים בזכותו לנגישות, והכל בהתאם להוראות פרק זה.

*Gist:* a person with a disability may not be discriminated against or denied accessibility, as set out in Chapter ה׳1. ([Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, s. 19ג](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שוויון_זכויות_לאנשים_עם_מוגבלות#סעיף_19ג))

> דין אחד יהיה לאישה ולאיש לכל פעולה משפטית; וכל הוראת חוק המפלה לרעה את האישה, באשר היא אישה, לכל פעולה משפטית – אין נוהגים לפיה.

*Gist:* one law for women and men; a provision that discriminates against women as women is not applied, and s. 1א(ב) adds that intent to discriminate does not matter. ([Women's Equal Rights Law, s. 1א](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_שיווי_זכויות_האשה#סעיף_1א))

> לענין חוק זה דין המדינה כדין כל אדם.

*Gist:* the services law binds the state like anyone else, so a ministry or municipality that runs a public service is covered. ([Products and Services Law, s. 11](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_איסור_הפליה_במוצרים,_בשירותים_ובכניסה_למקומות_בידור_ולמקומות_ציבוריים#סעיף_11))

English translations of these laws exist but are unofficial and out of date; the Hebrew text governs.

## How it works in practice

### The map

| Area | Statute | Who is bound | Where you go | Commission |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shops, services, entry to public places (not disability) | Products and Services Law 2000 | Anyone who supplies a product or public service or runs a public place, profit or not, paid or free (s. 3(ב)); the state (s. 11) | Magistrates' Court, or Small Claims Court for an individual up to the ceiling | None (complaints about racism: the Government Unit for Coordinating the Fight against Racism) |
| Work: hiring, pay, promotion, dismissal | Equal Employment Opportunities Law 1988 (and the Equal Pay Law 1996) | Employers of 6 or more (s. 21(ג)); manpower and service contractors | Labour Court only (s. 10) | Equal Employment Opportunities Commission |
| Disability: services, places, accessibility, work | Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law 1998 | Service providers, public places, public bodies, employers | Civil court for services and accessibility; Labour Court for work (s. 14) | Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities |
| Schools | Pupils' Rights Law 2000, s. 5 | Local education authorities, schools | A letter to the school and the local authority, then an administrative petition; breach is also an offence (s. 5(ב)) | None |
| The state and public bodies generally | Constitutional equality (03.4), Women's Equal Rights Law, administrative law | Every public authority | Administrative petition or HCJ (05.6–05.7) | — |

### Grounds are not the same everywhere

Read the list in the law that applies, not a general idea of "discrimination":

- **Place of residence** (מקום מגורים) is a ground in services (s. 3(א1), added in 2017) and in employment. A local authority may still prefer its own residents where its functions require it.
- **Reserve duty** is a ground only in employment (s. 2(א)), and the employer may not ask for your IDF medical profile (s. 2א).
- **Wearing the uniform** of the security and rescue forces is a ground only in services.
- **Political view** (השקפה) and party affiliation appear in both services and employment.
- **Socio-economic background** and **gender identity** appear in the Pupils' Rights Law; gender identity is not listed by name in the services or employment law.
- **Disability** is never claimed under the services law; s. 3(ה) sends it to the disability law.

### Exceptions you must expect

Every law lets the defendant argue that the distinction is required by the nature of the job, product, service or place (services s. 3(ד)(1); employment s. 2(ג); disability s. 19ו(ג)). The services law also allows non-profit clubs serving their members' special needs, and separate frameworks for men and women where they are justified (s. 3(ד)(2)–(3)). The Women's Equal Rights Law allows distinctions required by real differences and allows affirmative action (s. 1ב). Expect the other side to raise these, and collect facts that answer them (lesson 09.5).

### Compensation, prosecution, class actions

- Services and disability-services claims allow compensation without proof of damage up to an indexed ceiling (lessons 09.2, 09.4; see [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
- Labour Court may award compensation for non-monetary damage "בשיעור שייראה לו" — at its discretion — in employment cases (s. 10(א)(1)).
- Discrimination in services is also a criminal offence punished by a fine (s. 9), but prosecution is up to the authorities.
- A class action is possible in all three areas (Class Actions Law, Second Schedule, items 7–9).
- In services and employment cases an organisation that protects the rights of the group may sue, with the victim's consent where the claim is about a specific person (services s. 7; employment s. 12).

## Worked example

**Situation.** Menachem and Rivka, a Haredi couple from Bnei Brak with five children, try to book a holiday apartment in a guesthouse in the Galilee. On the phone they are told, "We don't take Haredi families, it doesn't fit our guests." The same week their secular neighbours book the same dates without a problem.

1. **Area.** A guesthouse is a public place (s. 2 lists "בית הארחה"); renting rooms is a public service. Services law.
2. **Ground.** Religion or religious group (דת או קבוצה דתית); possibly also "parenthood" if the refusal was about the children. Both are listed in s. 3(א).
3. **Exception?** Is it required by the nature of the service? A guesthouse open to the public cannot rely on its other guests' preferences; the "nature of the service" exception is narrow. A members-only non-profit club would be different (s. 3(ד)(2)) — it is not.
4. **Forum.** A claim in the Magistrates' Court, or in the Small Claims Court by each of them individually up to the ceiling. Compensation without proof of damage (s. 5(ב)).
5. **Commission?** None for services; they may also complain to the Government Unit for Coordinating the Fight against Racism, which handles discrimination against Haredim among other groups.
6. **Evidence.** The neighbours' booking is exactly the comparison s. 6(2) needs (lesson 09.2). They should write down the call at once and keep the phone log (lesson 09.5).

## Do it yourself

### You need

- An incident you know of (yours, or a public report), or one of the fictional incidents below.
- The Wikisource texts of the three main laws (links above).
- 45 minutes.

Fictional incidents (choose one):

- **A.** Yusuf, an Arab citizen from Tayibe, applies for a warehouse job with a 40-employee logistics company. The form asks for "army unit and medical profile". He leaves it blank and is not called.
- **B.** Daniel, who lives in the settlement of Eli, orders a sofa online from a Tel Aviv furniture shop. The shop cancels: "We do not deliver to or sell to residents of the territories."
- **C.** Tamar, who is deaf, is refused a bank appointment unless she brings a hearing person to "interpret".
- **D.** A municipal community centre runs a computer course and rejects a 68-year-old applicant: "Only up to 60."

### Steps

1. Write the incident in three neutral sentences: who did what, to whom, when.
2. Identify the area of life and the statute. Copy the section number.
3. Find the ground in the statute's list and copy the exact Hebrew words. If it is not listed, write which other law or principle might help.
4. Write the exception the other side will most likely claim, and one fact that would answer it.
5. Name the forum and any commission. Note whether an organisation could sue with consent.
6. Note the deadline to check: employment claims have a special limitation period (s. 14); others follow the general rules (lessons 09.2–09.3).

### Template

No template for this step. Use a plain one-page table with these headings: incident, area, statute and section, ground (Hebrew), likely exception, answer, forum, commission, deadline. You will reuse it in the module project, together with [discrimination-evidence-log.md](../../templates/discrimination-evidence-log.md).

### How you know it worked

- For incident A you chose the employment law, the Labour Court, and noticed s. 2א on the medical profile as well as nationality.
- For B you found "מקום מגורים" in s. 3(א1), not in s. 3(א).
- For C you went to the disability law, not the services law.
- For D you identified age as a ground and the municipality as bound by the law (s. 11).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suing an employer in the Magistrates' Court | Claim transferred or dismissed for lack of jurisdiction | Refile in the Labour Court within the limitation period | Employment = Labour Court (s. 10) |
| Claiming disability discrimination under the services law | Wrong cause of action (s. 3(ה)) | Amend to the disability law, ch. ה׳1 | Check the area first |
| Relying on a ground the statute does not list | Claim fails or needs a weaker general argument | Look for a listed ground that fits the facts, or another law | Copy the ground from the statute |
| Ignoring the "nature of the service" exception | The defence wins on facts you never addressed | Add facts showing the distinction is not needed | Write the likely exception before you write the claim |
| Treating a public body as exempt | Missed claim | The services law binds the state (s. 11) | Remember s. 11 and administrative petitions |

## Review

- Israel's anti-discrimination law is a set of separate statutes: services, employment, disability, education, women's equal rights, plus constitutional equality against the state.
- Each has its own list of grounds — check it; place of residence, reserve duty and uniforms appear only in some.
- Employment cases go only to the Labour Court; services and disability-services cases go to the civil courts.
- Two commissions exist: one for employment and one for disability; services have none.
- Every law has a "nature of the job, product or place" exception that the other side will raise.
