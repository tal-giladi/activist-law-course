# 11.6 · Fee Exemptions and Legal Aid

Money should not decide whether you can go to court. Two tools help: the court-fee exemption for people who cannot pay, and state Legal Aid, which gives eligible people a lawyer for free or for a small fee. In this lesson you check your eligibility for both, prepare a fee-exemption request with its affidavit, and plan a Legal Aid application.

## Why it matters

A court fee of a few hundred or a few thousand shekels stops many people from filing a good claim or petition; an unrepresented defendant in a large claim risks much more. The fee exemption is decided by a judge on your affidavit, and certain documents make it almost automatic. Legal Aid covers more than most people think — money claims, housing, employment, National Insurance — and for some subjects there is no means test at all. Knowing both before a deadline saves you from filing late or not at all.

## The law

### The fee exemption

> בעל דין, הטוען שאין ביכולתו לשלם אגרה, יצרף לתובענה, עם הבאתה לראשונה לבית המשפט, בקשה לפטור מתשלום אגרה בגין אותה תובענה, בצירוף תצהיר שיפרט בו את רכושו, רכוש בן זוגו ורכוש הוריו אם הוא סמוך על שולחנם, ומקורות הכנסתו בששת החודשים שקדמו לתאריך הבקשה.

*Gist:* file the request together with the claim, with an affidavit on your assets, your spouse's assets (and your parents' if they support you) and your income in the last six months. ([Court Fees Regulations, reg. 14(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_המשפט_(אגרות)#סעיף_14))

> הוגשה בקשה לפטור מתשלום אגרה וראה בית משפט שאין ביכולתו של המבקש לשלם את האגרה, ונראה לבית המשפט שההליך מגלה עילה, רשאי בית המשפט לפטור את המבקש מתשלום האגרה, כולה או חלקה;

*Gist:* two conditions: you cannot pay, and your claim shows a cause of action; the exemption may be full or partial. ([reg. 14(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_המשפט_(אגרות)#סעיף_14))

> קבלת גמלה לפי חוק הבטחת הכנסה, התשמ״א–1980 בעת הגשת הבקשה לפטור מאגרה, תשמש ראיה לכאורה לחוסר יכולתו של המבקש לשלם את האגרה כאמור בתקנת משנה (ג).

*Gist:* receiving income support when you apply is prima facie proof that you cannot pay. ([reg. 14(ה)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_בתי_המשפט_(אגרות)#סעיף_14)) So is, within the last two years, a court fee exemption, a declaration as a debtor of limited means, bankruptcy, or an insolvency order (reg. 14(ד)).

Other points in reg. 14: if refused or partly granted, the court may let you pay in instalments (14(ז)); a full exemption covers later fees in the same proceeding (14(ט)); if you win, the court may make the other side pay the fee (14(י)). The request itself costs nothing (reg. 20(4)). A small-claims fee is covered by an exemption under these regulations ([Small Claims Regulations, reg. 6(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_6)). Someone represented by Legal Aid because they cannot afford a lawyer is exempt by status (reg. 19(א)(7)).

### Legal Aid

> לשכת סיוע משפטי תתן שירות משפטי, לרבות ההוצאות הכרוכות בו, בענינים ובהיקף שנקבעו בתקנות, למי שידו אינה משגת לשאת בהם, או למי שנקבע בתוספת בענינים ובהיקף שנקבעו בה;

*Gist:* Legal Aid gives legal services, including expenses, in the subjects set by regulation to people who cannot afford them, and to groups listed in the Schedule. ([Legal Aid Law s. 2](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_2))

> ראש הלשכה רשאי לדחות בקשת שירות משפטי, אם היה סבור שענינו של המבקש אינו אלא טרדני או קנטרני, או שאין לו על מה לסמוך, או שאין לו בסיס סביר מבחינת הדין, העובדות או הראיות,

*Gist:* a request may be refused if the case has no reasonable basis in law, facts or evidence — the merits test. ([s. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_4)) A refusal can be appealed to the District Court (s. 5) within 30 days of the notice ([Legal Aid Regulations, reg. 14(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_14)).

> (1) אם המבקש הוא יחיד או משפחה של שלוש נפשות לכל היותר – גובה הכנסת המבקש עד 67% מהשכר הממוצע במשק;

*Gist:* the income test is 67% of the average wage for a single person or a family of up to three, plus 6% for each additional person; realisable assets must not exceed three times the average wage, and the home you live in is not counted. ([reg. 2(א)–(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_2)) Today's figures are on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

> (3) ענינים כספיים, לרבות נזיקין, …

*Gist:* the subjects include family matters, housing (including eviction), money matters and torts, Labour Court matters, disability and bereaved-family benefits, discharged soldiers' rights, citizenship and population registry, and business or professional licensing. ([reg. 5](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_הסיוע_המשפטי#סעיף_5)) Service includes advice, drafting and court representation (reg. 6).

Checked on Hebrew Wikisource and gov.il on 13 October 2026.

## How it works in practice

### Fee exemption: step by step

1. **Decide early.** The request is filed **with** the claim or petition (reg. 14(א)), not after.
2. **Collect proof:** income support approval, an Execution Office "limited means" decision, an insolvency order, or a previous fee exemption from the last two years — any of these makes your case much easier. Otherwise: payslips or benefit statements for six months, bank statements, a list of assets.
3. **Fill the form** from the judiciary's service page ("לקבלת הטופס") and the affidavit. Use [fee-exemption-request.md](../../templates/fee-exemption-request.md) to prepare the content.
4. **Certify the affidavit** before a lawyer or at a court registry counter (a fee is charged for certifying at the registry, gov.il).
5. **File.** The judiciary's page lists online filing in Net HaMishpat for lawyers; a party without a lawyer files by registered mail to the registry or at a registry service counter (some have volunteers who help with the form). For a small claim, ask *3852 whether the online claim service accepts the request with the claim.
6. **Decision.** A judge or registrar decides, sometimes after inviting you. If refused, ask for instalments (14(ז)) or pay; you may appeal the decision without a fee (reg. 20(4)).

### Legal Aid: three tests

The Ministry of Justice describes three cumulative conditions:

1. **Subject.** Is your matter one Legal Aid handles? (reg. 5; the gov.il page lists money claims, Execution Office debts, housing, employment, family and personal status, National Insurance and more.)
2. **Means.** Income and assets under the thresholds — **unless** the subject is exempt from the means test. The gov.il page lists, among others: National Insurance, Holocaust survivors, involuntary hospitalisation, victims of trafficking and of sex offences, terror victims, bereaved families, and whistleblowers who exposed corruption at work (also in the Law's Schedule, item 7). Claims under the services discrimination law are also exempt from the means test (lesson 09.2).
3. **Merits.** A reasonable legal chance of success (s. 4).

### Applying

- Online through the Ministry of Justice service (the form on gov.il "בקשה לסיוע משפטי"), by phone at *6405 (Sun–Thu 8:00–16:00, also chat), or at a district office by appointment (Be'er Sheva, Jerusalem, Lod, Tel Aviv, Haifa, Nazareth).
- Attach proof of income and assets, the court documents and any deadline. Say clearly if a deadline is close: the head of the office may give **temporary service** in urgent cases (reg. 3(ב)) and start before the fee is paid (reg. 3א(ה)).
- Fees: a small advance when you apply and a participation fee if approved (regs 1א, 3א); some people and subjects are exempt (reg. 3ב). Amounts on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).
- If approved, Legal Aid appoints a private lawyer, or may instruct you in writing to appear yourself with guidance (reg. 4(א)(5)).
- If refused: appeal to the District Court within 30 days (reg. 14).

### Just above the line?

Look at the other routes on [Where to Get Help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md): university legal clinics, public-interest organisations, and the Bar Association's pro bono programme "שכר מצווה".

## Worked example

**Situation.** Moshe, 58, from Ashdod, uses a wheelchair and campaigns for accessible buses. He receives income support. His landlord kept his ₪7,000 deposit after he moved out, claiming "damage", and now also sues him in the Magistrates' Court for ₪45,000 for "lost rent".

1. **His own claim (deposit).** A small claim for ₪7,000. The fee is small but real for him. He prepares a fee-exemption request: income support approval (prima facie proof, reg. 14(ה)), a six-month statement of income, no assets but a 12-year-old car. He files at the registry counter with the claim and certifies the affidavit there.
2. **The claim against him.** Defending a ₪45,000 claim is harder (lesson 11.4). He applies to Legal Aid by phone (*6405) the day he is served, saying the defence is due in 45 days (fast track).
3. **The three tests.** Subject: housing and money matters (reg. 5(2)–(3)). Means: income support, within the thresholds. Merits: he has the move-out photos and the landlord's WhatsApp "all fine".
4. **Result.** Legal Aid approves, appoints a lawyer and collects the participation fee; as a Legal Aid client he is exempt from the court fee by status (reg. 19(א)(7)). His own small claim continues separately, with the fee exemption granted.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your own figures, or the fictional profile below.
- The template [fee-exemption-request.md](../../templates/fee-exemption-request.md) and, for the affidavit form, [affidavit.md](../../templates/affidavit.md).
- 45 minutes.

Fictional profile: Noor, 24, a student from Tayibe and a volunteer in a campaign for safer streets, works part-time and earns about ₪4,100 a month, lives with her parents (who do not support her financially), has ₪6,000 in savings, and wants to file a ₪22,000 small claim against a phone company, and possibly an administrative petition (fee in lesson 05.6) against her municipality.

### Steps

1. Calculate the court fee for each proceeding (see [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)).
2. Decide for each: pay, request an exemption, or seek Legal Aid. Write one sentence of reasons.
3. List the proof you would attach to a fee-exemption request; mark whether any of the reg. 14(ד)–(ה) prima facie proofs applies.
4. Fill the fee-exemption template: the request, the affidavit paragraphs (assets, spouse, parents if relevant, six months' income), the cause of action in two sentences.
5. Run the three Legal Aid tests for the petition: subject, means (using the current thresholds), merits.
6. Write the date by which you would file each document, and what you would do if the exemption were refused.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Prepare the request as a dry run. If you file a real fee-exemption request or Legal Aid application: act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. A false affidavit is a criminal offence (lesson 02.6).

### How you know it worked

- Your request is filed with the claim, not after it, and states both conditions: inability to pay and a cause of action.
- Your affidavit covers assets, spouse, parents only if they support you, and six months of income, with documents.
- Your Legal Aid check names the reg. 5 subject, compares income and assets to the current thresholds, and states the merits in two sentences.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing the claim first, the exemption later | The registry may not open the file; delay | File the request at once with the affidavit | File both together (reg. 14(א)) |
| Affidavit without six months' income or without assets | Request refused | File a supplementary affidavit | Use the template's full list |
| Hiding savings or a car | Refusal; a false affidavit is an offence | Correct the affidavit | Declare everything; explain why it cannot be used |
| A claim that shows no cause of action | Exemption refused (reg. 14(ג)) | Fix the claim | Make the legal basis clear in two sentences |
| Applying to Legal Aid the week before a deadline | No time to appoint a lawyer | Ask for temporary service and an extension from the court | Apply the day you are served |
| Assuming "I earn too much" | Missing a subject with no means test | Check the gov.il list | Check the subject list first |
| Missing the 30-day appeal on a Legal Aid refusal | Final | — | Diarise it the day you get the refusal |

## Review

- A fee exemption needs two things: inability to pay and a claim that shows a cause of action; file it with the claim, with an affidavit on assets and six months of income (reg. 14).
- Income support, a limited-means declaration, insolvency or a recent exemption are prima facie proof; the request itself is free.
- Legal Aid has three tests: subject, means (67% of the average wage; assets up to three times it) and merits.
- Several subjects have no means test, including National Insurance, terror victims and whistleblowers.
- Apply early, ask for temporary service if urgent, and appeal a refusal within 30 days.
