# 11.4 · What Self-Represented Litigants Must Know from the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018

Above the small-claims ceiling, or when a company or amuta sues or is sued in the regular courts, the Civil Procedure Regulations 2018 (in force since 1.1.2021, amended many times since) run the case. You do not need all 180 regulations; you need about twenty. In this lesson you learn those rules — the pleadings, the deadlines, what silence admits, the fast track, mediation and costs — and draft the core of a statement of defence.

## Why it matters

Activists meet the regular civil courts most often as **defendants**: a defamation suit over a post (lesson 08.3), a claim by a developer against protesters, a landlord's claim. The regulations punish the passive. A fact you do not deny explicitly is admitted (reg. 14(ב)); a defence filed late can mean judgment against you without a hearing (reg. 130); skipping a mediation meeting costs money (reg. 37(י)). The same rules apply when you are the claimant. A party without a lawyer gets some patience, but not different rules.

## The law

> חובת בעלי הדין ובאי כוחם היא לסייע לבית המשפט בקיום המוטל עליו לפי תקנות אלה, וכן לנהוג בתום לב ובהגינות דיונית תוך שהם מסייעים במימוש התכלית הדיונית, ובכלל זה העמדת הפלוגתות האמיתיות שבמחלוקת בין בעלי הדין, מיקודן, בירורן והכרעה בהן.

*Gist:* every party must act in good faith and help the court focus on the real issues. ([reg. 3(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_3)) Reg. 4 forbids abuse of process, including steps out of proportion to the case.

> כתב הגנה יוגש בתוך שישים ימים ממועד המצאת כתב התביעה לידי הנתבע, …; בית המשפט רשאי להאריך את המועדים כאמור אם השתכנע כי קיימים טעמים המצדיקים זאת.

*Gist:* a defence is due 60 days after the claim is served on you; the court may extend for good reasons. ([reg. 9(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_9)) In the fast track (claims up to ₪75,000 and some others, reg. 78) it is 45 days (reg. 80(א)).

> כתב תביעה וכתב הגנה יכללו שלושה חלקים לפי הסדר המפורט להלן:
> (1) כותרת;
> (2) תמצית הטענות;
> (3) פירוט הטענות.

*Gist:* every claim and defence has three parts — heading, summary, details; in the Magistrates' Court the summary is up to two pages and the details up to nine (reg. 9(ד)). ([reg. 9(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_9))

> הנתבע ייחשב מודה בכל העובדות הכלולות בכתב התביעה, זולת אלה שהכחיש באופן מפורש ומפורט בחלקו השלישי של כתב ההגנה, ואולם לעניין שיעור דמי נזק יראו אותו כשנוי במחלוקת זולת אם הנתבע הודה בו במפורש.

*Gist:* every fact in the claim that you do not deny explicitly and in detail is treated as admitted — except the amount of damages. ([reg. 14(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_14))

> תובע יכלול בכתב תביעה את מלוא הסעד שלטענתו הוא זכאי לו בשל עילת התביעה ולא יוכל להגיש תביעה בשל החלק שלא תבע.

*Gist:* claim everything you are owed for one cause in one claim; you cannot sue later for the part you left out. ([reg. 25(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_25))

> בתוך שלושים ימים ממועד המצאת כתב הטענות האחרון ינהלו בעלי הדין, זולת בעל דין שאיננו מיוצג בידי עורך דין, דיון מקדמי, …

*Gist:* represented parties must hold a preliminary exchange within 30 days of the last pleading — but a party without a lawyer is exempt from this duty. ([reg. 35(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_35))

> לא יאוחר מארבעים וחמישה ימים לאחר שהוגש כתב הטענות האחרון, ימנה הממונה מגשר מהו״ת לצורך קיום פגישת מהו״ת בתובענה שסכומה או ששווי נושאה עולה על 40,000 שקלים חדשים, … בין אם בעל הדין באותה תובענה מיוצג בידי עורך דין ובין אם לאו;

*Gist:* in claims above ₪40,000 (not bodily injury or road accidents), the court appoints a mediator for a free information meeting (מהו״ת) — whether or not you have a lawyer; it takes place within 30 days and you must attend (reg. 37(ד), (ט)). ([reg. 37(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_37)) Not attending means paying the mediator's and the other side's costs of the meeting (reg. 37(י)).

> נתבע שלא הגיש כתב הגנה בתוך המועד שנקבע לכך או שכתב ההגנה נמחק, רשאי בית המשפט לדרוש מהתובע הוכחה מספקת של התביעה, כולה או מקצתה, או לתת פסק דין על יסוד כתב התביעה בלבד.

*Gist:* no defence in time can mean judgment on the claim alone. ([reg. 130](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_130)) A decision given in your absence may be set aside if you ask within 30 days of its service (reg. 131).

> סכום שכר טרחת עורך דין שיפסוק בית המשפט לטובת בעל הדין שזכה בהליך, … לא יפחת מן התעריף שנקבע לעניין שכר טרחת עורך דין בכללי לשכת עורכי הדין (התעריף המינימלי המומלץ), זולת אם הורה בית המשפט, מטעמים מיוחדים, על תשלום סכום נמוך יותר מהסכום האמור.

*Gist:* a winner with a lawyer is normally awarded at least the Bar's recommended minimum fee; the court weighs the result against the amount claimed, how the case was run and its complexity (reg. 153(ג)). ([reg. 153(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_153))

> תקופת פגרה של בית המשפט לא תובא במניין הימים שנקבעו בתקנות אלה או שנקבעו בידי בית המשפט, זולת אם בית המשפט הורה אחרת.

*Gist:* in the regular courts, recess days are not counted in deadlines (the opposite of small claims, lesson 11.2). ([reg. 179(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_179))

**What the regulations do not say.** We searched the current text for a general duty to send a pre-action letter before suing and found none (checked 13.10.2026). A demand letter is still wise: it can settle the case, and the court weighs how each side behaved when it sets costs (regs 151(א), 153(ג)). Some laws require a specific notice before suing; check the law behind your claim.

## How it works in practice

### The twenty rules on one page

| Stage | Rule | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Principles | regs 3–5 | Good faith, focus, proportionality; the court may sanction games |
| Which court | Courts Law s. 51; regs 7–8 | Magistrates' Court for money up to its ceiling; venue by the defendant's place, the place of the act and others |
| Claim | regs 9–11, 15 | Three parts; heading items in a fixed order, including the fee item or exemption (reg. 10(10)); essential documents attached |
| Everything at once | regs 24–25 | All causes from the same facts in one claim; all remedies, or ask to split |
| Defence | regs 9(ב), 12–14 | 60 days (fast track 45); deny each fact explicitly and in detail, or it is admitted |
| Filing | reg. 33 | The registry checks form (pages, heading); a refused document counts as not filed; seven days to fix |
| Preliminary exchange | reg. 35 | Not required of a party without a lawyer, but the court can still order steps |
| מהו״ת | reg. 37 | Free mediation information meeting above ₪40,000; attendance compulsory |
| Fast track | regs 78–80 | Up to ₪75,000: affidavit and documents with the pleading, one pre-trial within 90 days, trial within 6 months of the last defence |
| Pre-trial | reg. 61 | You may be ordered to attend in person; a video pre-trial needs your consent when you have no lawyer |
| Not attending | reg. 75 | Claimant absent: claim struck; defendant absent: claimant proves the case |
| No defence | regs 130–131 | Default judgment; 30 days to ask to set it aside |
| Affidavits | reg. 178 | First person, numbered, facts you know; it is evidence only if you come to be cross-examined |
| Counting days | reg. 179 | From service; recess not counted |
| Costs | regs 151–157 | Usually the loser pays; extra costs for wasting time (reg. 156); security for costs may be ordered (reg. 157) |
| Appeal | reg. 137 | 60 days from service of the judgment |

### The fee in the Magistrates' Court

For a money claim, 2.5% of the amount with a minimum, paid in two halves: one on filing and one up to 20 days before the first hearing (Court Fees Regulations reg. 6(א); Schedule item 1). If the case ends early by agreement, part is refunded (reg. 6(ב); item 34). Amounts on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md); exemptions in lesson 11.6.

### Writing a defence that works

1. **Heading** (reg. 12): court, case number, the parties, your address and contacts, and the last date for filing.
2. **Summary** (reg. 13): preliminary arguments (for example, limitation or wrong court — raise them here, reg. 28), a short summary of your defence, and your position on the remedy.
3. **Details** (reg. 14): go through the claim paragraph by paragraph. For each: "admitted", "denied — the true facts are…", or "not known to the defendant and denied". Never "denied in general".
4. **Annexes** (reg. 15): essential documents.
5. **Affidavit** (fast track, reg. 79(א)(1)): verify the facts — template [affidavit.md](../../templates/affidavit.md), lesson 02.6.

### Costs: what raises your exposure

- Losing — costs usually follow the result (reg. 152).
- Wasting time: needless requests, late filings, arguments without basis (reg. 156).
- Not complying with the preliminary-stage rules (reg. 38(ב)).
- A claim far larger than what you win (reg. 153(ג)).

What lowers it: a fair settlement offer in writing, short and focused pleadings, keeping every deadline.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Fadi, a freelance journalist from Nazareth who reports on crime in Arab towns, posted that a named contractor "builds with stolen municipal funds". The contractor sues him in the Nazareth Magistrates' Court for ₪150,000 for defamation. The claim is served on 10 March. Fadi has no lawyer.

1. **Deadline.** ₪150,000 is above the fast-track limit, so the regular track applies: 60 days from 10 March (reg. 9(ב)). The Passover recess falls inside the period and is not counted (reg. 179(ב)); he asks the registry for the recess dates and plans to file well before the end.
2. **Help first.** He checks Legal Aid (lesson 11.6) — defamation defences are a money matter — and an anti-SLAPP clinic (lesson 08.3, [Where to Get Help](../../references/where-to-get-help.md)).
3. **Defence structure.** Summary: truth and public interest (Defamation Law s. 14) and good faith (s. 15), lesson 08.2; the amount is excessive.
4. **Details.** He answers all 23 paragraphs of the claim: 1–4 admitted (he is a journalist; he wrote the post); 5 denied — "the post referred to a State Comptroller report, attached as Appendix 1"; 6–23 each answered. He admits no damages amount.
5. **מהו״ת.** The claim is above ₪40,000, so a mediator is appointed within 45 days after his defence. He attends; the parties agree to mediation; the case ends with a clarification post and no payment.
6. **Costs.** Because it ended by agreement before evidence, each side bears its own costs and the contractor gets part of his fee refunded.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- The fictional claim below, or a real claim against you or by you.
- The template [affidavit.md](../../templates/affidavit.md) if you choose the fast-track version.
- 60 minutes.

Fictional claim (Magistrates' Court, ₪62,000): a property developer sues you and two others for "damage to the project" after you organised a protest at the site of a planned tower near a nature reserve. The claim says: (1) you organised the protest on 4 May; (2) protesters blocked the gate for six hours; (3) you personally damaged a fence; (4) the developer lost ₪62,000 of work. You organised a lawful protest with a police licence, nobody blocked the gate, and you never touched the fence; you have the police licence and a video of the event.

### Steps

1. Decide the track (regular or fast) and why; write the defence deadline if the claim was served on 1 June and a recess ran from 1 to 31 July (check your answer with reg. 179).
2. List what you must attach in this track (reg. 79).
3. Write the summary part of the defence: preliminary arguments (if any), the defence in three sentences, your position on the amount.
4. Write the detailed part for paragraphs (1)–(4): admitted / denied with the true facts / not known and denied.
5. Write whether there will be a מהו״ת meeting and what happens if you skip it.
6. List two things you will do to keep your costs exposure low.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is a dry run. If you are really sued, get help early (lesson 11.6, lesson 08.3) and act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### How you know it worked

- You chose the fast track (₪62,000 is under the fast-track limit), so the defence is due in 45 days, recess days not counted, with an affidavit and a documents list attached.
- Every paragraph of the claim has an explicit answer; nothing is "denied in general"; you did not admit the amount.
- You know the claim is above ₪40,000, so a מהו״ת meeting is expected and attendance is compulsory.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defence filed late | Default judgment (reg. 130) | Ask to set it aside within 30 days, with your defence and the reason (reg. 131) | Diarise day 1 the date of service; file early |
| "The defendant denies all the claims" | Every fact not explicitly denied is admitted (reg. 14(ב)) | Ask to amend the defence (reg. 46) | Answer paragraph by paragraph |
| Splitting a claim to stay under a ceiling | The part not claimed is lost (reg. 25) | Claim the whole in the right court | Add up all losses from the same facts |
| Skipping the מהו״ת meeting | Costs of the mediator and the other side (reg. 37(י)) | Pay, then attend the next step | Put the date in your calendar when notified |
| Counting recess days in the regular court, or skipping them in small claims | Filing too early is harmless; too late is not | — | Check reg. 179(ב) or small-claims reg. 16א(ב) |
| Filing request after request | Costs for wasting time (reg. 156) | Withdraw needless requests | One focused request, when needed |
| Affidavit, then not coming to cross-examination | The affidavit is not evidence (reg. 178(ג)) | Ask the court's permission with special reasons | Come to every evidence hearing |

## Review

- In the regular courts the 2018 regulations govern; good faith and focus are duties, not slogans (regs 3–4).
- Defence: 60 days, or 45 in the fast track (up to ₪75,000); recess days not counted.
- Three-part pleadings; every fact you do not deny explicitly and in detail is admitted (reg. 14(ב)).
- No general pre-action letter duty exists in the regulations, but behaviour affects costs; a free, compulsory מהו״ת meeting applies above ₪40,000.
- The loser usually pays costs; wasting time adds more (regs 151–157).
