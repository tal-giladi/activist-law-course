# 11.5 · Evidence and the Day of the Hearing

Cases are won on evidence, not on how strongly you feel. In this lesson you turn your claim into an evidence plan — one proof for every fact — prepare your witnesses, and rehearse the hearing itself, where a small-claims judge asks the questions and expects short, factual answers. You finish with a hearing checklist you can take to court.

## Why it matters

The Small Claims Court is flexible about evidence: it may accept what other courts would reject. That flexibility tempts people to arrive with a story and a phone full of unsorted screenshots. The judge has one hearing, often 20–30 minutes, to decide. The side whose evidence is sorted, labelled and matched to each disputed fact usually wins. In the regular courts the rules are stricter: affidavits replace your oral story, and you must come to be cross-examined.

## The law

> בית משפט לתביעות קטנות יהיה רשאי לקבל ראיה אף אם לא היתה קבילה בבית משפט אחר; אין בהוראה זו כדי לגרוע מדיני חסינות עדים וראיות חסויות.

*Gist:* the Small Claims Court may accept evidence another court would not, but privileges still apply. ([Courts Law s. 62(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_62))

> בית המשפט יקבע את סדר הבאת ראיות וטענות הצדדים בדרך שנראית לו ויברר את המחלוקת באופן פעיל.

*Gist:* the judge decides the order of the hearing and actively investigates the dispute. ([Small Claims Regulations, reg. 13(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_13)) Since 11.4.2025 the court may also hold the hearing by video or decide on the papers when that is fair (reg. 13(ב)–(ג)).

> בתום הדיון בתביעה יתן בית המשפט את פסק הדין, אולם הוא יהיה רשאי לדחות את מתן פסק הדין לתקופה שלא תעלה על 7 ימים.

*Gist:* judgment comes at the end of the hearing or within seven days, with brief reasons and how to challenge it. ([reg. 15](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_15))

> לא יצלם אדם באולם בית משפט ולא יפרסם תצלום כזה אלא ברשות בית המשפט.

*Gist:* no photographing in the courtroom without the court's permission. ([Courts Law s. 70(ב)(1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_70)) Hearings are public unless the court orders otherwise (s. 68(א)).

> דין מצהיר בתצהיר חלף עדות ראשית כדין עד במשפט, ואולם התצהיר ייחשב כראיה רק אם המצהיר התייצב לחקירה נגדית.

*Gist:* in the regular courts an affidavit replaces direct testimony, but it counts as evidence only if the person comes to be cross-examined. ([Civil Procedure Regulations, reg. 178(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_סדר_הדין_האזרחי#סעיף_178))

## How it works in practice

### The evidence plan

Take your statement of claim (lesson 11.2) and make a table. One row per fact the other side may dispute:

| # | Fact | Proof | Original kept where | Exhibit | Weak spot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | We agreed delivery by 31 May | WhatsApp message, 20 May | Phone + exported chat | 1 | — |
| 2 | Goods arrived 5 June | Delivery note | Paper | 2 | Not signed by me |

Rules of thumb:

- **Documents beat memories.** Receipts, contracts, emails, messages with dates.
- **Originals.** Bring the original paper or the phone with the message; the copy is in the file.
- **Photos and video.** Note when and by whom they were taken; bring a laptop to show video (the court may not have a screen). In small claims, audio and video go to the registry on USB drives with a transcript (gov.il).
- **Recordings.** You may record a conversation you take part in; recording others' conversations is a crime. Lesson 09.5 has the rules.
- **Your own numbers.** Show how you calculated each shekel: quote, invoice, receipt.
- **Expert opinion.** For a technical fault, a short written opinion or a repair quote from a professional helps; the court may accept it in small claims.

### Witnesses

- In small claims you invite your witnesses yourself (gov.il).
- If someone may not come, ask the court to summon them **at least 60 days before the hearing**, with their address, phone and why their evidence is needed; you deposit a witness fee the court sets (gov.il; reg. 9 gives the court the power to pay witnesses).
- Prepare, do not script. Tell the witness what the hearing is about, ask them to bring any document they have, and to say "I don't know" when they don't. Coaching a witness to say things they did not see destroys credibility and can be an offence.
- In the regular courts, witnesses usually give affidavits in advance and come for cross-examination (reg. 178).

### The day of the hearing

1. **Arrive early** with ID. There is a security check; give yourself 30 minutes.
2. **Find the courtroom** on the screens or at the registry. Several cases are listed at the same time; wait to be called.
3. **Turn off your phone.** Do not film or photograph in the courtroom (s. 70(ב)).
4. **Address the judge** as "כבוד השופט" / "כבודה". Stand when the judge enters if others do.
5. **Opening (2 minutes).** What happened, what you want, why the law gives it to you. The judge has read the file.
6. **Answer the question asked.** The judge leads (reg. 13(א)). Short, factual answers; point to the exhibit number.
7. **Questions to the other side.** If the judge lets you, ask short questions that get yes/no answers: "On 20 May you wrote that delivery would be by 31 May — correct?" Do not argue with the answers.
8. **Closing (1 minute).** The amount, line by line, and any order you ask for. Ask for your expenses.
9. **The record.** The judge dictates a summary to the protocol (reg. 14). If something important is missing, ask politely that it be recorded.
10. **Settlement offers.** Judges often suggest a compromise. You may ask for a few minutes to think. A settlement can be given the force of a judgment.

### If you cannot come

Ask in writing to move the hearing as early as possible (a new request in Net HaMishpat, lesson 11.3), with the reason and proof. Not coming means your claim is dismissed if you are the claimant, or judgment on the claim if you are the defendant (reg. 11; in the regular courts, reg. 75).

### Language and accessibility

Claims are filed in Hebrew (the judiciary's page; Kol Zchut also mentions Arabic for paper filing — check with *3852). Ask for a court interpreter in writing at least 7 days before the hearing. The judiciary publishes accessibility arrangements; ask for what you need in advance.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Tamar, a retired teacher from Kfar Saba who protests weekly against the government, was filming a counter-demonstration when a man grabbed her phone, threw it on the ground and tore her sign. Her friend Dalia filmed it. Tamar filed a police complaint (lesson 06.4) and a small claim for ₪3,900: the phone repair quote (₪2,950), the sign (₪150) and ₪800 for distress. The police file gave the man's name; she found his address through the Population Authority's paid address service.

1. **Evidence plan.**

| # | Fact | Proof | Exhibit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The defendant grabbed and threw the phone | Dalia's video (USB + transcript), Dalia as witness | 1 |
| 2 | The phone was damaged | Lab repair quote; the broken phone itself | 2 |
| 3 | The sign was destroyed | Photo; receipt from the print shop | 3 |
| 4 | Identity of the defendant | Police complaint confirmation; a frame from the video | 4 |

2. **Witness.** Dalia agrees to come. Tamar sends her the date, asks her to bring the original video on her phone and to describe only what she saw.
3. **Hearing.** The defendant says Tamar "provoked him by filming". The judge asks Tamar three questions; she answers each in one sentence and points to exhibit 1. Dalia confirms the video is hers and unedited. Tamar asks the defendant one question: "Is that you in the video at 00:14?" He says yes.
4. **Closing.** ₪2,950 + ₪150 + distress at the court's discretion, plus expenses. She does not insult him and does not talk politics.
5. **Judgment** within a week: ₪3,100 plus ₪500 for distress and expenses. Filming in a public place is not provocation that justifies damage.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your draft claim from lesson 11.2.
- The template [hearing-checklist.md](../../templates/hearing-checklist.md).
- 60 minutes, and a friend for 15 minutes of role play.

### Steps

1. List every fact in your claim that the other side may dispute.
2. Fill the evidence plan table: proof, where the original is, exhibit number, weak spot.
3. For each weak spot, write how you will fix it (a document to request, a witness, a photo).
4. List your witnesses; decide for each whether to invite them or ask the court to summon them, and the latest date for the request.
5. Write a two-minute opening and a one-minute closing with the amount line by line.
6. Role play: your friend plays the judge and asks five hard questions. Answer each in one or two sentences, pointing to an exhibit.
7. Fill the hearing checklist template.

### Template

- [hearing-checklist.md](../../templates/hearing-checklist.md) — רשימת הכנה לדיון: evidence plan, witnesses, what to bring, opening and closing notes, after the hearing.

### How you know it worked

- Every disputed fact has at least one proof, and every proof has an exhibit number.
- Your opening fits in two minutes and names the amount.
- In the role play you answered the question asked, not a different one.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| A phone full of unsorted screenshots | The judge cannot see what proves what | Ask for a short break to find the item | Number exhibits; one table per fact |
| Key witness does not come | The fact may stay unproven | Ask to hear the witness later, with reasons | Invite early; request a summons 60 days ahead |
| Video only on your phone | Cannot be shown or kept in the file | Ask to file it after the hearing | USB copies to the registry, transcript, laptop |
| Recording people talking among themselves | Inadmissible and possibly a crime | Do not use it; get advice | Record only conversations you take part in (09.5) |
| Arguing politics or insulting the other side | Lost credibility | Apologise and return to the facts | Rehearse a calm, factual opening |
| Filming the hearing | Offence; removal from the courtroom | Stop and delete if ordered | Phone off in the courtroom |
| Missing the hearing | Claim dismissed (reg. 11) | Ask to set aside within 30 days (reg. 12) | Request a new date in advance |

## Review

- Small-claims judges may accept evidence other courts would not, and they lead the hearing (s. 62; reg. 13).
- One proof per disputed fact, originals in hand, exhibits numbered; video on USB with a transcript and a laptop.
- Invite your witnesses; ask for a court summons at least 60 days ahead.
- On the day: early, phone off, no filming, short answers, a two-minute opening and a one-minute closing.
- Judgment comes at the end or within seven days (reg. 15); absence loses the case for the absent side.
