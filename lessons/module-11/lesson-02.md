# 11.2 · Small Claims Court Step by Step

The Small Claims Court is built for people without lawyers: one short statement of claim, a fee of about 1%, one hearing, a judge who asks the questions, and a judgment within days. In this lesson you follow a claim from the demand letter to the request for leave to appeal, using the procedure rules as amended on 11 April 2025, and you draft your own statement of claim.

## Why it matters

Lesson 09.2 showed that a discrimination claim can be won in the Small Claims Court; this lesson shows how to actually run one, or any other small claim — a refund, a damaged sign, a deposit, spam. Most claimants who lose here lose on procedure: the wrong court, a defendant who was never served, documents left at home, a missed hearing, an appeal filed late. All of these are avoidable with the checklist below.

## The law

> תביעה תוגש לבית המשפט שאזור שיפוטו הוא אחד המקומות המנויים להלן, והוא על אף האמור בכל הסכם שבין בעלי הדין; ואלה הם:
> (1) מקום מגוריו או מקום עסקו של הנתבע;
> (5) מקום המעשה או המחדל שבשלו תובעים;

*Gist:* file where the defendant lives or does business, or where the act or omission happened — whatever a contract says. ([Small Claims Regulations, reg. 2(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_2))

> על אף האמור בתקנת משנה (א), תובענה בשל פרסום או סחר ברשת האינטרנט תוגש לבית המשפט שבאזור שיפוטו מצוי מקום מגוריו או מקום עסקו של הנתבע או מקום מגוריו או מקום עסקו של התובע, בלבד.

*Gist:* for an online publication or online purchase, you may also file where *you* live or do business. ([reg. 2(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_2))

> בגוף כתב התביעה יפרט התובע את כל העניינים האלה:
> (1) מקום המעשה או המחדל שבשלו תובעים;
> (2) הסעד המבוקש;
> (3) נימוקי התביעה;
> (4) רשימת המסמכים שעליהם הוא מבקש להסתמך בכתב התביעה;
> (5) רשימת העדים שבכוונתו להעיד לתמיכה בתביעה;
> (6) אם הוא מעדיף לקיים את הדיון באמצעי טכנולוגי כאמור בתקנה 13(ב), וכן אם הוא מעדיף הכרעה על יסוד הכתב כאמור בתקנה 13(ג);
> (7) מספר התביעות הקטנות שהגיש במהלך השנה האחרונה.

*Gist:* since 11.4.2025 the body of the claim must state the place of the act, the remedy, the reasons, the documents, the witnesses, whether you prefer a video hearing or a decision on the papers, and how many small claims you filed in the last year. ([reg. 3(ג)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_3)) The heading must include your ID, address, email and mobile number, and the defendant's details and company number (reg. 3(ב)); you attach your documents and a declaration that the claim is true, naming the source of what you know (reg. 3(ד)); and the body may not exceed five pages (reg. 3(ה)).

> בהזמנה לדין יוזמן הנתבע להגיש כתב הגנה תוך 30 יום מיום המצאת ההזמנה, ולהתייצב למשפט במועד שנקוב בהזמנה – אם הגיש כתב הגנה כאמור.

*Gist:* the defendant has 30 days from service to file a defence. ([reg. 4(ב)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_4)) If no defence is filed, the court gives judgment on the claim alone, or invites both sides anyway if justice requires (reg. 10).

> לא הומצא כתב התביעה עד 60 ימים לפני הדיון בידי פקיד בית המשפט כאמור בתקנת משנה (ב), ימציא התובע או מי מטעמו את כתב התביעה לנמענים 40 ימים לפחות לפני הדיון.

*Gist:* the court serves the claim, but if it has not been served 60 days before the hearing, you must serve it yourself at least 40 days before. ([reg. 7(ב1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_7))

> נמסרה על הדיון הודעה כראוי ואיש מבעלי הדין לא התייצב – תימחק התביעה; התייצב התובע והנתבע לא התייצב – יתן בית המשפט פסק דין על יסוד כתב התביעה, ובלבד שהתובע הצהיר בפני בית המשפט על אמיתות האמור בכתב תביעתו; התייצב הנתבע והתובע לא התייצב – תידחה התביעה.

*Gist:* if you do not come, your claim is dismissed; if the defendant does not come, you get judgment after declaring your claim true. ([reg. 11](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_11)) A party judged in their absence may ask to set it aside within 30 days of service (reg. 12).

> המועד להגשת בקשה לרשות ערעור על פסק דינו של בית המשפט או על החלטתו לפי תקנה 12 לגבי בקשה לבטל החלטה שלא בפני בעל דין, הוא 30 ימים מיום מתן פסק הדין או ההחלטה.

*Gist:* for claims opened from 11.4.2025, a request for leave to appeal is due within 30 days of the judgment (it was 15 days before). ([reg. 16(א)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_שיפוט_בתביעות_קטנות_(סדרי_דין)#סעיף_16)) The request goes to a single District Court judge, who must first grant leave ([Courts Law s. 64](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_64)). Court recess periods **are counted** in small-claims deadlines unless the court orders otherwise (reg. 16א(ב)) — unlike the regular courts (lesson 11.4).

> ייצוג בעל דין בבית משפט לתביעות קטנות על ידי עורך דין יהיה רק ברשות בית המשפט ומטעמים מיוחדים שיירשמו;

*Gist:* lawyers appear only with the court's permission for special reasons; a non-lawyer may represent you with the court's approval only if they do not do it regularly, as a business or for payment. ([Courts Law s. 63](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_בתי_המשפט#סעיף_63))

The regulations were checked on Hebrew Wikisource on 13 October 2026; the last amendment shown is from 2025 (ק״ת תשפ״ה, 1168).

## How it works in practice

### The timeline

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Demand letter, 14 days] --> B[File the claim, pay ~1%]
  B --> C[Court serves the defendant]
  C --> D[Defence within 30 days]
  D --> E[One hearing]
  E --> F[Judgment, usually within 7 days]
  F --> G[Leave to appeal: 30 days]
```

### Before filing

1. **Demand letter.** There is no rule requiring one, but the judiciary's own guide asks you to describe your attempts to reach agreement, and a written refusal is often your best evidence. Use [pre-action-demand-letter.md](../../templates/pre-action-demand-letter.md) (for discrimination, [discrimination-demand-letter.md](../../templates/discrimination-demand-letter.md)). Send by registered mail and email.
2. **Defendant details.** A company: name and number from the Companies Registrar (free). An amuta: GuideStar. A self-employed person: usually the number on the receipt. A private person: an address search at the Population Authority costs a fee. You may file without the defendant's ID number, but without it you cannot open an Execution Office file if they do not pay (gov.il).
3. **Venue.** Reg. 2: the defendant's home or business, or where it happened; for online purchases and publications, also your own home. With several defendants, any one of them sets the venue (reg. 2(ג)). The judiciary publishes a spreadsheet of towns and courts on the filing page.
4. **Amount.** Add up what you lost and can prove. Do not round up to the ceiling. The fee is 1% of the claim, at least the minimum, rounded up to a multiple of ₪5 (reg. 6(א)); amounts on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md).

### Writing the claim

Use [small-claim-statement.md](../../templates/small-claim-statement.md). It follows reg. 3:

- **Heading:** you (name, ID, address, email, mobile), the defendant (name, ID or company number, address, phone and email if known), the remedy.
- **Body, five pages or less:** where it happened; the facts in date order, numbered, each pointing to a document; why the defendant is liable (the rule, in one or two sentences); how you calculated the amount; your documents; your witnesses; whether you prefer a video hearing or a decision on the papers; how many small claims you filed in the last year.
- **Declaration:** that everything is true or to the best of your knowledge, naming the source.
- **Attachments:** everything you rely on. Adding documents later needs the court's permission (reg. 3(ד)).

The judiciary's guide lists what to write and attach for common claims — products and services, cars, spam, flights, rent, discrimination, bodily injury (see Learn more).

### Filing and paying

Online through the judiciary's small-claims service (from a computer, with National Digital Identity; lesson 11.3), or on paper at the court registry in three copies plus one per extra defendant. Online, attachments are PDFs (up to 70 MB per the judiciary's page) and the fee is paid by credit card; audio and video go on USB drives to the registry with a transcript. Up to five claimants and five defendants online. If you cannot pay, see lesson 11.6. Volunteer stations in some courts help with the form for free.

### After filing

| What | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| Summons and claim sent to the defendant | Court, by registered mail | Soon after filing |
| Check that the defendant was served | You, in Net HaMishpat or at *3852 | At least 60 days before the hearing |
| If not served: serve it yourself | You | At least 40 days before the hearing (reg. 7(ב1)) |
| Defence (and any counterclaim) | Defendant | 30 days from service (reg. 4(ב), 5) |
| Your defence to a counterclaim | You | 30 days from its service |
| Ask the court to summon a reluctant witness | You, with a deposit for the witness's fee | At least 60 days before the hearing (gov.il) |
| Interpreter request | You | At least 7 days before the hearing (gov.il) |
| Hearing | Both sides | One hearing; the judge leads (reg. 13; lesson 11.5) |
| Judgment | Court | At the end, or within 7 days, briefly reasoned (reg. 15) |
| Leave to appeal | Either side | 30 days, to the District Court (reg. 16; s. 64) |

### Mediation and settlement

The court may invite you to mediation, and with both sides' consent the judge may act as an arbitrator (Courts Law s. 65). A settlement can be given the force of a judgment — read it carefully before you sign.

### Appealing

There is no automatic appeal. The request for leave to appeal (בר״ע) goes to the District Court with a fee (Schedule item 13; amount on [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md)). Leave is granted rarely, mainly for a clear legal error or a serious procedural flaw — not because you disagree with how the judge weighed the evidence. File it through Net HaMishpat (lesson 11.3).

## Worked example

**Situation.** Menachem, a Haredi activist from Bnei Brak who organises Torah-study evenings for at-risk youth, ordered a portable sound system online for ₪3,450 from a company registered in Haifa. It arrived with a broken amplifier. Over three weeks he sent four emails; the company answered once: "Not covered."

1. **Demand letter.** On 1 March he emails and posts a letter: the order, the defect, his four emails, a demand to replace the system or refund ₪3,450 within 14 days, and the next step. No reply.
2. **Venue.** It was an online purchase, so reg. 2(ב) lets him file in his own area — the court that serves Bnei Brak — instead of Haifa.
3. **Defendant.** He finds the company's name and number on the Companies Registrar and copies the registered address.
4. **Amount.** ₪3,450 for the system, plus ₪350 he paid to rent a replacement for two evenings (receipts). Total ₪3,800. Fee: 1% is ₪38, so the minimum fee applies.
5. **Claim.** Three pages: facts 1–9 in date order, each with an exhibit (order, delivery note, photos and a short video of the defect, the four emails, the reply, the demand letter, the rental receipts). He states no witnesses, no preference for video, and "0" claims in the last year.
6. **After filing.** Sixty-five days before the hearing he checks Net HaMishpat: the defendant was served. The company files a defence blaming "misuse".
7. **Hearing and judgment.** He brings the device and a laptop with the video. Judgment: refund of ₪3,450 against return of the device, ₪350, and modest expenses.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- Your dispute from lesson 11.1 (real or fictional).
- The templates [pre-action-demand-letter.md](../../templates/pre-action-demand-letter.md) and [small-claim-statement.md](../../templates/small-claim-statement.md).
- 90 minutes.

### Steps

1. Fill and send (or dry-run) the demand letter: facts, demand, 14-day deadline, next step.
2. Find the defendant's legal name and number; write the address you will use for service.
3. Choose the court under reg. 2 and write the reason in one line.
4. Calculate the claim amount line by line, and the fee.
5. Draft the statement of claim: heading (reg. 3(ב)), body (reg. 3(ג), seven items), declaration (reg. 3(ד)(2)), exhibits.
6. Count the pages of the body: five or fewer.
7. Write your timeline with real dates: filing, the 60-day service check, the 40-day self-service date, the 30-day appeal date after a judgment.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In this lesson the claim is a dry run. If you file a real claim: act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Template

- [pre-action-demand-letter.md](../../templates/pre-action-demand-letter.md) — מכתב דרישה לפני הגשת תביעה.
- [small-claim-statement.md](../../templates/small-claim-statement.md) — כתב תביעה קטנה, with a reg. 3 checklist.

### How you know it worked

- Every item in reg. 3(ב)–(ה) is in your draft and you can point to it.
- Each fact has an exhibit number; the exhibits list matches.
- Your court choice cites reg. 2(א) or 2(ב).
- Your calendar has the 60-day check, the 40-day service date and the 30-day appeal date.

### Self-check

- [ ] I sent, or drafted, a demand letter and kept proof of sending.
- [ ] The defendant is named by its legal name and number.
- [ ] The body of my claim is five pages or less.
- [ ] I stated my video / papers preference and my number of claims this year.
- [ ] I know I lose automatically if I do not come to the hearing.
- [ ] I know the leave-to-appeal deadline and that recess days count.

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filing in the wrong area | Transfer and delay | Ask to transfer, or file again | Check reg. 2 and the court spreadsheet |
| Defendant never served | No hearing; the claim may be struck | Serve it yourself and file proof | Check 60 days before; serve by day 40 |
| Documents left out of the claim | Need permission to add them | Ask the court with a reason | Attach everything on filing |
| A ten-page story | Rejected or ignored; reg. 3(ה) limit is five pages | Cut to facts, exhibits, amount | Numbered facts, one line each |
| Not coming to the hearing | Claim dismissed (reg. 11) | Ask to set aside within 30 days (reg. 12) with a reason | Ask in advance to move the date if you cannot come |
| Appeal "because the judge was wrong about the facts" | Leave refused | — | Appeal only clear legal or procedural errors |
| Counting 30 days but skipping the recess | Late request | Ask for an extension at once | Recess days count in small claims (reg. 16א(ב)) |

## Review

- File where the defendant lives or does business, or where it happened; online purchases and publications also allow your own area (reg. 2).
- Since 11.4.2025 the claim follows reg. 3: full heading, a five-page body with seven items, a truth declaration and all documents.
- The court serves the claim, but check by day 60 before the hearing and serve yourself by day 40 if needed.
- The defendant has 30 days to defend; not coming to the hearing loses the case for the absent side.
- Leave to appeal: 30 days, District Court, granted rarely; recess days count.
