# 07.5 · After the Case: Closed Files, Criminal and Police Records

By the end of this lesson you will know what stays on record after a protest file is closed or decided, how to find the ground on which your file was closed and ask the prosecutor to change it, when and how to ask the police to cancel a closed-file record, and how to view your own record — and you will draft both requests.

> [!WARNING]
> If your file is still open, or a request might reopen questions about the facts, talk to a lawyer or the Public Defender before you write to the prosecutor. A request to change closure grounds is your own reasoned argument; it can include facts that the prosecution did not have. This lesson teaches the procedure, not your defence. It is not legal advice.

## Why it matters

Most activists' files never reach a courtroom: they are closed. But "closed" does not mean "gone". A file closed for insufficient evidence stays on the police register for years and can be disclosed to bodies such as security agencies, some licensing authorities and employers in sensitive sectors. For some people that matters little; for a teacher, a security-cleared worker, a gun-licence holder, a lawyer-to-be or someone applying for a visa, it can matter a lot.

The law gives you three tools: ask the prosecutor to change the ground to "lack of guilt", which removes the record; ask the police to cancel a closed-file record after a waiting period; and view your own record to know what is there. Few people use them, because few people know they exist.

## The law

> (4) החשוד יהיה רשאי לפנות לתובע שסגר את התיק בבקשה מנומקת לשנות את עילת הסגירה; תיק שנסגר בשל חוסר אשמה, יוסר רישומו מהמרשם המשטרתי כהגדרתו בחוק המידע הפלילי ותקנת השבים, התשע״ט–2019.

*Gist:* a suspect may send the prosecutor who closed the file a reasoned request to change the closure ground; a file closed for lack of guilt is removed from the police register. ([חוק סדר הדין הפלילי, s. 62(ב)(4)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_62))

> (1) על החלטה שלא להעמיד לדין תימסר לחשוד הודעה בכתב בלא ציון עילת סגירת התיק, ואולם יצוין בה כי החשוד רשאי לברר את עילת סגירת התיק באמצעות הגעה לכל תחנת משטרה …

*Gist:* the closure notice you receive does not state the ground, but tells you that you may find it out at any police station or by the other ways listed in the notice. ([s. 62(ב)(1)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_סדר_הדין_הפלילי#סעיף_62))

> (2) מידע על תיק שנסגר בשל אחד מאלה (להלן – תיק סגור): … (ב) החלטה שלא להעמיד לדין כאמור בסעיף 62 לחוק סדר הדין הפלילי, אלא אם כן ההחלטה ניתנה בשל חוסר אשמה;

*Gist:* the police register holds closed files of felonies and misdemeanours, except those closed for lack of guilt. ([חוק המידע הפלילי ותקנת השבים, s. 27(א)(2)](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים#סעיף_27))

> (ג) … פרט רישום משטרתי שעניינו תיק סגור, בעבירה שאינה פשע, יבוטל בתום שבע שנים מיום האירוע, ולעניין קטינים – בתום חמש שנים מיום האירוע, אלא אם כן נקבע אחרת באמות המידה …

*Gist:* a closed-file record of a non-felony is cancelled automatically after seven years from the event (five for minors), unless the criteria say otherwise; earlier cancellation is by request (s. 33(א)). ([s. 33](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים#סעיף_33))

> (א) כל אדם זכאי לעיין במידע הפלילי על עצמו.

*Gist:* everyone may view his own criminal information; it is shown on a computer screen at a police station, you may take notes but not copy or photograph it (s. 4(ג)), and a lawyer with a special power of attorney may get a printout (s. 4(ד)). ([s. 4](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים#סעיף_4))

> (א) אדם אינו חייב לגלות מידע פלילי על עצמו, שהוא פרט רישום שהתיישן או שנמחק, תיק סגור או תיק שעוכבו בו ההליכים, למי שאינו זכאי לקבל את המידע לפי חוק זה.

*Gist:* you need not tell anyone who is not entitled to the information about a closed file, a stayed case, or an expired or deleted conviction, and you bear no liability for not telling. ([s. 37](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המידע_הפלילי_ותקנת_השבים#סעיף_37))

## How it works in practice

### Which law?

The Criminal Information and Rehabilitation of Offenders Law, 2019, in force since 12 July 2022 (s. 58), replaced the Criminal Register and Rehabilitation of Offenders Law, 1981 (s. 56). Older guides, letters and forms still say "חוק המרשם הפלילי"; some 1981-era regulations stay in force under s. 57, including the 2009 criteria for cancelling police records.

### What is on which register

| Outcome of the file | Criminal register | Police register |
|---|---|---|
| Closed for lack of guilt | No | No (removed) |
| Closed for insufficient evidence | No | Yes, until cancelled |
| Closed because the circumstances as a whole do not suit prosecution | No | Yes, until cancelled |
| Closed by a conditional arrangement (הסדר מותנה, s. 67א) | No | Yes (s. 27(א)(2)(ג)) |
| Acquitted | No | Recorded, but its disclosure is banned except narrow security uses (s. 32) |
| Convicted, or a probation or community-service order without conviction | Yes, with limitation and deletion periods (ss. 19, 22) | — |
| Fine offence paid (e.g. some graffiti) | Not included (s. 8(ג)) | — |

### Step 1: find out the ground

The closure letter does not state it (s. 62(ב)(1)). Go to any police station with your ID, or use the method listed in the letter, and ask for the closure ground (עילת סגירה). Or view your full record (step 4). Write down the file number (פל"א), the date, the closing body (police prosecution unit or a State Attorney district office) and the ground.

### Step 2: decide whether to ask for a change

Ask yourself, ideally with a lawyer:

- Does the evidence actually show you did not do what was suspected — a video, witnesses, a timeline? "Lack of guilt" means the prosecutor is satisfied you did not commit the offence, not that the evidence was weak.
- Do you know what is in the file? You may ask the prosecution to let you view or copy the investigation material of a closed file; it is a discretionary decision (Kol Zchut). Seeing the file first makes your request stronger.
- Could your request raise facts that hurt you or others? If yes, stop and get a lawyer.

### Step 3: write the request

Address it to the prosecutor who closed the file (s. 62(ב)(4)): for most protest misdemeanours, the police prosecution unit (יחידת התביעות) of the district; for files the State Attorney handled, the district attorney's office. Use [closure-grounds-request.md](../../templates/closure-grounds-request.md). A good request:

1. identifies the file (number, station, date of the event, date of closure);
2. states the current ground and the ground you ask for;
3. tells the facts briefly, in numbered paragraphs, in the first person;
4. points to the evidence that shows you did not commit the offence, and attaches it or asks the prosecutor to look at specific items in the file;
5. explains why the remaining ground hurts you (work, licence, studies) — useful but not required;
6. asks for a written answer.

There is no statutory deadline for this request, and no statutory deadline for the answer. Follow up after about 45 days with a short reminder (lesson 02.5).

### Step 4: view your own record

At any police station, during the hours the police publish, with your ID (biometric ID first; otherwise the other IDs listed in the viewing regulations, reg. 6). The record is shown on a screen; a police officer may be present; you may be asked to deposit your phone; you may take notes (regs 2(ב)–(ה)). Copy: every entry, its file number, date, offence and status or ground. Use the request form in [police-records-request.md](../../templates/police-records-request.md) to make sure you ask about both registers.

### Step 5: ask to cancel a closed-file record

If the ground cannot be changed, or after the change was refused, a closed-file record can be cancelled:

| Who | Felony (פשע) | Misdemeanour (עוון) |
|---|---|---|
| Adult, on request | After 7 years from the event | After 5 years from the event |
| Minor at the time, on request | After 5 years | After 3 years |
| Automatic cancellation (non-felony) | — | 7 years for adults, 5 for minors, unless the criteria say otherwise |

The waiting periods are from the 2009 criteria regulations and the Israel Police's page (updated 4 October 2026); see also [Current Numbers](../../references/current-numbers.md). The police weigh the seriousness of the offence, the harm, other records, earlier cancellations and your personal circumstances (reg. 5), and expect no open files. Attach documents: service, studies or work letters, recommendations, and a short note on the circumstances. A refusal can be re-submitted for review, according to the police page. The request is filed through the online link on that page.

Only the President can shorten limitation and deletion periods of a *conviction* (s. 25); that is a pardon-type request, not a police request.

### When someone asks about your past

An employer who is not entitled to receive criminal information may not get it from the police, and you need not tell them about closed files, stayed cases, or expired or deleted convictions (s. 37). Some bodies listed in the Law's schedules are entitled to information about closed files (s. 31 and the Sixth Schedule); if you apply to such a body, ask a lawyer what they will see.

## Worked example

**Situation.** Ronit, a wheelchair user, joined a disability-benefits protest in 2024 at which protesters blocked a junction in Jerusalem. She was detained and questioned under warning for blocking traffic (s. 215) and released. In 2025 she got a letter: "הוחלט שלא להעמיד אותך לדין". In 2026 she applies to be a teacher's assistant and wants a clean record.

1. **Ground.** At a police station she asks for the closure ground: "חוסר ראיות מספיקות". The closing body is the Jerusalem police prosecution unit.
2. **Evidence.** A friend's video, time-stamped, shows Ronit on the pavement, not on the road, for the whole time police were clearing the junction. Two other protesters can confirm it.
3. **Request.** She writes a two-page request to the Jerusalem prosecution unit using the template: file number, current ground, requested ground "חוסר אשמה", the facts in five paragraphs, the video on a USB stick and a link, the two witnesses' names, and her reason (a job working with children).
4. **Follow-up.** After 45 days without an answer she sends a reminder.
5. **Outcome A.** The ground is changed to lack of guilt; the record is removed (s. 62(ב)(4)). She views her record six weeks later to confirm.
6. **Outcome B.** If the request is refused, Ronit notes the date of the event and can ask for cancellation once five years have passed (misdemeanour, adult), attaching letters from her disability-rights work.

## Do it yourself

### You need

- A real closed file of your own, or the fictional one below.
- The templates: [closure-grounds-request.md](../../templates/closure-grounds-request.md) and [police-records-request.md](../../templates/police-records-request.md).
- 60 minutes.

Fictional file: Avraham, 52, a Haredi activist, was questioned in 2023 after a protest against a road project that would close a synagogue's parking lot. He was suspected of improper conduct (s. 216) and of breaching a lawful order (s. 287) by not leaving the site. The file was closed in 2024 on the ground "נסיבות העניין בכללותן אינן מתאימות להעמדה לדין". He says he left as soon as the order was given, and a municipal security camera faces the site.

### Steps

1. Fill in the record table for the file: offence, class, closure ground, register, the date from which the waiting period runs.
2. Decide: request to change the ground, or wait and request cancellation? Write two sentences on why.
3. Draft the request to change closure grounds with the template. Ask the prosecution to review the camera footage, and say what it will show.
4. Draft the record-viewing and cancellation request with the second template, for the day the waiting period ends.
5. Write the dates in a calendar: the reminder after 45 days; the earliest cancellation date.
6. Write one sentence you would say if an employer who is not entitled asked, "Have you ever had a police file?"

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Sending is optional; a dry run is enough. If you send for real: act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing.

### Template

- [closure-grounds-request.md](../../templates/closure-grounds-request.md) — בקשה לשינוי עילת סגירה, to the prosecutor who closed the file.
- [police-records-request.md](../../templates/police-records-request.md) — viewing your own record, correcting an error, and cancelling a closed-file record.

### How you know it worked

- Your request identifies the file and asks for a specific new ground, with evidence that supports lack of guilt, not just weak evidence.
- Your cancellation request is dated after the right waiting period, with supporting documents listed.
- You can say which of your records an ordinary employer may see (none of the closed files).

## What goes wrong

| Mistake | Consequence | How to fix | How to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assuming "closed" means "deleted" | Surprise at a security check or licence application | Find the ground; request a change or cancellation | Check your record after every file |
| Sending the request to the police station instead of the prosecutor who closed the file | Delay or no answer | Re-send to the prosecution unit or district attorney | Find the closing body in step 1 |
| Arguing "the evidence was weak" | That is the ground you already have | Show why you did not commit the offence | Base the request on evidence of innocence |
| Adding new admissions in the request | Facts that hurt you or others | Talk to a lawyer before sending anything more | Lawyer review if the file is sensitive |
| Applying for cancellation before the waiting period | Refusal | Re-apply after the date | Count from the date of the event |
| Citing the 1981 Criminal Register Law | Weakens your letter | Cite the 2019 Criminal Information Law | Check the law's current name (lesson 02.1) |

## Review

- The criminal register holds convictions and some court orders; the police register holds open and closed files and acquittals.
- Only a closure for lack of guilt removes a closed file from the police register; ask the prosecutor who closed it, with reasons and evidence (s. 62(ב)(4)).
- Closed-file records can be cancelled on request after 5 years (misdemeanour) or 7 (felony) for adults, and non-felony records are cancelled automatically after 7 years.
- You may view your own record at any police station; a lawyer with a special power of attorney can get a printout.
- You need not disclose closed files or expired convictions to anyone not entitled to them (s. 37).
