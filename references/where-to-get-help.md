# Where to Get Help

Most help is free. Get it when the stakes are high, for example a criminal charge, an order to pay the other side's costs, or a complicated petition.

## Free legal help

- **Legal Aid (הסיוע המשפטי, Ministry of Justice).** Civil legal representation for people who pass the income and asset tests. The limits are in [current numbers](current-numbers.md).
- **Public Defender (הסנגוריה הציבורית).** Criminal defence, including for detainees.
- **University legal clinics.** Students take selected cases, supervised by lawyers. Clinics run at:
  - Tel Aviv
  - Hebrew University
  - Haifa
  - Bar-Ilan
  - Reichman
  - Sapir
  - Ono
- **Kol Zchut (kolzchut.org.il).** Plain-Hebrew guides to rights and procedures.
- **Courts information line:** *3852.
