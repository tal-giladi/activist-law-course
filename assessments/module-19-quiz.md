# Module 19 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can carry out the practicum's real-world actions lawfully and well: attend court hearings and know which are closed and what you may not photograph or publish, run a Freedom of Information campaign and escalate through the right route, choose an eligible complaint and the right oversight body, take part in a Knesset committee or a council meeting within the rules and the parliamentary calendar, volunteer within the limits of Bar Association Law s. 20, and make a UN submission that meets the criteria and protects the people in it. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The scenarios follow learners from different communities and causes. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.

## Revisit by topic

- The four eligibility tests, choosing one oversight body, writing to the body first, court deadlines: [19.3 · A Real Complaint to an Oversight Body](../lessons/module-19/lesson-03.md)

- Choosing and requesting a placement, written agreements, what volunteers may and may not do, confidentiality: [19.5 · A Clinic or NGO Placement](../lessons/module-19/lesson-05.md)
- Choosing the UN route, live deadlines, coalitions, consent and art. 9 criteria: [19.6 · A Real International Submission](../lessons/module-19/lesson-06.md)
