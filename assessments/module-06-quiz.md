# Module 6 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can plan and run a protest within the law and protect yourself and others: apply the near-certainty test, decide whether a licence or notice is needed, handle dispersal, detention and filming, choose the right channel for a complaint about police conduct, and keep signs and posts on the right side of the incitement offences. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions describe protests from different camps and ask what the law allows or what you would do next. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
