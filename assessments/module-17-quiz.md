# Module 17 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can document human-rights violations so that the record is trustworthy, lawful and safe: set a purpose and keep facts apart from analysis, decide when an interview should not happen and obtain informed consent, collect and verify open-source material to the Berkeley Protocol standard without crossing the Computers Law line, preserve evidence with SHA-256 hashes and a chain of custody, handle metadata, and protect your sources and your team digitally. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The scenarios follow documenters from different communities and causes. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
