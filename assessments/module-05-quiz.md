# Module 5 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can act against a public authority on your own: choose the right tool and forum, write and follow up a Freedom of Information request, use the ombudsman and the State Comptroller, send a pre-petition letter, and prepare an administrative or High Court petition that survives the threshold. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions describe realistic situations and ask what you would do next. Deadlines and forums matter more than wording. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
