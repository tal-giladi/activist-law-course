# Module 13 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can use international law accurately in Israeli practice: sort sources into binding and persuasive, read a treaty's status and reservations, test a claim that a rule is customary, explain what the International Court of Justice can and cannot do, and choose the right route for an international-law argument in an Israeli court. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions describe realistic situations from different parts of the political spectrum and ask what you would do or say next. Routes, status and attribution matter more than wording. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
