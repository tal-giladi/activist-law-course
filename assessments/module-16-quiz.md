# Module 16 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can work with international criminal law accurately and neutrally: test a label against the elements of a crime, explain how the ICC claims jurisdiction in the Palestine situation and what its decisions did and did not decide, use Israel's complaint channels and explain complementarity, identify the basis of a foreign or Israeli court's jurisdiction over crimes abroad, and prepare an Article 15 communication that is specific and safe. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions follow people from different parts of the political map. They test the law and the procedure, never an opinion about the conflict. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
