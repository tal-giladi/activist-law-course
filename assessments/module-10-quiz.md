# Module 10 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can use the planning and environmental systems on your own: tell which body decides a plan or permit, read a plan and its deadline, write an objection that will be heard, present it, choose the right appeal route in time, and take an environmental hazard from a complaint to a 60-day notice. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions follow residents, farmers, communities and organisations from different parts of the map and ask what the law says or what you would do next. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
