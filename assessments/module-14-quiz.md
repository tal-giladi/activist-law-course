# Module 14 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can use the UN human-rights system on your own: know which treaties bind Israel and what it reserved, see why no individual complaint against Israel is possible, time a shadow report to a committee, plan a UPR submission that meets the binding rules, send a Special Procedures communication with proper consent, and present Human Rights Council and commission of inquiry material with every position attributed. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions follow individuals and organisations from different parts of the political map and ask which UN route fits and what the rules require. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.

## Revisit by topic

- The seven treaties, reservations, the art. 4 derogation, no individual complaints, the territorial-scope dispute: [14.1 · The Core Treaties Israel Ratified](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md)
- The reporting cycle, Israel's calendar per committee, the LOIPR stage, writing and sending a shadow report, follow-up: [14.2 · Treaty Bodies and Shadow Reports](../lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md)
- The three UPR documents, supported and noted recommendations, Israel's record, the 5th cycle, word limits and the submissions system: [14.3 · The Universal Periodic Review](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md)
- Choosing a mandate, the Code of Conduct criteria, the online form, consent and disclosure, Israel's replies: [14.4 · Special Procedures: Sending a Communication](../lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md)
- The Council, item 7, commissions of inquiry, Israel's and other states' positions, ways to engage, the complaint procedure: [14.5 · The Human Rights Council and Commissions of Inquiry](../lessons/module-14/lesson-05.md)
