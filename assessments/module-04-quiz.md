# Module 4 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can audit a government decision the way a court would: authority, considerations, reasonableness and proportionality, fair procedure, and the threshold questions that decide whether a court will hear the case at all. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

Each question describes a realistic decision and asks what the strongest argument or the right next step is. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.

## Revisit by topic

- Enabling provisions, delegation, express authority for fees and limits on rights: [04.1 · Authority and the Principle of Legality](../lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)
- The law's purpose, relevant and extraneous considerations, fettering and dictation: [04.2 · Discretion: Relevant and Extraneous Considerations](../lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- Extreme unreasonableness, the 2023 amendment and its annulment, the proportionality tests: [04.3 · Reasonableness and Proportionality in Administrative Decisions](../lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md)
- Hearings, reasons and conflicts of interest: [04.4 · Fair Procedure: Hearing, Reasons, Conflict of Interest](../lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- Standing, justiciability, prior requests, alternative remedies, delay and forum: [04.5 · Standing, Justiciability and Exhausting Remedies](../lessons/module-04/lesson-05.md)
