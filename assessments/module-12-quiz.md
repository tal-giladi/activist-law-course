# Module 12 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can take part in law-making and local government on your own: follow a bill through its stages and know when it lapses or can be revived after the elections, get into a Knesset committee session, write a position paper a committee can use, work with MKs and parliamentary questions within the lobbying rules, and use your council's meetings, its complaints commissioner and its FOI officer. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions follow residents, parents, business owners, volunteers and organisations from different parts of the political map and ask what the rules say or what you would do next. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
