# Module 7 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can protect yourself in the criminal process after a protest or direct action: recognise the offence and its class, use your rights from detention to the station, handle an interrogation and get a lawyer or a public defender, deal with remand requests and release conditions, and clean up your record after a file is closed. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions describe realistic situations and ask what you would do or what the law allows. Time limits and who decides matter more than wording. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start. Nothing in this quiz is legal advice; in a real case, get a lawyer or the Public Defender.
