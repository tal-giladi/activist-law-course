# Module 3 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can use Israel's constitutional law the way a careful advocate does: find the Basic Law and its entrenchment, run the two-stage analysis under the limitation clause, apply the proportionality tests, match a situation to the ruling that protects a derived right, and describe the 2023–2026 constitutional dispute accurately and neutrally. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

Each question describes a realistic situation and asks for the strongest argument, the right next step or the accurate statement. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.

## Revisit by topic

- The Harari decision, the thirteen Basic Laws, entrenchment, Bergman and Mizrahi Bank: [03.1 · A Constitution Built from Basic Laws](../lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- The rights in the 1992 Basic Laws, the two stages, express authorisation, s. 9, s. 12 and the override clause: [03.2 · Basic Law: Human Dignity and Liberty and the Limitation Clause](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- Proper purpose, rational connection, least harmful means, narrow sense and the margin of proportionality: [03.3 · Proportionality Step by Step](../lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- Equality, expression, religion and protest as derived rights, and their leading rulings: [03.4 · Rights the Courts Derived: Equality, Expression, Religion, Protest](../lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- Amendment No. 4 to Basic Law: The Judiciary, review of Basic Laws, the Attorney General dispute and the positions on each side: [03.5 · Judicial Review and the 2023–2026 Constitutional Dispute](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md)
