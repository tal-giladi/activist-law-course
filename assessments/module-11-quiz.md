# Module 11 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can handle a civil dispute on your own: decide whether court is worth it, run a small claim from the demand letter to the request for leave to appeal, use Net HaMishpat without a lawyer, survive the first stages of a regular civil case under the 2018 regulations, prepare evidence and the hearing, and use fee exemptions and Legal Aid. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions describe realistic disputes and ask which court, which deadline, which filing path or which next step fits. Exact rules and dates matter more than a general sense of fairness. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start. Nothing in this quiz is legal advice.
