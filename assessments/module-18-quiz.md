# Module 18 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can organise lawfully: choose between an informal group and an amuta, register an amuta in your own names, keep it in good standing with its fee, reports and proper-management certificate, handle donations including those from foreign state entities, help other people without crossing into the acts the Bar Association Law reserves to lawyers, and use lawyers, clinics and pro bono help where they add the most. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions follow residents' groups, parents, reservists' families, land-use watchdogs, coexistence projects and protest networks from different parts of the political map, and ask what the law requires or what you would do next. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
