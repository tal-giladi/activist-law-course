# Module 8 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can publish about people and fight back against attacks within the law: spot defamation, pick the defence that protects a claim, answer a demand letter and recognise a SLAPP, publish photos and keep supporter lists under the Privacy Law and Amendment 13, and choose the right route against online harassment or a takedown. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions describe campaigns from different camps and ask what the law says or what you would do next. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
