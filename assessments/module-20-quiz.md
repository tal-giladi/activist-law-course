# Module 20 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can plan and run a real campaign with the tools of the whole course: choose a cause and a goal that a named decision-maker can grant, build a theory of change, order the tools from soft to hard inside the hard deadlines, keep an execution log that turns silence and refusals into legal facts, speak to the media without breaching defamation, privacy, incitement or publication-ban rules, and close or hand over a campaign safely. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions follow campaigns by parents, residents, farmers, reservists' families, religious and secular groups and minority communities from across the political map, and ask which tool, which order or which next step fits. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.
