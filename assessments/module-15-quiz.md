# Module 15 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can work with the law of armed conflict and occupation accurately and neutrally: decide which rules apply and how they bind Israel, apply the occupation test, use the Israeli Supreme Court's rulings for what they actually held, report the ICJ's advisory opinions together with the disagreements and Israel's positions, and analyse an incident without going beyond the evidence. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions test the rules, the method and who says what. None asks you which side is right. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start.

## Revisit by topic

- When IHL applies, treaty and custom for Israel, distinction, proportionality and precautions: [15.1 · The Basics of International Humanitarian Law](../lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md)
- The occupation test, the occupant's duties, positions on the West Bank, East Jerusalem and Gaza: [15.2 · The Law of Occupation](../lessons/module-15/lesson-02.md)
- The Supreme Court's framework and leading rulings, and critiques from different sides: [15.3 · The Israeli High Court on the Territories](../lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md)
- The 2004, 2024 and 2025 advisory opinions, the dissents, Israel's positions, South Africa v. Israel: [15.4 · ICJ Advisory Opinions and Israel's Positions](../lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md)
- The six-step method, grading sources, competing interpretations, limited conclusions: [15.5 · Analysing an Incident Under IHL, Neutrally](../lessons/module-15/lesson-05.md)
