# Module 9 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can use Israel's anti-discrimination laws: place an incident on the map of statutes and forums, build a services-law claim on the presumptions, use the employment law and its Commission, handle a disability or accessibility case, and collect evidence without breaking the Wiretap or Privacy laws. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%.

The questions describe realistic situations across communities and ask which law, which forum, which presumption or which step fits. The exact section matters more than general ideas of fairness. If you are unsure about a topic, revisit the lesson before you start. Nothing in this quiz is legal advice.
