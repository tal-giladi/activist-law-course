# Activist Law: A Free Practical Course

A free, practical course in Israeli public law, human rights and international law for activists who want to fix what is broken through the right legal channels, on their own and without paying a lawyer. Every lesson ends with something you actually do: find a law, read a ruling, draft a letter, file a request.

This free course teaches you to act for yourself in Israeli and international legal processes. It is not a law degree, does not license you to practise law, and is not legal advice.

## Who it is for

Activists and engaged citizens in Israel, from any political direction, with no legal background. You read Hebrew and English.

## How it works

- **20 modules in four stages:**
  - foundations;
  - the public-law toolbox;
  - international law and human rights;
  - a practical stage, with real filings in your own name and a capstone campaign.
- **Language.** Explanations are in English. Israeli laws, rulings, forms and filing templates are in Hebrew, because that is what you read and file.
- **Outside courses.** Where a free online course or guide already teaches something well, the lesson sends you there with an exact link. You then practise it here.
- **Fees, ceilings and deadlines** change every year. They are kept in one place: [current numbers](references/current-numbers.md).

## The one legal limit to remember

You may always act for yourself, including on public issues.

Some acts for other people are reserved to lawyers when you do them as an occupation or for payment (Bar Association Law 1961, s. 20):

- representing them;
- drafting legal documents for them;
- giving them legal advice.

Module 18 explains where the line is.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Acting on your own can carry legal risk: arrest, defamation suits and court costs. Check current rules and act only in your own name. When the stakes are high, get help from a legal clinic or Legal Aid. See [where to get help](references/where-to-get-help.md).
