## Corrections for the research file

- `sources-israel.md` M04 describes the Versa viewpoint "The Deri Appointment: Further Hearing Rules with Bite…" as covering the 2023 Deri litigation. It is about the **2016** ruling on Deri's appointment as Interior Minister and the refused further hearing (Orly Rachmilovitz, 24.8.2016). Not used in Module 4.
- HCJ 5658/23: the official Versa abstract says 12 of 15 justices held that Basic Laws may be reviewed; Times of Israel and Hebrew Wikipedia say 13. Lesson 04.3 gives 12 (official abstract) and notes the other count.
- No free OpenLearn or MOOC unit on Israeli or general administrative-law judicial review was found (OpenLearn search for "judicial review" returned nothing).
