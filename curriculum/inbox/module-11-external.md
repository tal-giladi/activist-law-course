## Not assigned, noted for the main session

- The judiciary's fee-exemption page says online filing in Net HaMishpat for that request is for lawyers only; parties without a lawyer file by registered mail or at a registry counter (affidavit can be certified at the counter, for a fee). Kol Zchut says the small-claims exemption form can be filed "באתר נט המשפט". Lessons tell learners to ask *3852. Worth checking before publishing.
- The gov.il page "agree_to_receive_court_documents_by_email" linked from the Wikisource annotation to reg. 162 returns a gov.il 404 (checked 2026-10-13). Not used.
- Language of small claims: the gov.il filing page says "בעברית בלבד"; Kol Zchut says Hebrew or Arabic (paper filing). Lesson 11.5 says to check with *3852.
- The Legal Aid "economic eligibility table" PDF (https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/legal_aid_application/he/application-1701.pdf) and guideline 1.pdf were not opened; they may hold the official 2026 thresholds.
