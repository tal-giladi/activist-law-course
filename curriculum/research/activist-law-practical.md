# Activist law — practical playbook (Israel + UN)

Status as of Oct 2026. Fees change every year (usually in October), so check the official page before paying.
✅ = confirmed from an official or reliable source · ⚠️ = partly verified, check before acting

## 0. Accounts you need first

- **Government ID login (הזדהות לאומית)**. You need it for gov.il services and Net HaMishpat. ✅
- **Net HaMishpat (נט המשפט)** — the courts' online filing system. Since 6.9.2022, people without a lawyer can file claims and documents online without a smart card. You type your name at the end of the document instead of a digital signature. ✅ Support line *3852 / 077-2703333.
  https://www.ice.co.il/law/news/article/889096 · https://protocol.co.il/net-court/

## 1. Freedom of Information request ✅

- **Who:** any citizen or resident. No reason needed.
- **Where:** foi.gov.il. Find the information officer (ממונה) of the body you want in the officer database ("מאגר הממונים"), then fill in the online form. You get a confirmation receipt.
  https://www.gov.il/he/service/make-a-freedom-of-information-act-request · https://www.gov.il/he/Departments/DynamicCollectors/memunim-test
- **Cost (from 1.10.2024):** ₪24 request fee, paid in the form. You also sign an undertaking to pay a handling fee of up to ₪173. The first 3 hours of work are free.
- **Exempt from the fees:** people asking about themselves or their own rights, NGOs with a proper-management certificate (ניהול תקין), academic research, and people receiving a state allowance.
- **Deadline:** the authority must answer within 30 days. It can extend that.
- **If they ignore you or refuse:**
  1. Send a reminder.
  2. If it is a government ministry or agency, complain to the Government FOI Unit (free): https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הגשת_תלונה_ליחידה_הממשלתית_לחופש_המידע
  3. File an administrative petition (עתירה מנהלית) to the District Court sitting as Administrative Court, under section 17 of the FOI Law. ⚠️ The deadline is about 45 days from the decision (regulation 3 of the Administrative Courts Procedure Regulations). File through Net HaMishpat.
- **Templates and guide:** Movement for Freedom of Information, https://www.meida.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2007/11/how-to-FOI.pdf (old fee amounts, but the process is still valid).
- Government FOI procedure (Dec 2025): https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/regulation-4-1/he/freedom_of_information_procedure_foi_webpage_dec2025.pdf

## 2. Public Complaints Commissioner (נציב תלונות הציבור) ✅

- **Free.** No lawyer needed.
- **Covers:** government ministries, local authorities, state institutions, government companies, and their employees. It does NOT cover private companies such as banks, cell providers or insurers.
- **When:** an act that hurt *you* directly and was illegal, done without legal authority, against proper administration, or unjust. General issues that don't hurt you personally go to the State Comptroller's office instead.
- **How:** https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הגשת_תלונה_לנציב_תלונות_הציבור_(מבקר_המדינה). The online form is at mevaker.gov.il.

## 3. Objection to a building or zoning plan (התנגדות לתכנית) ✅

- **Who:** anyone who sees themselves harmed by the plan, or anyone entitled under section 100 of the Planning and Building Law. You must show your connection (זיקה) to the land or plan.
- **Deadline:** usually 60 days from the last newspaper publication of the deposit notice (הודעת הפקדה). Check the exact date in the notice.
- **See the plan:** at the local committee office, or online at mavat.iplan.gov.il.
- **File:**
  - District committee plans: online form on mavat.iplan.gov.il.
  - Local committee plans: deliver at the office, or send by mail or email. Send a copy to the district committee.
- **Must include:** written, detailed reasons. An affidavit (תצהיר) verifying the facts, **signed in front of a lawyer**. This is the one step that costs a small notary-type fee, typically a few hundred shekels. Your address and phone number. Optionally, the changes you propose.
- Example notices: https://apps.land.gov.il/IturTabotData/nispachim-h/merkaz/4052551/100/%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9D%20%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%93%D7%94.pdf
- **Help:** Bimkom (bimkom.org) helps communities with planning objections.

## 4. Knesset committees ⚠️

- Most legislation work happens in committees, between first and second reading. Source: IDI, https://idi.org.il/parliaments/11110/11125
- **How:** email the committee secretariat (contacts are on each committee page on knesset.gov.il). Send a position paper and ask to be invited. Registration usually closes the day before the session.
- Speaking for *yourself* is fine. Representing an organization you are not employed by requires a lobbyist permit (Knesset Law, chapter 12).

## 5. High Court petition (עתירה לבג"ץ) ⚠️

- Individuals may petition without a lawyer. Courts hear unrepresented petitioners, though they are often dismissed for form or procedure.
- **Rules:** High Court of Justice Procedure Regulations, and Court Fees Regulations. You can ask for a fee waiver for financial inability: https://www.gov.il/he/service/exempt_from_payment_of_a_fee
- **Before filing:** you must first approach the authority in writing and wait for its answer (מיצוי הליכים). Petitions skipping this step are dismissed.
- **Standing:** ACRI's guide to public-petitioner standing, https://law.acri.org.il/he/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/zchut-haamida-210517.pdf
- **Not yet verified online:** the exact fee amount and the official step-by-step guide. Call *3852 or the Supreme Court secretariat before filing.

## 6. Small claims court ✅ (for consumer and private disputes)

- Up to ₪39,900 (Jan 2026). No lawyer allowed or needed.
- https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הגשת_תביעה_קטנה

## 7. UN Special Rapporteurs ✅

- **Who:** any individual, group or NGO. No need to exhaust Israeli courts first, and no need for Israel to have ratified anything.
- **How:** the OHCHR online submission form (preferred). Alternatives are email to urgent-action@ohchr.org, or post to OHCHR-UNOG, 8-14 Avenue de la Paix, 1211 Geneva 10.
- **Tips:**
  - Use first-hand facts, not only media reports.
  - Focus on recent events.
  - Get consent from the people you name.
  - Check the communications database for similar cases.
- **Exception:** the Working Groups on arbitrary detention and on enforced disappearances accept only the victim, their family or a representative.
- **Guides:**
  - ISHR, https://academy.ishr.ch/learn/special-procedures/requesting-a-communication
  - https://www.srpoverty.org/submission-of-information-to-the-special-procedures/
  - ISHR 2026 guide, https://ishr.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/UN-2026-EN.pdf
