# Sources — international modules (M13–M17) + free course catalog

Researched 2026-10-12. Scope: public international law, the UN human rights system, IHL and the law of occupation, international criminal law, human rights documentation, plus a catalog of free external courses that lessons can send learners to.

Verification legend:
- ✅ = opened with WebFetch and the content was confirmed.
- ✅ (200) = the URL answered HTTP 200 on a direct check, but the page is a PDF or JavaScript app, so its content was not read in full.
- ⚠️ = blocked to automated fetch (ohchr.org HTML pages, icc-cpi.int, gov.il HTML pages and casebook.icrc.org all return 403/504 to bots), or found only through search, or not opened. Open it in a browser before publishing.

Neutrality rule for lesson writers: in M15 and M16, every contested finding (ICJ, ICC, Commission of Inquiry, treaty bodies) is paired with Israel's official position from the MFA's own legal site, israelihl.mfa.gov.il. State who says what. Don't write findings in the course's own voice.

### How Israel absorbs international law
Doctrine to teach, consistent across the sources below: customary international law is part of Israeli law unless a Knesset statute contradicts it. Treaties bind Israel internationally, but Israeli courts apply them only if the Knesset legislates them in. There is also an interpretive presumption that Israeli law conforms to Israel's treaty obligations.
- [Ajuri v. IDF Commander in West Bank, HCJ 7015/02 (3 Sept 2002) — Versa English](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/ajuri-v-idf-commander-west-bank) — L13.4 + M15. The Court treats the Hague Regulations as customary law. It notes that the State disputes that GC IV is customary, and applies GC IV's humanitarian provisions without deciding the question — ✅
- [Dweikat et al. v. State (Elon Moreh), HCJ 390/79 — Versa English](https://versa.cardozo.yu.edu/opinions/dweikat-et-al-v-state) — L13.4 + M15. The Versa header gives the date as 10 Oct 1979; HaMoked and press reports give 22 Oct 1979 — ✅
- [HaMoked summary of HCJ 390/79 Duweikat](https://hamoked.org/document.php?dID=1670) — L13.4: the expropriation failed both Israeli law and customary international law — ✅ (200)
- [Verfassungsblog (2020), "Constitutional adjudication of international law violations" (Israel)](https://verfassungsblog.de/constitutional-adjudication-of-international-law-violations/) — L13.4 short secondary explainer of the custom/treaty split and the presumption of conformity — ✅ (200, body not read)
- [ICRC Casebook — Israel, Ayub v. Minister of Defence (Beth El, HCJ 606/78)](https://casebook.icrc.org/node/20823) — L13.4: the classic holding that customary rules (Hague Regulations) are enforceable in Israeli courts and treaty-only rules (GC IV) are not — ⚠️ (403 to bots)
- [Israel MFA — "General international law" section (Israel's ILC / Sixth Committee statements, 2025–2026)](https://israelihl.mfa.gov.il/general_international_law) — L13.5 "Israel as an international-law actor". Includes Israel's 2025 Sixth Committee statements on "general principles of law" and "subsidiary means" — ✅
