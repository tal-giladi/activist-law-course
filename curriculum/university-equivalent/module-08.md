# Module 8 — Defamation, Privacy and Online Speech: university equivalent

## University courses

- **Torts (דיני נזיקין).** Year 1, mandatory everywhere. At Tel Aviv University it is course 1411-9104, 4 ש"ס. Defamation and privacy usually appear only briefly in it, if at all.
- **Defamation (דיני לשון הרע), Hebrew University course 62531.** Elective, 2 נ"ז. It is taught by Adv. Uri Shenhar, author of the standard textbook. Its topics are:
  - the civil tort and the criminal offence;
  - publication and the mental element;
  - media and internet liability;
  - the defences and remedies;
  - practical dilemmas in running defamation litigation.
- **Defamation and privacy (ההגנה על הכבוד, השם הטוב והפרטיות בדיני נזיקין), Hebrew University course 62651.** Elective.
- **Privacy in the information age (From Big Brother to Big Data: פרטיות בעידן המידע), Hebrew University course 62249.** Elective.
- **Privacy Clinic (הקליניקה לפרטיות), Tel Aviv University.** Clinical elective.

## What this module covers

- Defamation basics under the Prohibition of Defamation Law 1965 (lesson 08.1).
- The defences: truth plus public interest (s. 14) and good faith (s. 15) (08.2).
- Threat letters and SLAPP suits: how to respond and when to get a lawyer (08.3).
- Privacy law for activists: photos, personal data, and Amendment 13 to the Protection of Privacy Law (08.4).
- Platforms, takedowns and online harassment (08.5).

## What we left out

**Defamation doctrine:**
- the "reasonable reader" test;
- opinion versus fact;
- the absolute privileges (s. 13);
- the extended media defence after the Dayan-Orbach line of cases (CA 751/10; FHC 2121/12);
- compensation without proof of damage (s. 7A);
- criminal defamation.

**Privacy doctrine:**
- privacy as a constitutional right;
- data protection law in full: databases, registration, the Privacy Protection Authority's enforcement powers, and cross-border transfers;
- surveillance and secret recording (Wiretap Law 1979);
- employee privacy in the workplace.

**Neighbouring areas:**
- Intermediary liability doctrine and the unmasking of anonymous users (Mor v. Barak ITC, LCA 4447/07).
- Comparative law: the US actual-malice rule (New York Times v. Sullivan), and the GDPR.
- Torts general part, the frame for both wrongs: negligence, causation and damages.

## What would make it a whole university course

The nearest whole course is a defamation elective plus a privacy elective, built on first-year torts.

Topics to add:
- Full defamation doctrine with 15–20 rulings.
- Remedies and damages.
- Data protection law in full.
- The wiretap and surveillance regime.
- Platform liability.
- Comparative units.

Core readings (real, standard works):
- Uri Shenhar, דיני לשון הרע (1997), the standard Israeli textbook.
- Michael Birnhack, מרחב פרטי: הזכות לפרטיות בין משפט לטכנולוגיה (2010).
- The Protection of Privacy Law 1981 and the Protection of Privacy (Data Security) Regulations 2017, read in full.

Free sources:
- The Privacy Protection Authority guidance on gov.il.
- Mishpatim and Iyunei Mishpat articles on defamation and privacy.
- Versa translations of expression and privacy rulings.

Rough hours to reach the full courses:
- **Defamation (2 נ"ז):** about 26 lecture hours, 50 reading hours and 20 hours of exam prep.
- **Privacy (2 נ"ז):** about 26 lecture hours, 50 reading hours and 20 hours of exam prep.
- Total: roughly 190 hours, against about 10 hours in this module. First-year torts would add about 190 more.
