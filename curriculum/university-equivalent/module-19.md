# Module 19 — Practicum: university equivalent

## University courses

There is no direct university course for this module. A practicum like this is not a classroom course. It replaces the practical side of legal training. The nearest equivalents are listed below.

- **Legal clinics (קליניקות משפטיות), inside the LL.B.**
  - Bar-Ilan University: a clinic is mandatory, 5 ש"ס in year 3.
  - Tel Aviv University: clinics are electives of 8 ש"ס of practical work, running a full year. Examples relevant to this course:
    - Human Rights Clinic (הקליניקה לזכויות אדם, 1411-5240-01);
    - Knesset Clinic (קליניקת הכנסת, 1411-7589-01);
    - clinics for rights in criminal proceedings, refugees' rights, workers' rights, environmental justice, privacy, and class actions.
  - Students work real cases under a supervising lawyer.
- **Internship — התמחות.** This is the closest match in function. It comes after the LL.B. and before the bar exams, under the Israel Bar Association Law. It is a supervised, full-time placement with a lawyer, a court, or a public body such as the State Attorney, the Public Defender, Legal Aid or an NGO legal department. Its length was shortened from 18 to 12 months by temporary wartime legislation, and the Knesset has moved to make 12 months permanent. Check the current rule with the Israel Bar.
- **Moot courts and practical workshops (משפטים מבוימים, סדנאות מעשיות).** Tel Aviv lists these as a separate category of credit.
- **International equivalent.** The Geneva Academy LL.M. has "professionalising activities" (6 ECTS): a curricular internship with one of more than 80 partner organisations (including OHCHR, the ICRC and UNHCR), the Jean-Pictet Competition, or the Geneva IHL Lab.

## What this module covers

These are real actions, not exercises.

- Observing 10 court hearings.
- A real freedom-of-information campaign.
- A real complaint to an oversight body.
- Taking part in a Knesset committee or local council meeting.
- A placement of 3 months or more with a clinic or NGO.
- A real submission to an international body.
- A portfolio documenting all of the above.

## What we left out

- Supervision by a licensed lawyer with professional responsibility for the work. This is the core of both clinics and the internship. The learner here works on their own behalf or under an NGO, not under a supervising advocate.
- Representing third parties in court. That is legally reserved to lawyers, so this course can neither include nor replace it.
- The internship's breadth: drafting pleadings for clients, appearing in court as an intern, and working across many practice areas.
- The clinic's academic part: weekly seminar sessions, reflective writing and graded case files.
- Assessment by the Bar: the bar exams (written and, where applicable, oral) and admission to practice.
- Professional socialisation: office practice, client handling, billing and ethics enforcement.

## What would make it a whole university course

- **To match a clinic.** A year-long placement with a university clinic or an NGO legal department, under a named supervising lawyer. It should include a weekly seminar and graded written work. The pro bono and clinic programmes are listed in lesson 18.5.
- **To match the internship.** This is not possible without an LL.B. and registration with the Israel Bar. Say so plainly to learners.
- **Core readings.** The clinic's own reader. For background on clinical legal education generally, use the faculty clinic programme descriptions. I could not confirm one standard Hebrew text, so none is named here.
- **Free sources.**
  - Kol Zchut.
  - The FOI Unit guides on gov.il.
  - ISHR Academy for the international submission.
  - Knesset committee schedules.
  - Court hearing lists (יומני דיונים) on the Judiciary site.
- **Rough hours.**
  - A clinic is about 200–300 hours a year.
  - The internship is about 12 months full-time, that is more than 1,800 hours.
  - The Geneva professionalising activity is about 150–180 hours.
  - This module's 3-month placement plus the other tasks comes to roughly 150–250 hours, depending on the learner.
