# Module 4 — Administrative Law Principles: university equivalent

## University courses

- **Tel Aviv University: משפט מנהלי (Administrative Law), course 1411-9224.** Year 2, 4 ש"ס. It is one of TAU's year-2 core courses (קורסי יסוד): students must take at least five from a list, and almost all take this one.
- **Hebrew University: דיני מנהל ציבורי (Public Administration Law), course 62819.** 6 נ"ז, undergraduate. Its topics are introduction, authority, administrative procedure, administrative discretion, theoretical aspects and regulation. The earlier version of the course was 62632.
- **Other faculties.** Administrative law is a core second-year course at every Israeli faculty.

## What this module covers

- Authority and the principle of legality (lesson 04.1).
- Discretion: relevant and extraneous considerations (04.2).
- Reasonableness and proportionality in administrative decisions, including the post-2023 position on reasonableness (04.3).
- Fair procedure: the right to a hearing, the duty to give reasons and conflict of interest (04.4).
- Threshold issues: standing, justiciability and exhausting remedies (04.5). Module 5 continues them in practice.

## What we left out

- **The structure of the executive:**
  - classification of administrative powers;
  - delegation and agency;
  - the role and binding force of the Attorney General's opinions.
- **Other grounds of review:**
  - discrimination as a ground of review;
  - estoppel and administrative promises (הבטחה מינהלית);
  - changing a decision (שינוי החלטה).
- **The rest of administrative law:**
  - administrative contracts and tenders (דיני מכרזים);
  - public appointments, including the Deri-Pinhasi and Eisenberg line, in depth;
  - remedies: voidness versus voidability (relative voidness), and tort liability of public authorities;
  - regulation theory and independent regulators;
  - administrative enforcement and sanctions (HUJI teaches this separately in 62577);
  - local government law (TAU offers משפט השלטון המקומי, 1411-9312, as a separate year-3 course);
  - privatisation and hybrid bodies (גופים דו-מהותיים);
  - theoretical approaches to judicial review of the administration.

## What would make it a whole university course

Topics to add:
- Units on delegation, administrative promises, tenders and administrative contracts.
- Units on appointments, remedies and relative voidness.
- Units on regulation, privatisation and dual-character bodies.
- 30–40 leading rulings. Dapei Zahav (HCJ 389/80), Ressler (HCJ 910/86) and Deri-Pinhasi (HCJ 3094/93) are only the start.

Core readings (real, standard works):
- Itzhak Zamir, הסמכות המינהלית, the multi-volume standard treatise.
- Daphne Barak-Erez, משפט מינהלי, the multi-volume treatise.
- The Administrative Procedure Amendment (Decisions and Reasons) Law 1958, and the AG guidelines that bind ministries.

Free sources:
- Versa, for leading HCJ rulings in English.
- Mishpatim, Iyunei Mishpat and Mishpat uMimshal, for many free administrative-law articles.
- The Attorney General guidelines database on gov.il.

Rough hours to reach the full course:
- About 52 lecture hours (4 ש"ס).
- About 110 reading hours.
- About 40 hours of exam prep.
- Total: roughly 200 hours, against about 10 hours in this module.
