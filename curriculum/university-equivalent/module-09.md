# Module 9 — Equality and Anti-Discrimination: university equivalent

## University courses

No Israeli LL.B. has a mandatory anti-discrimination course. The material comes from:

- **First-year constitutional law (משפט חוקתי).** The equality unit of the mandatory course: at TAU, course 1411-9102, 4 ש"ס; at HUJI, course 62110. HUJI's syllabus includes age discrimination and equal representation of women.
- **Labour law (דיני עבודה).** Its employment-discrimination unit. At TAU this is a year-2 core course, 4 ש"ס.
- **TAU בין אפליה לשוויון במשפט (Between Discrimination and Equality in Law), course 1411-7791.** A year 3–4 seminar, 4 ש"ס.
- **TAU הגנת הצרכן ואיסור אפליה בעידן הבינה המלאכותית (Consumer Protection and Anti-Discrimination in the AI Era), course 1411-9364.** An advanced course, 2 ש"ס.
- **HUJI משפט ומגדר (Law and Gender), course 62656.** An elective, with a unit on the Equal Employment Opportunities Law 1988.
- **Clinics.** Disability-rights and anti-discrimination clinics are offered at several faculties.

## What this module covers

- A map of the anti-discrimination laws (lesson 09.1).
- Discrimination in services and entry under the Prohibition of Discrimination in Products, Services and Entry Law 2000. It includes suing without proof of damage (09.2).
- Employment discrimination under the Equal Employment Opportunities Law 1988, and the Equal Employment Opportunities Commission (09.3).
- Disability rights under the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law 1998, and accessibility complaints (09.4).
- Collecting evidence lawfully, including testers and records (09.5).

## What we left out

- **Equality theory:**
  - formal versus substantive equality;
  - the "group" versus "individual" models;
  - affirmative action theory.
- **Constitutional equality doctrine:** equality as part of human dignity, and the leading rulings in depth:
  - Alice Miller (HCJ 4541/94);
  - Ka'adan (HCJ 6698/95);
  - Botzer (HCJ 7081/93, school accessibility).
- **Employment discrimination in depth:**
  - burden-shifting (s. 9);
  - indirect and disparate-impact discrimination;
  - equal pay (Equal Pay Law 1996);
  - pregnancy and parenthood protections;
  - sexual harassment (Prevention of Sexual Harassment Law 1998).
- **Other grounds and fields:**
  - discrimination in land allocation and housing;
  - discrimination by public authorities as a ground of administrative review;
  - education and the rights of minority groups (Arab citizens, Haredim, LGBTQ people, migrants);
  - the Women's Equal Rights Law 1951 and representation (appointment of women to boards).
- **Disability rights in depth:**
  - the accessibility regulations;
  - legal capacity and supported decision-making (Legal Capacity and Guardianship Law amendments);
  - the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.
- **Comparative law:** US Title VII and EU equality directives.
- **Algorithmic discrimination:** the subject of TAU's 1411-9364.

## What would make it a whole university course

The nearest whole course is an equality-law elective or seminar.

Topics to add:
- Equality theory.
- Constitutional equality case law.
- Employment discrimination doctrine with labour-court rulings.
- Sexual harassment.
- Disability law and the CRPD.
- Housing and land discrimination.
- Comparative law and algorithmic discrimination.
- For a seminar, a 25–30 page paper.

Core readings (real works):
- Aharon Barak, כבוד האדם: הזכות החוקתית ובנותיה (2014), the chapters on equality.
- Frances Raday, Carmel Shalev and Michal Liban-Kooby (eds.), מעמד האשה בחברה ובמשפט (1995).
- The anti-discrimination statutes above, read in full, with the labour-court case law collected in labour-law textbooks.

Free sources:
- The Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities and the Equal Employment Opportunities Commission (both on gov.il).
- Kol Zchut.
- Free journal articles (Mishpatim, Iyunei Mishpat, Mishpat uMimshal,).

Rough hours to reach the full course:
- About 26–52 contact hours.
- About 70 reading hours.
- Either about 30 hours of exam prep, or about 60 hours for a seminar paper.
- Total: roughly 130–180 hours, against about 10 hours in this module.
