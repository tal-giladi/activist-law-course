# Module 18 — Organising and Working Within the Law: university equivalent

## University courses

This module draws on parts of three LL.B. courses. No single course matches it.

- **Corporate law — דיני תאגידים / דיני חברות.** It matches lessons 18.1–18.3, the amuta part only.
  - Tel Aviv University: *דיני תאגידים* (Corporations, 1411-9222-01, 4 ש"ס) is a second-year core course. A student must take at least five from a list of about ten.
  - Bar-Ilan University: *דיני חברות* (Companies, 99-205, 5 ש"ס) is mandatory in year 2.
  - Both courses centre on the Companies Law (חוק החברות, התשנ"ט-1999). Non-profits get little time.
- **Professional ethics — אתיקה מקצועית.** It matches lessons 18.4–18.5: what non-lawyers may do, and how to use lawyers.
  - Tel Aviv: *המקצוע המשפטי – תיאוריה, פרקטיקה ואתיקה* (The Legal Profession – Theory, Practice and Ethics, 1411-9306-01, 4 ש"ס), in the year 3–4 basic-course pool.
  - Bar-Ilan: *אתיקה מקצועית* (99-380, 3 ש"ס), mandatory in year 4.
  - Ethics is also a bar-exam subject.
- **Non-profit and philanthropy electives.** Some faculties offer these. Tel Aviv's 2025–26 booklet has a seminar *פילנתרופיה – מבט רב-תחומי* (Philanthropy – a Multidisciplinary View, 4 ש"ס).
- **Clinics.** Clinics are where students meet amutot as clients. Lesson 18.5 points learners to them.

## What this module covers

- Acting as an informal group versus registering an amuta, with the trade-offs.
- Registering an amuta yourself under the Amutot Law (חוק העמותות, התש"ם-1980) and with the Registrar of Amutot.
- Running an amuta:
  - the bodies (general assembly, board, audit committee);
  - the proper-management certificate (אישור ניהול תקין);
  - donations, including foreign-government funding reporting;
  - annual reports.
- What non-lawyers may and may not do for others: the acts the Israel Bar Association Law (חוק לשכת עורכי הדין, התשכ"א-1961) reserves for lawyers.
- Using lawyers wisely: clinics, pro bono work, the Legal Aid department, and the Public Defender.

## What we left out

- General corporate law: separate legal personality, piercing the veil, organs and agency, and directors' fiduciary duties in depth.
- Other legal forms: public benefit companies (חל"צ), cooperative societies, public endowments (הקדש ציבורי) and charitable trusts, compared in full.
- Taxation of non-profits: the Section 46 donation credit, VAT status as a non-profit (מלכ"ר), and the Section 9(2) public-institution status in detail.
- Labour and employment law for an organisation's staff and volunteers.
- The ethics course itself: confidentiality and privilege, conflicts of interest, fees, duties to the court, discipline, and lawyers' advertising.
- Theory of the legal profession: access to justice, cause lawyering, and the regulation of legal services.
- Governance of civil society: freedom of association as a constitutional right, and the regulation of foreign funding as policy.

## What would make it a whole university course

- **Topics to add.** Corporate law up to the standard of the Companies Law course. A full professional-ethics course built on the Bar Association Law and the Bar's Professional Ethics Rules (כללי לשכת עורכי הדין (אתיקה מקצועית), התשמ"ו-1986). A unit on non-profit forms and tax.
- **Core readings.**
  - Use the faculty's own reader for Israeli corporate law and for legal ethics.
  - I could not confirm a single current standard Hebrew textbook on amutot or on legal ethics, so none is named here.
  - The primary legislation listed above is the core.
- **Free sources.**
  - The Registrar of Amutot (רשם העמותות) guides on gov.il, including the proper-management guide.
  - GuideStar Israel (גיידסטאר) organisation data.
  - Kol Zchut pages on amutot and Legal Aid.
  - The Israel Bar Association ethics decisions.
- **Rough hours.**
  - Corporate law: about 200 hours.
  - Ethics: about 150 hours.
  - A non-profit seminar: about 150 hours.
  - Total: about 500 hours, against roughly 10–12 hours in this module, which takes only the practical non-profit slice.
