## One-line summary

Learn Israeli public law, human rights and international law well enough to file, complain, object, petition and campaign on your own — without paying a lawyer.

## Who it is for

Activists and engaged citizens in Israel, from any political direction, with no legal background.

## What you will learn

- Find and read any Israeli law or ruling for free, and build a legal argument.
- Use Freedom of Information, the Ombudsman and the State Comptroller against any public body.
- Write pre-petition letters and represent yourself in administrative and High Court petitions and small claims.
- Protest lawfully, know your rights with the police, and handle arrest and interrogation.
- Avoid and answer defamation threats; use privacy and anti-discrimination law.
- Object to planning decisions and take part in Knesset and local council processes.
- Use the UN human-rights system and document violations to evidential standards.
- Register and run an amuta and know what you may and may not do for others.

## Risk notice (shown to learners before they start)

This course is not legal advice and does not make you a lawyer. Acting on your own can carry legal risk — arrest, defamation suits and court costs — so check current rules, act only in your own name, and get advice from a clinic or Legal Aid when the stakes are high.
