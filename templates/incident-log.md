# Incident Log (יומן אירועים)

A bilingual log for documenting incidents of human-rights violations: Hebrew field names (so the log can be handed to an Israeli lawyer, police investigator or court as it is) with English instructions and English column names. One log per project; one row per incident or per item of information. Taught in [lesson 17.1](../lessons/module-17/lesson-01.md) and used in the [Module 17 project](../projects/m17-documentation-kit.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own name or your group's own name. This is not legal advice. Collect only what your stated purpose needs. Do not record conversations you are not part of (Wiretap Law ss. 1–2), do not access anyone's accounts or devices (Computers Law s. 4), and do not publish testimonies or photos of injured people without consent (Protection of Privacy Law ss. 2(9), 2(4א)). Remote and open-source work only: never go to a dangerous place to document.

## How to use it

1. Fill part A once, before you collect anything.
2. Give each incident a number (I-01, I-02 …) and each person a code (V-01 for victims, W-01 for witnesses, S-01 for suspects or units). Keep the key that links codes to names in a **separate, encrypted file** (part D; lessons 17.4 and 17.5).
3. In part B, write facts only: acts, not labels. Put labels used by a source in quotation marks, with the source.
4. Every fact gets a source and a source type: first-hand (ראשוני), documentary (מסמך / סרטון / תמונה), official (רשמי) or hearsay (שמיעה).
5. Fill the "doubts" and "harm check" columns every time. An empty doubts column is a warning sign.
6. Link each item of evidence to the chain-of-custody form ([evidence-chain-of-custody.md](evidence-chain-of-custody.md)) by its item number.
7. Corrections: never delete a row. Add a new row that corrects it, and write the date and reason.

## Checklist

- [ ] Purpose written before collecting.
- [ ] Facts only in the "what happened" column; labels quoted with their source.
- [ ] Every fact has a source and a source type.
- [ ] Doubts and harm check filled for every row.
- [ ] Names only in the separate, encrypted key.
- [ ] Evidence items numbered and linked to the chain-of-custody form.
- [ ] Corrections added as new rows, never by deleting.
