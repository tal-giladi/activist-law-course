# Article 15 Communication to the ICC Prosecutor

An English template for sending information about alleged crimes under the Rome Statute to the Office of the Prosecutor (OTP) of the International Criminal Court, under Article 15 of the Statute. It follows the order in which an analyst reads a file: summary, incidents, crimes and elements, alleged perpetrators, domestic proceedings, evidence, security and consent. Taught in [lesson 16.5](../lessons/module-16/lesson-05.md); the crime elements come from [lesson 16.1](../lessons/module-16/lesson-01.md) and the domestic steps from [lesson 16.3](../lessons/module-16/lesson-03.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own name, or for a person or group that asked you to act for them, only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The OTP itself gives no legal advice. Do not submit without the informed consent of every person you name, and never submit information you know or suspect to be false.

> [!WARNING]
> Information you send may reach other people, including the defence if a case goes to trial. Do not collect testimony in a dangerous place. Keep files encrypted, keep the originals untouched, and record anyone who could be at risk if identified. If you are a US citizen or resident, US sanctions on persons connected with the ICC may affect you; get advice from a sanctions lawyer first.

## How to use it

1. Write the whole text offline in this template. Replace everything in `[square brackets]`; delete what does not apply.
2. Write in English or French. Attach Hebrew or Arabic originals with an English summary of each.
3. Keep the summary to five lines. Put details in the incident table and the evidence list.
4. Use neutral, factual language. Describe acts; attribute allegations; never write "committed" about a person who has not been convicted.
5. Submit through OTPLink (https://otplink.icc-cpi.int/) or by post to the address on the ICC's contact page. Check the current upload limits in [Current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md).
6. Save the acknowledgement and fill the follow-up log (Part F).

## Part A — Before you write (keep; do not send)

```text
Is this the right body?
  Alleged crime under the Rome Statute (art. 6, 7, 8 or 8 bis): [yes — which / no — use another route]
  Within a situation the Court covers? Territory: [ ]  Dates: [ ]
  (For Palestine: Gaza and the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, since 13 June 2014.)
  Domestic complaint filed? [police / MAG / other — reference no. and date / no — why not]
  Other bodies informed? [UN Special Procedures / commission of inquiry / foreign prosecutor / none]

Do I know this first-hand, or from reliable direct sources? [explain]
Does every person named consent? [yes / no — then do not name them]
```

## Part B — The communication

```text
TITLE
Communication under Article 15 of the Rome Statute concerning [incident(s)], [place], [date(s)]
Situation: [e.g. Situation in the State of Palestine, ICC-01/18]

1. SENDER
   Name or organisation: [ ]   (or: the sender asks to remain anonymous)
   Relationship to the events: [victim / relative / witness / organisation with direct information]
   Contact (secure): [e-mail / phone / contact through lawyer ___]
   Preferred language: [English / French / Arabic / Hebrew with English summary]

2. SUMMARY (five lines at most)
   [What happened, where, when, to whom, who is alleged to be responsible, which crime(s).]

3. INCIDENTS (one row per incident)
   | No. | Date and time | Exact place (address / coordinates) | What happened | Persons harmed | Source(s) |
   | 1   | [ ]           | [ ]                                 | [ ]           | [ ]            | [E1, E3]  |

4. ALLEGED CRIMES AND ELEMENTS
   Incident [1]:
   Crime: [e.g. war crime of hostage-taking, art. 8(2)(a)(viii)]
   Element — act: [facts] (source: [ ])
   Element — context (armed conflict / widespread or systematic attack / genocidal intent): [facts] (source: [ ])
   Element — knowledge or intent: [facts] (source: [ ])
   What is not known: [ ]

5. ALLEGED PERPETRATORS (as far as known)
   [Name, rank, unit or organisation, role; or "unknown — the unit/organisation responsible is ___"]
   Basis for the identification: [ ]

6. CONTEXT (short)
   [Relevant pattern, orders, statements or policy, with sources. Attribute every allegation.]

7. DOMESTIC PROCEEDINGS (complementarity)
   | Date | Body | Step (complaint / investigation / closure / appeal / petition / indictment) | Reference no. | Outcome |
   | [ ]  | [Israel Police / MAG / court / other] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |
   If none: [why no domestic complaint was filed, or why it could not be]

8. EVIDENCE LIST
   | ID | Description | Type | Original held by | How and when obtained | SHA-256 hash | Attached? |
   | E1 | [ ]         | [video / photo / document / statement] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [yes / available on request] |

9. SECURITY AND CONSENT
   Persons who could be at risk if identified: [ ]
   Persons who agree to be contacted by the OTP: [name — how to contact]
   Persons who do not agree to be named: [described as "Witness A", etc.]
   Any other concern the Office should know: [ ]

10. DECLARATION
   To the best of my knowledge the information above is accurate. I understand that the Office of
   the Prosecutor does not provide legal advice and may not inform me of how it uses this
   information.
   Name or "anonymous": [ ]   Date: [ ]
```

## Part C — Pre-submission check

- [ ] The summary alone says what, where, when, who and which crime.
- [ ] Every fact has a date and a source; opinions and slogans removed.
- [ ] Each crime is matched to its Statute article and each element to facts.
- [ ] Domestic steps listed with reference numbers, or their absence explained.
- [ ] Evidence originals untouched; hashes recorded; copies attached or offered.
- [ ] Consent recorded for every person named; risks stated.
- [ ] Language checked: allegations attributed; no "committed" without a conviction.

## Part D — Consent record (keep; do not send)

```text
Person: [name]
Date: [ ]
Explained in: [language]
I agree that [sender] sends the information described to the ICC Office of the Prosecutor.
I agree / do not agree to be named.
I agree / do not agree to be contacted by the Office (through: ___).
I understand that if a case goes to trial, information may be disclosed to the defence.
I understand that I may withdraw this consent before the information is sent.
Signature or recorded confirmation: [ ]
```
