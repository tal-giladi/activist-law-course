# Press Release (הודעה לעיתונות)

A one-page press release that reports a campaign milestone — a fact on the record — and asks one decision-maker for one decision. Hebrew version for Israeli media, English version for international outlets and UN-related steps. Used in lesson [20.4](../lessons/module-20/lesson-04.md) and in the [capstone case study](../projects/m20-capstone.md). For use in your own name, or in the name of a group that authorised you; this is not legal advice.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You are liable for what you bring to the media, together with the editor (Defamation Law s. 11). Before sending, run the seven-question check in lesson 20.4 and the [pre-publication checklist](pre-publication-checklist.md). Check for publication bans and pending criminal cases (Courts Law ss. 70–71). Do not include private people's details, minors' faces or anyone's health information without written consent.

## How to fill it in

1. Choose one milestone from your execution log. If there is no new fact, do not send a release.
2. Every figure and every factual sentence about a person or body points to a document you can share (exhibit number in your log).
3. Your opinion appears only in the quote, clearly as your opinion.
4. Ask the decision-maker for comment before sending, and write what they answered (or that they did not answer by the date you set).
5. Keep it to one page. Attach or link the documents.

## Hebrew version (נוסח עברי)

> **הודעה לעיתונות**
>
> [לפרסום מיידי / אמברגו עד: יום ___, תאריך ___, שעה ___]
>
> **[כותרת: העובדה, לא תואר. למשל: "העירייה לא השיבה לתושבים במשך שלושה חודשים בעניין ___"]**
>
> **[כותרת משנה: הבקשה במשפט אחד — מי צריך להחליט מה, ועד מתי]**
>
> [עיר], [תאריך] — [מי] [עשה / קיבל / גילה מה] [מתי]. [משפט אחד: למה זה חשוב לציבור].
>
> לפי [המסמך: תשובה לבקשת חופש מידע מיום ___ / פרוטוקול ישיבת המועצה מיום ___ / החלטת בית המשפט מיום ___], [העובדה המרכזית, עם המספרים].
>
> [שם], [תפקיד בקמפיין / תושב/ת ___]: "[ציטוט קצר — דעתך, בשמך בלבד]."
>
> אנו פונים ל[בעל הסמכות: שם התפקיד] ומבקשים כי [ההחלטה המבוקשת] עד [תאריך].
>
> [תגובת הרשות: "___" / הרשות התבקשה להגיב ביום ___ ולא השיבה עד למועד פרסום ההודעה.]
>
> **רקע:** [שתיים–שלוש שורות: מה נעשה עד כה — פנייה מיום ___, בקשת מידע מיום ___, שאילתה מיום ___].
>
> **מסמכים:** [קישור למסמכים שאתם מוכנים לפרסם]
>
> **לפרטים נוספים:** [שם], [טלפון], [דוא״ל]

## English version

> **PRESS RELEASE**
>
> [For immediate release / Embargoed until: day ___, date ___, time ___ (Israel time)]
>
> **[Headline: the fact, not an adjective. For example: "Israeli municipality left residents' request unanswered for three months"]**
>
> **[Subheading: the request in one sentence — who must decide what, by when]**
>
> [City], Israel, [date] — [Who] [did, received or found what] [when]. [One sentence: why it matters to the public.]
>
> According to [the document: a Freedom of Information answer dated ___ / the minutes of the council meeting of ___ / the court's decision of ___], [the key fact, with figures].
>
> "[Short quote — your opinion, in your own name only]," said [name], [role in the campaign / resident of ___].
>
> We call on [the decision-maker: title] to [the decision] by [date].
>
> [The authority's response: "___" / The authority was asked for comment on ___ and had not responded at the time of publication.]
>
> [For international steps: The Government of Israel's position, as stated in [source, date], is that ___.]
>
> **Background:** [Two or three lines: steps so far — letter of ___, FOI request of ___, submission to ___ on ___.]
>
> **Documents:** [link to documents you are willing to publish]
>
> **Contact:** [name], [phone with +972], [e-mail]
