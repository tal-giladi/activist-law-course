# Portfolio Entry (רשומת תיק עבודות)

A standard record for each real action in the practicum: what you did, in whose name, the documents, the timeline, the outcome, what you learned, and the safety and legal checks you made. Use one entry per action. Taught throughout Module 19 ([lessons 19.1–19.6](../lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)) and assembled in the [Module 19 portfolio](../projects/m19-portfolio.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The portfolio records actions you took in your own name, or in the name of a group or organisation that authorised you. This is not legal advice. Before you share the portfolio with anyone, remove ID numbers, addresses, phone numbers and the names of private people who did not agree to be named.

## How to use it

1. Open a new entry the day you start an action, not when it ends: the timeline is the most valuable part.
2. Attach every document as an exhibit (PDF or screenshot), numbered in the entry (A1, A2, ...).
3. Update the timeline every time something happens: sent, received, reminded, answered, refused, escalated.
4. Close the entry with the outcome, the reflection and the self-assessment score from the lesson's rubric.
