# Campaign Execution Log (יומן ביצוע של קמפיין)

The dated record of every action, document, clock and result of a real campaign, with a weekly review. Used in lesson [20.3](../lessons/module-20/lesson-03.md) and as part 3 of the [capstone case study](../projects/m20-capstone.md). For use in your own name, or in the name of a group that authorised you; this is not legal advice.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The log holds other people's personal data: collect only what the campaign needs, protect the file, and do not share it for any other purpose (Privacy Protection Law s. 2(9)).

## How to use it

1. Write each line on the day the event happens.
2. Every "sent" line needs proof: registered mail receipt, online form confirmation, e-mail with full header, or a hand-delivery stamp.
3. Number every document (A01, A02 …). Name files `YYYY-MM-DD_A07_short-description.pdf`. Keep originals unedited.
4. Every line that starts a clock gets a calendar reminder a few days before the clock ends. Take the periods from [Current Numbers](../references/current-numbers.md).
5. Once a week, add a review line (part 3).

## 3. Weekly review

Copy this block every week.

> **Week of:** …
>
> 1. What happened (line numbers): …
> 2. Clocks ending in the next two weeks, and what I do about each: …
> 3. Assumptions tested (confirmed / false): …
> 4. Preconditions — closer, same, or further than last week: …
> 5. **Decision:** continue / adjust / escalate — because …
> 6. Hours spent this week, and is that sustainable? …

## 4. When to change the plan

| Event | Typical next step | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Positive decision | Get it in writing; log implementation dates; check it is carried out | 20.3 |
| Reasoned refusal | Analyse the reasons; pre-petition letter or appeal inside the deadline | 04.x, 05.5 |
| Silence past the legal period | Reminder; complaint; consider a pre-petition letter (deemed refusal, Decisions and Reasons Law s. 6(ב)) | 05.3, 05.4 |
| Partial answer | Accept what you got; target the rest precisely | 05.3 |
| New facts that undercut the case | Revise the theory of change | 20.1 |
| Threat letter or claim | Stop publishing on that point; follow the first-hour, first-day, first-week steps | 08.3 |
| Police summons, detention or arrest | Lawyer or Public Defender first | 07.2, 07.3 |
| The decision-maker changes | Write again to the new holder of the power | 04.1 |
