# Issue map (מפת סוגיה)

A one-page worksheet that maps a real issue to its laws, the authorities that decide it, and the forum where it can be challenged. Used in Module 1 (lessons 01.1–01.6 and the project `m01-issue-map`) and reused at the start of every later project. For use in your own name only; this is not legal advice.

Copy the blocks below into your own document and fill them in. Keep every link you use.
