# Position Paper to a Knesset Committee (נייר עמדה לוועדת כנסת)

A Hebrew position paper for a Knesset committee discussing a bill or a topic: a one-page summary with numbered requests, reasoned arguments that each end in a proposed wording, a wording table, sources and a sending checklist. Taught in [lesson 12.3](../lessons/module-12/lesson-03.md); the request to take part in the session is in [committee-participation-request.md](committee-participation-request.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own name, or in the name of the group or amuta that is submitting it, only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. If anyone in your group is paid by a client to persuade MKs, check the lobbyist rules in [lesson 12.4](../lessons/module-12/lesson-04.md) first.

## How to use it

1. Read the bill, its explanatory notes and the latest committee draft (נוסח לדיון) on the committee's page.
2. Choose two to four requests. Each must stay within the bill's subject (Rules of Procedure s. 85).
3. Write the summary page **last**, but put it first.
4. Every factual claim gets a source in section 6. Mark your own data as your own.
5. Keep it to two to four pages. Attach long data as appendices.
6. Replace everything in `[square brackets]`. Delete what does not apply.

## Checklist

- [ ] Page one: who we are, a one-sentence position, numbered requests.
- [ ] Each request: problem, evidence with a source, exact wording.
- [ ] Every request is within the bill's subject (s. 85).
- [ ] The other side's strongest argument is answered.
- [ ] Own data marked as such; method described.
- [ ] No personal attacks on MKs, officials or opponents.
- [ ] Two to four pages, plus appendices.
- [ ] Sent to the secretariat, the committee's legal adviser, the chair and members from the coalition and the opposition, at least a few days before the session.
- [ ] Record kept: what was sent, to whom, when; later, what the protocol and the next draft say.
