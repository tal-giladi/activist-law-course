# Administrative Petition (עתירה מינהלית)

A Hebrew skeleton for an administrative petition to a District Court sitting as an Administrative Court, filed without a lawyer, with a checklist of every element in reg. 5(ב) of the Administrative Courts (Procedure) Regulations. Taught in [lesson 05.6](../lessons/module-05/lesson-06.md). The supporting affidavit uses the [affidavit template](affidavit.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own name (or in the name of the amuta that is the petitioner) only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing: the regulations, the fee on [Current Numbers](../references/current-numbers.md), and the filing steps with the judiciary information centre (*3852). Drafting petitions for others as an occupation or for payment is reserved to lawyers (Bar Association Law s. 20).

## How to use it

1. Do the five checks from lesson 05.6: forum (First Schedule item), venue (reg. 2), 45 days (reg. 3), exhaustion (your pre-petition letter), other proceedings.
2. List every respondent: the deciding authority, any other authority involved, and anyone who may be harmed if you win (reg. 6(א)).
3. Write the remedies first. Then fill in the sections in order.
4. Every fact points to an exhibit (נספח). Every legal argument names its source.
5. Write the affidavit (Form 2): personal knowledge first, then belief with its source (reg. 5(ג)). Have it certified (lesson 02.6).
6. Fee: cite item 20 of the schedule to the Court Fees Regulations, or attach a fee-exemption request with its own affidavit on assets and six months of income (reg. 14).
7. File in Net HaMishpat; serve every respondent within three days (reg. 35(א)); upload proof of service (reg. 7(ב)).
8. Aim for 8–15 pages before exhibits.

עת"מ ________ (יינתן בעת ההגשה)

              [כתובת למסירת כתבי בי-דין]

                                  – נ ג ד –

              2. [רשות נוספת הנוגעת בדבר, אם יש]

9. [עובדה, תאריך, נספח.]

ח. אגרה (תקנה 5(ב)(8))

נספח 4 — מכתב ההתראה ותשובת המשיב .. עמ' ___

## The supporting affidavit

Use [affidavit.md](affidavit.md). In paragraph 1 write: "אני העותר/ת בעתירה זו, ואני נותן/ת תצהיר זה לאימות העובדות המפורטות בה." Keep facts within your personal knowledge apart from facts you believe, and for each belief write its source ("מקור ידיעתי: ___"), as reg. 5(ג) requires.

## The reg. 5(ב) checklist

| Element | Regulation | Paragraph in my petition |
|---|---|---|
| Petitioner's name, ID, address, contact details | 5(ב)(1) | |
| Respondents, their addresses and contact details, and each one's interest | 5(ב)(2) | |
| Minor, legally incompetent person or corporation noted, if relevant | 5(ב)(2א) | |
| The decision and the date of publication, notice or knowledge | 5(ב)(3) | |
| Subject-matter and local jurisdiction: empowering law and First Schedule item | 5(ב)(3א) | |
| Petitioner's interest | 5(ב)(4) | |
| Factual and legal grounds | 5(ב)(5) | |
| Prior correspondence attached, or reasons for none | 5(ב)(6) | |
| Remedies | 5(ב)(7) | |
| Fee item, or the regulation granting exemption | 5(ב)(8) | |
| Other proceedings on the same facts or decision | 5(ב)(9) | |
| Affidavit in Form 2, knowledge separated from belief | 5(ג) | |
| Copy of the decision and relevant documents attached | 5(ד) | |

## Before and after filing

- [ ] Filed within 45 days, in the district where the decision was made.
- [ ] Fee paid, or exemption request with its affidavit attached.
- [ ] Affidavit certified; exhibits marked and listed.
- [ ] Served on every respondent within three days (reg. 35(א)).
- [ ] Proof of service uploaded (reg. 7(ב)).
- [ ] If the decision is about to be carried out: a separate interim-order request with an affidavit, served the same day (regs 9, 35(ב)).
