# Protest Legal Plan (תוכנית משפטית להפגנה)

A planning sheet that takes a protest from idea to aftermath: the rights analysis, permits and notices, roles and scripts for the day, a review of signs and posts, and the plan for complaints and follow-up. Used throughout Module 6 and in the [Module 6 project](../projects/m06-protest-plan.md). The working language is English; the documents you file are in Hebrew (see [protest-notice.md](protest-notice.md) and [police-complaint.md](police-complaint.md)).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For planning a protest you organise or take part in, in your own name or your group's. This is not legal advice — check current rules before you act. Share the day-of sections (3 and 4) with your stewards; keep personal data of participants to the minimum you need.

### Liaison script

```text
שלום, אני [שם], איש/אשת הקשר של המארגנים. יש לנו [רישיון / תיאום] מיום [___] עם התנאים הבאים: [___].
אבקש את שמך, דרגתך ומספרך האישי.
אם יש הוראה חדשה, אבקש לקבל אותה בכתב או לרשום אותה כאן בנוכחותך, ואת הנימוק לה.
```

### Detention card (each participant carries it)

```text
האם אני מעוכב/ת או עצור/ה? באיזה חשד?
מה שמך ומספרך האישי?
אני מוסר/ת את פרטי הזהות שלי.
אני מבקש/ת לדבר עם עורך דין: [מספר הקו החם].
איני מסכים/ה לחיפוש בטלפון שלי.
```

- Detention: up to 3 hours; up to 6 with written reasons when many are detained (Arrests Law s. 73).
- Never resist physically. Comply first, complain later.

### Personal kit

ID card · charged phone with cloud backup · hotline number written on the arm · water · medication · no knives or items that look like weapons.

## 4. Messages: signs, chants, posts (06.5)

| Text (exact) | Where / audience | Offence considered | Risk (none / maybe / high) | Rewrite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | s. 144ד2 / s. 144ב / CT Law s. 24 / s. 192 / defamation | | |

Group sign and chant rules (read at the briefing):

1. Only approved signs.
2. No calls to violence, praise of attacks, or threats — against anyone.
3. No personal details or home addresses of opponents or officials.
4. If an officer demands that a sign come down: ask which offence, comply, film, tell the liaison.
5. Admins remove forbidden posts from group chats at once.
