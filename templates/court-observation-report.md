# Court Observation Report (דוח צפייה בדיון)

A one-page report for each court hearing you watch from the public seats: where and when, what kind of hearing, who spoke, what the court decided, which rules of procedure you saw in action, and what you learned for your own cases. Taught in [lesson 19.1](../lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md); ten of these reports are the first part of the [Module 19 portfolio](../projects/m19-portfolio.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You attend as a member of the public. Do not photograph in the courtroom, and do not record, without the court's permission (Courts Law s. 70(ב)). Do not publish anything about a hearing held behind closed doors (s. 70(א)), anything that identifies a minor (s. 70(ג)), or anything a publication ban (צו איסור פרסום) covers. In this report, refer to private parties by role or initials only. This is not legal advice.

## How to use it

1. Fill in sections 1–3 in the courtroom, on paper or in a notes app with the phone in flight mode (check the court's rules on phones first).
2. Fill in sections 4–6 the same day, while you still remember.
3. If a written decision is published later, find it on Net HaMishpat or the Supreme Court site and add section 7.
4. Keep the reports in the portfolio folder, numbered 01–10.

## 2. What happened (facts only)

Write in order, in short lines, with times. Who spoke, what they asked for, what the judge asked, what was decided. No opinions in this section.

```text
09:12  The judge opens; the prosecutor asks for ___ .
09:15  Defence counsel answers: ___ .
09:20  The judge asks the self-represented party: ___ .
09:31  Decision: ___ .
```

## 4. Analysis (your own view, kept separate)

1. What was the real issue the judge had to decide, in one sentence?
2. Which argument seemed to persuade the judge, and why?
3. What did a party do well, or badly, in preparation or presentation?
4. If you were a self-represented party in this hearing, what would you have done differently?
