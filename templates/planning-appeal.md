# Planning Appeal (ערר לוועדת ערר)

A Hebrew appeal (כתב ערר) to a district appeals committee for planning and building, following reg. 3 of the Planning and Building (Procedure in Appeals Committees) Regulations 1996, with a request for leave to appeal for the routes that need it. Taught in [lesson 10.4](../lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own name only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Appeal deadlines are very short (15, sometimes 7 days) and some routes need leave first. Confirm the route, the number of copies and the fee with the appeals committee secretariat on the day the decision arrives.

## Which route? (Planning and Building Law)

| Decision | Route | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Local committee approved a plan in its powers and rejected your objection | Appeal to the district appeals committee (s. 112) | 15 days from service of the decision |
| Same, by an **independent** local committee | Request for leave to the appeals committee chair, then the appeal (s. 112(א1)) | Request within 15 days; appeal within 15 days of leave |
| Local committee or licensing authority rejected your objection to a הקלה or non-conforming use | Appeal to the district appeals committee (s. 152(א)(1)) | 30 days from service |
| Appeals committee decided an appeal on a plan | Request for leave to the appeals committee chair to appeal to the district committee (s. 111) | 7 days for the request; 7 days from leave |
| District committee approved or rejected a plan | Request for leave to the district committee chair, then appeal to the National Council (s. 110) | Request within 15 days; appeal within 30 days |

Deadlines are also on [Current Numbers](../references/current-numbers.md). The National Council and district-committee routes have their own regulations; adapt the headings below.

## How to use it

1. Write the date the decision was **served on you** at the top of your file and calendar the deadline and a personal deadline three days earlier.
2. Attack the **decision**: what it failed to address, what it got wrong, what it ignored. Your objection is background, attached as an exhibit.
3. Name every respondent (reg. 4): for a plan — the local committee, the plan's initiator, and anyone whose objection was accepted or who was heard on the point; for a permit or הקלה — the local committee or licensing authority and the applicant.
4. Attach a copy of the decision notice (reg. 3(א)(4)).
5. **Filing (reg. 2):** either six printed copies plus one for each respondent, **or** two printed copies plus one for each respondent **and** the online appeal form on the Planning Administration site ("הגשת ערר לוועדת ערר לתכנון ובניה", National Digital Identity login) — all by the deadline. Online filings after 16:00 on Sunday–Thursday, or on Friday or a rest day, count as filed on the next working day.
6. **Fee:** an appeal fee is payable; the secretariat confirms receipt after payment (reg. 2(ג)). Ask for the current amount.
7. You may ask the appeals committee to hear an expert (s. 12ב(ב)); list the expert in "main evidence".

## Request for leave to appeal (בקשת רשות לערור)

Use it for an independent local committee (s. 112(א1); reg. 20), for the s. 111 route, or adapted for the s. 110 route. Reg. 20(ב) requires reasons, the decision and plan details, a copy of the notice and the date it was served. The chair considers whether the request shows a ground or raises an important question, and that it is not vexatious (s. 112(א1)(2)).

```text
לכבוד
יו"ר [ועדת הערר לתכנון ולבניה מחוז ___ / הוועדה המחוזית ___]
באמצעות המזכירות

הנדון: בקשת רשות לערור על החלטת [___] מיום [___] בעניין תכנית מס' [___]
לפי סעיף [112(א1) / 111 / 110] לחוק התכנון והבניה ותקנה 20 לתקנות סדרי הדין בועדות ערר

1. המבקש/ת: [שם, כתובת]. התנגדתי לתכנית וההתנגדות נדחתה.
2. ההחלטה: [תיאור]; העתק ההודעה מצורף (נספח 1); ההודעה הומצאה לי ביום [___].
3. הבקשה מגלה עילה: [שתיים־שלוש שורות — הטענה המרכזית שלא נדונה / הטעות].
4. הבקשה מעוררת שאלה בעלת חשיבות או השלכה רחבה: [למשל: הצללת מוסדות ציבור / תקדים לשכונה כולה].
5. הבקשה אינה קנטרנית או טורדנית: [הוגשה התנגדות מנומקת עם תצהיר; מבוקש סעד מצומצם].
6. לפיכך אבקש ליתן לי רשות לערור. כתב הערר המוצע מצורף (נספח 2).

[תאריך, חתימה, שם]
```

## Checklist before filing

- [ ] Route and body chosen from the table; leave requested where needed.
- [ ] Service date and deadline written; personal deadline earlier.
- [ ] Every reg. 3 element: appellant and address, plan or request, decision and its copy, respondents and addresses, reasons, main evidence.
- [ ] Reasons attack the decision, not only the plan.
- [ ] Remedies numbered, with a fallback.
- [ ] Copies: six, or two plus the online form; plus one per respondent.
- [ ] Fee paid or arranged; receipt kept.
- [ ] Next step if it fails noted (s. 111 leave, National Council, or administrative petition within 45 days).
