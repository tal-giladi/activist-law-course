# Special Procedures Communication

An English draft of a communication to UN Special Procedures (Special Rapporteurs, Independent Experts, Working Groups), laid out in the order of the OHCHR online submission form, with a consent record, a Code of Conduct check and a follow-up log. Taught in [lesson 14.4](../lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md); also the facts-and-consent checklist for information sent to a commission of inquiry ([lesson 14.5](../lessons/module-14/lesson-05.md)).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own name, or for a person who asked you to act for them, only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Do not submit without the written consent of every person you name. The Working Group on Arbitrary Detention and the Working Group on Enforced or Involuntary Disappearances accept cases only from the person concerned, the family or their representatives.

> [!WARNING]
> Information you send may reach the government. If anyone could face retaliation, say so clearly in the submission so names can be withheld, keep your files encrypted, and do not collect testimony in a dangerous place.

## How to use it

1. Write the whole text offline in this template. The online form at spsubmission.ohchr.org keeps a draft for **24 hours only**.
2. Write in English, French or Spanish.
3. Keep it short: put the essentials in the first paragraph; attach only key documents.
4. When final, open the form, paste each section, submit, and save the reference number and password.
5. Replace everything in `[square brackets]`. Delete what does not apply.

## Part A — Before you write

```text
Situation in one sentence: [who, what, when, where]
Recent or imminent? [date of the last event / expected date]
Urgent (life, health or grave imminent harm)? [yes / no — why]

Mandates addressed (1–3), and why:
1. [Special Rapporteur on ___] — [reason]
2. [Working Group on ___] — [reason]
Joint communication requested? [yes / no — why]

Is this the right tool? [yes — a specific case with first-hand facts / no — use a shadow report or the UPR]
```

## Part B — The communication (in the order of the online form)

```text
1. SUBMISSION TYPE
   [Individual case / group of people / legislation or policy]

2. VICTIM(S)
   Name: [ ]   Age: [ ]   Gender: [ ]   Residence: [ ]
   [Or a description of the affected group and its size.]
   Minor? [yes / no]   Victim of sexual violence? [yes / no]
   (Names of minors and of victims of sexual violence are not published in public reports.)

3. SUBMITTER
   Name / organisation: [ ]
   Relationship to the victim: [self / family / representative / organisation with direct knowledge]
   Contact: [e-mail, phone]

4. DISCLOSURE AND CONSENT
   May the victim's name be disclosed to the Government of Israel? [yes / no]
   May it appear in the public report to the Human Rights Council? [yes / no]
   Security concerns (required if names must be withheld): [describe the risk]

5. CASE INFORMATION
   5.1 Summary (3–5 lines): [who, what, when, where, by whom]
   5.2 Facts in date order:
       [date] — [what happened] — [source: statement / document / court record / photo]
       [date] — [ ]
   5.3 Laws or policies involved: [name and section; attach if needed]
   5.4 Action already taken in Israel: [complaints, police, petitions, court decisions with case
       numbers and dates, and the result]
   5.5 Other international action: [none / other mandates / treaty bodies]
   5.6 What you ask the mandate-holders to do:
       [e.g. seek information from the Government on ___; urge ___; comment on the draft law ___]

6. ALLEGED PERPETRATOR(S)
   [Name, title or unit, if known; otherwise the body responsible]

7. ATTACHMENTS (key documents only)
   [list]
```

## Part C — Code of Conduct check (HRC resolution 5/2, art. 9)

- [ ] (a) Not manifestly unfounded or politically motivated.
- [ ] (b) A factual description of the alleged violations.
- [ ] (c) No abusive language.
- [ ] (d) Sent by the victim, or by a person or group in good faith with direct or reliable knowledge, backed by clear information.
- [ ] (e) Not based only on media reports.

## Part D — Consent record (keep it; do not send it)

```text
Person: [name]
Date: [ ]
I agree that [submitter] sends the information described to the UN Special Procedures.
I agree / do not agree that my name is disclosed to the Government of Israel.
I agree / do not agree that my name appears in a public UN report.
I understand that I may withdraw this consent before the information is sent.
Signature or recorded confirmation: [ ]
Language in which this was explained: [ ]
```

## Part E — Follow-up log

```text
Submitted on: [date]   Reference: [ ]   Password stored at: [ ]
[date] — automatic acknowledgement received (this does not mean action was taken)
[date] — OHCHR request for more information: [ ]
[date] — update sent to OHCHR: [release / new threat / court ruling]
[date] — checked the communications database (spcommreports.ohchr.org) for a published letter: [found / not yet]
[date] — Government reply published: [summary]
```
