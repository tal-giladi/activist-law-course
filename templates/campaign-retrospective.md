# Campaign Retrospective and Hand-Over (תחקיר קמפיין ותיק העברה)

The closing document of a campaign: an honest comparison of plan and outcome, a hand-over pack that lets someone else continue lawfully, and a safety and personal-data clean-up. Used in lesson [20.5](../lessons/module-20/lesson-05.md) and as parts 5–6 of the [capstone case study](../projects/m20-capstone.md). For use in your own name, or in the name of a group that authorised you; this is not legal advice.

Write it within two weeks of the end. Cite a log line or an exhibit (from your [execution log](campaign-execution-log.md)) for every claim.
