## 3. Proper purpose (תכלית ראויה)

- The stated purpose (quote the explanatory notes, the minutes or the decision):
- Is it a legitimate aim (protecting other rights or an important public interest)?
- Is there evidence of a different real purpose?

## 4. Rational connection (קשר רציונלי)

- Does the measure actually advance the purpose? What evidence shows it?
- Is it over-inclusive (hits people it does not need to) or under-inclusive (misses the main problem)?

## 7. Remedy and forum

- What exactly do you ask for (amend a section, add an exception, set a time limit, cancel the measure)?
- Who can grant it (the Knesset committee, the minister, the authority, a court)? Which forum (Module 5)?
- If a court: would it more likely declare the measure void, suspend the declaration, or read it narrowly?
