# UPR Stakeholder Submission

An English skeleton for an individual or joint stakeholder submission to the Universal Periodic Review of Israel: cover page, introduction, follow-up on the last cycle's recommendations, issues with SMART recommendations, and a form checklist based on the OHCHR technical guidelines for stakeholders. Taught in [lesson 14.3](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own name, or in the name of the group or amuta that is submitting it, only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The UPR is not confidential: accepted submissions are published with the submitter's name. Mention an individual case only if nobody's safety is put at risk.

## How to use it

1. Find the stakeholder deadline for Israel's review on the OHCHR UPR page (UPR Info's Israel page gives an early, provisional date). Late submissions are not considered.
2. In the Universal Human Rights Index (uhri.ohchr.org), list every recommendation from the last cycle on your topic, with the recommending state and Israel's position (supported or noted).
3. Draft in Word. Keep within the word limit: 2,815 words for one organisation, 5,630 for a joint submission (see [Current numbers](../references/current-numbers.md)). The cover page and footnotes do not count, but footnotes are not used in the OHCHR summary.
4. Number every paragraph and page.
5. Replace everything in `[square brackets]`. Delete what does not apply.

## Cover page (not counted in the word limit)

```text
[Organisation name and acronym, logo if any, website]

Submission to the Universal Periodic Review of Israel
[5th] cycle, [nth] session of the UPR Working Group, [month year]

Submitted by: [organisation / coalition members]
Contact: [name, e-mail]
Date: [ ]
Word count: [____] (limit: 2,815 individual / 5,630 joint)

About the submitting organisation(s):
[Main activities, date of establishment, where you work. One short paragraph.]
```

## The submission

```text
I. INTRODUCTION
1. [Who you are in one sentence. The issues covered. The period covered: since the last review in
   [May 2023].]
2. [Method: how you collected the information — own monitoring, interviews with consent, FOI
   requests, official data.]

II. FOLLOW-UP TO THE PREVIOUS REVIEW
3. In the [4th] cycle, Israel [supported / noted] recommendation [number] by [State]: "[text]".
   [Implemented / partly / not implemented]: [evidence with date and source].
4. [Repeat for each relevant recommendation.]

III. [ISSUE 1 TITLE]
5. Background: [the law or policy, in two or three sentences].
6. Developments since the last review: [first-hand facts, dates, numbers].
7. [Impact on the people concerned, without identifying anyone who has not consented.]

IV. [ISSUE 2 TITLE]
8. [ ]

V. RECOMMENDATIONS
We recommend that Israel:
9. [Action verb] [what], [by when], [how it is measured]. (Issue III)
10. [ ] (Issue III)
11. [ ] (Issue IV)
```

## The SMART recommendation formula

```text
[Adopt / Publish / Amend / Allocate / Establish] + [the specific measure] + [by a date or within a period]
+ [a measurable result or reporting duty]

Example: "Publish annually, from 2029, data on stops and searches by the police broken down by
ethnic origin."
```

Avoid: "Respect the rights of…", "Consider improving…", "Continue efforts to…" — no state can measure them, and they are rarely taken up.

## Form checklist (binding rules in the OHCHR technical guidelines)

- [ ] Within the word limit (individual 2,815 / joint 5,630).
- [ ] Saved as a Word document.
- [ ] Paragraphs and pages numbered.
- [ ] Cover page clearly identifies the submitter; short paragraph about the organisation.
- [ ] Written in English, French or Spanish.
- [ ] Submitted through the online UPR submissions system (uprdoc.ohchr.org) by 23:59 on the deadline day.
- [ ] Final version: revisions are not accepted after submission.

## Content checklist (advisory)

- [ ] Covers the period since the last review and starts from follow-up on its recommendations.
- [ ] First-hand information first; second-hand only in footnotes.
- [ ] Does not list Israel's treaties or committee findings (OHCHR's UN compilation covers them).
- [ ] No pictures, maps, annual reports or other organisations' reports as annexes.
- [ ] Each recommendation linked to an issue.

## After submitting

- [ ] One-page advocacy factsheet with two or three recommendations.
- [ ] List of states to brief (those that made similar recommendations last cycle) and their embassies or Geneva missions.
- [ ] Apply for a UPR Info pre-session, if relevant.
- [ ] Watch the Working Group dialogue; record which states made your recommendation and Israel's response.
