# Helping-Others Boundaries Card (מה מותר ומה אסור כשעוזרים לאחרים)

A one-page card for activists, volunteers and groups that people turn to for help with legal problems. Print it, pin it in the group chat, and give it to every new volunteer. Taught in [lesson 18.4](../lessons/module-18/lesson-04.md) and used in the [Module 18 project](../projects/m18-org-plan.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Not legal advice — this card is our reading of Bar Association Law ss. 20, 21 and 96, Courts Law s. 63(ב), the Supreme Court's judgment in ע״א 4223/12 and the Bar's 2009 ethics decision; no lawyer reviewed it. If your group runs an organised service for the public, have it checked by a lawyer or a university legal clinic.

## Part A — The four-step check

Before you help, ask:

1. **For whom?** For yourself, or for your group acting in its own name → fine. For another person → go on.
2. **Is there a trigger?** Is it regular or organised ("דרך עיסוק")? Is there any payment or benefit, even once ("בתמורה")? If either is yes, the reserved acts below are off-limits.
3. **Which act?** Representing them before a court or a quasi-judicial body (s. 20(1)), or before the Execution Office, Land Registry, registrars or tax authorities (s. 20(2)); drafting a legal document for them (s. 20(3)); advising on their case (s. 20(4)).
4. **Is there a special rule?** Small claims: with the court's approval and a power of attorney, not regularly, not in business, not for payment (Courts Law s. 63(ב)). Other bodies may have their own rules — check the specific law (s. 21(3)).

## Part B — Do and don't

| Do (fine) | With care | Don't (refer) |
|---|---|---|
| Explain what the law says in general, in workshops, posts and videos | Type or translate a person's own words when they cannot do it themselves, adding no legal content | Tell a person what to claim, whether they will win, or what to answer in their case |
| Share blank templates marked "לשימוש בשמך בלבד" | Fill in fixed factual fields of an official form, at their request and in front of them | Write their letter, claim, appeal or contract in your words |
| Link to official guides, Kol Zchut and the law text | Sit with them, as a support person, in the public seats | Speak for them in court, at a medical committee, or before an appeals or planning committee |
| Help them collect and organise their own documents and timeline | Represent a relative once, unpaid, in small claims with the court's approval | Take money, "expenses", a share of compensation or membership in return for help |
| Run a group action where each person signs their own copy | Host a volunteer lawyer's clinic — the lawyer decides, the group does not interfere | Call yourself "legal adviser" or use any title that suggests you are a lawyer |
| File your group's own FOI request, objection or petition in its own name | Give feedback on whether a draft is clear and complete (not on its legal arguments) | Run a regular "advice desk" without lawyers |

## Part C — What to say (standard wording)

> אני לא עורך/ת דין ואינני יכול/ה לייעץ לך בעניינך האישי. אני יכול/ה להסביר מה החוק אומר באופן כללי, לתת לך תבנית ריקה למילוי בשמך, ולהפנות אותך למי שיכול לייעץ לך — סיוע משפטי, הסנגוריה הציבורית, קליניקה משפטית או עורך/ת דין.

*Gist:* I am not a lawyer and cannot advise you on your own case; I can explain the law in general, give you a blank template to fill in yourself, and refer you to someone who can advise you.
