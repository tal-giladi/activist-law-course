# Shadow Report Outline (UN treaty body)

An English outline for a shadow (alternative) report or a list-of-issues submission to a UN treaty body reviewing Israel: a treaty map, a one-page summary, an issue-by-issue table that ends in questions and recommendations, a follow-up table and a sending checklist. Taught in [lesson 14.1](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md) (section 1) and [lesson 14.2](../lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md) (the rest). Filed in English because the committees work in English, French and Spanish.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own name, or in the name of the group or amuta that is submitting it, only. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Submissions are usually published: get written consent from anyone you name, and ask OHCHR in advance for confidentiality if anyone could be put at risk.

## How to use it

1. Fill in section 1 (treaty map) first, using the UN Treaty Collection and the OHCHR Treaty Body Database.
2. Decide the stage: comments on Israel's draft report (to the Ministry of Justice), list of issues prior to reporting (LOIPR), review, or follow-up. For a LOIPR submission, keep it to two or three pages and focus on questions.
3. Read Israel's latest report and the committee's last concluding observations before writing section 4.
4. Write the summary (section 3) **last**, but put it first.
5. Over five pages: add a contents list. If you write in a language other than English, French or Spanish, add an English summary of up to two pages.
6. Replace everything in `[square brackets]`. Delete what does not apply.

## Section 1 — Treaty map (lesson 14.1)

```text
Issue in one sentence: [who is affected, by what, where]

| Treaty | Article | Israel ratified | Reservation on this article? | Note |
|--------|---------|-----------------|------------------------------|------|
| [ICCPR] | [26] | [3 Oct 1991] | [No] | [ ] |
| [CEDAW] | [16] | [3 Oct 1991] | [Yes — personal-status laws] | [argue from art. 2 instead] |

Territorial scope (only if the issue is outside Israel's national territory):
"Israel's position is that the treaty applies only to its national territory; the Committee's
position is that it applies to all territory under Israel's jurisdiction ([document, para.])."

Open route chosen: [shadow report / LOIPR input / follow-up / UPR / Special Procedures] — because [ ].
```

## Section 2 — Cover page

```text
Submission to the [Committee name]
[Session number and dates, or "for the list of issues prior to reporting on Israel's [n]th report"]

Submitted by: [organisation or group name; amuta number if any]
Contact: [name, e-mail, phone]
Date: [ ]
About us (2–3 lines): [who you are, what you do, since when]
Joint submission: [names of partner organisations, if any]
Publication: [Public] / [Please keep confidential: the whole submission / the names in paras __ / our contact details]
```

## Section 3 — Summary (one page)

```text
This submission addresses [n] issues under articles [__] of the [treaty]:

1. [Issue 1 title] (art. [__])
   Suggested question: [one question the Committee could ask Israel]
   Suggested recommendation: [one action, a time frame, a measure]

2. [Issue 2 title] (art. [__])
   Suggested question: [ ]
   Suggested recommendation: [ ]
```

## Section 4 — Issues (repeat for each issue)

```text
[n]. [Issue title] — article [__]

[n].1 What Israel reported
   [Quote the paragraph of Israel's report or replies, with the document symbol and paragraph number.
    If Israel's report is silent on the issue, say so.]

[n].2 What is happening
   [Facts in date order, with numbers and sources. Mark your own data as your own and explain how
    you collected it. First-hand information first. No names without written consent.]

[n].3 The gap
   [Two or three sentences: how the facts fall short of the article, citing the committee's general
    comments or previous concluding observations if relevant.]

[n].4 Suggested questions for Israel
   - [ ]
   - [ ]

[n].5 Suggested recommendations
   - [Action verb] + [what] + [by when] + [how progress is measured].
```

## Sending checklist

- [ ] Committee, session and deadline taken from the session page's "Information for civil society" note (or the LOIPR due date in the OHCHR database).
- [ ] Submission route confirmed: OHCHR online platform, secretariat e-mail, or Child Rights Connect for the CRC.
- [ ] Article-by-article structure; Israel's reservations acknowledged.
- [ ] Israel's own report quoted and answered.
- [ ] Every issue ends in a question and a recommendation.
- [ ] Consent recorded for every identifiable person; confidentiality requested in advance where needed.
- [ ] Summary page first; contents list if over five pages; English summary if written in another language.
- [ ] Plan for after the review: use the concluding observations in Israeli advocacy, and diarise the follow-up date.
