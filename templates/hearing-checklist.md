# Hearing Checklist (רשימת הכנה לדיון)

A preparation sheet for a Small Claims Court hearing (it also works for a pre-trial or evidence hearing in the Magistrates' Court): the evidence plan, witnesses, what to bring, your opening and closing notes, and what to do after the hearing. Taught in [lesson 11.5](../lessons/module-11/lesson-05.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own case only. This is not legal advice — check current rules and the court's instructions in your summons before the hearing. Do not film or photograph in the courtroom without the court's permission (Courts Law s. 70(ב)).

## 4. Deadlines before the hearing

- [ ] 60+ days before: checked in Net HaMishpat or at *3852 that the defendant was served.
- [ ] 40+ days before: served the claim myself if the court had not (small-claims reg. 7(ב1)).
- [ ] 60+ days before: requested a summons for any witness who may not come; deposited the witness fee.
- [ ] 7+ days before: requested an interpreter, if needed.
- [ ] As early as possible: asked to move the date if I cannot come (new request in Net HaMishpat).
- [ ] Audio or video: USB drives for the court and each defendant, with a transcript, filed at the registry.

## 5. What to bring

- [ ] ID card
- [ ] The full case file, printed: claim, defence, decisions
- [ ] Originals of every exhibit; the phone that holds the original messages
- [ ] Three extra copies of any document not yet filed (court, other side, me) and a short request to file it
- [ ] Laptop, charged, with the video files and the transcript
- [ ] The physical object, if relevant (a broken device, a torn sign)
- [ ] Receipts for the amount claimed and for your expenses (travel, lost workday)
- [ ] This sheet, with notes
- [ ] Water; time for the security check (arrive 30 minutes early)

## 7. On the day

- [ ] Phone off in the courtroom; no filming or photographing.
- [ ] Answer the question asked, briefly; point to the exhibit number.
- [ ] Do not interrupt; do not argue politics; address the judge as "כבוד השופט/ת".
- [ ] If something important is missing from the protocol, ask politely that it be recorded.
- [ ] If a settlement is proposed: ask for a few minutes; read every word before agreeing.
