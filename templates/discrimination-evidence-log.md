# Discrimination Evidence Log (יומן ראיות להפליה)

A log for collecting and keeping evidence in a discrimination case, with a tester plan and a skeleton for witness statements. Taught in [lesson 09.5](../lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md) and used in the [Module 9 project](../projects/m09-discrimination-file.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For use in your own case or your group's own case. This is not legal advice. Record only conversations you take part in (Wiretap Law ss. 1–2); photograph barriers and scenes from public areas, not people in private areas (Protection of Privacy Law s. 2(3)); check defamation and privacy before publishing anything (lessons 08.1–08.4).

## How to use it

1. Open the log on the day of the incident. One log per incident.
2. Give every item a number. Keep the original file untouched; work on copies.
3. Store originals in two places (for example the phone and a cloud folder) and write both locations.
4. For each item, write which element of the claim or which presumption it supports.
5. Write witness statements the same day, signed and dated.
6. If you use testers, plan the check first (part B) and change one variable only.
7. Ask the business in writing to preserve CCTV within a day; log the request.

What we will *not* do, and why:

- No recording of conversations we are not part of (Wiretap Law s. 2).
- No photographs of people in private areas (Privacy Law s. 2(3)).
- No invented documents or identities.
- No posting faces or names before the legal step and the 08.1–08.4 check.

## D. CCTV preservation request

```text
לכבוד [שם העסק / הגוף]
[דוא"ל]

הנדון: בקשה לשמירת צילומי מצלמות אבטחה

ביום [תאריך] בין השעות [___] ל-[___] התרחש אירוע בכניסה ל[שם המקום], שבו [תיאור קצר וניטרלי].
אבקש לשמור את צילומי מצלמות האבטחה המתעדים את הכניסה ואת אזור הקופה / הדלפק בטווח השעות האמור, ולא למחוק אותם, לצורך הליך משפטי אפשרי.

בכבוד רב,
[שם מלא] [טלפון]
```

*Gist:* on [date] between [times] an incident took place at your entrance; please keep the security-camera footage of the entrance and the desk for those hours and do not delete it, as it may be needed in legal proceedings.

## E. Witness statement skeleton

```text
הצהרה

אני, [שם מלא], ת"ז [מספר], מצהיר/ה כדלקמן:

1. ביום [תאריך] בשעה [שעה] בערך הייתי ב[מקום] יחד עם [שמות].
2. ראיתי ושמעתי את הדברים הבאים: [עובדות בלבד, לפי הסדר, בגוף ראשון].
3. [אם בודק/ת: ביקשתי ___, באותן מילים שבהן השתמש/ה ___; התשובה הייתה ___.]
4. [אם הקלטתי שיחה שאני צד לה: ההקלטה נשמרה ב___, בשעה ___, אורכה ___.]
5. כתבתי הצהרה זו ביום [תאריך], בשעה [שעה].

[חתימה]
```

*Gist:* who I am; where and when I was there and with whom; what I saw and heard, as facts, in order; if I was a tester, what I asked and what I was told; if I recorded my own conversation, where the file is; when I wrote this statement. For court, it may later need to become an affidavit (lesson 02.6).

## Checklist

- [ ] Log opened on the day; every item numbered.
- [ ] Originals untouched and stored in two places.
- [ ] Every recording is of a conversation the recorder took part in.
- [ ] Each item says what it proves.
- [ ] Statements written and signed the same day.
- [ ] CCTV preservation request sent within a day.
- [ ] Nothing published before the legal step and the 08.1–08.4 check.
