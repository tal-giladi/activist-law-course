# Glossary

The Hebrew legal terms used in the course, each given in Hebrew, then a transliteration, then its English meaning. Terms are added module by module.

## Terms

- **אבחנה** (avchana) — Distinguishing: showing a ruling does not apply to your facts.
- **אגודה עותמאנית** (aguda otma'anit) — Ottoman association; pre-1980 form, not used for new groups.
- **אגרה שנתית** (agra shnatit) — Annual fee to the Registrar.
- **אגרת בקשה** (agrat bakasha) — FOI request fee, paid on filing.
- **אגרת הפקה** (agrat hafaka) — FOI production fee, per page or disc.
- **אגרת השתתפות** (agrat hishtatfut) — Participation fee paid by an approved Legal Aid applicant.
- **אגרת טיפול** (agrat tipul) — FOI handling fee, charged per hour from the fourth hour.
- **אזהרה מוקדמת אפקטיבית** (azhara mukdemet efektivit) — Effective advance warning of attacks (customary Rule 20).
- **איזון אנכי / איזון אופקי** (izun anchi / izun ofki) — Vertical balance (right against public interest) / horizontal balance (right against another person's right).
- **אימוץ בחקיקה** (imutz bechakika) — Incorporation of a treaty into Israeli law by legislation.
- **אימות** (imut) — Verification: establishing whether information is accurate and genuine.
- **אימות דו־שלבי** (imut du-shlavi) — Two-factor authentication (2FA).
- **איסור פרסום** (isur pirsum) — Publication ban (gag order).
- **אישור מסירה** (ishur mesira) — Proof of delivery.
- **אישור ניהול תקין** (ishur nihul takin) — Proper-management certificate issued by the Registrar.
- **איתור מיקום** (itur mikum) — Geolocation: finding where an image or video was taken.
- **אמנה** (amana) — Treaty (convention, covenant, agreement): a written agreement between states governed by international law.
- **אמנת זכויות אדם** (amanat zchuyot adam) — Human-rights treaty (convention, covenant).
- **אמצעי זהירות** (emtza'ei zehirut) — Precautions in attack and against the effects of attacks.
- **אמצעי עזר** (emtza'ei ezer) — Subsidiary means: judicial decisions and scholarly writing, used to find the law.
- **אמרת אגב** (imrat agav) — obiter dictum: a remark in passing, not binding.
- **אסיפה** (asefa) — Assembly: 50+ people gathered outdoors to hear a speech on a political matter (Police Ordinance s. 83).
- **אסיפה כללית** (asefa klalit) — General assembly of members; at least once a year.
- **אסמכתא** (asmachta) — Reference number of an electronic filing.
- **אסמכתה** (asmachta) — Authority: the source cited for a rule.
- **אפשרות ממשית** (efsharut mamashit) — Real possibility: the probability test for incitement to violence and terror.
- **אצילת סמכויות** (atzilat samchuyot) — Delegation of a power to another official.
- **ארגז כלים** (argaz kelim) — Toolbox: the set of procedures you can use against an authority.
- **אשרור** (ishrur) — Ratification: the international act by which a state consents to be bound by a treaty.
- **בג״ץ** (bagatz) — High Court of Justice.
- **בדיקה מקדימה** (bdika mukdemet) — Preliminary check before a criminal investigation.
- **בדלתיים סגורות** (bidlatayim sgurot) — In closed session.
- **בודק / בודקת** (bodek / bodeket) — Tester sent to check whether a business treats groups differently.
- **ביטול החלטה לפי צד אחד** (bitul hachlata lefi tzad echad) — Setting aside a decision given in a party's absence.
- **ביטול רישום** (bitul rishum) — Cancellation of a police-register entry.
- **ביקורת שיפוטית על חוקי יסוד** (bikoret shiputit al chukei yesod) — Judicial review of Basic Laws.
- **ביקורת שיפוטית על חוקים** (bikoret shiputit al chukim) — Judicial review of statutes.
- **בית דין לעבודה** (beit din la-avoda) — Labour Court.
- **בית הדין הבינלאומי לצדק** (beit hadin habeinleumi letzedek) — International Court of Justice (ICJ), the principal judicial organ of the UN.
- **בית הדין הפלילי הבינלאומי** (beit hadin haplili habeinle'umi) — International Criminal Court (ICC), The Hague; tries individuals, unlike the ICJ.
- **בית המשפט המחוזי** (beit ha-mishpat ha-mechozi) — District Court.
- **בית משפט השלום** (beit mishpat ha-shalom) — Magistrates' Court.
- **בית משפט לעניינים מינהליים** (beit mishpat le-inyanim minhaliyim) — Administrative Affairs Court.
- **בית משפט לתביעות קטנות** (beit mishpat litvi'ot ktanot) — Small Claims Court: a Magistrates' Court authorised under Courts Law s. 59; only an individual may file (s. 60).
- **בלי לפגוע בזכויות** (bli lifgo'a bezchuyot) — "Without prejudice to rights".
- **במעמד צד אחד** (bema'amad tzad echad) — Ex parte: in the absence of the other side.
- **בעל דין בלתי מיוצג** (ba'al din bilti meyutzag) — A party without a lawyer.
- **בעל שיחה** (ba'al sicha) — A party to a conversation (Wiretap Law s. 1).
- **בעל שליטה** (ba'al shlita) — Controller of a database: who decides the purposes of processing.
- **בעלי עניין** (ba'alei inyan) — Stakeholders in the UPR: NGOs, national institutions, academics and others.
- **בקשה להארכת מעצר** (bakasha leha'arachat ma'atzar) — Police request to extend custody (remand).
- **בקשת חופש מידע** (bakashat chofesh meida) — Freedom of Information (FOI) request.
- **בקשת רשות לערור** (bakashat reshut la'aror) — Request for leave to appeal.
- **בקשת רשות ערעור (בר״ע)** (bakashat reshut ir'ur) — Request for leave to appeal.
- **בשלות** (bshelut) — Ripeness: whether a case is ready to be decided.
- **בתמורה** (bitmura) — for payment or other consideration; the other trigger of s. 20.
- **גדר ההפרדה** (geder hahafrada) — The separation barrier ("seam-zone obstacle").
- **גוף מבוקר** (guf mevukar) — Audited body under s. 9 of the State Comptroller Law.
- **גוף מעין שיפוטי** (guf me'ein shiputi) — Quasi-judicial body, such as a medical appeals committee.
- **גוף נילון** (guf nilon) — The body a complaint is about.
- **גורם המפגע** (gorem hamifga) — The person or body causing an environmental hazard.
- **גישור** (gishur) — Mediation.
- **גריעה מהתחייבויות** (gri'a mehitchayvuyot) — Derogation: suspension of a treaty obligation in a public emergency (ICCPR art. 4); Israel's covers art. 9.
- **דואליזם** (dualizm) — Dualism: international and domestic law as separate systems.
- **דווח/ת מיוחד/ת** (dove'ach/dovachat meyuchad/et) — Special Rapporteur.
- **דוח ביקורת** (doch bikoret) — Audit report.
- **דוח כספי** (doch kaspi) — Annual financial report, filed by 30 June.
- **דוח מילולי** (doch miluli) — Annual verbal (activity) report, filed with the financial report.
- **דוח צל / דוח חלופי** (doch tzel / doch chalufi) — Shadow (alternative) report from civil society.
- **דוח רבעוני** (doch riv'oni) — Quarterly report on donations from foreign state entities.
- **דוח תקופתי** (doch tkufati) — Periodic report of a state to a treaty body.
- **דורש עבודה** (doresh avoda) — Job applicant.
- **דחייה על הסף** (dchiya al hasaf) — Dismissal at the threshold (in limine).
- **דיאלוג בונה** (di'alog bone) — Constructive dialogue: the public session between a committee and the state delegation.
- **דיוג** (diyug) — Phishing.
- **דיון מהיר** (diyun mahir) — Fast track for claims up to ₪75,000 and evictions (reg. 78).
- **דיון מוקדם / קריאה טרומית** (diyun mukdam / kri'a tromit) — Preliminary reading of a private bill, at least 45 days after tabling.
- **דיון נוסף** (diyun nosaf) — further hearing in the Supreme Court before a larger panel.
- **דין** (din) — law in the broad sense defined by the Interpretation Law.
- **דין וחשבון נכון והוגן** (din vecheshbon nachon vehogen) — Fair and accurate report, a defence to incitement offences.
- **דין רציפות** (din retzifut) — Continuity: reviving in the new Knesset a bill that passed its first reading in the outgoing one.
- **דינו** (dino) — "his penalty is"; introduces a criminal penalty.
- **דעה חולקת** (de'a choleket) — Dissenting opinion.
- **דעה נעולה** (de'a ne'ula) — A closed mind: a decision made in advance, whatever is said.
- **דעת רוב / דעת מיעוט** (da'at rov / da'at mi'ut) — majority / minority opinion.
- **דרך עיסוק** (derech isuk) — as an occupation; one trigger of the Bar Association Law s. 20 reservation.
- **האזנת סתר** (ha'azanat seter) — Wiretapping: listening without the consent of any party (Wiretap Law s. 1).
- **הבטחת הכנסה** (havtachat hachnasa) — Income support from National Insurance; prima facie proof of inability to pay a court fee.
- **הבעת דעה** (havat de'a) — Expression of opinion, as opposed to a statement of fact (קביעה עובדתית).
- **הגדרות** (hagdarot) — definitions section.
- **הגנת אמת הפרסום** (haganat emet hapirsum) — Truth defence: true and in the public interest (s. 14).
- **הגנת תום לב** (haganat tom lev) — Good-faith defence in one of the circumstances listed in s. 15.
- **הגשה מהירה** (hagasha mehira) — Quick filing in Net HaMishpat, without entering the case; cannot open a proceeding.
- **ההליכים המיוחדים** (hahalichim hameyuchadim) — Special Procedures: independent experts appointed by the Human Rights Council.
- **ההסכמים יש לקיימם** (hahaskamim yesh lekayemam) — Pacta sunt servanda: treaties in force must be performed in good faith.
- **ההתנתקות** (hahitnatkut) — The 2005 disengagement from the Gaza Strip.
- **הודעת הארכה** (hoda'at ha'arracha) — Written, reasoned notice extending an FOI deadline.
- **הודעת עדכון** (hoda'at idkun) — Update notice, often filed by the State when it changes position.
- **הוועדה לבחירת שופטים** (havaada livchirat shoftim) — The Judicial Selection Committee.
- **הוועדה למתחמים מועדפים לדיור (ותמ״ל)** (vatmal) — Preferred Housing Compounds Committee under a 2014 temporary law; anyone may object.
- **הוועדה לתשתיות לאומיות (ות״ל)** (vatal) — National Infrastructure Committee; its plans are approved by the Government.
- **הוצאה לפועל** (hotza'a lapo'al) — Execution Office: enforces unpaid judgments.
- **הוצאות** (hotza'ot) — costs a party is ordered to pay.
- **הוצאות משפט** (hotza'ot mishpat) — Costs a court orders one party to pay another.
- **הוראות התכנית** (hora'ot hatochnit) — A plan's written instructions.
- **הוראת הסמכה** (hora'at hasmacha) — The enabling provision that gives an authority its power.
- **הוראת התפזרות** (hora'at hitpazrut) — Dispersal order (Penal Law s. 153).
- **הוראת מינהל** (hora'at minhal) — administrative instruction (permit, notice, appointment) given in writing under a law.
- **הוראת שעה** (hora'at sha'a) — Temporary provision with an end date.
- **הזדהות עם ארגון טרור** (hizdahut im irgun teror) — Identifying with a terror organisation (Counter-Terrorism Law s. 24).
- **הזכות לשוויון** (hazchut leshivyon) — The right to equality.
- **הזמנה לדין** (hazmana ladin) — Summons to the hearing.
- **החלטה** (hachlata) — Decision: a ruling on an interim matter.
- **החלטת הררי** (hachlatat Harari) — The Harari decision (13.6.1950): the constitution will be written chapter by chapter, each chapter a Basic Law.
- **החלק האופרטיבי** (hachelek ha'operativi) — The operative part (dispositif) of an ICJ decision or opinion: the numbered conclusions and votes.
- **הטרדה מאיימת** (hatrada me'ayemet) — Threatening harassment (stalking), including online (Prevention of Stalking Law s. 2).
- **היוועצות בעורך דין** (hiva'atzut be'orech din) — Consulting a lawyer before or during interrogation.
- **היחידה הממשלתית לחופש המידע** (hayechida hamemshaltit lechofesh hameida) — Government FOI Unit in the Ministry of Justice, supervising ministries and their units.
- **היחידה לתלונות הציבור** (hayechida letlunot hatzibur) — Police Public Complaints Unit (disciplinary and service complaints).
- **היקש** (hekesh) — analogy.
- **היתר בנייה** (heter bniya) — Building permit.
- **היתר פליטה** (heter plita) — Emission permit under the Clean Air Law 2008.
- **היתר שדלן** (heter shadlan) — Lobbyist permit issued by a Knesset committee (Knesset Law s. 66).
- **הכרזת בטלות מושהית** (hachrazat batlut mushhet) — Suspended declaration of invalidity.
- **הכרעה על יסוד הכתב** (hachra'a al yesod haktav) — Decision on the papers, without a hearing (small-claims reg. 13(ג), from 11.4.2025).
- **הכתבה** (hachtava) — Dictation: deciding on someone else's instruction instead of using one's own discretion.
- **הליך התלונות של המועצה** (halich hatlunot shel hamo'etza) — The Council's confidential complaint procedure for patterns of gross violations.
- **הליך מעקב** (halich ma'akav) — Follow-up procedure on priority recommendations, usually after a year.
- **הליך שנוי במחלוקת** (halich shanuy bemachloket) — Contentious case at the ICJ: a dispute between states ending in a binding judgment.
- **הלכה** (halacha) — Binding legal rule set by a court (precedent).
- **הלכה פסוקה / הלכה** (halacha psuka / halacha) — binding precedent; the holding of a case.
- **הלשכה המקדמית** (halishka hamukdemet) — Pre-Trial Chamber of the ICC, which authorises investigations and issues warrants.
- **המהפכה החוקתית** (hamahapecha hachukatit) — The "constitutional revolution": the 1992 Basic Laws and the Mizrahi Bank ruling (1995).
- **המועצה הארצית לתכנון ולבניה** (hamo'atza ha'artzit) — National Planning and Building Council; decides national outline plans and appeals against district committees (s. 110).
- **המחלקה לחקירות שוטרים (מח״ש)** (hamachlaka lechakirot shotrim, Machash) — Police Investigations Department in the Ministry of Justice.
- **המלצה נתמכת / המלצה שנרשמה** (hamlatza nitmechet / hamlatza shenirshema) — Supported / noted UPR recommendation.
- **הממונה על המחוז** (hamemune al hamachoz) — District Commissioner of the Interior Ministry.
- **הממונה על תלונות הציבור ברשות המקומית** (hamemune al tlunot hatzibur) — The local authority's Public Complaints Commissioner, usually its internal auditor.
- **המעצמה הכובשת** (hama'atzama hakoveshet) — Occupying Power.
- **המפקד הסביר** (hamefaked hasavir) — The "reasonable commander" standard for judging an attack on the information available at the time.
- **המצאה** (hamtza'a) — Service of court documents.
- **המרשם המשטרתי** (hamirsham hamishtarti) — Police register: open and closed files, stayed cases, acquittals.
- **המרשם הפלילי** (hamirsham hapli'li) — Criminal register: convictions and some court orders.
- **המשפט ההומניטרי הבינלאומי / דיני הלחימה** (hamishpat hahumanitari habeinleumi / dinei halechima) — International humanitarian law (IHL), also called the law of armed conflict (LOAC).
- **המשפט המוניציפאלי** (hamishpat hamunitzipali) — Municipal (domestic) law, as opposed to international law.
- **המתנגד העקבי** (hamitnaged ha'akavi) — Persistent objector: a state not bound by a customary rule it objected to while the rule was forming.
- **הנדון** (hanadon) — "Re:", the subject line of a letter.
- **הנחה על שולחן הכנסת** (hanacha al shulchan haknesset) — Tabling: formal publication of a bill in the Knesset.
- **הנחיות היועמ״ש** (hanchayot ha-yoamash) — Attorney General guidelines.
- **הנחיות היועץ המשפטי לממשלה** (hanchayot hayoetz hamishpati lamemshala) — Attorney General guidelines, binding inside government.
- **הסגת גבול המקצוע** (hasagat gvul ha-miktzoa) — unauthorised practice of law (Bar Association Law s. 96).
- **הסדר למניעת ניגוד עניינים** (hesder limni'at nigud inyanim) — Conflict-of-interest arrangement set by a legal adviser.
- **הסדרים ראשוניים** (hesderim rishoniyim) — Primary arrangements: basic policy choices the Knesset itself must make.
- **הסיוע המשפטי** (hasiyu'a hamishpati) — The Legal Aid department of the Ministry of Justice.
- **הסכם התארגנות** (heskem hitargenut) — Written group agreement of an unregistered group.
- **הסכם שכר טרחה** (heskem schar tircha) — Written fee agreement with a lawyer.
- **הסכמה מדעת** (haskama mida'at) — Informed consent.
- **הסמכה** (hasmacha) — authorisation: the section that gives a power.
- **הסמכה מפורשת** (hasmacha meforeshet) — Express statutory authorisation, required to limit a basic right.
- **הסקירה התקופתית האוניברסלית** (haskira hatkufatit ha'universalit) — Universal Periodic Review (UPR) of every UN member by other states.
- **הסרת תוכן** (hasarat tochen) — Content removal (takedown).
- **הסתה לאלימות** (hasata le'alimut) — Incitement to violence (Penal Law s. 144ד2).
- **הסתה לגזענות** (hasata legiz'anut) — Incitement to racism (Penal Law s. 144ב).
- **הסתייגות** (histaygut) — Reservation: an amendment rejected in committee and attached to the bill for the second reading.
- **העברת אוכלוסייה** (ha'avarat ochlusiya) — Transfer of population (Fourth Geneva Convention art. 49).
- **העדפה מתקנת** (ha'adafa metakenet) — Affirmative action (Women's Equal Rights Law s. 1ב).
- **הערות מסכמות** (he'arot mesakmot) — Concluding observations: a committee's findings and recommendations.
- **העתק** (he'etek) — Copy (cc).
- **הפליה** (haflaya) — Discrimination.
- **הפליה / אפליה** (haflaya / aflaya) — Discrimination; statutes use הפליה, everyday Hebrew says אפליה.
- **הפקדה** (hafkada) — Deposit: publication of a plan for public objections.
- **הפרעה לשוטר** (hafra'a leshoter) — Obstructing a police officer (Penal Law s. 275).
- **הפרקליט הצבאי הראשי (פצ״ר)** (hapraklit hatzva'i harashi, patzar) — Military Advocate General (MAG), head of the IDF's legal and prosecution corps.
- **הפרת הוראה חוקית** (hafarat hora'a chukit) — Breach of a lawful order (Penal Law s. 287).
- **הצדק במלחמה / הצדק לפתוח במלחמה** (hatzedek bamilchama / hatzedek liftoach bemilchama) — Jus in bello (conduct of war) / jus ad bellum (resort to force).
- **הצהרה לפי סעיף 36(2)** (hatzhara lefi se'if 36(2)) — Optional-clause declaration accepting the ICJ's compulsory jurisdiction (Israel has none in force).
- **הצהרה פרשנית** (hatzhara parshanit) — Interpretative declaration: a state's statement of how it understands a treaty provision.
- **הצטרפות** (hitztarfut) — Accession: joining a treaty without having signed it first.
- **הצעת חוק מטעם ועדה** (hatza'at chok mita'am va'ada) — Committee bill; only certain committees, on Basic Laws, the Knesset, elections, parties and the State Comptroller (Rules s. 80).
- **הצעת חוק ממשלתית** (hatza'at chok memshaltit) — Government bill, submitted by the government as a whole.
- **הצעת חוק פרטית** (hatza'at chok pratit) — Private member's bill, submitted by an MK who is not a minister or deputy minister.
- **הצעת נוסח** (hatza'at nusach) — Proposed wording of a clause.
- **הצפנה מקצה לקצה** (hatzpana mikatze lekatze) — End-to-end encryption.
- **הקלה** (hakala) — Permitted deviation from a plan, decided at permit stage after a 15-day objection period (s. 149).
- **הקצין הממונה** (hakatzin hamemune) — Officer in charge at the station who decides on custody or release.
- **הרחקה** (harchaka) — Exclusion order from a place or area.
- **הרכב** (herkev) — panel of judges.
- **הרשות להגנת הפרטיות** (harashut lehaganat hapratiyut) — Privacy Protection Authority, Ministry of Justice.
- **השגה** (hasaga) — Challenge to a national infrastructure plan (s. 76ג(8)).
- **השמדת עם (רצח עם)** (hashmadat am, retzach am) — Genocide: listed acts committed with intent to destroy a national, ethnic, racial or religious group as such; defined in Israel by the Genocide Law of 1950.
- **השתתפות ישירה בלחימה** (hishtatfut yeshira belechima) — Direct participation in hostilities: civilians lose protection only for such time as they take part.
- **התאמות נגישות** (hat'amot negishut) — Accessibility adjustments.
- **התארגנות / קבוצה לא רשומה** (hitargenut / kvutza lo rshuma) — Informal, unregistered group; not a legal person.
- **התוספת הראשונה** (hatosefet harishona) — First Schedule of the Administrative Courts Law: matters the Administrative Court hears.
- **התוספת הרביעית** (hatosefet harevi'it) — Fourth Schedule: Judea and Samaria matters heard by the Administrative Court in Jerusalem.
- **התחזות לעורך דין** (hitchazut le-orech din) — Impersonating a lawyer (s. 97).
- **התיישנות / מחיקה** (hitya'shnut / mechika) — Limitation / deletion periods of a criminal-register entry.
- **התייתרות העתירה** (hityatrut ha'atira) — A petition becoming redundant because the remedy was given.
- **התנגדות** (hitnagdut) — Objection to a deposited plan (ss. 100–103א).
- **התפרעות** (hitpar'ut) — Riot (Penal Law s. 152).
- **התקהלות אסורה** (hitkahalut asura) — Unlawful assembly under the Penal Law: 3+ people whose conduct gives reasonable grounds to fear a breach of the peace (s. 151).
- **התקהלות בלתי חוקית** (hitkahalut bilti chukit) — Unlawful assembly under the Police Ordinance (s. 89).
- **ובלבד ש…** (u-vilvad she…) — provided that.
