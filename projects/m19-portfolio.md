# Module 19 project: The practicum portfolio

Assemble one portfolio of every real action you took in the practicum: ten court observation reports, a Freedom of Information campaign, a complaint to an oversight body, participation in a Knesset committee or council meeting, a placement of at least three months, and an international submission. Each action is a portfolio entry with its documents, timeline, outcome, reflection and rubric score. The portfolio replaces the internship's record of practice: it shows what you have actually done, in your own name, and what you learned.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Every action in the portfolio is real and was taken in your own name, or in the name of a group or organisation that authorised you. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing anything. Before you show the portfolio to anyone, remove ID numbers, addresses, phone numbers and the names of private people who did not agree to be named; keep consent records in a separate, secure folder.

## What you produce

1. **Cover page** (half a page): your name, the period of the practicum, the causes you worked on, total hands-on hours, and a one-paragraph summary.
2. **Index** (a table): entry number, lesson, action, body, dates, status (closed / pending), rubric score.
3. **Six entries**, each on the [portfolio entry](../templates/portfolio-entry.md) template:
   - **P-01** Court observation (lesson 19.1): ten [court observation reports](../templates/court-observation-report.md) attached.
   - **P-02** FOI campaign (lesson 19.2): requests, receipts, tracking table, follow-up, analysis and output.
   - **P-03** Oversight complaint (lesson 19.3): eligibility check, letter to the body, complaint, finding.
   - **P-04** Knesset or council (lesson 19.4): request or question, paper, attendance notes, protocol extract.
   - **P-05** Placement (lesson 19.5): agreement, placement log, work samples, supervisor's confirmation.
   - **P-06** International submission (lesson 19.6): deadline proof, submission or section, acknowledgement.
4. **Cross-cutting reflection** (one page): how the actions connect (for example, FOI data used in the complaint and the UN submission); what you would do first next time; which skill you need to build next; and how you kept to the legal limits on helping others.
5. **Safety and privacy statement** (half a page): what you redacted, where consent records are kept, and who may see the portfolio.
6. **Next steps** (five lines): what you will carry into the Module 20 capstone campaign.

Time: about 6 hours to assemble, after the six lessons. The practicum itself takes about 95 hours of hands-on work over four to six months (see each lesson).

## Steps

1. **Open the folder on day one of the module.** One subfolder per entry (P-01 to P-06) and a separate encrypted folder for consent records (Module 17).
2. **Run the six lessons in parallel.** Start the placement (19.5) and the FOI campaign (19.2) first — they take longest. Fit court days (19.1) and the council or committee session (19.4) around them. Start the complaint (19.3) when a real grievance arises. Plan the international submission (19.6) around a live deadline.
3. **Update each entry as things happen,** not at the end. The timeline is the evidence.
4. **Score each entry** with its lesson's rubric, with evidence for each score.
5. **Write the cover page, index, reflection and safety statement.**
6. **Redact.** Go through every exhibit and remove personal details that must not be shared.
7. **Self-assess** the whole portfolio with the rubric below, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 16 or more out of 20 means your portfolio is complete; below 12, finish or redo the weakest entries before Module 20.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completeness | Three or fewer entries | Four or five entries | All six entries, with pending items marked and dated |
| Reality and own name | Dry runs presented as real, or acts for others | Real actions, authority for group actions not recorded | Every action real, in own name or with written authorisation recorded |
| Court observation (P-01) | Fewer than ten reports | Ten, limited variety | Ten, varied, facts separate from analysis, lawful conduct |
| FOI campaign (P-02) | One request | Three requests, weak tracking or no output | Campaign with tracking, correct escalation and an output |
| Complaint (P-03) | Invented or ineligible | Eligible, weak exhibits or no follow-up | Eligible, letter first, facts-harm-defect-remedy, outcome tracked |
| Knesset or council (P-04) | Watched only | Attended without written input | Attended and a written input on the record, protocol checked |
| Placement (P-05) | Under two months | Two to three months or no confirmation | Three months or more, about 50 hours, log, samples, confirmation, s. 20 kept |
| International (P-06) | Not submitted | Submitted without consent records or deadline proof | Right route, live deadline checked, consent, acknowledgement |
| Reflection | None | Lists of what was done | Connections between actions, lessons learned, next skill |
| Safety and privacy | Personal details exposed | Partly redacted | Fully redacted; consent records separate and secure; sharing rules stated |
