# Module 5 project: From FOI request to petition

Take one real issue with a public authority all the way from a Freedom of Information request to a petition-ready file: the request, the follow-up, a complaint where it fits, the pre-petition letter, and a complete draft petition with its affidavit, fee route and service plan. You file the FOI request for real if you choose; the petition stays a draft unless you decide, on your own, to file it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only (or in the name of the group or amuta that is acting). This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing: the fees on [Current Numbers](../references/current-numbers.md), the regulations quoted in lessons 05.6–05.7, and the filing route with the judiciary information centre (*3852). Filing a petition can lead to a costs order against you; do not file one only to complete this project.

## What you produce

1. **A tool plan** (05.1): the problem in one sentence, the tools in order, the forum with its First Schedule item (or why the HCJ), and every deadline.
2. **An FOI request** using [foi-request.md](../templates/foi-request.md), with the undertaking and the fee decision.
3. **A follow-up file** using [foi-unit-complaint.md](../templates/foi-unit-complaint.md): the timeline, a reminder, and either an FOI Unit complaint (ministries) or the alternative route; plus a written analysis of the answer or refusal (the six-question refusal check from 05.3).
4. **Optional:** an ombudsman complaint or a Comptroller inquiry using [ombudsman-complaint.md](../templates/ombudsman-complaint.md), if your issue fits (05.4).
5. **A pre-petition letter** using [pre-petition-letter.md](../templates/pre-petition-letter.md).
6. **A petition-ready file**: a draft petition using [administrative-petition.md](../templates/administrative-petition.md) or [hcj-petition.md](../templates/hcj-petition.md), the supporting affidavit using [affidavit.md](../templates/affidavit.md) (unsigned), the exhibits list, the fee or exemption decision, and the service plan.

Time: about 8–10 hours of work, spread over 6–10 weeks because you wait for the authority's answers. If you do a dry run, invent realistic answers and dates, and say so at the top of the file.

## Steps

1. **Choose the issue.** A public authority holds information you need and has made (or may make) a decision you want changed. One sentence. Political direction does not matter; precision does.
2. **Tool plan (05.1).** Walk the decision tree. Check the First Schedule item and its exclusions. Write all clocks in one calendar.
3. **FOI request (05.2).** Search the answers database, find the FOI officer, name records, dates and format, decide the fee route, sign the undertaking, file (or prepare to file). Record receipt and payment dates.
4. **Day 30 (05.3).** Classify what happened. Send the reminder. File the FOI Unit complaint if the body is a ministry. Analyse any refusal item by item: section, type, partial release, carve-outs, third parties, public interest.
5. **Complaint or inquiry (05.4), if it fits.** Check the four conditions. Keep it parallel to court deadlines.
6. **Pre-petition letter (05.5).** From the decision date, count 45 days and work backwards. State the defects with their rules and the remedy as a court order. Send to the decision-maker with a copy to the legal adviser.
7. **Petition draft (05.6 or 05.7).** Forum and venue; respondents (including anyone harmed if you win); remedies first; facts with exhibits; exhaustion and timing; grounds; other proceedings; fee item or exemption. Fill in the reg. 5(ב) checklist (administrative) or the threshold checklist (HCJ).
8. **Affidavit.** Knowledge separated from belief, each belief with its source; unsigned; find a certifier and the price (02.6).
9. **Service and filing plan.** Who is served, at which address, how, by which date; when proof of service is uploaded; for the HCJ, the answer you got from *3852 about the filing route, with its date.
10. **Self-assess** with the rubric, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 16 or more out of 20 means your file is petition-ready; below 12, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tool plan and forum | No plan, or wrong court | Right tools, but forum or First Schedule item not checked | Tools in order, forum with item number and exclusions checked, all deadlines in one calendar |
| FOI request | Vague ("all information") or to the wrong body | Specific, but no format, partial-release or fee decision | Named officer, records, dates, format, partial release, reasoned-refusal request, fee or exemption, undertaking |
| Deadlines | Not calculated | Calculated but from the wrong start | 30 days from receipt and payment; extensions; 45 days from the refusal, marked |
| Follow-up | None | Reminder only, or complaint to the wrong body | Reminder citing s. 7(ב) and reg. 6א; FOI Unit complaint only for a ministry, or the right alternative |
| Refusal analysis | Missing | Sections named, no counter-arguments | Each item: section, must/may, s. 11, carve-outs, third parties, s. 17(ד) |
| Pre-petition letter | Missing, or after the deadline | Sent in time but with a vague remedy | Decision, defects with rules, remedy as an order, response date inside the deadline, legal adviser copied |
| Petition structure | Opinion piece | Most required elements, some missing | Every reg. 5(ב) element (or HCJ regs 2–4 and threshold) with paragraph references |
| Respondents | Only the obvious authority | Authorities complete, third party missed | Deciding authority, other authorities, and anyone harmed if you win |
| Affidavit and exhibits | Missing | Mixes argument with facts, or belief without source | Personal knowledge separated from belief with source; every fact has an exhibit |
| Fee, service and limits | Not considered | Fee known, service or proof-of-service missing | Fee item or exemption with affidavit; service within three days and proof; own name; real steps flagged |

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

This is a condensed model based on a realistic dry run. Your file will be about your own issue.

**1. Tool plan.**

Problem: the regional council approved, without a tender, a five-year lease of the community sports hall to a private club, and now charges residents' groups an hourly rate they cannot afford. Tools in order: FOI request (lease, council minutes, valuation) → reminder → refusal analysis → pre-petition letter to the council head → administrative petition. Forum: the FOI refusal is First Schedule item 2 (חופש המידע); the lease itself, if challenged later, is a local-authority and tender matter, items 8 (רשויות מקומיות) and 5 (מכרזים); no exclusion applies → Administrative Court, the District Court for the council's district (reg. 2(א)). No FOI Unit complaint: a regional council is not a ministry. Ombudsman: not used — the harm is to the group as a whole, and a petition is likely.

| Tool | Sent | Deadline | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| FOI request | 2.11 (receipt and payment) | 2.12 (30 days); 1.1 if extended | Reminder |
| Reminder | 3.12 | 10.12 | Refusal analysis, pre-petition letter |
| Pre-petition letter | 21.12 (after the refusal of 15.12) | Answer by 4.1 | Petition |
| Petition | Filed 20.1 | 45 days from 15.12 = 29.1 | — |

**2. FOI request (extract).**

```text
1.1 הסכם השכירות או ההרשאה בין המועצה לבין [המועדון] בעניין אולם הספורט ב___, לרבות נספחיו.
1.2 פרוטוקולי ישיבות מליאת המועצה, ועדת הכספים וועדת המכרזים שבהם נדון ההסכם, מן התקופה 1.1.2025 עד 30.9.2026.
1.3 כל שומה או חוות דעת שמאית שהוכנה לקביעת דמי השימוש באולם.
2. ... בקובץ PDF הניתן לחיפוש.
3. ... השמטת הפרטים החסויים וציון מקום ההשמטה (סעיף 11) ... הודעה מנומקת (סעיף 7(ו)).
5. אגרת הבקשה שולמה ביום 2.11; כתב התחייבות מצורף.
```

**3. Follow-up and refusal analysis.** No answer by 2.12 and no extension notice; a reminder on 3.12 cites s. 7(ב), reg. 9(א) and reg. 6א. On 15.12 the council releases the minutes but refuses the lease agreement as "a trade secret of the club" (s. 9(ב)(6)) and the valuation as "internal deliberation" (s. 9(ב)(4)).

| Item | Section | Must/may | Partial release? | Counter-argument |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lease | 9(ב)(6) | May | Yes: the rent and term can be released with the club's internal details redacted | A lease of public property is not the club's trade secret; public interest in the use of public assets (s. 17(ד)); the club must be notified as a third party (s. 13) |
| Valuation | 9(ב)(4) | May | Yes | A valuation is a professional opinion relied on for a decision already made, not ongoing deliberation; s. 10 interest |

**4. Pre-petition letter (extract).**

```text
הנדון: החלטת המועצה מיום 15.12 לסרב למסור את הסכם השכירות והשומה — מכתב התראה לפני הגשת עתירה
...
3.1 הסכם להקצאת נכס ציבורי אינו "סוד מסחרי" של המתקשר; ככל שיש בו פרטים מסחריים רגישים, ניתן למסור אותו תוך השמטתם (סעיף 11 לחוק).
3.2 השומה אינה "דיון פנימי" — היא חוות דעת מקצועית שעליה התבססה החלטה שכבר התקבלה, ולגילויה עניין ציבורי מובהק (סעיפים 10 ו-17(ד)).
4.1 למסור לי את ההסכם ואת השומה, בהשמטת פרטים מסחריים רגישים בלבד ובציון מקומם;
4.4 להשיב עד ליום 4.1.
```

Sent by email and registered mail to the council head, copy to the council's legal adviser. On 3.1 the legal adviser repeats the refusal.

**5. Petition (outline).**

- Court: District Court, sitting as an Administrative Court, for the council's district. Respondents: (1) the regional council; (2) the club, which may be harmed by disclosure (reg. 6(א); FOI Law s. 17(ג)).
- Remedies: (א) order the council to deliver the lease agreement and the valuation; (ב) alternatively, deliver them with specified commercial details redacted; (ג) costs.
- Jurisdiction (reg. 5(ב)(3א)): decision under FOI Law s. 7; First Schedule item 2 (חופש המידע).
- Exhaustion and timing: the request, the reminder, the refusal of 15.12, the pre-petition letter, the answer of 3.1; filed within 45 days of 15.12.
- Grounds: the s. 9(ב)(6) and 9(ב)(4) grounds do not fit; no consideration of partial release (s. 11); no weighing of the public interest (s. 10); the court may order disclosure under s. 17(ד).
- Other proceedings: none. Fee: item 20, paid.
- Affidavit: personal knowledge — the request, payments, the correspondence; belief with source — the hourly rate charged to groups, from the council's published price list (exhibit 6).
- Service: the council's legal adviser and the club's registered office by 23.1; proof uploaded the same day. Calendar: preliminary response if ordered; 60 days for an answer if ordered (reg. 10(א)).

Rubric self-score for this model: 18/20 (the ombudsman route was considered and rightly not used; the fee-and-service row loses a point because the plan names no back-up service address for the club).
