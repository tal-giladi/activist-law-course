# Module 10 project: Planning objection

Choose one deposited plan — or a request for a הקלה — that affects a place you care about, and build the complete file a planning activist would bring to the committee: the planning map of the case, a reading of the plan, the objection with its affidavit, a hearing summary, an appeal plan with every deadline, and, where the plan raises an environmental hazard, a complaint. The project is a dry run unless the plan really affects you and the objection period is open.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only (or as the representative of a group that signed). This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing: objection periods, appeal deadlines and appeal fees must be confirmed with the committee secretariat. Do not file an objection only to complete this project, and never put another person's details in a published objection without their consent.

## What you produce

1. **Case map** (one page): the plan, the deciding body, the special track if any, the objection body and the copy, the appeal route, the court ([issue-map.md](../templates/issue-map.md); lesson 10.1).
2. **Plan reading** (1–2 pages): purpose in one sentence, part A of the [planning-objection template](../templates/planning-objection.md), and a change table with at least five rows, each pointing to a chapter, table row or map layer (lesson 10.2).
3. **The objection** (3–6 pages) in Hebrew, with standing, deadline calculation, numbered requested changes, reasons and at least one alternative (lesson 10.3).
4. **The affidavit**, ready for certification, verifying every fact the objection relies on, with numbered exhibits.
5. **Hearing summary** (one page): a five-minute talk, who speaks, what map you show, what you ask the committee to decide (lesson 10.4).
6. **Appeal plan** (half a page): for an imagined rejection served on a date you choose — route, leave if needed, body, deadline, respondents, fee check, next court step ([planning-appeal.md](../templates/planning-appeal.md)).
7. **Optional:** if the plan creates or continues an environmental hazard, a Part A complaint and a list of emission-permit or other decision points ([environmental-complaint.md](../templates/environmental-complaint.md); lesson 10.5).

Time: about 5 hours, spread over the objection period.

## Steps

1. **Choose the plan.** Use Mavat or Meirim alerts. Prefer a deposited plan with an open objection period. Any cause, from any part of the political map: housing, open space, a road, a public building, a farm, a community's recognition, a settlement's plan, a factory.
2. **Map it (10.1).** Fill the case map, including the special-track check.
3. **Read it (10.2).** Status and deadline, purpose, both maps, rights table, appendices (binding or guiding), environmental review, deposit protocol. Send one FOI request for a missing document, if needed.
4. **Decide your position.** Write the harm in two sentences and the three to five changes you want. Decide: individual or representative objection (s. 103א(ב)).
5. **Write the objection (10.3)** with the template, strongest argument first.
6. **Write the affidavit.** Facts only; each exhibit mentioned.
7. **Plan the filing.** Online or paper, the copy and proof, a personal deadline a week early. Remove personal identifiers from the published text.
8. **Prepare the hearing (10.4).** One-page summary and one marked map.
9. **Plan the appeal (10.4).** Imagine a two-line rejection; write the route and deadlines.
10. **Environment (10.5), if relevant.** Complaint and decision points.
11. **Self-assess** with the rubric, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 13 or more out of 16 means your file is ready to file; below 9, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Case map | Deciding body guessed | Body right, route incomplete | Body by s. 62א or special track, objection body and copy, appeal route with leave rules, court by First Schedule |
| Plan reading | Purpose only | Map read; rights table skimmed | Approved vs proposed compared; rights table rows quoted; appendices marked binding or guiding; protocol read |
| Standing and deadline | Missing | Stated loosely | Interest and specific harm (s. 100; reg. 3); deadline from the later newspaper date (s. 102) and checked against Mavat |
| Requested changes | "Reject the plan" only | Some changes, vague | Numbered, specific changes on page one, with an alternative |
| Reasons | General complaints | Arguments without sources | Each argument: provision → harm → evidence → change; legal and planning arguments separated |
| Affidavit and exhibits | None | Affidavit with arguments in it | Facts only, each exhibit numbered and mentioned; group rules applied correctly |
| Filing and privacy | Not planned | Body right, copy forgotten | Right body, copy with proof, online or four copies, no identifiers in the published file |
| Hearing and appeal plan | None | Hearing notes only | One-page summary opening with the requests; correct appeal route, leave, deadlines, respondents and court step |
