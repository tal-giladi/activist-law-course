# Module 11 project: Small claim file

Build a complete, court-ready file for one civil dispute — real or realistic — from the go/no-go decision to the hearing: a decision memo, a demand letter, a statement of claim that meets the 2025 rules, an evidence plan, a fee decision, a filing-and-hearing timeline and a hearing checklist. Everything stays a dry run unless the dispute is your own and you decide, on your own, to send and file.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only. Only an individual may file a small claim, and only for their own claim. This is not legal advice — check current rules, the ceiling, the fee and every deadline on [Current Numbers](../references/current-numbers.md) and the judiciary's pages before you send or file anything. If you are the defendant in a regular-court case, get help early ([Where to Get Help](../references/where-to-get-help.md); lesson 11.6).

## What you produce

1. **A decision memo** (11.1), one page: what you want, the legal rule, claimant and defendant (legal names and numbers), the ladder of options and where you start, the cost-and-risk sheet, and your decision with dates.
2. **A demand letter** (11.2) using [pre-action-demand-letter.md](../templates/pre-action-demand-letter.md), or [discrimination-demand-letter.md](../templates/discrimination-demand-letter.md) for a discrimination case (lesson 09.2).
3. **A statement of claim** (11.2) using [small-claim-statement.md](../templates/small-claim-statement.md): full heading, a body of five pages or less with all seven reg. 3(ג) items, the truth declaration and a numbered exhibits list.
4. **An evidence plan** (11.5): one row per disputed fact, with proof, original, exhibit number and weak spot.
5. **A fee decision** (11.6): the fee calculated; pay or request an exemption. If you request one, draft it with [fee-exemption-request.md](../templates/fee-exemption-request.md).
6. **A timeline** (11.2–11.3): the letter deadline, filing day, the 60-day service check, the 40-day self-service date, the defence date, witness-summons and interpreter dates, the hearing, the judgment and the 30-day leave-to-appeal date. Note which Net HaMishpat path you would use for one likely later filing (for example, a postponement request).
7. **A hearing checklist** (11.5) using [hearing-checklist.md](../templates/hearing-checklist.md), with your two-minute opening and one-minute closing.

Time: about 5 hours. If you use a fictional dispute, say so at the top. You may use one of the disputes in lessons 11.1–11.6, but change at least two facts.

## Steps

1. **Choose the dispute.** Something you can prove: a deposit not returned, an event cancelled, equipment damaged, goods not delivered, spam, a discriminatory refusal. Write it in three neutral sentences.
2. **Decide (11.1).** Fill the memo. Check that you are the right claimant and that the amount fits under the ceiling; if it does not, write why you choose the Magistrates' Court and what changes (lesson 11.4).
3. **Write the demand letter (11.2).** Facts, rule, amount line by line, a 14-day deadline, the next step. Note how and when you would send it.
4. **Choose the court.** Apply reg. 2 and write the sub-regulation you rely on.
5. **Draft the claim (11.2).** Use the template; run its reg. 3 checklist line by line; count the pages.
6. **Build the evidence plan (11.5).** Mark weak spots and how you will fix them; decide on witnesses and whether a summons is needed.
7. **Decide the fee (11.6).** Calculate it; if you cannot pay, draft the exemption request and list your prima facie proof.
8. **Write the timeline (11.2–11.3)** with real dates.
9. **Fill the hearing checklist (11.5)** and rehearse your opening once aloud.
10. **Self-assess** with the rubric, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 16 or more out of 20 means your file is ready; below 12, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision memo | Missing, or "sue" with no reasons | Options listed, costs or risks missing | Ladder, cost-and-risk sheet, evidence and collectability weighed, dated decision |
| Claimant and court | Wrong claimant (a body in small claims) or wrong court | Right court, venue not justified | Right claimant, right court, reg. 2 sub-regulation cited |
| Defendant | Trade name only | Legal name, no number or address | Legal name, ID or company number, address for service |
| Demand letter | Missing or threatening | Facts or demand vague | Numbered dated facts, the rule, a calculated amount, a date, only the lawful next step |
| Claim: reg. 3 items | Several items missing | One or two missing | Every item of reg. 3(ב)–(ד) present, body five pages or less |
| Amount | A round number near the ceiling | Calculated, not supported | Line by line, each line with an exhibit |
| Evidence plan | A list of files | Facts without exhibits or weak spots | Every disputed fact with proof, original, exhibit and a fix for weak spots |
| Fee | Ignored | Calculated, no decision | Calculated with the current figure; pay or a complete exemption draft with proof |
| Timeline | Missing | Some dates wrong or missing | Service check, self-service, defence, witnesses, hearing, 30-day appeal, all right |
| Hearing preparation | Missing | Checklist without opening or closing | Checklist, two-minute opening, one-minute closing with the amount |

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

This is a condensed model based on a fictional dispute. Your file will be about your own dispute.

**Dispute (fictional).** Avital, an environmental activist from Jerusalem, booked a lecture hall from "Merkaz Hadarim Ltd" for a public evening on air pollution on 15 September 2026. She paid a ₪3,200 deposit by credit card on 1 August. The contract says the deposit is refunded in full if the hall cancels. On 12 September the hall cancelled "for a private event" and refused the refund. She had also paid ₪640 for printed invitations that became useless.

**1. Decision memo (extract).**

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| What I want | ₪3,200 deposit + ₪640 printing |
| Rule | Contract clause 7 (refund if the hall cancels); breach of contract |
| Claimant | Avital personally (she paid with her own card) |
| Defendant | Merkaz Hadarim Ltd, company no. 51-XXXXXXX, registered address from the Companies Registrar |
| Ladder | Demand letter → small claim (Jerusalem) |
| Cost and risk | Fee: 1% of ₪3,840 is under the minimum, so the minimum fee applies; one hearing day; modest costs risk; strong documents |
| Collectability | Active company, operating hall |
| Decision | Send letter 14.9; file 1.10 if no refund |

**2. Demand letter (extract).**

```text
הנדון: דרישה להשבת פיקדון בסך 3,200 ש"ח — הזמנת אולם ליום 15.9.2026
1. ביום 1.8.2026 הזמנתי מכם את אולם "הדרים" לערב ציבורי ביום 15.9.2026, ושילמתי פיקדון בסך 3,200 ש"ח בכרטיס אשראי. (נספח 1)
2. לפי סעיף 7 להסכם, "ביטול מצד המרכז יזכה את המזמין בהחזר מלא של הפיקדון". (נספח 2)
3. ביום 12.9.2026 הודעתם לי בוואטסאפ על ביטול האירוע "בשל אירוע פרטי", וסירבתם להשיב את הפיקדון. (נספח 3)
6.1 להשיב לי 3,200 ש"ח, וכן 640 ש"ח שהוצאתי על הדפסת הזמנות (נספח 4);
עד ליום 28.9.2026.
8. אם לא אקבל מענה עד המועד האמור, אשקול להגיש נגדכם תביעה בבית המשפט לתביעות קטנות בירושלים.
```

**3. Claim (checklist result).** Heading complete with her mobile and email and the company number. Body three pages: place (Jerusalem, reg. 2(א)(1) and (5)); facts 2.1–2.6 with exhibits 1–5; reason: breach of clause 7; amount: ₪3,200 + ₪640 = ₪3,840 plus expenses; documents list; witness: none besides herself; no preference for video, no decision on the papers; small claims in the last year: 0. Truth declaration: personal knowledge and the attached documents.

**4. Evidence plan (extract).**

| # | Fact | Proof | Original | Exhibit | Weak spot / fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deposit paid | Card statement, booking confirmation | Bank app; email | 1 | — |
| 2 | Refund if hall cancels | Signed contract, clause 7 | Paper | 2 | — |
| 3 | Hall cancelled | WhatsApp 12.9 | Phone | 3 | Export the chat with dates |
| 4 | Printing cost | Print-shop invoice | Paper | 4 | Hall may say it is not their fault — the invitations named the hall and date |
| 5 | Demand letter and no answer | Letter, postal receipt | Paper | 5 | — |

**5. Fee.** Avital works and can pay; she pays the minimum fee online by credit card.

**6. Timeline.** Letter 14.9 (deadline 28.9) → filing 1.10 → check service by 60 days before the hearing; if not served, serve herself by day 40 → defence due 30 days after service → hearing (date from the summons) → judgment within 7 days → leave to appeal 30 days, recess counted. If the hall asks to postpone: respond under its request number in "הגשות".

**7. Hearing checklist (extract).** Opening: "I booked and paid; clause 7 says the deposit is refunded if the hall cancels; the hall cancelled on 12 September in writing (exhibit 3) and kept ₪3,200. I ask for ₪3,200, ₪640 printing, and expenses." Closing: the table of ₪3,840 with exhibits 1 and 4.

Rubric self-score for this model: 18/20 (the amount row loses a point because the ₪640 printing claim needs one sentence on why the loss was foreseeable to the hall; the evidence row loses a point because no request was planned for the hall's booking calendar, which would show the "private event").
