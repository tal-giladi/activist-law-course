# Module 7 project: Arrest kit

Build a personal and group arrest kit for a real or realistic action, and an after-case plan for a closed file: a charge map, a filled-in arrest card, a briefed support person, an interrogation rule, a remand-hearing alternatives sheet, and two draft letters for cleaning up a record. Everything stays a dry run unless you have a real closed file and decide, on your own, to send the letters.

> [!WARNING]
> This project prepares you to protect your rights; it does not prepare you to defend yourself in court. In a real criminal case, get a lawyer or the Public Defender. Do not plan or carry out an offence to complete this project. Nothing here is legal advice; check current rules and phone numbers before any action.

## What you produce

1. **A charge map** (07.1): your planned action as a list of acts, each with the likely section, its class, the element the prosecution must prove, and an "I will not…" line.
2. **A filled arrest card** using [arrest-card.md](../templates/arrest-card.md) (07.2–07.3): front sentences learnt, back side filled, Public Defender number for your district checked this week.
3. **A support-person brief** (07.2, 07.4): the support-person sheet filled in, with a "not back by" time and the documents for a remand hearing.
4. **A timing sheet** (07.2): the latest time to a judge for three arrest times you choose, one of them on a Friday afternoon, each with its section.
5. **An interrogation rule and drill notes** (07.3): your three-line rule for the room, and notes from one drill with a partner.
6. **A remand alternatives sheet** (07.4): for the conditions the police would likely ask for after your action, a narrower alternative for each, with exceptions for work or studies, and the routes and deadlines to challenge each kind of decision.
7. **An after-case pack** (07.5): a record table for a real or fictional closed file, a draft request to change closure grounds using [closure-grounds-request.md](../templates/closure-grounds-request.md), and a draft viewing and cancellation request using [police-records-request.md](../templates/police-records-request.md), each with its date.

Time: about 5 hours of work, plus a 20-minute drill with a partner and a 15-minute briefing with your support person. If you use a fictional action or file, say so at the top.

## Steps

1. **Choose the action.** A protest, a sit-in, a vigil, a march — any cause, any side. One sentence on what you will do, where and when.
2. **Charge map (07.1).** Break the action into physical acts. For each: section, class (פשע / עוון / חטא), the element to prove, arrestable or not, and your "I will not" line. Mark the moment of greatest risk (usually the arrest itself).
3. **Arrest card (07.2–07.3).** Fill the front and back. Check the Public Defender's arrest-department number for your district on the Public Defender's gov.il page. Write your lawyer's or hotline number where you will carry it.
4. **Timing sheet (07.2).** For three arrest times, work out the latest time to a judge (s. 29(א)–(ד)), and when the 48-hour exception (s. 30) could apply.
5. **Support person (07.2, 07.4).** Choose someone who will not be at the action. Fill the support-person sheet together: address for an exclusion, a supervisor, work or study proof, the deposit you could afford. Agree the "not back by" time.
6. **Interrogation drill (07.3).** Run the drill from lesson 07.3 with a partner. Write your three-line rule and two things you would do differently.
7. **Remand alternatives (07.4).** Predict the conditions the police would ask for after your action. For each, write a narrower alternative (place, time, exceptions). Add a table of routes: officer's conditions (refuse → judge within 24 hours; ערר within 14 days), court decision (review any time on new facts; appeal within 30 days).
8. **After-case pack (07.5).** Use a real closed file or the fictional one in lesson 07.5. Fill the record table. Draft the request to change closure grounds, with evidence of lack of guilt. Draft the viewing note and the cancellation request, dated for the first day the waiting period ends.
9. **Self-assess** with the rubric, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 16 or more out of 20 means your kit is ready; below 12, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Charge map | Missing, or sections guessed | Sections right, classes or elements missing | Every act with section, class, element, arrestable or not, and an "I will not" line |
| Arrest card front | Missing | Filled but not learnt by heart | Learnt by heart; covers detention vs arrest, identity, lawyer, Public Defender, notice, phone search |
| Arrest card back and numbers | Missing | Filled, numbers not checked | Filled; Public Defender number checked this week with the date; lawyer or hotline written where it will survive |
| Timing sheet | Missing | Ordinary case right, Shabbat case wrong | All three right, with the s. 29 sub-section used each time |
| Support person | Nobody chosen | Chosen, but no documents or "not back by" time | Briefed; sheet complete; knows whom to call first and what to bring to court |
| Interrogation rule | Missing | A rule, but no drill | Rule tested in a drill; two improvements written |
| Remand alternatives | Missing | Alternatives vague ("release her") | Each condition with a narrower, specific alternative and exceptions |
| Routes and deadlines | Missing | Some routes confused (ערר vs appeal) | Refuse / ערר 14 days / review / appeal 30 days, each matched to the right decision |
| Request to change closure grounds | Missing | Argues weak evidence, or sent to the wrong body | Right body; current and requested grounds; facts and evidence of lack of guilt; reminder date |
| Records and cancellation | Missing | Viewing only, or waiting period wrong | Viewing note, record table, cancellation request dated after the right waiting period, with documents |
