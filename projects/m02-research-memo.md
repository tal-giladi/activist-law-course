# Module 2 project: Research memo

Write a two-to-four-page legal research memo on a real problem you care about, then use it to draft a formal letter to the responsible authority and, if your facts need proving, your own affidavit. This is the package you will build on in every later module: before you file anything, you research it this way.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This project is a dry run. You may send the letter if you wish: act in your own name only (or in the name of the group that is acting). This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. Do not sign the affidavit unless you are filing it for real, and then only in front of an authorised certifier.

## What you produce

1. **A research memo** using [research-memo.md](../templates/research-memo.md): question, short answer, the law, rulings, source map, analysis, missing proof, next step.
2. **A formal letter** using [formal-letter.md](../templates/formal-letter.md), built from the memo's analysis.
3. **An affidavit draft** using [affidavit.md](../templates/affidavit.md), covering the facts in your letter that someone could dispute (skip only if nothing in your letter is disputable, and say why in the memo).

Time: about 4–6 hours, spread over a week if you want to wait for answers to emails or phone calls.

## Steps

1. **Choose the problem.** A real, specific problem with a public authority: a ministry, a local authority, a public body. It can be yours, your neighbourhood's or your group's. One sentence.
2. **Write the question.** One question that can be answered yes or no (memo section 1).
3. **Find the law (02.1).** At least one statute, quoted exactly, checked against the Knesset National Legislation Database, with the newest amendment and the "checked on" date. Add regulations if your issue involves fees, forms or procedures.
4. **Find rulings (02.2).** At least one Supreme Court or HCJ ruling relevant to your rule, with the holding quoted and a "still good law" check. Try to find one that cuts against you.
5. **Map the sources (02.3).** At least three secondary sources (Kol Zchut, a Knesset Research paper, a think-tank or NGO paper, a journal article), each dated and each verified against the primary source it relies on. If your issue is contested, include two viewpoints.
6. **Analyse (02.4).** Issue, rule, element table (element | my fact | my proof), the strongest counter-argument and your answer, conclusion.
7. **List missing proof.** For each element without proof, how you will get it.
8. **Short answer and next step.** Write memo sections 2 and 8 last, now that you know the answer.
9. **Write the letter (02.5).** To the official with the power, with copies to the legal adviser; numbered facts, quoted law, one specific request with a date; attachments numbered; a delivery method that proves receipt; the 45-day and three-month dates in your calendar.
10. **Draft the affidavit (02.6).** Only facts you know personally, one per paragraph, exhibits numbered, unsigned. Find out who would certify it and at what price.
11. **Self-assess** with the rubric below, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 16 or more out of 20 means your package is ready to use; below 12, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | Missing, or several questions mixed | One question, but vague | One precise yes/no question |
| Law quoted and current | Paraphrased, or no source | Quoted, but not checked against the Knesset database | Exact quote, full title, database number, newest amendment, date checked |
| Rulings | None | Ruling cited without a "still good law" check | Holding quoted, further hearing and later rulings checked, dated |
| Opposing material | Ignored | Mentioned but not answered | An opposing ruling or source found and answered |
| Source map | No secondary sources, or used as legal authority | Sources listed but not verified or dated | Three or more, dated, each verified against a primary source |
| Element table | Missing | Elements listed, some facts or proofs missing and not flagged | Every element has a fact and a proof, or is in "missing proof" with a plan |
| Counter-argument | Missing | Named but not answered | Named, and answered with law or facts |
| Letter | Missing, or to the wrong body | Right body, but vague request or no proof-of-receipt plan | Right official, numbered, quoted law, specific timed request, proof of receipt, dates in calendar |
| Affidavit | Missing when needed | Includes hearsay, arguments or adjectives | Personal facts only, numbered, exhibits referred to, certifier and price known |
| Own name and limits | Written for someone else, or for payment | Own name but no "not legal advice" awareness | Own name or own group, limits respected, real steps flagged |

<details><summary>Model answer</summary>

This is a condensed model, based on the worked example in lesson 02.4. Your memo will be about your own issue and may be longer.

**Problem.** Parents in a neighbourhood asked their municipality on 1 June to install a pedestrian crossing outside a girls' school. On 20 August they received a one-line refusal with no reasons.

**1. Question.** Did the municipality act lawfully when it refused, 80 days after receiving the request, without giving reasons?

**2. Short answer.** No. It missed the 45-day deadline without the written notice the law requires, and refused without the written reasons the law requires. The refusal is not void for that alone, but the municipality now carries the burden of proving its decision lawful, and it must give reasons on request.

**3. The law.**

> נתבקש עובד הציבור, בכתב, להשתמש בסמכות שניתנה לו על פי דין, יחליט בבקשה וישיב למבקש בכתב בהקדם, אך לא יאוחר מארבעים וחמישה ימים מיום קבלת הבקשה.

חוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות), התשי״ט–1958, s. 2(א). Also ss. 1 (definition includes local-authority employees), 2(ב)–(ג), 2א, 6(א). Knesset database 2000832; newest amendment 1995; no pending bill; checked 12.10.2026. No future-dated wording. The municipal power over crossings comes from the traffic legislation, quoted in the memo with its own card.

**4. Rulings.** One HCJ ruling on an authority's duty to give reasons, holding quoted with paragraph number; no further hearing; cited approvingly in two later rulings; checked 12.10.2026. One ruling found that upheld a refusal where the authority had given detailed safety reasons — distinguished: here no reasons were given at all.

**5. Source map.**

| Source | Type | What it says | Checked | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kol Zchut page on the duty to answer | Secondary | Explains 45 days and reasons | 12.10.2026 | ✔ against the law |
| Knesset Research paper on road safety near schools | Secondary | Data on accidents near schools | 12.10.2026 | ✔ data only, not cited as law |
| Municipal road-safety plan (published) | Primary (municipal document) | Lists the street as "high risk" | 12.10.2026 | ✔ |

**6. Analysis.** Element table:

| Element | Fact | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Public servant | Municipal engineer | Municipal website |
| Written request | Letter of 1 June | Copy + email receipt |
| Use of a legal power | Placing crossings is a municipal power | Quoted traffic legislation |
| 45 days | Reply after 80 days | Date on reply |
| Reasons for refusal | None given | The reply |

Counter-argument: "the matter needed further examination" (s. 2(ב)(3)). Answer: s. 2(ג) required written notice of that reason within 45 days; none was given. Conclusion: ask for written reasons within 14 days and for reconsideration with the parents' traffic count attached.

**7. Missing proof.** A traffic count at school hours — two parents will count on three mornings and photograph the street; each will write their own affidavit.

**8. Next step.** Letter to the municipal engineer, copies to the legal adviser and the mayor's office, by registered mail with proof of delivery and by email; answer requested within 14 days; calendar: day 45 and three months from receipt. Affidavit: one per parent who counted; a local lawyer quoted a price for certifying a ready-written affidavit; checked 12.10.2026.

**Letter (opening paragraphs).**

```text
הנדון: בקשה לנימוקים ולשקילה מחדש — מעבר חציה ברחוב ___ ליד בית הספר ___
סימוכין: פנייתנו מיום 1.6 (נספח 1); תשובתכם מיום 20.8 (נספח 2)

1. ביום 1.6 פנינו אליך בכתב בבקשה להציב מעבר חציה ברחוב ___. הפנייה התקבלה ביום 1.6 (נספח 1).
2. ביום 20.8, 80 ימים לאחר מכן, התקבלה תשובה בת שורה אחת: "הבקשה נדחתה" (נספח 2). לא נמסרו נימוקים.
3. סעיף 2(א) לחוק לתיקון סדרי המינהל (החלטות והנמקות) מחייב החלטה ותשובה בתוך 45 ימים, וסעיף 2א מחייב הודעה בכתב על נימוקי סירוב.
4. לפיכך אבקש לקבל את נימוקי הסירוב בכתב עד ליום ___, ולשקול את הבקשה מחדש בהתחשב בספירת התנועה המצורפת (נספח 3).
```
