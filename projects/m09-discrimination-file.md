# Module 9 project: Discrimination file

Build a complete, court-ready file for one discrimination incident — real or realistic — from the first night to the first legal step: a discrimination map, an evidence log, a tester plan, a demand letter or commission complaint, and a decision memo on the route and the dates. Everything stays a dry run unless the incident happened to you and you decide, on your own, to send.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only (or with the written consent of the person affected, for a commission complaint). This is not legal advice — check current rules, ceilings and deadlines before you send or file anything. Do not run a real tester check or publish anything for this project unless the incident is your own and you have read lessons 08.1–08.4 and 09.5.

## What you produce

1. **A discrimination map** (09.1): one page — incident, area, statute and section, ground in the statute's words, likely exception and your answer, forum, commission, deadlines.
2. **An evidence log** (09.5) using [discrimination-evidence-log.md](../templates/discrimination-evidence-log.md): at least six items, each with what it proves, plus a CCTV preservation request.
3. **A tester plan** (09.2, 09.5): who, when, what they ask, what they record, one variable changed — or, for employment, a matched-application plan. If testing does not fit your case, explain why in two sentences and what replaces it.
4. **The first legal document**, one of:
   - a demand letter under the services law using [discrimination-demand-letter.md](../templates/discrimination-demand-letter.md) (09.2);
   - a complaint to the Equal Employment Opportunities Commission (09.3);
   - a complaint and, if useful, a query to the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities using [accessibility-complaint.md](../templates/accessibility-complaint.md) (09.4).
5. **A route-and-dates memo** (half a page): which route comes next if there is no answer (small claims, Magistrates', Labour Court, commission), why, the amount or remedy, the limitation date, and what you will and will not publish.

Time: about 5 hours. If you use a fictional incident, say so at the top. You may use one of the fictional incidents in lessons 09.1–09.5, but change at least two facts.

## Steps

1. **Choose the incident.** Any community, any ground: a Haredi family refused a holiday flat, an Arab job applicant, a settler refused delivery, a gay couple and a wedding venue, a blind person and a guide dog, a reservist dismissed. Write it in three neutral sentences.
2. **Map it (09.1).** Statute, section, the ground in Hebrew as the statute writes it, the exception the other side will raise, and the forum. Check that the ground is actually listed in that law.
3. **Find the burden shift (09.2–09.4).** Name the presumption (services s. 6; disability s. 19סה) or the burden shift (employment s. 9) and the fact that triggers it. If you have none yet, say what evidence would create it.
4. **Open the evidence log (09.5).** Enter what exists (messages, notes, photos, statements) and what you will ask for (CCTV, records). Mark each item with the element it proves. Draft the CCTV preservation request.
5. **Plan the test.** Write the tester or matched-application plan. List what you will not do and why (wiretapping, privacy, deception).
6. **Write the first document.** Use the template that fits. Facts numbered and dated; the law quoted; the presumption named; a specific demand or request; a date.
7. **Write the memo.** The next route, the amount or remedy, the limitation date and the commission time limits, Legal Aid if relevant, and your publication decision.
8. **Self-assess** with the rubric, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 16 or more out of 20 means your file is ready; below 12, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statute and forum | Wrong law or forum | Right law, forum or commission missing | Right law, section, forum and commission |
| Ground | Not identified | Described in general words | Quoted from the statute that applies, with the section |
| Exception | Ignored | Named but not answered | Named, with a fact that answers it |
| Burden shift | Missing | Named without the triggering fact | Named section and the fact that meets it |
| Evidence log | Missing or a list of files | Items listed without what they prove | Six or more numbered items, originals stored twice, each linked to an element |
| Lawfulness of evidence | Plan includes recording others or photos in private areas | Lawful, but limits not written | Lawful, with a written "will not do" list and reasons |
| Tester or matched plan | Missing | Changes more than one variable | One variable changed; same time, request and circumstances; statements planned |
| First document | Missing | Facts or demand vague | Numbered dated facts, the law, the presumption, a specific demand and date |
| Route and dates | Missing | Route chosen, dates wrong or missing | Next route with reasons; limitation and commission dates right |
| Publication decision | Not considered | "Will post later" | Reasoned decision linked to 08.1–08.4, facts only, after the legal step |

This is a condensed model based on a fictional incident. Your file will be about your own incident.

**Incident (fictional).** On Thursday 3 September 2026 at about 21:40, Eliya and Nadav, two yeshiva students from Elad wearing kippot and tzitzit, were refused entry to a bar-restaurant on the Tel Aviv promenade. The doorman said, "Tonight is private, list only." Their friend Gal, who is secular and was not on any list, arrived at 21:50 and was seated at once.

**1. Map.**

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Area | Entry to a public place and service there (a restaurant is listed in s. 2) |
| Statute and section | Products and Services Law 2000, s. 3(א) |
| Ground | "דת או קבוצה דתית" (s. 3(א)) |
| Likely exception | "Private event" — a condition not required of others (s. 3(ג1)); "nature of the place" (s. 3(ד)(1)) does not fit a restaurant open to the public |
| Answer | Gal was seated without a list ten minutes later; the restaurant's Instagram showed an open evening that night |
| Forum | Small Claims Court, each claimant separately, up to the ceiling; Magistrates' Court if more is claimed |
| Commission | None for services; parallel complaint possible to the Government Unit for Coordinating the Fight against Racism |
| Deadlines | General limitation 7 years (Limitation Law s. 5(1)); act now for evidence |

**2–3. Burden shift.** s. 6(2): refused, while Gal, not of the group, was admitted in the same circumstances; s. 6(3): a list condition imposed only on them.

**4. Evidence log (extract).**

| # | Collected | By | Type | Original / copy | Proves | Doubts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.9 21:43 | Eliya | Note on phone of the doorman's words | Phone / cloud | Refusal and stated reason | Own note |
| 2 | 3.9 21:51 | Gal | WhatsApp "sat down, nobody asked" | Phone / export | s. 6(2) comparison | Gal is a friend |
| 3 | 3.9 21:52 | Gal | Photo of her table with the time | Phone / cloud | Same evening, same place | — |
| 4 | 3.9 22:10 | Nadav | Screenshot of the restaurant's Instagram "open tonight" | Phone / cloud | Answers "private event" | Post might be edited |
| 5 | 3.9 23:30 | All three | Signed statements | Paper / scan | Facts and times | Written same night |
| 6 | 4.9 09:00 | Eliya | Email asking to preserve CCTV 21:30–22:15 | Email | Preservation | No reply yet |

**5. Tester plan.** Not run: the evidence already contains a natural comparison (Gal). If the restaurant denies the policy, two matched pairs (religious and secular dress, same age, same request for a table for two) would ask on two Thursdays at 21:30; each records only their own exchange with the doorman. Will not do: record staff talking among themselves; photograph diners' faces; post the video before the demand letter.

**6. Demand letter (extract).**

```text

...
3. בשעה 21:50 בערך הגיעה חברתנו, גל ___, שאינה חובשת כיפה ואינה נמנית עם רשימה כלשהי, והושבה מיד.
5. העובדות האמורות מקימות חזקה לפי סעיפים 6(2) ו-6(3) לחוק.
8.1 לשלם לכל אחד מאיתנו פיצוי בסך 12,000 ש"ח;
8.3 לשמור את צילומי מצלמות האבטחה מיום 3.9.2026 בין 21:30 ל-22:15;
```

**7. Memo.** If no answer by 18 September: each files a small claim for ₪12,000 in the Tel Aviv Small Claims Court in October, with Gal as a witness. Legal Aid is available without a means test, but small claims do not need a lawyer. Publication: no post naming the restaurant before the claim; afterwards only proven facts and the court's decision.

Rubric self-score for this model: 18/20 (the exception row loses a point because the "private event" defence would be stronger if they had asked the doorman who was on the list; the tester row loses a point because no repeat check was planned before the letter).
