# Module 18 project: Organisation plan

Write the organisation plan for a real group you belong to or want to start: the legal form you choose and why, the documents that set it up, a year of compliance, a donations policy, the rules on helping other people, and a map of legal help. A new coordinator should be able to run the group lawfully from this file alone. The project is a dry run unless your group has decided to register.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only, or for a group that has authorised you. This is not legal advice — check current rules before filing. The rules in part 5 are our reading of the Bar Association Law, not a lawyer's opinion; have an organised service for the public checked by a lawyer or a clinic.

## What you produce

1. **Form decision** (half a page): the five tests from lesson 18.1 scored with evidence, and your decision — informal group, new amuta, or hosting by an existing amuta.
2. **Founding documents.** For an informal group: a completed [group agreement](../templates/group-agreement.md). For an amuta: name and two alternatives checked on GuideStar, three to six objectives in Hebrew, and the [bylaws checklist](../templates/amuta-bylaws-checklist.md) completed with your model-or-own decision; a list of founders and roles (lesson 18.2).
3. **Registration file** (amuta only): a dry-run application sheet and the first-60-days plan (lesson 18.2).
4. **Compliance and donations** (one page): a twelve-month compliance calendar with names, a donations procedure including the foreign-state-entity check, and donor wording on tax receipts (lesson 18.3). For an informal group: the money rules from the agreement and a monthly report format.
5. **Helping-others policy**: the [boundaries card](../templates/helping-others-boundaries.md) filled in for your group, and your group's five most common requests analysed with the two triggers and four acts (lesson 18.4).
6. **Legal-help map** (half a page): the help ladder for your issue, a referral list with checked contacts, and a one-page brief for a lawyer on one question (lesson 18.5).

Time: about 5 hours.

## Steps

1. **Choose the group** and write its purpose in one sentence.
2. **Decide the form (18.1)** with the five tests.
3. **Draft the founding documents (18.1–18.2).**
4. **Plan the year (18.3)**: calendar, donations, receipts.
5. **Write the helping-others policy (18.4).**
6. **Map legal help and write the brief (18.5).**
7. **Self-assess** with the rubric, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 11 or more out of 12 means the plan is ready to adopt; below 7, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Form decision | Chosen by habit | Tests scored without evidence | Five tests with evidence; decision follows from them; capacity assessed honestly |
| Founding documents | Missing | Agreement or objectives too generic; roles unclear | Group agreement covers purpose, decisions, money, spokespeople, ending; or name checked, objectives specific and lawful (s. 3, no "legal advice"), bylaws decision reasoned, three roles with no s. 32 overlap |
| Compliance calendar | None | Some dates; no names | Every task (fee, assembly, reports, changes, quarterly reports when relevant, certificate) with a date and a person |
| Donations | None | Records only | Records, foreign-state check with s. 6 diligence, correct duties for each case, honest receipt wording |
| Helping-others policy | None or "we help everyone" | Do/don't list without analysis | Card completed; five requests analysed with triggers, acts and savings; standard wording; reviewer named |
| Legal-help map and brief | None | List of names only | Ladder for the issue, checked free contacts, one-page brief with chronology and numbered documents |

This is a condensed model for an invented group. Your plan will be about your own.

**The group.** "הורים לתחבורה נגישה — חיפה", 11 parents of children with disabilities who campaign for accessible school transport in Haifa and help other families with the process.

**1. Form decision.** Money: yes (₪6,200 raised, a ₪50,000 foundation grant offered to amutot only). Contracts: yes (a part-time coordinator). Duration: yes (three years at least). Credibility: yes (the grant; parents want receipts). Capacity: yes (five willing to serve; one bookkeeper). Decision: register an amuta; the group agreement governs until registration.

**2. Founding documents.** Group agreement signed by all 11: the treasurer holds the money in a separate account, spending above ₪300 needs two further approvals, monthly balance posted, assets move to the amuta within 30 days of registration. Name: "הורים לתחבורה נגישה חיפה (ע״ר)", checked on GuideStar (no similar name). Objectives: (1) לקדם הסעה נגישה ובטוחה לתלמידים עם מוגבלות בחיפה ובסביבתה; (2) להנגיש מידע כללי על זכויות בתחום ההסעות והנגישות; (3) לפעול מול הרשות המקומית, משרד החינוך והכנסת לקידום מדיניות בתחום. No "legal advice". Bylaws: own bylaws changing only video meetings, a committee of three for two years, no pay for office holders, and the dissolution clause to a s. 46 public institution; everything else from the model. Roles: committee — Dana, Yusuf, Miriam; audit committee — Avi; books — Lior. Nobody holds two roles.

**3. Registration file.** Dry-run sheet complete; digital address a new group e-mail; Dana's home as the Israeli address. First 60 days: certificate; bank account with two signatories (notified within two weeks); registers in a shared spreadsheet; accountant on Tax Authority registration; first assembly in week 6.

**4. Compliance and donations.** Calendar: fee in February (the low first-years rate); books closed in March; audit committee receives the report by 1 May; assembly 15 May; filing by 10 June with two signatures; certificate application in July; change notices within two weeks. Donations: every donation logged; grants checked for state sources by asking the funder in writing; none so far; if a state-entity grant arrives, quarterly report within a week after the quarter. Receipt wording: "קבלה על תרומה. העמותה אינה מוכרת כמוסד ציבורי לעניין סעיף 46 לפקודת מס הכנסה" until approval.

**5. Helping-others policy.** Offers: monthly information evening, blank templates "לשימוש בשמך בלבד", technical help filling fixed fields for parents who do not read Hebrew, accompanying parents to meetings as support. Never: telling a family what its entitlement is, writing appeals in our words, speaking for a parent at an appeals committee, any payment. Five requests analysed: (1) "Is my child entitled to an escort?" — individual advice: refer; (2) "Help me fill in the transport form" — technical, fixed fields: fine with care; (3) "Come and speak for me at the appeals committee" — quasi-judicial representation: refer; (4) "Can you explain the procedure?" — general information: fine; (5) "Write a complaint for me" — drafting for another: give the template, they write it. Standard wording adopted; Miriam reviews new kinds of help; a volunteer lawyer from a disability clinic holds a monthly session, which the amuta does not direct.

**6. Legal-help map.** For families: Legal Aid for those who qualify (lesson 11.6), the Bar's "שכר מצווה", the Haifa University clinics, a disability-rights NGO. For the amuta: a two-hour limited-scope review of its pre-petition letter to the municipality, by e-mail engagement with a fixed fee. Brief: question ("does the municipality's 2026 transport policy breach its duties, and by when must we act?"), goal, chronology of 9 dated items, 12 numbered documents, deadline (45 days from the decision).

**Mirror test.** The same plan would serve a group of farmers protesting water quotas, a Druze women's leadership forum, a student group for religious pluralism, or a neighbourhood watch in a border town. Only the facts change.

**Rubric self-score for this model:** 11/12. The donations row loses a point because the plan does not yet say who checks the s. 5א "main funding" test at year end.
