# Module 8 project: Publication check

Take one real or realistic publication that names people or organisations — an investigative post, a campaign video, a leaflet, a report — and run the full legal check before it goes out: a defamation scan, an evidence and defence file, a right-of-reply record, a privacy check, a data audit of your group's list, and an online-safety plan. Then write the reply you would send if a demand letter arrived after publication. The project is a dry run unless you are actually about to publish.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Act in your own name only (or in the name of the group that is publishing). This is not legal advice — check current rules before publishing or replying: compensation ceilings change every month with the CPI, and the 2026 anti-SLAPP bills may be tabled again after the elections. Do not publish an accusation only to complete this project, and do not send a right-of-reply request unless you really intend to publish.

## What you produce

1. **A completed pre-publication checklist** using [pre-publication-checklist.md](../templates/pre-publication-checklist.md), sections 0–6, for one publication with at least five sentences that name or point to someone (about 3–5 pages).
2. **The final text** of the publication, after rewrites, with every factual sentence marked with its evidence and defence.
3. **A right-of-reply email** to the main person or body named (drafted; sent only if you will publish).
4. **A data audit** of the list your group uses to collect supporters or volunteers: fields kept and dropped, the s. 11 notice, three security rules.
5. **A reply to an imagined demand letter** after publication, using [reply-to-threat-letter.md](../templates/reply-to-threat-letter.md).
6. **A one-page harassment response plan**: evidence-log format, routes by type of attack, the restraining-order checklist, and safety contacts.

Time: about 4 hours, spread over a week.

## Steps

1. **Choose the publication.** A cause you care about, from any part of the political map. It must name at least one private person or company and one public official or body.
2. **Scan (08.1).** Copy every relevant sentence exactly into section 1 and run the four questions.
3. **Evidence and defences (08.2).** For every medium or high row, write the sting, the evidence, the public-interest reason and the defence paragraph. Send anything that belongs to an authority there instead.
4. **Right of reply (08.2).** Draft the email; record (or imagine) the response and show how the publication reflects it.
5. **Privacy (08.4).** Check every photo, video, screenshot and personal detail; blur, crop or drop.
6. **Your list (08.4).** Audit the supporters' form or spreadsheet under Amendment 13.
7. **Online safety (08.5).** Write the harassment response plan.
8. **After publication (08.3).** Imagine the strongest demand letter the main person named could send. Write the reply, choosing per sentence.
9. **Self-assess** with the rubric, then compare with the model answer.

## Self-assessment rubric

Score each row 0, 1 or 2. 14 or more out of 16 means your publication is ready; below 10, redo the weakest rows.

| Criterion | 0 — not yet | 1 — partly | 2 — done well |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defamation scan | Sentences paraphrased or missing | All sentences listed, questions answered loosely | Exact text; identifiability (s. 3), s. 1 harm, s. 2 publication and fact/opinion decided for each; groups (s. 4) handled |
| Evidence and defences | "It's true" with no evidence | Evidence listed but not matched to the sting | Each sting matched to dated evidence, a public-interest reason and a specific s. 13/14/15 paragraph |
| Rewrites | None, or the message lost | Risky words softened with "allegedly" | Every unprovable fact removed or sent to an authority; opinions framed on stated facts; message kept |
| Right of reply | None | Sent, response ignored | Specific, neutral, real deadline; the response reported fairly |
| Privacy | Faces, addresses or private messages published | Some blurring | Each item tested against s. 2 and s. 18; bystanders, victims and children protected; no private messages without a strong case |
| Data audit | No audit | Fields listed | Excess fields dropped; s. 11 notice written; access and security rules; political-opinion sensitivity recognised |
| Demand-letter reply | Apology for everything, or an angry reply | Correct option, weak drafting | Per-sentence decision; "בלי לפגוע בזכויות"; no admission, no new accusation; deadlines and SLAPP signs noted |
| Harassment plan and neutrality | None; or plan to "expose" attackers | Plan without routes | Evidence log, routes by attack type, restraining-order checklist, safety contacts; mirror test applied to the publication |

This is a condensed model based on a realistic dry run. Your file will be about your own publication.

**0. The publication.** A Facebook post and a two-page PDF by "Haredi Parents for Safe Transport", a group of parents in Beit Shemesh, about school buses: "Contractor's buses failed safety tests — the council knew." Expected reach: about 8,000 people. Published in the group's name.

**1. Scan (extract).**

| # | Exact text | Target | Identifiable | Lowers | Published | Fact/opinion | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Four of the contractor's buses failed the annual safety test in March." | The bus company (a corporation) | Yes, named | Yes | Yes | Fact | Medium |
| 2 | "The council's transport manager, Shmuel R., knew and hid it." | Shmuel R. | Yes | Yes | Yes | Fact (concealment) | High |
| 3 | "The owner is a crook who cares only about money." | The owner | Yes | Yes | Yes | Insult plus implied fact | High |
| 4 | "Sending children on these buses was irresponsible." | The council | Yes (public body) | Mildly | Yes | Opinion on stated facts | Low |
| 5 | Photo of a driver at the wheel, face visible | The driver | Yes | Possibly | Yes | — | Privacy |

**2. Evidence and defences.**

| # | Sting | Evidence | Public interest | Defence | Unproven |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Four buses failed the March test | Ministry of Transport test results received in an FOI answer dated 4.6 | Children's safety; public contract | s. 14 | — |
| 2 | The manager knew and hid it | Email from the manager dated 20.3 acknowledging the results; no evidence of hiding | Same | s. 14 for "knew"; nothing for "hid" | Concealment |
| 3 | The owner is dishonest | None | — | None | All |
| 4 | Irresponsible | Facts in 1–2 | Same | s. 15(4) | — |

Decision: row 2 rewritten to "The council's transport department received the test results on 20 March (email attached). The buses kept running until May." Row 3 deleted. A complaint about the manager's handling was sent to the council's internal auditor and to the State Comptroller (s. 15(8)).

**4. Right of reply.** Email to the council spokesperson and the company on 8.6, deadline 13.6. The council replied that the buses were repaired and re-tested in April. The post now says: "The council says the buses were repaired and re-tested in April; the re-test results we requested under FOI have not yet arrived."

**3. Privacy.** The driver's photo replaced with a photo of the bus without people. No names of children or parents. Screenshots of the parents' WhatsApp group not used (s. 2(5)).

**Data audit.** The group's Google Form asked for name, phone, ID number, children's names and schools. Kept: name, phone, school. Dropped: ID number and children's names. Notice added: "מסירת הפרטים רשותית. הפרטים ישמשו לעדכונים על הקמפיין בלבד ולא יימסרו לאחרים. האחראית: ___, ___@___. ניתן לבקש לעיין בפרטים, לתקנם או למחקם." Access limited to two coordinators with two-factor login.

**5. Demand letter and reply.** Imagined letter from the company: remove the post, apologise, pay ₪169,000. Reply (option A for row 1, noting the deletion of row 3 earlier): the test results come from Ministry of Transport data received under FOI; children's safety is a clear public interest (s. 14); the opinion in row 4 is protected (s. 15(4)); no basis for compensation; any claim will be defended and costs sought. SLAPP signs: 2 of 5 (a company against a parents' group; maximum sum). Not paid; the post stays.

**6. Harassment plan (extract).** Evidence log columns: date, platform, URL, account, text, screenshot file. Routes: threats → police and platform; posting a coordinator's address → platform private-information report and a restraining order if the person is known; false accusations → legal-removal form. Safety contacts: two coordinators, a volunteer lawyer.

**Mirror test.** The same check would apply to a secular parents' group publishing about a Haredi contractor, or to a contractor's association publishing about a parents' group: the same evidence, defences and privacy rules.

**Rubric self-score for this model:** 15/16. The privacy row loses a point because the first draft had included a child's photo before the check removed it.
